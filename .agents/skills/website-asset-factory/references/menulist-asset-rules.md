# MenuList Asset Rules

MenuList website assets must show customer-facing business truth infrastructure.

Use:

- public menu proof;
- Official Business Page proof;
- QR/share/public-surface proof;
- setup, review, and publish workflow proof;
- fictional or founder-approved demo data only.

Avoid:

- generic restaurant stock photography;
- fake customer proof;
- unsupported external-platform sync claims;
- POS, payroll, CRM, campaign, canvas, or ordering UI;
- private tenant data;
- AI-themed visuals.

Primary source: `packages/asset-factory/brand/menulist.asset-context.md`.
