# Quick Start: View Your Menu Analytics

## 1. Set Up Google Analytics (5 minutes)
1. Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Click "Start measuring"
3. Follow the setup wizard
4. Copy your Measurement ID (starts with "G-")
5. Paste ID in MenuListAI settings

## 2. View Your Data (After 24-48 hours)

### View Popular Menu Items
1. Open [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Go to "Reports" > "Engagement" > "Events"
3. Look for "menu_item_view" events

### See Customer Locations
1. Go to "Reports" > "User" > "Demographics"
2. View city and country breakdown

### Track Orders
1. Go to "Reports" > "Engagement" > "Events"
2. Look for "purchase" events
