Part 1: The Core Brief
You are to design and build a complete, premium landing page for a SaaS product called MenuListAI.
Product Description: MenuListAI is an AI-powered platform that lets any business instantly create a stunning digital catalog of their products or services. A user can upload any price list (PDF, JPG, even a phone photo), and our AI automatically extracts all items, descriptions, and prices. The AI can also generate compelling descriptions and beautiful, unique images for each item (either one-by-one or for the entire catalog in a batch). The user then has full creative control in a powerful visual editor to customize the design before publishing and sharing with a link or a custom-branded QR code.
Core Unique Selling Proposition (USP): The #1 message is speed and effortless creation. The platform turns a slow, tedious, multi-day process into a magical, 60-second experience, from a messy file to a beautiful, shareable digital catalog.
Primary Goal of the Page: To get non-technical small business owners and developers to sign up for a free account by clearly communicating the value, building trust, and showing them the powerful, beautiful end-product.
Part 2: Visual Design System & Brand Identity
Aesthetic: The design must be premium, sophisticated, tech-forward, and trustworthy, inspired by the visual language of modern tech sites like Google DeepMind's Gemini page. It should feel like a powerful, intelligent platform.
Core Metaphor: "Light in the Darkness." Use light, color, and glow effects to guide the user's eye.
Use of Space: Extremely generous "blackspace." Every section must have significant padding and feel uncluttered.
Color Palette:

Primary Accent / CTA Color: A vibrant, electric blue: #3B82F6.
Borders & Dividers: A very subtle, dark gray: #374151.
Glow Effects: A soft, semi-transparent version of the accent blue (#3B82F6 at 15% opacity).
Typography:
Font: Inter.
Headlines (H1, H2): Extra Bold weight.
Sub-headlines & Card Titles (H3): Semi-Bold weight.
Body Text (p): Regular weight, with a generous line height (1.7).
Component Styling:
Buttons:
Primary: Filled with the accent blue (#3B82F6) and a subtle bloom/glow on hover.
Secondary: A thin, semi-transparent white border that fills on hover.
Cards:
Background: A dark gray, slightly lighter than the main background (#1F2937).
Style: Slightly rounded corners (rounded-xl) and a subtle border (border-white/10).
Hover State: On hover, the card's border should illuminate with a soft, glowing blue.
Special Cards: The "Pro" pricing plan and the "For Developers" platform card should have a persistent, subtle glowing blue border.
