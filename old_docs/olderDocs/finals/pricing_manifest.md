### **Part 3: The Indian Pricing Explained - Why ₹499 is a Strategic Weapon**

This section addresses the valid concern that the INR Starter plan is "too cheap" and might "degrade the product's value." The reality is the opposite.

**The Goal of the ₹499 Plan:** Its only job is to be a **marketing tool**. It is the most powerful customer acquisition engine we have. It is not designed to be a comfortable home for our users; it is designed to be the **irresistible hook** that gets them to experience our magic and makes them want to upgrade.

**The Psychology of the Price:**

- **The "Sub-₹500" Magic:** This price point is an **impulse purchase.** It requires zero thought or approval. By pricing here, we remove all friction and maximize the number of users entering our system.
- **The "Feature Wall" Strategy:** We are not trying to solve all the user's problems for ₹499. We are intentionally creating a "Feature Wall." After a user falls in love with the product, they will realize they _need_ a **Custom Domain** and to **remove our branding** to look professional. The only way to solve this is to upgrade to the Pro plan. The Starter plan's limitations are its most important business feature.

**Why Not ₹699 or ₹799?**
Moving the price up creates a psychological barrier, turning an impulse buy into a "considered purchase." This would dramatically reduce the number of new users. Even with a higher price per user, our total revenue would be lower because the top of our sales funnel would be significantly smaller. **The ₹499 price will make us more money.**

### **Part 4: The Credit System & Unit Economics - Why We Are Safe**

This section proves that our model is not just strategically sound, but financially bulletproof.
