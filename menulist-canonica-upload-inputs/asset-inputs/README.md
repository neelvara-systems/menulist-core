# Asset Inputs

This folder prepares MenuList visual material for Canonica website and marketing asset work.

## Subfolders

| Folder | Status | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `current-approved-assets/` | Generated MenuList website demo assets | Safe as generated demo visuals. Do not call them real customer screenshots. |
| `private-reference-captures/` | Private synthetic browser captures | Internal planning only. Do not publish without approval. |

## Planning Files

| File | Purpose |

| `asset-source-map.json` | Machine-readable asset/source map. |

## Boundary

Use MenuList visuals to make Canonica concrete, but do not blur product identities.

Canonica visuals should show governed support knowledge moving from product surfaces, docs, FAQs, tickets, and releases into approved support answers and controlled customer-facing surfaces. MenuList screenshots can be the example product surface, not the Canonica product identity.

## Production Proof Rule

Generated MenuList assets are useful placeholders. Final Canonica website proof should come from a real MenuList Canonica production workspace after:

- MenuList is legally approved and onboarded as a Canonica production client;
- the Canonica dashboard has meaningful MenuList knowledge, product-surface, widget, support, and governance state;
- screenshots are scrubbed and approved for public use.
