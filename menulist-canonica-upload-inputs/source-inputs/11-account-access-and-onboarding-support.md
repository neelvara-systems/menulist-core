# MenuList Account, Access, And Onboarding Support

## What Canonica Should Know

MenuList owners and staff can sign in through several supported paths:

- Google sign-in.
- Email and password.
- WhatsApp number and passcode.
- Staff ID and passcode for staff who do not have their own email.

Owners may also claim a business from a MenuList onboarding link, including WhatsApp-led onboarding. The claim flow links the prepared digital menu/business to the owner account.

## Common Owner Questions

### How do I sign in?

Go to `menulist.ai`, choose Sign In, and use the method connected to your account: Google, email/password, WhatsApp number/passcode, or Staff ID/passcode.

### I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page if the account uses email/password. Staff without email should ask the owner to reset access from Users.

### I am staff and just got added.

If the owner gave a Staff ID and passcode, use those on the sign-in page. If the owner used your phone number, enter the phone number and passcode. If the owner used your email, use the setup email or Forgot password.

### I see Access Denied.

The signed-in account may not have access to that store. Ask the account owner or admin to grant access.

### I signed in with the wrong Google account.

Sign out and sign in with the correct Google account. If account merging is needed, contact support.

## What Canonica Must Not Do

- Do not reveal whether another email or phone number exists.
- Do not show or guess an existing password.
- Do not expose Staff ID, passcode, tenant ID, store ID, or owner contact details.
- Do not promise account merge, ownership transfer, or data deletion without support review.

## Support Escalation

Escalate to MenuList support when:

- a business was claimed by the wrong person;
- the owner lost access to the only owner account;
- a staff member cannot reset access;
- a store appears under the wrong account;
- the user asks to merge accounts, transfer ownership, or delete account data.
