# Public Surfaces And Discovery

## Official Business Page

The Official Business Page is the canonical public identity endpoint for a business powered by MenuList.

It is one official link for the business. It is not a website builder. It shows business identity, live status, and a primary path into the menu.

## Public Menu And Customer Actions

The public menu is the customer consumption surface. The Official Business Page is the business identity surface.

The customer-facing surface should show:

- business identity;
- menu content;
- status/freshness where enabled;
- search and section navigation;
- language support where configured;
- customer actions such as call, WhatsApp, directions, photos, and menu.

## Menu Kit

Menu Kit is an auto-generated launch pack of print-ready and social-ready assets owners receive when their menu is published.

It closes the gap between "menu is live" and "customers can scan it."

Frozen kit contents include:

- table tent;
- counter sticker;
- entrance poster;
- delivery bag sticker;
- takeaway card;
- Instagram story;
- WhatsApp status;
- Google Maps upload image;
- placement guide;
- staff script;
- print instructions.

## Digital Screens

Digital Screens extends MenuList into in-store TV/tablet surfaces.

The owner opens a screen link on the TV. Menu content management stays menu management; there is no separate screen CMS.

## Public Discovery Infrastructure

MenuList has shipped structured public discovery infrastructure.

Current production contract includes:

- server-rendered public pages;
- Schema.org JSON-LD;
- robots and sitemap;
- per-store discovery files;
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`;
- agent-readable boundaries;
- public API and POS surfaces where enabled.

## Claim Boundary

MenuList prepares a clearer official public source. It does not guarantee Google ranking, ChatGPT citation, Google Maps placement, or automatic external-platform refresh.

Use careful language:

```text
Search engines and AI systems get a clearer official source to read.
```

Avoid:

```text
MenuList guarantees ranking or updates every external platform automatically.
```
