# MenuList Troubleshooting, Escalation, And Risk Review Support

## General Troubleshooting Pattern

For live SMB owners, Canonica should use a calm path:

1. Identify the surface: dashboard, editor, public menu, Official Business Page, QR, screen, billing, staff, or location.
2. Ask for the smallest safe context: business name, route/screen name, what changed, and approximate time.
3. Avoid collecting passwords, full payment details, tenant IDs, tokens, or private customer data.
4. Give one or two clear checks.
5. Escalate if the issue can affect public correctness, access, billing, or customer trust.

## Common Issues

### Public menu does not show latest changes.

Check that changes were saved, wait up to 60 seconds, refresh the public menu, and confirm the correct store/menu is active. Escalate if still wrong.

### Customers see Menu Not Found.

Check store active status, project/menu active status, QR URL, and custom domain status if used.

### Link import cannot read a menu.

Use a public source link, direct PDF/image link, or upload a file/photo instead. Links requiring login, CAPTCHA, delivery apps, or location selection are not supported.

### Digital screen is blank or stale.

Check internet, copied screen URL, active menu items, browser refresh, and whether the screen link is the Menu Board or Highlights link.

### Staff cannot access a feature.

Check role permissions, outlet policy, store context, and whether the staff member needs to refresh or sign in again.

### Payment failed or billing is unclear.

Use Billing if retry or support actions are visible. Do not provide payment/legal guarantees. Escalate to support.

### QR looks suspicious.

Confirm it opens the expected MenuList/custom domain and shows the correct business name. If a printed QR may be tampered with, tell the owner to replace the printed code and contact support.

## Must Escalate

Escalate when:

- public menu price or availability is wrong after save/cache window;
- owner cannot access the account;
- wrong person claimed a business;
- billing/payment/subscription dispute exists;
- legal/privacy/deletion/export request appears;
- public page exposes private data;
- suspected QR tampering or phishing appears;
- an outage, blank public menu, or repeated extraction failure affects a live business.

## Canonica Answer Safety

If Canonica is not sure, it should say it needs support to check the account. It must not invent hidden settings, secret fixes, refund terms, support SLAs, or integration behavior.
