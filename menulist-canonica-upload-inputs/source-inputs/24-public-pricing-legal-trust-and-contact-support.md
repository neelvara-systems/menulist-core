# MenuList Public Pricing, Legal, Trust, And Contact Support

## Purpose

This source captures support-sensitive public website facts from the live MenuList pricing, trust/security, privacy, terms, refund, and contact pages.

## Pricing Page Facts

The live pricing page frames the offer as keeping one current public menu live, not merely buying software.

Current public plan structure shown on the website:

- Starter: for getting one business menu online quickly. The live site displayed `$290 / yr`.
- Pro: for better customer-facing presentation and owner controls. The live site displayed `$790 / yr`.
- Premium: for brands managing multiple locations. The live site displayed `$1,490 / yr`.

The pricing page also says:

- a 7-day starter setup exists before choosing a paid plan;
- owners can upload, review, publish a starter menu, and use the real link/QR before payment;
- paid plans preserve the same URL and unlock the operational workspace;
- AI/content enhancements are controlled by credits;
- a content credit pack exists for more generated images, descriptions, and translations. The live site displayed `$29`.

## Pricing Answer Boundary

Canonica can explain the plan shape and direct owners to the Pricing/Billing page. Exact prices, currency conversion, discounts, tax, invoices, or account-specific billing should be checked against the live Pricing/Billing page before becoming a canonical answer.

## Terms Facts

The live Terms page says:

- MenuList helps businesses manage their official menu from one place.
- Owners are responsible for safeguarding owner and staff access.
- Staff access should use the correct role and be removed when no longer needed.
- Uploaded content remains the owner's content.
- Generated output belongs to the owner, but must be reviewed for accuracy.
- Subscriptions may renew monthly or annually unless cancelled.
- Razorpay processes payments, and MenuList says it does not store card details.
- Accounts or staff access may be suspended for non-payment, policy breach, or illegal/fraudulent activity.
- Indian law governs the terms.

## Refund Facts

The live Refund Policy says:

- subscription fees are final and non-refundable once the billing cycle starts;
- credit pack purchases are non-refundable once added to the account;
- automatic renewals are non-refundable, so cancellation should happen before renewal;
- cancellation can happen from account settings;
- access continues until the end of the current billing period;
- data is preserved for 30 days after subscription expiry;
- after 30 days, data may be permanently deleted.

Canonica must not promise refunds. Refund questions should use this policy and escalate if the owner has an account-specific billing dispute.

## Privacy Facts

The live Privacy Policy says MenuList may collect:

- account information;
- staff account information;
- business content such as menus, price lists, images, and documents;
- payment information through Razorpay, without storing credit card details;
- generated content stored on behalf of the owner;
- technical/device/security usage data.

The live policy also says:

- business content is not used to train models;
- generated assets belong to the owner;
- public media prepared by MenuList removes original image metadata before storage;
- menu analytics do not collect customer identities;
- optional feedback may include contact details a guest chooses to provide;
- owners can control staff access through roles, store assignments, passcode reset, and session sign-out;
- privacy rights requests should go to `privacy@menulist.ai`.

## Trust And Security Facts

The live Trust & Security page says:

- MenuList does not store plain-text passwords or passcodes.
- Business data is isolated by account/tenant context.
- MenuList does not sell business data to third parties.
- Customer identities are not collected in analytics.
- MenuList runs on Google Cloud/Firebase infrastructure.
- Dashboard, public menu, and official page are served over HTTPS.
- Owners can manage staff roles, reset, deactivation, removal, and sign-out.
- Security reports should go to `security@menulist.ai`.
