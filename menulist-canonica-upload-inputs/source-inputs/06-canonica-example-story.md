# Why MenuList Is The Canonica Example

## Short Story

MenuList is the best internal example for Canonica because it has everything Canonica needs to explain:

- product truth;
- source authority;
- public-facing output;
- owner review;
- page and surface context;
- support boundaries;
- visual proof;
- risk-sensitive claims.

Canonica can use MenuList to show how product knowledge moves from scattered source material into governed support answers, product-surface help, and trusted website proof.

## Canonica Mapping

| Canonica concept | MenuList example |
| --- | --- |
| Product ontology | Menus, stores, projects, outlets, public surfaces, staff roles, screen links, menu versions. |
| Canonical answers | Stable answers about upload, review, publish, public menu, OBP, QR, Menu Kit, Digital Screens, staff, and multi-location. |
| Drift governance | Menu correctness, freshness signals, public cache invalidation, source-version thinking, release/change boundaries. |
| Signal mutation | Customer-reported public issues and owner corrections can become knowledge gaps or review items. |
| Product surfaces | Public menu, OBP, owner dashboard, mobile share, digital screen, Canonica widget on owner routes. |
| Website proof | MenuList public menu and OBP visuals show what Canonica means by product-source-backed support. |

## Website And Marketing Use

Canonica website and marketing assets can use MenuList to demonstrate:

- page-aware widget support on a real product surface;
- product context moving into support answers;
- screenshots from public menu, OBP, owner dashboard, and Canonica dashboard review;
- source-backed support without claiming autonomous support replacement.

Recommended Canonica visual story:

```text
MenuList product surface -> Canonica understands the page -> Canonica returns approved support knowledge -> owner can review gaps and drift
```

## Important Boundary

Canonica must not become MenuList marketing copy.

Use MenuList as evidence and example, but keep Canonica positioned as the Support Knowledge Control Plane.

Do not make Canonica look like:

- a restaurant menu product;
- a QR menu tool;
- a helpdesk;
- a CMS;
- an autonomous chatbot;
- an asset studio.
