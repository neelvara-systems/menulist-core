# MenuList Knowledge Output Targets In Canonica

## Purpose

After MenuList is imported into Canonica, the dashboard should show real reviewed outputs. These targets make the expected output concrete before screenshots are captured.

## Knowledge Base Drafts

Create and review article drafts for:

- MenuList overview and product promise.
- Uploading or importing a menu.
- Reviewing extracted menu content.
- Publishing and updating the public menu.
- Official Business Page basics.
- QR, saved shortcut, and Menu Kit behavior.
- Digital Screens.
- Staff access and permissions.
- Multi-location and outlets.
- Customer feedback and public issue handling.
- External Menu Sync boundaries.
- Website/demo screenshot approval rules.
- Signing in, staff access, and claiming a business.
- Business settings, working hours, temporary status, and custom domain support.
- Billing status, failed payment, enhancement packs, and paid locations.
- Dashboard, analytics, feedback, and Help Center navigation.
- Troubleshooting and escalation rules.

## FAQ Targets

The FAQ layer should cover:

- buyer questions about what MenuList does;
- owner questions about setup and publishing;
- customer-facing public menu questions;
- staff access questions;
- multi-location questions;
- billing and subscription questions only if the source is verified;
- privacy and screenshot questions with conservative answers.

## Canonical Answer Targets

Approve canonical answers only for stable facts:

- MenuList is the official source for restaurant menu and public business truth.
- MenuList does not publish extracted menu changes without owner review.
- Public surfaces should show current approved menu state.
- Official Business Page is a public business truth surface.
- Menu Kit packages shareable deployment assets.
- Canonica screenshots using MenuList production data require approval and privacy scrub.

Keep these as review drafts until verified:

- exact pricing;
- refund or cancellation behavior;
- POS or third-party sync behavior;
- legal/privacy claims;
- support SLA;
- analytics claims.

- locations/outlets;
- feedback;

## Support Signal Targets

Use safe test questions to create signal history:

- "How do I update a menu item?"
- "Why is my public menu not showing the latest change?"
- "What is the Official Business Page?"
- "How do I add another location?"
- "Can a staff member edit the menu?"
- "Can I use a MenuList screenshot in Canonica marketing?"
- "I cannot sign in with my Staff ID. What should I do?"
- "Why does my public menu still show the old price?"
- "Why does my outlet not follow the master price?"
- "My payment failed. What happens now?"
- "My screen is blank. What should I check first?"
- "Can you delete my account data?"

Expected outputs:

- widget questions visible in Canonica runtime history;
- feedback items for at least one helpful answer and one missing answer;
- support board cards for missing or stale knowledge;
- governance review items for risky or unsupported claims.
- support board cards for account access, billing, public correctness, and privacy questions that need human review.

## Website Asset Targets

The Canonica website should be able to show:

- a MenuList product surface with the Canonica widget context;
- Canonica Knowledge Intake with MenuList sources;
- Canonica Product Surfaces mapped to MenuList routes;
- Canonica canonical answer review for a MenuList support question;
- Canonica Activation showing MenuList launch readiness;
- Canonica Support Board showing a MenuList support gap moving into review.
