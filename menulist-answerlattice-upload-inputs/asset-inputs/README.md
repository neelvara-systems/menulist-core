## Boundary

Use MenuList visuals to make Answerlattice concrete, but do not blur product identities.

Answerlattice visuals should show governed support knowledge moving from product surfaces, docs, FAQs, tickets, and releases into approved support answers and controlled customer-facing surfaces. MenuList screenshots can be the example product surface, not the Answerlattice product identity.

## Production Proof Rule

Generated MenuList assets are useful placeholders. Final Answerlattice website proof should come from a real MenuList Answerlattice production workspace after:

- MenuList is legally approved and onboarded as an Answerlattice production client;
- the Answerlattice dashboard has meaningful MenuList knowledge, product-surface, widget, support, and governance state;
- screenshots are scrubbed and approved for public use.
