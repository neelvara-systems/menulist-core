## Pricing Page Facts

The current pricing source frames the offer as keeping one current public menu live, not merely buying software.

Current plan families are Starter, Pro, and Premium, with monthly and yearly variants. Code-source prices are date-sensitive and must be read from the current Pricing/Billing surface before an account-specific answer. At this verification snapshot, the B2C source constants contain:

- Starter: ₹499/month or ₹4,990/year; US source price $29/month or $290/year.
- Pro: ₹1,499/month or ₹14,990/year; US source price $79/month or $790/year.
- Premium: ₹3,999/month or ₹39,990/year; US source price $149/month or $1,490/year.

The pricing page also says:

- a 7-day starter setup exists before choosing a paid plan;
- current Create Menu acquisition is sign-in-first;
- public setup copy describes a 7-day starter setup and plan selection;
- public URLs are designed to stay stable across eligible plan changes, but access and features remain plan/account specific;
- AI/content enhancements are controlled by credits;
- the current Content Credit Pack contains 250 credits and the source price is ₹2,999 or $29.

## Terms Facts

The current Terms source says:

- MenuList helps businesses manage their official menu from one place.
- Owners are responsible for safeguarding owner and staff access.
- Staff access should use the correct role and be removed when no longer needed.
- Uploaded content remains the owner's content.
- Owners retain rights they already hold in uploaded content.
- Generated outputs may be used subject to applicable law, input rights, and relevant provider terms, and must be reviewed for accuracy and rights before publishing.
- Subscriptions may renew monthly or annually unless cancelled.
- Razorpay processes payments, and MenuList says it does not store card details.
- Accounts or staff access may be suspended for non-payment, policy breach, or illegal/fraudulent activity.
- Indian law governs the terms.

## Refund Facts

The current Refund Policy says:

- subscription fees are generally final once a billing cycle starts, subject to applicable law and confirmed billing errors;
- credit pack purchases are generally non-refundable once credits are added or used, subject to applicable law and confirmed billing errors;
- cancel before renewal to avoid the next charge;
- cancellation can happen from account settings;
- the current paid plan continues until the end of its paid billing period;
- account and business data follow purpose-based Privacy Policy retention;
- deletion or anonymization may be limited by billing, security, dispute, or legal obligations.

Answerlattice must not promise or deny a specific refund. Refund questions should use the current policy, mention applicable-law/confirmed-billing-error review, and escalate account-specific disputes.

## Privacy Facts

The current Privacy Policy source says MenuList may collect:

- account information;
- staff account information;
- business content such as menus, price lists, images, and documents;
- payment information through Razorpay, without storing credit card details;
- generated content stored on behalf of the owner;
- technical/device/security usage data.

The current policy source also says:

- business content is not used to train models;
- generated outputs remain subject to applicable law, source rights, and relevant provider terms;
- public media prepared by MenuList removes original image metadata before storage;
- menu analytics do not collect customer identities;
- optional feedback may include contact details a guest chooses to provide;
- owners can control staff access through roles, store assignments, passcode reset, and session sign-out;
- privacy rights requests should go to `privacy@menulist.ai`.

## Trust And Security Facts

The current Trust & Security source says:

- MenuList does not store plain-text passwords or passcodes.
- Business data is isolated by account/tenant context.
- MenuList does not sell business data to third parties.
- Customer identities are not collected in analytics.
- MenuList runs on Google Cloud/Firebase infrastructure.
- Dashboard, public menu, and official page are served over HTTPS.
- Owners can manage staff roles, reset, deactivation, removal, and sign-out.
- Security reports should go to `security@menulist.ai`.
