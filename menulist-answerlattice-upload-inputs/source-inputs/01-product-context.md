# MenuList Product Context

## Canonical Product Identity

MenuList is customer-facing business truth infrastructure for businesses with menus, price lists, catalogues, or service lists that customers need to trust.

MenuList is not only a QR menu tool, not a generic restaurant website builder, not a marketplace, not a POS, not ordering infrastructure, and not a campaign automation product.

The public promise is:

```text
Upload your current menu. Publish one official version customers can trust.
```

The working product pattern is:

```text
Current menu input -> owner review -> official public menu and business surfaces
```

## Primary User

The primary MenuList owner is a non-technical SMB owner or operator who wants customers to see the current menu and correct business details without managing many public places manually.

Owner realities:

- The owner may only have a photo, PDF, existing link, or typed menu.
- The owner may be working from a phone.
- The owner should review before anything becomes public.
- The owner should not need to understand internal extraction, schema, cache, or sync details.

## Customer-Facing Result

Customers should see one current public source:

- public menu;
- Official Business Page;
- QR/share link;
- saved menu shortcut;
- customer actions such as call, WhatsApp, directions, photos, and menu;
- clear freshness and status signals where enabled.

## Important Built Product Capabilities

MenuList has product depth beyond a basic QR menu:

- public menu creation and owner review;
- existing public menu link import for owner-controlled sources;
- Official Business Page;
- Menu Kit print/social asset generation;
- Digital Screens;
- Menu Correctness Engine;
- customer-facing trust signals;
- owner-facing quality signals;
- multi-outlet master/outlet model;
- staff roles and permissions;
- External Menu Sync through signed full-menu snapshots;
- structured public discovery through JSON-LD, robots, sitemap, and LLM discovery files.

These capabilities should be used as evidence for Answerlattice's website and marketing examples, but the public story should stay simple: MenuList turns the current menu into the official customer-facing version.

## Approved MenuList Language

Use:

- official source;
- customer-facing version;
- owner-approved source;
- public menu;
- Official Business Page;
- upload, review, publish;
- one menu customers can trust;
- current menu;
- no technical setup;
- review before publishing.

Avoid:

- AI-powered;
- smart;
- dynamic;
- autonomous publishing;
- automatic Google sync;
- ranking or citation guarantees;
- marketplace crawling;
- works with any POS;
- real-time POS sync;
- restaurant-only positioning.
