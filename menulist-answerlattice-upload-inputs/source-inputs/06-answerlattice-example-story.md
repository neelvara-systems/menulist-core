# Why MenuList Is The Answerlattice Example

## Short Story

MenuList is the best internal example for Answerlattice because it has everything Answerlattice needs to explain:

- product truth;
- source authority;
- public-facing output;
- owner review;
- page and surface context;
- support boundaries;
- visual proof;
- risk-sensitive claims.

Answerlattice can use MenuList to show how product knowledge moves from scattered source material into governed support answers, product-surface help, and trusted website proof.

## Answerlattice Mapping

| Answerlattice concept | MenuList example |
| --- | --- |
| Product ontology | Menus, stores, projects, outlets, public surfaces, staff roles, screen links, menu versions. |
| Canonical answers | Stable answers about upload, review, publish, public menu, OBP, QR, Menu Kit, Digital Screens, staff, and multi-location. |
| Drift governance | Menu correctness, freshness signals, public cache invalidation, source-version thinking, release/change boundaries. |
| Signal mutation | Customer-reported public issues and owner corrections can become knowledge gaps or review items. |
| Product surfaces | Public menu, OBP, owner dashboard, mobile share, digital screen, Answerlattice widget on owner routes. |
| Website proof | MenuList public menu and OBP visuals show what Answerlattice means by product-source-backed support. |

## Website And Marketing Use

Answerlattice website and marketing assets can use MenuList to demonstrate:

- page-aware widget support on a real product surface;
- product context moving into support answers;
- screenshots from public menu, OBP, owner dashboard, and Answerlattice dashboard review;
- source-backed support without claiming autonomous support replacement.

Recommended Answerlattice visual story:

```text
MenuList product surface -> Answerlattice understands the page -> Answerlattice returns approved support knowledge -> owner can review gaps and drift
```

## Important Boundary

Answerlattice must not become MenuList marketing copy.

Use MenuList as evidence and example, but keep Answerlattice positioned as the Governed Answer Infrastructure.

Do not make Answerlattice look like:

- a restaurant menu product;
- a QR menu tool;
- a helpdesk;
- a CMS;
- an autonomous chatbot;
- an asset studio.
