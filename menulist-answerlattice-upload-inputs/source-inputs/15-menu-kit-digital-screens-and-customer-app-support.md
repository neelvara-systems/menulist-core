# MenuList Menu Kit, Digital Screens, And Customer App Support

**Verified:** 2026-07-20 against current Menu Kit/print exports, Digital Screen links/content, Customer App PWA, QR permanence, and public-freshness boundaries.

## Menu Kit

Menu Kit gives owners ready-to-use files to get customers scanning the menu. It can include table tent, counter sticker, entrance poster, delivery bag sticker, takeaway card, Instagram Story, WhatsApp Status, Google Maps image, and a placement guide.

Menu Kit files use the restaurant name and QR code. Owners should download the kit from the project share area and print or post the files.

Important support points:

- QR files should point to the owner's official MenuList destination.
- Owners do not need to reprint when menu prices/items change, because the QR destination stays current.
- Owners should check printed QR codes periodically for tampering or physical damage.
- Answerlattice should not describe Menu Kit as advertising automation; it is operational deployment support.

## Digital Screens

Digital Screens display MenuList menu data on a TV, tablet, or browser screen.

Supported modes:

- Menu Board: full menu with categories, items, and prices.
- Highlights: rotating promotional slides with featured/campaign/custom content and QR.

Owners set up screens by copying the screen link from Digital Screen settings and opening it on the TV/tablet browser. Menu Board is the default; Highlights uses a mode parameter.

Screen content follows menu data:

- adding an item adds it to the screen;
- changing a price updates the screen;
- marking an item sold out removes it from screen output;
- custom highlights uploads can expire automatically.

If a screen is blank, check internet, correct URL, active menu items, and refresh the browser. The screen should show fallback brand content rather than a raw blank state.

## Customer App

The Customer App lets customers add the restaurant's menu to the phone home screen when the feature is enabled and prerequisites are met.

Support only verified account state:

- menu must be published/active;
- store must be active;
- logo/app icon should be available;
- owner can enable installable app where the surface is available.

If a customer sees an old menu in an installed app, close and reopen the app with internet on. The app should fetch current data when reopened or after returning from background.
