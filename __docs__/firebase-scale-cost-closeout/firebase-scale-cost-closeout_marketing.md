# Firebase Scale And Cost Closeout - Marketing Boundary

**Status:** Internal engineering evidence only
**Last verified:** July 17, 2026

Do not turn an internal scheduler optimization into a public feature or a
specific savings claim. Real Firebase cost varies with traffic, region, billing
edition, storage, network, indexes, provider calls, and deployed configuration.

Public product copy may continue to describe reliable menu availability and
owner workflows only where implemented. It must not promise a fixed Firebase
cost, percentage saving, unlimited scale, or zero downtime.
