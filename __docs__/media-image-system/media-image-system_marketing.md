# Media Image System Marketing

## Internal Positioning

MenuList keeps public business images consistent before they reach customers.

## Why It Matters

- public menus stay fast
- menu cards look stable
- owner uploads do not break layout
- business logos remain clear
- image storage and bandwidth stay controlled

## Sales-Safe Framing

Use:

- "MenuList keeps menu images consistent."
- "Images are prepared automatically before they go live."
- "Public menus stay fast on phones."

Avoid:

- "AI-powered image optimization"
- "smart image engine"
- "advanced creative editor"
