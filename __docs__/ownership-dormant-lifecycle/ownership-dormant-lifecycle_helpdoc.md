# Ownership And Dormant Lifecycle Help

## Give someone Owner access

In Staff, assign **Owner** only to a person who should have full operational
permissions. This does not transfer the business account, billing history,
notification recipients, or existing subscription.

## Transfer the business account

Contact `support@menulist.ai` from the current account email where possible.
Provide the business, current owner, intended new owner, and affected
locations. Support verifies both authority and the billing/access implications
before any change. Do not remove the current last Owner first.

## Old or unused account

Old publishing activity may produce a reminder. MenuList does not automatically
deactivate a business or public menu because it has not been edited recently.
Billing, security blocks, explicit outlet deactivation, and account/privacy
requests follow their own documented lifecycles.
