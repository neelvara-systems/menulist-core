## Browsing the Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base contains organized help articles covering every part of the platform. You can browse by topic or let the AI search find what you need.

---

## How to Browse

1. Open the **Help Center** from your dashboard menu
2. Click **Knowledge Base**
3. You'll see categories displayed as cards — click any category to explore
4. Inside a category, you'll find **sections** grouping related articles
5. Click a section to see its articles
6. Click any article to read the full content

---

## Navigation

### Breadcrumbs
At the top of the page, breadcrumbs show where you are:
**Help Center > Category Name > Section Name**

Click any breadcrumb to go back to that level.

### Sidebar (Large Screens)
On larger screens, a sidebar on the left shows the full category tree. You can click any category, section, or article to navigate directly.

### On This Page (Large Screens)
For longer articles, a sidebar on the right shows section headings. Click any heading to jump directly to that section.

---

## Giving Feedback on Articles

Found an article helpful? Click the thumbs up icon. Not helpful? Click thumbs down. Your feedback helps us improve the documentation.

---

## Tips

- **Use the AI search first** — It finds the most relevant article automatically
- **Browse categories** when you're exploring what's available
- **Check related sections** — Similar topics are usually grouped together
- **Bookmark important articles** — Use your browser bookmarks for articles you reference often
