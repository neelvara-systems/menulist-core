# First Trusted Answers Distribution And Evidence Runbook

**Status:** Repository assets ready; founder-led recruitment and evidence collection pending
**Updated:** 2026-07-19

## Distribution Wedge

Sell one bounded outcome to founder-led SaaS teams:

> Bring the ten questions most likely to interrupt your launch. Leave with reviewed answer routes, deterministic checks, a verified widget setup, and a weekly review loop.

Do not lead with the complete governance inventory. Lead with time to first trustworthy answer.

## Channel Doctrine

The July 28 portfolio review of the founder-supplied `distribution 101` article
is recorded in the [Product Portfolio Distribution Insight
Ledger](../../strategy/product-portfolio-distribution-insight-ledger.md).

For Answerlattice, retain only these channel rules:

- the exact audience is AI-built SaaS founders and small product studios that
  already have real support questions and reviewable product truth;
- founder/studio recruitment remains the primary path for the first five
  workloads;
- X may teach a specific support-correctness lesson to that same audience, but
  it is not an algorithm-gaming or follower-growth program;
- YouTube is the strongest durable education hypothesis after the first
  workload is verified: demonstrate first-ten question preparation, canonical
  checks, safe refusal, widget context, release drift, and the weekly review
  loop;
- short-form clips may be derived from a verified walkthrough or static demo;
  they must not substitute for evidence;
- every topic must solve a buyer sub-problem such as stale billing answers,
  launch-question preparation, page-aware support, or release drift;
- measure qualified founders, prepared question sets, useful test results,
  widget verification, explicit outcomes, and return for a second weekly
  review—not broad AI impressions or follower count.

Do not expand posting volume or create partner amplification before the founder
test passes. Do not use fake-US accounts, proxies, aged accounts, engagement
groups, undisclosed affiliate arrangements, guaranteed growth, fixed algorithm
thresholds, or manipulative engagement bait.

## Founder Test

Recruit five founder-led SaaS products that have:

- at least twenty real historical support questions;
- current product docs, owner notes, or a public product website;
- recurring onboarding, billing, permissions, integration, or error questions;
- willingness to review generated drafts and test results;
- permission to use only agreed anonymous evidence.

For each founder:

1. Collect twenty to fifty historical questions with personal data removed.
2. Select the top ten by frequency, risk, and founder interruption cost.
3. Prepare sources through the Pre-Onboarding Kit.
4. Record time to first ten saved tests.
5. Record owner review time for answer proposals.
6. Run the dedicated exact First 10 canonical check and, when needed, capped full-runtime checks.
7. Verify two real product surfaces in the widget.
8. Observe explicit solved/not-solved outcomes for seven days.
9. Record recontact, correction, abstention, and escalation results.

## Studio Partner Test

Recruit up to five small product studios or agencies. One studio is not one Answerlattice tenant: every client product keeps a separate workspace and credentials.

Partner qualification:

- ships or maintains at least two SaaS products per year;
- can supply one real launch workload first;
- accepts owner approval and no-auto-publish boundaries;
- does not require Answerlattice to replace its helpdesk or project manager;
- agrees that public proof requires separate written consent.

Offer the reusable operating kit, not shared customer truth:

- tool-specific pre-onboarding package;
- source and privacy checklist;
- first-ten question template;
- widget install verifier;
- release check runbook;
- consented proof template.

## Evidence Record

Before adding a verified proof entry to `src/data/answerlattice/proofEvidence.ts`, retain a private evidence record containing:

- customer/workspace reference kept outside public code;
- measurement window;
- baseline method;
- question set;
- source set;
- review time;
- test results;
- exact generic or product launch-set identity, suite revision, proof time, and any stale reason;
- explicit outcome counts;
- recontact counts;
- corrections and failures;
- approved public wording;
- consent date, scope, and revocation contact.

The public registry stores only the approved summary and an internal source reference. It must not contain private customer content, tenant/store IDs, credentials, or raw conversations.

## Pass Criteria

Continue the wedge when at least three of five founders can:

- prepare ten relevant questions without founder confusion;
- reach a useful canonical-only test result in one working session;
- identify at least one real support gap or stale answer;
- verify the widget on real product surfaces;
- explain confirmed resolution versus no escalation;
- return for a second weekly review without assisted prompting.

## Stop Criteria

Do not expand distribution when:

- founders cannot provide or review source truth;
- most questions are customer-specific account actions;
- review effort exceeds the support interruption being removed;
- answer tests do not change a launch or support decision;
- explicit outcome collection is too low to evaluate;
- studios repeatedly try to mix client products into one workspace.

## Public Claim Gate

Do not replace example workloads with customer claims until the evidence record is complete and consent is explicit. Recruitment targets, interviews, and private pilot observations are not public proof by themselves.
