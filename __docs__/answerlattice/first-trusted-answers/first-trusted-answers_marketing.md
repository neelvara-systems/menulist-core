# First Trusted Answers Marketing

## Positioning

Use:

> Turn the ten questions most likely to interrupt your launch into tested, owner-approved support answers.

## Safe Claims

- One selected Knowledge Intake can prepare ten product-specific editable questions and governed answer drafts, reducing blank-page setup without making generated text authoritative.
- Canonical-only tests run without an AI provider call.
- Missing coverage remains visible for owner review.
- Explicit end-user outcomes separate confirmed resolution from unverified non-escalation.
- Examples are labelled; customer proof requires consent and evidence.

## Claims To Avoid

- Ten questions automatically become correct answers.
- Passing tests proves every future answer is correct.
- Positive feedback proves permanent resolution.
- Answerlattice eliminates support staff.
- Example workloads are customer case studies.
- Studio partners or customers exist until verified externally.
