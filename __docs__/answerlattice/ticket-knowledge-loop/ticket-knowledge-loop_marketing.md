# Ticket-to-Knowledge Loop - Marketing Boundary

## Safe Positioning

Repeated resolved tickets can become a governed knowledge proposal, so founders review reusable support answers instead of starting from a blank page.

## Safe Claims

- Uses repeated resolution evidence from real support work.
- Keeps ticket evidence linked to a reviewable proposal.
- Can suggest a new answer or refinement depending on current canonical coverage.
- Requires founder review before canonical publication.
- Invalid or ambiguous cases remain pending or are skipped.

## Claims Requiring Real Customer Proof

- Fewer repeated tickets.
- Minutes saved per resolved question.
- Faster time from support issue to approved answer.
- Improved verified resolution.

## Do Not Claim

- Tickets automatically become correct knowledge articles.
- The help center grows without manual work.
- Duplicates are impossible.
- The model understands every resolution.
- Ticket content is fully anonymized.
