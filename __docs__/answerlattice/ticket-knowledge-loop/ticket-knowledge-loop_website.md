## §3 — How It Works Section

### Step 1: Tickets Accumulate

When your team resolves support tickets, Answerlattice quietly tracks which product areas generate the most questions.

### Step 2: Patterns Detected

The system detects when 3+ tickets cluster around the same topic — a clear signal that knowledge is missing.

### Step 3: Knowledge Proposed

AI extracts the common resolution pattern from multiple tickets and drafts a structured help article.

### Step 4: You Approve

Review the draft, edit if needed, and publish with one click. Your knowledge base grows from real support interactions.

## §6 — FAQ

**Q: Will it create low-quality articles?**
A: No. The system requires 3+ tickets about the same topic before proposing. You review and approve everything.

**Q: What if we don't have many tickets?**
A: The system adapts to your volume. Even 10 tickets/month can surface patterns over time.

**Q: Is sensitive ticket content exposed?**
A: No. AI strips personal information before extraction. You review everything before publishing.

**Q: Does it work with existing knowledge?**
A: Yes. The system checks for duplicate content and only proposes genuinely new knowledge.
