# Ticket-to-Knowledge Loop - Website Content Contract

> This is verified copy guidance, not an instruction to publish a new page.

## Recommended Heading

Turn repeated resolved tickets into review-ready answers.

## Supporting Copy

Answerlattice groups repeated ticket resolutions by product area, prepares a governed answer proposal, and keeps the final facts and publication decision with your team.

## Trust Copy

Tickets are evidence, not truth. Ambiguous cases are held for review, new evidence invalidates stale drafts, and no ticket-derived answer is published automatically.

## Prohibited Copy

- "Every ticket trains your support AI"
- "Automatic help-center growth"
- "Zero-touch knowledge creation"
- "Guaranteed deduplication"
- "Fully anonymized ticket processing"
