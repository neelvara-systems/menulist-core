# Support Truth Change Control Marketing Boundary

## Approved Positioning

> Review the answers, evidence, mapped support surfaces, and delivery state connected to a product release before you publish it.

> Answerlattice keeps product changes and governed support truth in one owner review.

## Required Qualifiers

- directly linked or directly mapped;
- owner-reviewed;
- point-in-time evidence;
- affected answers are marked for review, not rewritten;
- compiled channels may require a rebuild.

## Prohibited Claims

- "Every dependency is detected."
- "All support updates automatically."
- "Conflicts are resolved automatically."
- "Your release is guaranteed safe."
- "Every customer surface has received the change."
- "Answerlattice deploys or controls your product release."
- "AI determines the authoritative source."

## Category Boundary

This is governed answer change control. It is not release management, observability, product analytics, documentation automation, or a workflow builder.
