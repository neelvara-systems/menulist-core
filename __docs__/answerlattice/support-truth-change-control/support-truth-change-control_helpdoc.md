# Keep Support Truth Aligned With A Release

When a versioned changelog entry is ready to publish, Answerlattice reviews the support truth connected to the product areas you changed.

When governed source evidence needs attention, the review shows a bounded sample of source names, linked-answer counts, and exact review reasons before you open Knowledge Intake.

## What You Will See

### Answers

Approved answers directly linked to the changed product areas. Answers affected by the new version will be marked for review after activation; Answerlattice does not rewrite them.

### Answer Tests

Current retained checks for directly linked critical questions. Missing, stale, or failing proof is shown for your decision. It does not deploy or block your product release.

### Evidence Sources

When Source Governance is enabled, Answerlattice checks the source records cited by the affected answers. Review any unapproved, due, missing, not-yet-effective, or conflict-marked source before relying on it.

### Product Surfaces

Product pages and workflows directly mapped to the changed product areas, together with visible mapped article, FAQ, and changelog counts. This is mapping evidence, not a claim that every sentence in those documents is affected.

### Delivery Proof

The current source-version and compiled-context state. Direct runtime channels continue from current governed data. Compiled channels require a rebuild after activation because the release marks the bundle stale.

## Your Choices

- **Activate and publish**: activate the release, mark affected answers for review, and publish the linked versioned note.
- **Keep as draft**: leave the release and note private while you review evidence.
- **Open related records**: use the provided links to review answers, tests, sources, product surfaces, or compiled context.

## Important Limits

- Only direct stored mappings are shown.
- Source conflicts are recorded by reviewers, not discovered or resolved automatically.
- A partial badge means the safe lookup cap was reached.
- Delivery proof covers Answerlattice control state, not every external cache or custom client.
