## Positioning

Owner Support Assistant helps a SaaS owner see which support knowledge needs review and what safe action to take next.

It is not positioned as a chatbot. It is a private support operations layer that explains Answerlattice's governed support signals in owner-readable language.

---

- Support Assistant

- support review command surface
- evidence-backed support brief
- review-ready next action

- auto-approve

---

## Value Points

| Point | Safe claim |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer dashboard hops | Owners can ask one operational question and get routed to the right review screen. |
| Evidence first | Answers show the source summary or record behind the recommendation. |
| Governance preserved | Publishing and approval remain in Answerlattice review flows. |
| Cost discipline | The assistant uses summary-first reads and no transcript store by default. |
| Mobile usable | Owners can review a support brief from the responsive Answerlattice dashboard. |
| Confirmed actions | Supported actions require preview, owner confirmation, and existing product write paths. |

---

## Buyer-Safe Summary

Owner Support Assistant gives teams a private way to ask what support knowledge needs review, see the evidence behind the answer, and move to the correct review workflow without bypassing governance.

Do not publish this as website copy until the route, API, cost proof, and mobile verification are implemented.

---

## Sales Boundaries

Allowed after runtime verification:

- "Helps teams decide what support knowledge needs review."
- "Shows evidence and routes changes through review."
- "Prepares supported actions for owner confirmation."
- "Uses Answerlattice's governed answer system."

Not allowed:

- "Automatically fixes support."
- "Publishes approved answers for you."
- "Replies to customers automatically."
- "Changes tickets automatically."
- "Reads every ticket and chat."
- "Replaces a helpdesk."
- "No human review needed."
