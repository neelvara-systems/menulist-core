# Conversation Monitoring Help

Open **Conversations** to review recent Answerlattice help sessions for the active workspace. Use status, priority, mode, date, feedback, tags, and search filters to narrow the current bounded result page.

## Review a conversation

Open the detail view to inspect the user question, answer, citations/context shown by the chat surface, answer feedback, and retry history. Add an internal note for support-team context; the note is private to authorized support operators.

Use status and priority to organize follow-up. These fields do not send a reply or change approved product knowledge. When a conversation reveals a repeated or material gap, move the evidence into the existing governed support-review workflow.

## Images and deletion

Chat images are private support evidence. Do not upload secrets or unrestricted customer data. Deleting a conversation removes the conversation record, but persisted images may remain while Answerlattice lacks safe workspace-wide proof that another session does not reference them.

## Metrics

Conversation counts, feedback rates, top questions, gap indicators, and ROI views are bounded operational signals. They do not by themselves prove customer resolution or answer correctness.
