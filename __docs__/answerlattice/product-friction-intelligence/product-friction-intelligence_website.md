# Product Friction Evidence Website Notes

Public copy may say that Answerlattice groups mapped support evidence by product area, compares completed seven-day windows, and helps founders prioritize what to review.

Public copy must also preserve these boundaries:

- support evidence is not automatically verified truth;
- weighted load is not an accuracy or product-health score;
- the weekly AI summary is advisory;
- entity mapping and support-event instrumentation are required;
- product or answer changes remain human-reviewed.

Do not show invented example volumes as customer proof. Numeric proof requires a verified implementation with the source surfaces, window, denominator, mapping coverage, and outcome definition stated.
