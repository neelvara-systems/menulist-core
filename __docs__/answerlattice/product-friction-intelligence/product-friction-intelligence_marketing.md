# Product Friction Evidence Marketing Notes

## Safe Promise

"Turn mapped support events and approved-answer fallbacks into a prioritized review list for the product areas creating the most support work."

An acceptable supporting phrase is:

> See which product concepts generated the most mapped support evidence and
> inspect the evidence mix before deciding what to change.

## Proof Required Before Numeric Claims

- verified support-signal instrumentation;
- exact product-entity mapping coverage;
- a complete stated UTC window and denominator;
- evidence that the reviewed item led to a knowledge or product improvement;
- separate customer-resolution evidence when making resolution claims.

## Prohibited Claims

- detects every product problem;
- measures product health or customer satisfaction;
- proves answer accuracy or customer resolution;
- requires no setup or instrumentation;
- automatically fixes the product or approved knowledge;
- replaces product analytics, support review, or customer research;
- AI independently determines the correct action.
- identifies the exact root cause of product friction;
- proves a release caused customer confusion;
- classifies every issue as a bug, UX problem, policy issue, or knowledge gap;
- replaces product analytics or customer research with a customer journey map.
