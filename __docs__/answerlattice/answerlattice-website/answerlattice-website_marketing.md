# AnswerLattice Website Marketing

> **Status:** Implemented public acquisition contract; outcome proof still requires real buyers
> **Last audited:** July 19, 2026

## Audience

The primary buyer is a solo founder or small founder-led SaaS team that has recurring setup, billing, onboarding, settings, integration, release, or error questions but no dedicated support-operations function.

Secondary audiences are small support teams and studios managing several SaaS launches. Enterprise governance buyers are not the initial public acquisition wedge.

## Job To Be Done

Help a founder understand, evaluate, buy, and begin AnswerLattice without first interpreting a generic chatbot, helpdesk, or RAG category.

The public path should prove:

1. existing support knowledge can become reviewed answers;
2. known questions can use approved truth before fallback;
3. unknown or conflicting questions remain review work;
4. the product can be installed and tested without replacing a helpdesk;
5. the plan, checkout, trust, and data boundaries are visible before purchase.

## Positioning

Approved public position:

> AnswerLattice is a support layer for founder-led SaaS that turns scattered product knowledge into approved answers, safe fallback, and reviewable support gaps.

Use `governed support layer` as the category. Describe help as available inside the product without implying uninterrupted infrastructure, a human support outsourcing service, or autonomous resolution of every request.

## Evidence Hierarchy

| Evidence | Public use |
|---|---|
| Deterministic seeded demo | Explain the governance loop; never present as a live customer result |
| Current source-backed product pages | Describe implemented capability and explicit rollout gates |
| Pricing source | Show current monthly INR/USD plan values and support credits |
| Trust/security/privacy pages | State implemented controls and current non-claims |
| Real buyer case study | Publish only after measured activation and founder workload evidence exists |

## Claim Boundaries

Do not claim zero hallucinations, guaranteed accuracy, guaranteed ticket reduction, automatic truth improvement, helpdesk replacement, certification, residency, customer adoption, or autonomous account actions.

Do not use `the first 24/7 support layer`; the superlative has no market evidence and the phrase can imply an uptime promise. Use `governed support layer` or `your first support layer`.

Do not treat demo stage completion, conversations, containment, or answer volume as customer resolution.

## Conversion Events

The useful funnel is:

`public route visit -> demo or install evaluation -> pricing selection -> Google sign-in -> valid setup form -> workspace and pending checkout -> payment -> activation proof -> repeated product use`

Public analytics remain consent-gated. Product activation and payment truth must come from authenticated product summaries and provider-confirmed billing state, not website events alone.

## Success Measures

- pricing-to-get-started transition;
- authenticated setup completion;
- valid workspace creation without assisted recovery;
- provider-confirmed paid activation;
- time to first trustworthy answer;
- time to verified widget install;
- first-ten answer-test pass;
- return usage after the first weekly review.

## External Proof Still Required

- real founder comprehension and objection interviews;
- hosted conversion and consent analytics;
- payment-provider test-mode checkout completion;
- indexed canonical/search-result checks;
- one measured founder case study;
- formal legal review before treating the terms summary as a complete commercial contract.
