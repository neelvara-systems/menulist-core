# Multi-Language Articles Marketing Boundary

## Current Claim

No public availability claim is allowed.

For a private design-partner conversation, the accurate statement is:

> Answerlattice has a rollout-gated translation-draft prototype. Customer publication and multilingual answer delivery are not implemented.

## Prohibited Claims

- multilingual help center;
- automatic localization;
- translation accuracy;
- any supported-language count as an available product promise;
- localized widget or AI answers;
- automatic freshness or synchronization;
- model-generated drafts as approved customer truth.

## Reconsideration Gate

Marketing may change only after deployed review/publish, fallback, retrieval, customer-delivery, security, and fluent-review evidence exists.
