# AI Failure Escalation - Marketing Boundary

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-20
> **Publication Status:** HOLD automatic-escalation claims until rollout evidence exists

## Safe Current Claim

When a widget answer does not resolve the question, the user can create an asynchronous support request without re-entering the original question. Answerlattice attaches bounded stored question and product-context evidence, and recurring failures remain review signals rather than automatic truth.

## Claims Not Approved

Do not claim:

- automatic detection is active in every workspace;
- automatic triggers work with no configuration;
- repeated browser failures are independently verified;
- a ticket is answered or resolved in seconds;
- users are automatically notified;
- a typical escalation rate or knowledge-conversion rate;
- the knowledge base writes or heals itself;
- every failure creates a canonical answer;
- Answerlattice is uniquely better than a named competitor without current sourced evidence.

## Proof Required Before Expansion

- hosted allowed-origin widget demonstration;
- measured ticket-creation and replay reliability;
- founder judgment that attached context reduced investigation work;
- verified customer resolution and recontact outcomes;
- representative automatic-evaluator precision/recall and false-interruption tests;
- current competitor evidence for any comparison.
