# Email Notifications Marketing Notes

## Customer value

Answerlattice keeps customers informed after they leave the product while preserving the ticket as the source of truth. Notification content is projected from persisted support evidence rather than accepted from the browser.

## Safe claims

- Ticket confirmations, eligible support-reply notices, and status-update notices.
- Server-derived recipients and content.
- Duplicate suppression and bounded delivery controls.
- A workspace notification test during activation.
- Ticket progress remains available even if email delivery fails.

## Claims to avoid

- Guaranteed delivery, inbox placement, reading, or resolution.
- Two-way email ticketing or reply-by-email.
- Omnichannel notification automation.
- Zero-duplicate delivery under every provider failure mode.
- Notifications as proof that support was useful.

The commercial outcome is continuity after handoff, not higher email volume.
