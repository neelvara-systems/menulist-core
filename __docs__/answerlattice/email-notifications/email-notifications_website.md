# Email Notifications Website Guidance

Email notifications are supporting evidence for Answerlattice's durable human fallback, not a standalone acquisition feature.

## Appropriate placement

- Ticket or escalation feature page.
- Activation/readiness checklist.
- Security and control explanation for server-derived notification content.

## Recommended wording

"Keep customers informed after a ticket is created, answered, or updated. Answerlattice derives each notification from the persisted ticket and keeps the ticket available even when email delivery fails."

## Do not claim

- Reply-by-email.
- Guaranteed delivery.
- Automated resolution.
- A full help-desk email channel.
- Notification volume as support success.

Public numerical delivery claims require provider and production evidence, not source-code verification alone.
