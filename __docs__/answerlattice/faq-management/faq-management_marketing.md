# FAQ Management Marketing Boundary

## Safe Promise

Answerlattice helps a SaaS owner turn repeated questions into short reviewed answers, link those answers to current supporting articles and product context, and see when customers found an answer unhelpful.

## Evidence-Backed Differentiation

- FAQ origin and generation lineage remain visible and system-owned.
- Linked FAQs cannot publish while their supporting article is draft or inactive.
- Product-surface summary matches are checked against the current FAQ before answering.
- Missing or unpublished linked articles are not shown as citations.
- Customer reactions become bounded review evidence; they do not automatically rewrite approved answers.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not claim every FAQ is correct because it has a citation.
- Do not claim automatic learning or automatic truth updates.
- Do not claim zero support tickets or guaranteed deflection.
- Do not claim FAQ generation is race-free without the current transaction and source-fingerprint checks deployed.
- Do not present imported tickets, reactions, or generated suggestions as approved truth before review.
