# FAQ Management Website Content

## Public Capability Statement

Create short approved answers for repeated questions, connect them to supporting product knowledge, and review customer reactions without letting feedback or generated drafts publish themselves.

## Product Detail

- Manual, imported, article-generated, ticket-signal, and Knowledge Intake origins remain traceable.
- Article-linked FAQs require current published source truth before publication.
- Contextual FAQ suggestions recheck the live FAQ before answering.
- Helpful/not-helpful reactions enter a governed review trail.

## Required Qualification

FAQ suggestions require owner review. A citation proves the linked source exists and is eligible; it does not by itself prove that every sentence is complete or correct. Published-answer quality must still be measured with representative Answer Tests and customer resolution evidence.
