# Answerlattice Staff Access Control Marketing Boundary

> Status: Internal product capability
> Last updated: 2026-07-19

Owner-safe team access is part of the paid Answerlattice workspace experience. Public copy may state that workspace owners can assign built-in or custom roles and manage workspace access. It must not imply that role design can be delegated without team-management authority, or claim editable built-in permissions, per-workspace credential control, real-time cross-workspace administration, instant token invalidation, enterprise identity federation, or perfect prevention of every compromised-session scenario.
