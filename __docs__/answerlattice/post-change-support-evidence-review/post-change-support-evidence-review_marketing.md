# Post-Change Support Evidence Review Marketing Boundary

## Internal One-Liner

Compare completed support-evidence windows before and after an approved release or knowledge correction, without turning correlation into a causal claim.

## Owner Value

- Replaces manual date filtering across support evidence.
- Keeps product and knowledge changes connected to the governed topics they affected.
- Makes low evidence, incomplete windows, retention limits, and high-volume saturation visible.
- Adds no recurring dashboard or owner maintenance task.

## Approved Claims

- `Compare support evidence around an approved change.`
- `See exact ticket, negative-feedback, and escalation counts for directly linked product topics.`
- `Know when the evidence is incomplete or too limited to interpret.`

## Prohibited Claims

- `Proves your release reduced support pain.`
- `Measures product impact or customer success.`
- `Shows affected users, churn, revenue, activation, or abandonment.`
- `Automatically identifies successful releases.`
- `Finds the root cause of customer friction.`

## Competitive Boundary

This is not product analytics. Its value comes from governed release and answer-change identity, direct ontology links, retained support evidence, explicit incompleteness, and owner-controlled interpretation.

## Sales Demonstration

1. Open Product Friction Evidence without triggering a comparison read.
2. Explicitly load recent completed changes.
3. Select one change with a complete after window.
4. Show exact counts and fixed limitations.
5. State that the result is correlation, not causation.
