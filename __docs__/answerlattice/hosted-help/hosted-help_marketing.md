# Hosted Help Center Marketing Boundary

**Status:** Internal claim guidance  
**Last verified:** July 18, 2026

## Safe claim

Answerlattice can publish reviewed Knowledge Base, FAQ, and changelog content on a supported client-owned help hostname with public navigation, search, canonical URLs, sitemap/robots controls, and workspace-scoped domain resolution.

## Proof points

- approved/public content only;
- exact domain-to-workspace registry;
- bounded public payloads;
- direct article links and client-side search;
- configurable indexing;
- no separate public ticket or conversation exposure;
- no shared CDN caching of request-specific HTML admission outcomes.

## Claims to avoid

- any custom domain works;
- instant DNS activation;
- automatic SEO growth;
- all connected sources are published;
- a citation or published article is automatically correct;
- zero downtime or universal availability;
- provider conflicts prove domain ownership;
- full documentation CMS replacement.

## Positioning role

Hosted Help is a deployment surface for governed answers. It is not Answerlattice's core differentiation by itself and should not be positioned as a generic website or chatbot builder.
