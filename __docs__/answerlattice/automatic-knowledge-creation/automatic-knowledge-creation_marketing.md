# Automatic Knowledge Creation - Marketing Boundary

## Safe Positioning

Answerlattice turns repeated support friction into review-ready knowledge proposals. It can prepare a draft, show the product area and evidence summary, and keep publication under founder control.

## Safe Claims

- Repeated support signals can become a governed review queue.
- Drafts reduce blank-page authoring work.
- Canonical knowledge changes require human approval.
- Failed or uncertain work remains visible instead of being silently published.
- Existing help desks can remain the conversation system while Answerlattice governs answer changes.

## Claims Requiring Customer Evidence

- Time saved per week.
- Reduction in repeat tickets.
- Faster documentation maintenance.
- Higher first-contact resolution.
- Lower cost per verified resolution.

## Do Not Claim

- The knowledge base improves by itself.
- Every unanswered question becomes a correct article.
- Ticket repetition proves the answer.
- Draft score equals accuracy.
- No manual effort is required.
- The system cannot hallucinate.
