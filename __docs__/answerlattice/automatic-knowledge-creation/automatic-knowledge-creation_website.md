# Automatic Knowledge Creation - Website Content Contract

> This file defines verified public wording. It does not require an immediate website change.

## Recommended Heading

Turn repeated support friction into answers you can review.

## Supporting Copy

Answerlattice groups recurring support signals into governed proposals, prepares a draft when there is enough structured context, and keeps every knowledge change under human approval.

## Proof Points

- Entity-scoped proposal queue.
- Draft and failed states remain visible.
- Approved canonical answers are changed through a server-owned governance transaction.
- Unknown or ambiguous evidence does not become automatic truth.

## Required Qualification

Drafts are starting points. Customers must verify facts, applicability, source authority, and privacy before approval.

## Prohibited Copy

- "Self-writing knowledge base"
- "Automatically correct documentation"
- "Zero maintenance"
- "Guaranteed accurate"
- "Every ticket teaches the AI the truth"
