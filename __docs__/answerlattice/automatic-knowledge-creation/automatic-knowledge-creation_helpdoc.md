# Review Knowledge Proposals

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-20

Answerlattice can group repeated support friction into a knowledge proposal and prepare a draft for review. A proposal is not a published answer.

## Review Flow

1. Open **Knowledge Governance** and select **Signal-to-Knowledge Queue**.
2. Open a pending proposal.
3. Review the linked product entity, signal or ticket counts, target answer, and review reason.
4. Generate or regenerate the draft when its state is pending or failed.
5. Check every factual statement against approved product sources.
6. Add or correct plan, role, version, region, state, warning, and prerequisite details.
7. Approve only when the proposed canonical answer is complete and current, or reject the proposal.

## Evidence Meaning

Ticket-resolution drafts may show an `Extractor score` review aid. Ordinary signal-cluster proposals show transparent ticket, negative-chat, and escalation counts instead of an overall signal-strength percentage. Neither is proof that the answer is correct, complete, current, or safe to publish.

## When to Reject

Reject or leave pending when:

- the evidence describes different problems;
- tickets contain customer-specific exceptions;
- the correct answer is not present in approved sources;
- more than one canonical answer could apply;
- product version or plan applicability is unclear;
- the proposed content would promise unsupported behavior.

## Privacy Note

Connected support content may contain customer data. Answerlattice applies bounded sanitization and obvious credential/contact redaction, but this is not complete DLP. Enable model processing only when your provider and workspace data-handling policy allow it.
