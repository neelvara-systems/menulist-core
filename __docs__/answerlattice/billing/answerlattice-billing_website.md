# Answerlattice Billing - Website Content Contract

> **Version:** 1.0.1  
> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-19  
> **Audience:** Website / Product

## Public Surfaces

- Answerlattice pricing may show only plans and support-credit packs derived from maintained product constants.
- Get Started may begin a monthly hosted Razorpay checkout after account and workspace admission.
- Public pages must distinguish checkout creation from activated paid entitlement.
- Billing management and transactions remain authenticated workspace surfaces.

## Required Wording

- Use **Continue to secure checkout** or equivalent for provider handoff.
- Use **Payment pending** until payment verification is complete.
- Use **Billing permission** rather than implying every team member can see financial records.

## Prohibited Wording

- Production-certified payments without hosted evidence.
- Guaranteed recovery, zero failures, or zero duplicate charges.
- Instant activation merely because a checkout was created.
- Refund or account-action capabilities not implemented by Answerlattice.

## Current Decision

No public website change is required by the Feature 30 hardening. The changes reduce browser data exposure, strengthen workspace/product read admission, normalize provider-hosted links, and prevent plan mutation when current billing truth is unavailable without changing plans, prices, checkout entry points, or public conversion copy.
