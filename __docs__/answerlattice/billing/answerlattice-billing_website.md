# Answerlattice Billing - Website Content Contract

> **Version:** 2.0.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-24
> **Audience:** Website / Product

## Public Surfaces

- Answerlattice pricing may show only plans and support-credit packs derived from maintained product constants.
- Get Started may begin a monthly hosted Razorpay checkout after account, workspace, billing-profile, regional-pricing, and tax admission. Yearly plans remain available through the authenticated Billing plan flow.
- Billing country determines INR or USD checkout. Public pages may show both maintained regional prices for comparison but cannot imply that currency is a preference selector.
- Public pages must distinguish checkout creation from activated paid entitlement.
- Billing management and transactions remain authenticated workspace surfaces.

## Required Wording

- Use **Continue to secure checkout** or equivalent for provider handoff.
- Use **Payment pending** until payment verification is complete.
- Use **Billing permission** rather than implying every team member can see financial records.
- Use **Available in Billing** for issued invoices and credit notes. Treat email and consented WhatsApp as delivery conveniences, not the only document copy.

## Prohibited Wording

- Production-certified payments without hosted evidence.
- Guaranteed recovery, zero failures, or zero duplicate charges.
- Instant activation merely because a checkout was created.
- Owner-initiated instant refund claims. Refund requests remain a support/provider process; signed settlement and linked credit-note handling are server-side.
- GST, export, e-invoicing, or international availability claims before the required legal configuration is enabled and verified.

## Current Decision

Public pricing and setup copy must use Launch, Growth, and Studio; show maintained INR and USD amounts without a customer-controlled currency selector; explain that billing country determines checkout currency; and avoid implying a free live workspace. Detailed invoice, tax, refund, and delivery mechanics stay in Billing, owner help, and legal surfaces rather than becoming the primary marketing promise.
