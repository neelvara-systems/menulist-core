# Answerlattice Billing - Marketing Boundary

> **Version:** 2.0.0
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-24
> **Audience:** Product / Marketing

## Safe Claims

- Transparent monthly and yearly Answerlattice plans and one-time support-credit packs.
- Billing country determines INR or USD checkout; customers do not select an unsupported regional price.
- Billing access is restricted to the Owner or an approved billing role.
- Interrupted checkouts and provider callbacks have bounded recovery paths.
- Subscription and support-credit application is designed to be idempotent.
- Billing history is scoped to the current Answerlattice workspace.
- Issued invoices and credit notes remain available in Billing; configured email and eligible consented WhatsApp delivery are supporting channels.

## Claims Requiring External Evidence

- Payments are production-certified.
- Every payment recovers automatically.
- Zero duplicate charges.
- A specific payment success, renewal, recovery, or uptime rate.
- Production-certified refund turnaround or refund approval.
- Any guarantee based only on local source or emulator tests.

## Product Boundary

Billing enables Answerlattice access; it is not the product differentiator. Public positioning should lead with governed support truth, trustworthy answers, and founder workload reduction. Do not market Answerlattice as a payment, accounting, or subscription-management platform.

Do not publish tax-registration, GST, LUT, e-invoicing, or international-availability claims until the corresponding owner/accountant configuration is complete and production readback is verified.
