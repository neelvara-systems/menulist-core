# Website Notes

The public website can describe Activation as:

"A controlled-launch checklist for your support layer: approved content, Product Pages & Flows, Trusted Answers, recent widget/context proof, safe origins, and fallback-evidence checks in one place."

Do not describe this feature as proof that customers were resolved. The product proves current setup and retained evidence, then asks the founder to run the real known-answer and fallback path.

Do not mention internal collection names or Firebase mechanics on the public website.

The first-value timestamps do not require a website change. They are authenticated technical evidence, not a public proof point, until real customer activation data supports a bounded claim.
