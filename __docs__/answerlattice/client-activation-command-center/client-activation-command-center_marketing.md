# Client Activation Command Center Marketing Notes

Answerlattice gives SaaS teams a clear launch checklist for governed support setup and controlled customer-path testing.

Positioning:

- One place to see whether support content, product surfaces, canonical answers, widget setup, page context, governance summaries, and signal-source testing are ready.
- Product owners do not need to remember tenant IDs, store IDs, or Firestore paths.
- The readiness page is built on compact summaries, so it stays cost-conscious as support content grows.
- First-client launch proof keeps integrations and extra distribution behind the core governed-answer loop.
- Current widget and page-context evidence expires instead of staying green forever.
- Ready-to-test labels keep configuration proof separate from verified customer resolution.

Avoid claims that Answerlattice automatically fixes every support gap. The product shows readiness and routes owners to the right management surface.
