# Help Doc: Launching Answerlattice

Open Activation from the Answerlattice dashboard.

Use the first-client launch proof and checklist from top to bottom:

1. Confirm your workspace profile, product URL, support email, and license.
2. Add help content.
3. Map product surfaces.
4. Review product entities and canonical answers.
5. Create or confirm the widget key.
6. Lock allowed origins.
7. Install the widget in your product.
8. Pass page context after route changes.
9. Test at least one fallback or ticket signal.
10. Check trust, coverage, and compiled context status.

When the first-client launch proof is clear, Answerlattice is ready for controlled customer traffic. Check Signal Queue before wider rollout to confirm generated proposals are useful. Keep Jira, native helpdesk connectors, and broad distribution out of the launch until this proof is complete.
