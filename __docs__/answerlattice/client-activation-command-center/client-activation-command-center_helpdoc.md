# Help Doc: Launching Answerlattice

Open Activation from the Answerlattice dashboard.

Use the first-client launch proof and checklist from top to bottom:

1. Confirm your workspace profile, product URL, support email, and license.
2. Add help content.
3. Map product surfaces.
4. Review product entities and canonical answers.
5. Create or confirm the widget key.
6. Lock allowed origins.
7. Install the widget in your product and open it so runtime proof is no older than seven days.
8. Pass page context after route changes and confirm the current context appears.
9. Ask one real known question and verify the approved answer and evidence.
10. Ask one unresolved question, submit the fallback, and inspect the resulting ticket context.
11. Check the resulting knowledge signal in Signal Queue.
12. Check trust, coverage, and compiled context status.

When the first-client launch proof is clear, configuration and retained evidence are ready for controlled customer-path testing. The green checks do not prove that a customer was resolved. Complete the manual known-answer and fallback path before relying on customer traffic, then check Signal Queue before wider rollout. Keep Jira, native helpdesk connectors, and broad distribution out of the launch until this proof is complete.

The percentage on Readiness Metrics is setup progress, not a launch decision. Use the factual launch-check count and the controlled-customer-testing message as the gate. A notification test is successful only after the configured support email passes validation and delivery succeeds; a compiled-context rebuild that finishes in a non-ready state remains a review item.
