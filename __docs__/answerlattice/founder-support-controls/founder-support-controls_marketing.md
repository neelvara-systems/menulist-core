# Founder Support Controls - Marketing Notes

> **Audience:** Internal sales and product

## Positioning

Founder Support Controls prove that approved support remains correct as a SaaS product changes.

## Buyer Outcomes

- Test the questions users will ask before launch.
- Recheck affected answers after a release.
- Show a temporary known-issue notice without rewriting permanent documentation.
- Use trusted plan and role context when the host product signs it.
- Keep debugging evidence attached without buying another recording system.
- Export approved support knowledge without exposing secrets.

## Allowed Claims After Runtime Verification

- Reusable answer tests with source and version evidence.
- Release-scoped checks and owner-reviewed rollback proposals.
- Contextual, expiring known-issue notices.
- Optional signed visitor context.
- Support-safe evidence links.
- Owner-triggered approved-knowledge export.

## Forbidden Claims

- Automatic quality guarantee.
- Automatic rollback or incident resolution.
- Full status page or subscriber notification system.
- Session replay or product analytics.
- Private account-data retrieval.
- Export of all support/customer data.

## Sales One-Liner

Test support before users rely on it, and keep the right message visible when the product changes.
