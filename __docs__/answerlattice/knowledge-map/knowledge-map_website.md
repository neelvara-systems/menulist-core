# Knowledge Map Website Truth

The Knowledge Map is buyer-relevant as a governance capability, not a homepage category change.

Approved product-page wording:

> Map product entities, approved-answer coverage, and review risk without turning private support knowledge into a public graph.

The website may call this a visual product-knowledge map in supporting copy.
The navigation and product feature name remain `Knowledge Map`. Do not claim a
customer-demand overlay, owner-editable hierarchy, automatic product
organisation, or dedicated Product Map route unless those capabilities are
separately implemented and verified.

Approved hosted-help wording:

> Readers can switch an article into a topic map and jump directly to the section they need.

Approved connected-owner wording:

> Product Friction Evidence can open the selected product area in Knowledge Map, and Knowledge Map can carry that validated product-area context into governed answer review.

The homepage may present `Knowledge Map` as one step in the owner decision
system after Daily Brief. The Product and Knowledge Governance pages own the
deeper explanation.

Do not present the feature as a generic mind-map builder, enterprise-search graph, or autonomous knowledge model.
