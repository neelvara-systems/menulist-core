# Knowledge Map Marketing Boundary

## Approved Description

See product knowledge, answer coverage, and review risk in one governed map.

An acceptable supporting explanation is:

> See how Answerlattice understands your approved product concepts and where
> support knowledge needs inspection.

## Supporting Claims

- Uses existing approved Answerlattice entities and relationships.
- Shows where canonical-answer coverage is missing.
- Keeps internal governance data separate from public article topic maps.
- Does not use model-generated relationships as product truth.

## Prohibited Claims

- “Understands every document automatically.”
- “Guarantees complete or correct documentation.”
- “Creates a perfect knowledge graph.”
- “Shows the complete truth of your product.”
- “Automatically organises every product correctly.”
- “Shows customer demand across every feature.”
- “Replaces product analytics.”
- “Automatically approves product relationships.”
- “Exposes your full support brain to customers.”
- “Eliminates support review.”

The map improves comprehension. It does not independently prove answer correctness.
