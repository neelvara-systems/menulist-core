# Workspace Lifecycle Internal Marketing Boundary

## Safe statements

- Workspace closure is reviewed and access is denied before erasure begins.
- Answerlattice supports a recovery window and bounded deletion workflow.
- API/widget credentials are revoked during closure.
- Permanent erasure is gated by billing, export, retention, and legal-hold review.

## Unsafe statements

- One-click deletion.
- Instant deletion everywhere.
- Every record is deleted.
- Billing is automatically cancelled or refunded.
- Support Truth Export is a complete legal export.
- Erased workspaces can be restored.
- Production deletion is certified without a recorded QA/production rehearsal.

This feature is a trust and operations control, not an acquisition headline.
