> **Status:** IMPLEMENTED

---

## Positioning

Repeated Reply Import helps Answerlattice own the layer before the support queue.

SupportLayer-style helpdesk products help teams manage queues, agents, SLAs, and assignments. Answerlattice should instead help a founder convert repeated manual answers into governed support knowledge before repeated questions become a queue.

---

## Message

> Turn the answer you keep typing into a reviewed support answer.

Supporting copy:

> Paste a repeated user question and the answer you already send. Answerlattice prepares a FAQ draft and a canonical answer proposal, then keeps approval in your hands.

---

## Differentiation

| Helpdesk framing | Answerlattice framing |
| --- | --- |
| Reply templates for agents | Founder-approved answer drafts |
| Email-to-ticket | Repeated reply to support knowledge |
| SLA reporting | Approved-answer coverage and support gaps |
| Queue ownership | Reduce what becomes a queue |

---

## Do Not Claim

- AI-powered inbox automation
- automatic customer replies
- support team productivity suite
- SLA ownership
- agent workload reduction
- native helpdesk sync
