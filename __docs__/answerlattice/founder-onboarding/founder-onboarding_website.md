## 5. FAQ Section

**Q: What document formats can I upload?**
A: PDF, images, website URLs, YouTube videos, Google Drive files, and plain text. Your existing documentation works as-is.

**Q: Does the AI answer immediately or do I need to review everything first?**
A: Immediately. Your AI support starts working the moment articles are published. Structured knowledge builds in the background — you review and approve gradually.

**Q: What if the auto-generated answers are wrong?**
A: Generated answers are drafts, not live canonical answers. They go into a review queue. Your AI uses the original article content (via RAG) for immediate answers, so customers always get accurate help.

**Q: How many documents can I upload?**
A: Up to 300 articles per import. Most companies need 50-200 articles for comprehensive coverage.
