# Drift Governance Marketing Boundary

## Safe claims

- Flags approved answers that may need review after product or support evidence changes.
- Shows specific probable drift reasons.
- Requires human review before drift is cleared.
- Checks all entities bound to an approved answer.
- Keeps drift review connected to canonical answer governance and audit history.

## Unsafe claims

- Guarantees that every stale or incorrect answer is detected.
- Automatically fixes product knowledge.
- Proves answer correctness from feedback or ticket volume.
- Continuously monitors every connected source in real time.
- Eliminates human knowledge review.
- Uses an opaque AI score to certify support truth.

## Commercial role

Drift governance supports the Answerlattice promise of current, governed answers. It is not a standalone analytics product and should be sold with canonical answers, releases, signals, and human review.
