# Autonomous Browser and Account-Changing Actions - Help

> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-20

Answerlattice does not control your product or perform account-changing actions.

Guided Resolution can:

- explain an approved procedure;
- highlight an exact control your product has marked;
- scroll that control into view;
- wait for a fixed event your product reports for the configured transition;
- open support with the blocked-step context.

The end user clicks, enters, selects, submits, and confirms product actions. A matching event advances the guide but is not independent proof of backend success. Do not include secrets, form values, customer records, tokens, or sensitive state in semantic target or event names.
