### Section 1: The Problem

**Headline:** Most AI support tools leave you guessing.

**Body:**
You deployed an AI support agent. Users are asking questions. But you have no idea if the answers are actually correct. You check conversation logs manually. You hope for the best. You find out about problems when users complain.

That's not how infrastructure should work.

---

### Section 2: The Solution

**Headline:** Trust, not analytics.

**Body:**
Answerlattice's Trust Dashboard shows you exactly four things:

- **Coverage** — What percentage of questions does AI handle?
- **Resolution** — How often does AI resolve without escalation?
- **Drift** — Are any answers going stale?
- **Health** — What's the overall health of your knowledge?

No charts. No dashboards. No configuration. Just the answer to: "Is this working?"

---

### Section 3: Fix What Matters

**Headline:** See exactly where AI fails.

**Body:**
The Trust Dashboard shows your top 5 failing knowledge areas — ranked by impact, not volume. Each entity shows query count, escalation rate, and reliability score. Click to fix. Knowledge improves. Trust score goes up. Automatically.

---

### Section 4: Escalation Intelligence

**Headline:** Understand WHY AI fails, not just that it fails.

**Body:**
Every escalation is classified into one of five categories: knowledge gap, low confidence, entity mismatch, retrieval failure, or user preference. This tells you whether to write more documentation, improve entity mapping, or accept that some users prefer humans.

---

### Section 5: Zero Setup

**Headline:** Works from the first query.

**Body:**
No configuration. No thresholds to set. No dashboards to build. The Trust Dashboard computes metrics automatically from every AI interaction and shows you the result every morning.
