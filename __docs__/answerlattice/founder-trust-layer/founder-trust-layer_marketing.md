# Answer Evidence Marketing Notes

## Safe Promise

"See whether approved answers were used, where escalation occurred, which answers may have drifted, and which product areas still lack an approved answer."

## Proof Required Before Numeric Claims

- A complete schema-v2 source window.
- Verified instrumentation for the relevant deployment surface.
- Explicit outcome volume when discussing confirmed resolution.
- Answer Tests and human review when discussing answer quality.

## Prohibited Claims

- Overall AI accuracy from these metrics.
- Guaranteed resolution or deflection.
- A single trust score.
- Product health inferred from support volume.
- Zero maintenance or automatic correctness.
