# Weekly Digest Website Guidance

Weekly Digest is supporting proof of Answerlattice's governed improvement loop. It should not define the category or appear as a generic analytics product.

## Approved Copy

> See the repeated questions and answer gaps from the latest settled week, with incomplete evidence shown before you act.

## Required Qualification

- Comparisons require two complete seven-day source windows.
- Feedback is recorded feedback, not inferred satisfaction.
- Recommendations create review work; they do not change approved truth.
- The current product does not claim automatic email delivery.

## Placement

Use inside product-detail, support-control, or governance explanations after canonical answers and safe fallback are understood. Do not create a standalone acquisition page unless customer evidence proves the digest independently drives purchase or retention.
