# Weekly Digest Marketing Boundaries

## Safe Position

> Review the latest settled week of repeated support questions, recorded feedback, and answer gaps before deciding what to improve.

## Safe Claims

- deterministic completed-week summary;
- workspace-scoped support evidence;
- source-day completeness shown;
- stale and partial summaries identified;
- review links respect current permissions;
- no automatic change to approved answers.

## Unsafe Claims

- AI-generated executive intelligence;
- guaranteed support resolution;
- customer satisfaction score;
- automatic weekly email;
- real-time monitoring;
- complete analysis when source days are missing;
- autonomous knowledge improvement;
- verified ticket deflection.

## Proof Before Numbers

Do not publish time saved, resolution uplift, accuracy, adoption, or support-volume reduction until real customer evidence measures those outcomes.
