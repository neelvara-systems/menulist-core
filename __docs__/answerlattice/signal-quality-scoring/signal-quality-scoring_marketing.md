# Signal-Quality Scoring - Marketing Boundary

> **Status:** No public scoring claim approved

## Safe Claim

Answerlattice turns repeated support friction into a review queue with visible evidence counts and human approval.

## Unsafe Claims

Do not claim:

- AI-ranked knowledge gaps;
- accurate signal-quality scores;
- intelligent severity scoring;
- calibrated proposal confidence;
- usage-based answer confidence;
- automatic prioritization of the most important issue;
- automatic knowledge improvement.

The reserved flag, legacy weighting utility, proposal score, and serve volume are not proof of answer correctness.
