## July 18 parity verdict

No public-site copy change is required for Feature 1. The current claims remain within source truth:

- drafts wait for human approval;
- approved answers are the official support object;
- missing or stale guidance becomes review work;
- Answerlattice is not presented as an autonomous helpdesk replacement.

The new stale-proposal protection is an operational trust control. It may be included in future technical proof or security material, but it is not a primary acquisition claim without customer evidence.
