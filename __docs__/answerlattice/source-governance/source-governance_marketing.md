# Source Governance Marketing And Sales Notes

> Status: Internal only; do not publish as a standalone claim until the feature flag is enabled and real-customer evidence exists.

## Elevator Pitch

Answerlattice helps a SaaS founder record which support sources are trusted, where they apply, and what conflicts before those sources shape an approved answer.

## Customer Problem

Founders usually have several plausible answers: current docs, an old macro, a release note, a ticket reply, and memory. Retrieval can find all of them, but retrieval alone cannot decide which one should govern support.

## Approved Positioning

- Review source authority before approving an answer.
- Keep private evidence out of public citations.
- Record plan, role, region, and version applicability.
- Hold conflicting evidence for human resolution.
- Preserve an audit trail of source decisions.

## Do Not Claim

- Automatic truth detection.
- Guaranteed correctness.
- Automatic conflict resolution.
- Continuous source synchronization.
- Compliance certification.
- That approved evidence is automatically published truth.

## Sales Discovery Questions

1. When documentation and ticket replies disagree, who decides which answer wins?
2. Can you tell which plans or product versions a source applies to?
3. Can your team see why an answer was approved?
4. Do old macros or PDFs still influence support after the product changes?

## Packaging

Source Governance belongs inside the governed-answer workflow. It should not be sold as a separate policy-management product.
