# Support Truth Export - Marketing Claims

## Safe Claims

- Download a bounded private JSON package of approved support truth.
- Carry canonical answers, scope/version context, approved citations, product surfaces, published help content, changelog links, and releases into a controlled review or migration workflow.
- Keep tickets, conversations, embeddings, secrets, and raw audit history outside the package.
- Every successful package generation is recorded with metadata-only audit evidence.

## Claims Requiring Hosted Proof

- exact generation time for a real customer workspace;
- compatibility with a named downstream tool;
- reliable mobile download behavior;
- successful import or migration outcomes.

## Prohibited Claims

- complete legal or compliance export;
- one-click backup/restore;
- exports every workspace record;
- automatic migration to any help desk or knowledge platform;
- guaranteed zero sensitive content regardless of what an owner approved into source content;
- scheduled or continuous export.

## Positioning

This feature supports Answerlattice's governed-answer control-plane position. It is portability for approved support truth, not a generic reporting or data-management product.
