# Menu Command Center — Help Documentation

**Version:** 1.1
**Last Updated:** February 14, 2026
**Audience:** Existing customers (restaurant/salon/spa owners, non-technical)

## Quick Summary

The Menu Command Center lets you change prices, availability, categories, and item visibility for many menu items at once. Instead of editing items one by one, you select what you want to change, see a preview, and apply — all in one place.

---

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- You need a published menu with at least one category and items
- You must be logged in as the store owner or an authorized team member

### Opening the Command Center

1. Go to your **Menu Editor** (the screen where you edit your menu items)
2. Click the **"More Actions"** button in the top toolbar
3. Click **"Menu Command Center"**
4. A large panel opens — this is your command center

## How-To Guides

### How to Update Prices for Many Items at Once

This is useful when supplier costs change or you need a seasonal price adjustment.

1. Open the **Menu Command Center**
2. In the left panel, select the items you want to change:
   - Click a **category name** to select all items in that category
   - Or click individual items one by one
   - Or click **"Select All"** to select your entire menu
3. The left panel shows how many items you selected
4. In the center panel, click **"Adjust Pricing"**
5. Choose how you want to change prices:
   - **Increase by %** — Example: +10% increases ₹200 to ₹220
   - **Decrease by %** — Example: -10% decreases ₹200 to ₹180
   - **Add flat amount** — Example: +₹50 increases ₹200 to ₹250
   - **Reduce flat amount** — Example: -₹50 decreases ₹200 to ₹150
   - **Set fixed price** — Sets all selected items to the same price
6. Enter the value (number only)
7. The right panel shows you a **preview** of what will change:
   - How many items will be affected
   - Average price before and after
   - A sample of individual item changes
8. If everything looks correct, click **"Apply Changes"**
9. A small confirmation appears — click **"Confirm"**
10. Done! A message appears: "Prices updated for X items"

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Pricing action with preview showing before/after prices
> 💡 **Tip:** All prices are rounded to the nearest whole rupee automatically.

### How to Mark Items as Unavailable (Out of Stock)

Use this when your kitchen runs out of certain items.

1. Open the **Menu Command Center**
2. Select the items that are out of stock
3. In the center panel, click **"Change Availability"**
4. Select **"Mark as Unavailable"**
5. The right panel shows how many items will change
6. Click **"Apply Changes"** and confirm
7. Those items will show as "Sold Out" on your customer menu

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Availability action with items being marked unavailable
> 💡 **Tip:** To make items available again later, select them and choose "Mark as Available."

### How to Show or Hide Items Permanently

Use this when you stop serving a dish or want to bring back a hidden item.

1. Open the **Menu Command Center**
2. Select the items you want to show or hide
3. In the center panel, click **"Show or Hide Items"**
4. Select **"Show on Menu"** or **"Hide from Menu"**
5. The right panel shows how many items will change
6. Click **"Apply Changes"** and confirm

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Show/Hide action with items being hidden
> 💡 **Tip:** "Hide from Menu" permanently removes items from the customer menu. For items that are just temporarily out of stock, use "Change Availability" instead.

### How to Move Items to a Different Category

Use this to reorganize your menu structure.

1. Open the **Menu Command Center**
2. Select the items you want to move
3. In the center panel, click **"Move to Category"**
4. Pick the destination category from the dropdown
5. Preview shows which items will move and where
6. Click **"Apply Changes"** and confirm

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Move category action with destination picker

### How to Do Multiple Changes in One Session

You can make different types of changes without closing the Command Center.

1. First change: Update prices → Apply
2. The Command Center stays open and shows the action list again
3. Second change: Mark items unavailable → Apply
4. Third change: Move items → Apply
5. When done, click the **X** button to close

Each action applies independently. You can undo the last action if needed.

## How to Undo a Change

If you applied a change and want to reverse it:

1. After clicking "Apply Changes", a message appears at the top: **"X items updated — Undo"**
2. Click **"Undo"** within 30 seconds
3. The change is reversed

> 💡 **Tip:** Undo only works for the last action. If you applied two changes, only the second one can be undone.

## Safety Features

The Command Center has built-in protections to prevent mistakes:

- **Preview before apply** — You always see what will change before it happens
- **Price limits** — Cannot increase by more than 200% or decrease by more than 80%
- **No zero prices** — Prices cannot be set to zero or negative
- **Confirmation step** — You must confirm before changes are applied
- **30-second undo** — Reverse the last change if something looks wrong

## For Multi-Outlet / Chain Owners

If you manage multiple branches:

- **Editing master menu**: Changes apply to all branches that follow master pricing. Branches with custom prices are not affected.
- **Editing a branch menu**: Changes only apply to that branch. Master menu and other branches are untouched.
- **Locked items**: If some items are controlled by the master menu, they appear with a lock icon and are automatically excluded from changes.

The left panel always shows which outlet you are editing and how many items are locked.

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

### "I can't apply changes — the button is greyed out"

**Why this happens:** No items are selected, or the value field is empty.
**How to fix it:**

1. Make sure you selected at least one item in the left panel
2. Make sure you entered a value in the pricing/action field

### "Some items didn't change price"

**Why this happens:** Items without a price set (empty price field) are skipped automatically. Also, locked items in outlet menus are excluded.
**How to fix it:**

1. Check the preview panel — it shows how many items were skipped and why
2. Items without prices need to have a price added manually first

### "My outlet prices didn't change when I updated the master"

**Why this happens:** Your outlet has custom price overrides for those items. Master price changes don't override local customizations.
**How to fix it:**

1. Switch to the outlet menu in the editor
2. Open the Command Center from there
3. Update prices for that specific outlet

### "I closed the Command Center and lost my changes"

**Why this happens:** If you entered values but didn't click "Apply Changes", the changes are not saved.
**How to fix it:** The system asks for confirmation before closing if you have unsaved work. Next time, click "Apply Changes" before closing.

## Tips & Best Practices

- 💡 **Start with preview**: Always check the preview panel before applying. Make sure the numbers look right.
- 💡 **Use category selection for fast updates**: If all beverages need a price increase, just click the "Beverages" category — all items inside get selected automatically.
- 💡 **Round numbers**: The system rounds prices to whole numbers automatically. ₹199.50 becomes ₹200.
- 💡 **Do big changes in the Command Center, small changes in the editor**: For 1-2 items, just edit directly. For 5+ items, use the Command Center.
- 💡 **Check your outlet**: Before making changes, verify which outlet you're editing in the left panel.
