## FAQ

- **Q:** Will this affect my outlet-specific pricing?
  **A:** No. If an outlet has custom pricing, those prices stay untouched. Only items following the master price will update.

- **Q:** What if I make a mistake?
  **A:** Every change shows a preview first. You see the exact impact before applying. If you still want to revert, click Undo within 30 seconds.

- **Q:** Does it work for non-price changes?
  **A:** Yes. You can also mark items as available/unavailable, permanently show or hide items, and move items between categories — all in bulk.

- **Q:** What’s the difference between "unavailable" and "hidden"?
  **A:** "Unavailable" means temporarily out of stock — the item shows as sold out. "Hidden" means permanently removed from the customer menu until you choose to show it again.

- **Q:** Is there a limit on how many items I can update at once?
  **A:** No. You can select your entire menu and update all items in one action.
