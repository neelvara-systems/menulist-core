## Getting Started

### What You Need

- A phone with WhatsApp
- Photos of your menu (or a PDF file)
- 5 minutes

### How It Works

1. **Save** the MenuList WhatsApp number: [number to be added]
2. **Send** your menu photos or PDF
3. **Wait** a moment — MenuList reads your photos and creates the menu
4. **Open** the preview link sent back to you

7. **Done** — your menu is live!

> 💡 **Tip:** Send clear, well-lit photos of each page of your menu. The clearer the photo, the better the result.

## How To Guides

### How to send your menu

1. Open WhatsApp
2. Open the chat with MenuList
3. Tap the attachment icon (📎)
4. Choose **Gallery** or **Camera**
5. Select all your menu pages (or take photos one by one)
6. Tap **Send**
7. You'll receive a reply: "Got it. Preparing your menu."

> 💡 **Tip:** You can send multiple photos — all pages of your menu. Send them all at once or one by one within 10 minutes.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** [WhatsApp chat showing menu photos being sent]

### How to send a PDF menu

1. Open WhatsApp
2. Open the chat with MenuList
3. Tap the attachment icon (📎)
4. Choose **Document**
5. Select your menu PDF file
6. Tap **Send**

> 💡 **Tip:** Make sure the PDF is not password-protected. If it is, take photos of the pages instead.

### How to review your preview

1. After sending photos, wait for the preview link message
2. Tap the link to open your menu preview
3. Check:
   - Are all items listed correctly?
   - Are prices correct?
   - Is the business name right?
4. You can edit the business name, phone, and address on the preview page
5. If everything looks good, tap **Approve & Publish**

> 📸 **Screenshot:** [Preview page showing menu with Approve button]

### How to request corrections

1. Open the preview link
2. Tap **Request Fix**
3. Select what needs fixing:
   - Price incorrect
   - Item missing
   - Spelling error
   - Wrong category
4. Add a short note if needed (optional)
5. Tap **Submit**
6. Send updated menu photos when asked

> 💡 **Tip:** For best results, send all menu photos again (not just the page with errors).

### How to manage your menu after publishing

1. After your menu is published, you'll receive a dashboard link on WhatsApp
2. Open the link and sign in
3. Use the dashboard to:
   - Update prices
   - Add or remove items
   - Change business hours
   - Download your QR code

> 💡 **Tip:** Your menu stays live even if you don't log in. The dashboard is there when you need it.

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

### My photos weren't accepted

**Why this happens:** The photos might be blurry, or they might not show a menu (e.g., a photo of the restaurant interior).

**How to fix it:**

1. Take new photos in good lighting
2. Make sure the menu text is readable
3. Send the photos again

### The preview has wrong prices

**Why this happens:** Sometimes prices are hard to read from photos.

**How to fix it:**

1. Open the preview link
2. Tap "Request Fix"
3. Select "Price incorrect"
4. Send clearer photos of the page with those prices

### I sent photos but didn't get a preview

**Why this happens:** The system might still be reading your photos (usually takes 1-2 minutes).

**How to fix it:**

1. Wait 2-3 minutes
2. If no preview arrives, send your photos again
3. Make sure you sent actual menu pages, not other images

### I accidentally approved the wrong preview

**Why this happens:** The preview was approved and menu published.

**How to fix it:**

1. Open the dashboard link you received
2. Sign in to your account
3. Edit any incorrect items directly in the dashboard

### Can I start over with new photos?

**Why this happens:** You want to replace the photos you sent.

**How to fix it:**

1. Send all your menu photos again
2. The system will create a new preview with the updated photos

## Tips & Best Practices

- 💡 Take photos in good lighting — natural daylight works best
- 💡 Send ALL pages of your menu, including drinks, desserts, and specials
- 💡 Hold the camera steady and straight — avoid angles
- 💡 If your menu is on a board, take one clear photo of the entire board
- 💡 PDF files usually give the best results — send PDF if you have one
- 💡 Check the preview carefully before approving — it's easier to fix now than later
