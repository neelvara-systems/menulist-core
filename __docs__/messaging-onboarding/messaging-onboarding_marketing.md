## Feature Narrative (Business Value)

### The Problem

Globally, most SMB owners resist digital onboarding because:

- They don't trust new software
- They don't have time to learn dashboards
- They avoid signup forms and email verification
- They've been burned by SaaS products before

The result: millions of businesses that WANT a digital presence but can't get past the onboarding wall.

### The Solution

MenuList Messaging Onboarding removes the wall entirely.

The owner doesn't sign up. Doesn't create an account. Doesn't learn a dashboard.

They send the current list on WhatsApp — the app they already use every day — and MenuList handles extraction, structuring, preview, and approval before the owner needs to learn a dashboard. Publishing happens only after the owner approves the preview.

After a few minutes of effort, the owner has:

- A live official customer link
- QR and official business page access through existing MenuList share surfaces
- A dashboard account for owner login after publish

### Why This Matters

- **For owners:** A current public list without dashboard-first effort
- **For MenuList:** Scalable acquisition without sales calls
- **For the market:** First infrastructure-grade messaging onboarding for SMBs

## Pricing/Packaging Story

**Onboarding is free.** This is deliberate.

The goal is activation volume. Revenue comes from:

1. Premium features on dashboard (digital screens, campaigns, multi-outlet)
2. Subscription plans (per existing Razorpay billing)
3. AI enhancement packs (image generation, translations, rewrites)

Never lead with pricing during onboarding. Lead with: "Get your menu live first."
