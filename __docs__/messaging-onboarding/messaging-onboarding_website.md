## FAQ (5 questions)

- **Q:** Is this really free?
  **A:** Going live via WhatsApp is free. Premium features like digital screens and multi-outlet management are available on paid plans.

- **Q:** What if the menu preview has mistakes?
  **A:** You can request corrections before publishing. Nothing goes live without your approval.

- **Q:** Do I need to create an account?
  **A:** Not to go live. After publishing, you automatically get a dashboard account to manage your menu when needed.

- **Q:** Can I update my menu later?
  **A:** Yes. After publishing, use your MenuList dashboard to update prices, add items, change hours, or anything else.

- **Q:** What happens to my photos after I send them?
  **A:** Photos are used to create your menu and remain attached to the menu project so you can view the original source files in the dashboard. Expired sessions that do not publish are cleaned up automatically.
