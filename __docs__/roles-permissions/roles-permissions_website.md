## Problem Statement

When everyone has admin access, mistakes happen. A staff member accidentally changes billing. A manager restructures the menu. You can't run a team on "everyone is admin."

## Solution Statement

MenuList gives every team member a role that matches their job. Three defaults (Owner, Manager, Staff) cover most teams. Need more control? Create custom roles with exactly the permissions you want across billing, menu, sharing, feedback, screens, public presence, integrations, and more. Non-technical staff can also use Staff ID and passcode login when email is not practical.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Three Roles, Ready to Go

Owner, Manager, Staff — pre-configured for how restaurants actually work. Assign once, done.

### 2. Custom Roles When You Need Them

Create a "Kitchen Manager" role with menu-only access. Or a "Marketing Lead" with public presence permissions. 29 toggles, your rules.

### 3. Features Hidden, Not Disabled

Staff never see buttons they can't use. No confusion, no "access denied" errors. Just a clean dashboard for their role.

### 4. Works with Multi-Location Chains

Combine staff roles with outlet policies to control both what people can do and what each store can do. Two layers, one system.

### 5. Owner Can End Access

Owners can reset a staff passcode or sign out a staff session when someone leaves the business. Old access is revoked without asking staff to handle a technical email flow.
