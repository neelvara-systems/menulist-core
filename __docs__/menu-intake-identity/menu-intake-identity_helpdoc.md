# Menu Intake Identity — Help Article

When you upload menu photos or a PDF, MenuList may check whether the upload looks like the right menu for the place you are editing.

If something looks off, you may see a short message before processing starts. For example:

- The upload looks like a different menu.
- The upload looks like another outlet.
- The upload looks like a special menu.
- Some files are not menu pages.
- The menu looks incomplete.

You can still continue when the upload is correct. The check is there to prevent adding the wrong menu to the wrong place.

When only some files are menu pages, MenuList processes the menu pages and ignores the rest.

If the upload shows a different business name, phone, address, or business type, MenuList may ask whether to save those details. Choose only the details you want to update.
