# Founder Public Presence Initial ChatGPT Source Review

**Status:** Complete baseline review
**Reviewed:** August 12, 2026
**Source:** Founder-supplied ChatGPT conversation and follow-up

## Executive Verdict

The first ChatGPT answer correctly rejected a generic indie-hacker identity and
recommended real proof, selective transparency, manual Reddit participation,
and strong product judgment. It incorrectly assumed the founder should use a
real name and face, made MenuList's public-business-truth category the dominant
identity, and used several overprecise cadence and platform claims.

The second answer correctly broadened the personal audience toward AI-assisted
founders and developers, which directly fits Answerlattice. It then broadened
the identity too far into generic “AI-native customer-facing infrastructure,”
treated three products as expressions of one public thesis before all three had
the same launch status, and prescribed excessive output targets.

The adopted strategy is the narrow hybrid recorded in this folder.

## Final Operating Principle

> Evidence first. Product judgment second. Public-safe proof third. Product
> connection only after privacy and evidence gates. Promotion rarely.
