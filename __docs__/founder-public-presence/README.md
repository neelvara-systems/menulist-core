# PresenceOS — Proof & State

**Status:** Active internal operating system
**Created:** August 12, 2026
**Owner:** Founder, with Codex as research, privacy, strategy, drafting, and review guide
**Public identity:** `Proof & State`
**First-choice handle:** `@proofandstate`

## Purpose

PresenceOS is the canonical, internal system for building the founder's
pseudonymous public reputation from zero. It covers identity safety, platform
research, daily guidance, content intake, native drafting, replies, measurement,
and continuous correction.

The public promise is:

> Practical, evidence-backed lessons for turning AI-assisted software into
> dependable products.

The account is pseudonymous by design. It is not a fictional person, an
impersonation, or a claim that the platforms do not know who operates it.

## Non-Negotiable Privacy Boundary

The public identity must not expose or make discoverable the founder's legal or
personal name, face, natural voice, personal accounts, private email or phone,
home or precise location, personal Git identity, private repository history,
customer material, or identifying screen and file metadata.

No product, company, domain, repository, or public artifact may be linked from
Proof & State until the [identity and privacy audit](./founder-public-presence_identity-and-privacy.md)
passes for that connection.

Public pseudonymity is distinct from platform anonymity. X, Reddit, Instagram,
Facebook, email providers, and payment or verification providers may privately
require accurate registration, phone, location, or identity information. Never
promise otherwise.

## PresenceOS Loop

```text
real work or current research
  -> capture one useful lesson
  -> evidence and privacy gates
  -> native draft
  -> founder review
  -> explicit manual external action
  -> measure relevant response
  -> keep, change, or stop
```

PresenceOS is documentation plus morning and evening Codex heartbeats. It is
not a public product, dashboard, database, scraping system, engagement bot, or
auto-posting runtime. Do not build those merely because the process is now
recurring.

## Proactive Maintenance Rule

Codex must update this folder when related work reveals a material public-useful
lesson, current platform change, privacy risk, successful-account pattern,
publishable artifact, audience response, post result, or rejected tactic.

Codex may research, capture, redact, prepare drafts, and maintain the ledgers.
It may not create an external account, publish, reply, comment, DM, follow,
subscribe, verify, purchase, advertise, or expose material without an explicit
instruction for that external action.
