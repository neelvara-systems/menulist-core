# Proof & State Strategy

**Status:** Active
**Last Updated:** August 14, 2026

## Objective

Build a durable pseudonymous reputation among serious AI-assisted founders and
small product teams by publishing useful, evidence-backed product and
engineering judgment from real work.

Success means relevant people repeatedly recognize Proof & State, apply or save
the lessons, return for later discussions, and eventually become credible
builder relationships, product-fit conversations, testers, referrers, or
collaborators. Follower count and impressions are diagnostics, not the goal.

## Audience

### Primary

Solo founders and small teams moving AI-assisted SaaS from a working demo to a
dependable product. Common problems include:

- code they did not fully author or understand;
- inconsistent authorization, data, and lifecycle paths;
- weak tenant isolation or server-side entitlements;
- unpredictable Firebase or AI cost;
- stale documentation and support answers;
- missing audit, recovery, observability, and release discipline;
- too many features and unclear product boundaries.

### Secondary

- product-minded developers;
- small studios building SaaS for clients;
- reliability, Firebase, AI-agent, and knowledge-system practitioners;
- future Answerlattice users and MenuList referrers after privacy and proof gates.

### Not the target

- generic syntax-tip or framework-news audiences;
- engagement-only build-in-public circles;
- broad consumer AI audiences;
- people attracted only by revenue screenshots, hype, or launch spectacle.

## Content Pillars

### Production patterns — about half

- full request-to-result flow mapping;
- server-side authorization and tenant boundaries;
- Firebase read/write and cache discipline;
- idempotency, invalidation, recovery, and observability;
- deterministic constraints around probabilistic AI;
- agent task contracts, validation, and audit methods.

### Product judgment — about one-third

- a feature intentionally refused;
- a default chosen instead of another setting;
- complexity removed from a nontechnical user;
- a boundary protected;
- an assumption corrected by evidence;
- the trade-off hidden beneath a simple interface.

### Proof artifacts — the remainder

- a sanitized diagram or state map;
- a proof card;
- a bounded before/after;
- a failure and correction;
- a measured result only when its evidence is public-safe;
- a useful unresolved question with the known boundary stated.

Direct product invitations remain rare and gated. Standalone value remains the
majority.

## Voice

- calm, direct, specific, and technically credible;
- plain language before internal vocabulary;
- mechanism and trade-off before advice;
- disclose uncertainty, affiliation, examples, and limits;
- no outrage, guru certainty, fake vulnerability, or motivational filler;
- write in first person only for things actually done, observed, or decided.

## Quality Gate

Publish only when both are true:

1. A serious builder could use it without buying anything.
2. It is likely to attract the people this account is intended to know.

It must also contain at least one non-obvious mechanism, reproducible pattern,
real trade-off, public-safe artifact, corrected assumption, or useful open
question.

## X Distribution Strategy

X's For You system is treated as a per-viewer prediction and filtering system,
not a universal account score or posting formula. Proof & State therefore
optimizes for the following durable behaviors:

- publish material that is closely matched to the intended technical-founder
  audience and useful without a product purchase;
- earn genuine discussion, sharing, following, and attentive reading through a
  specific mechanism, artifact, or decision rather than asking for engagement;
- avoid audience mismatch and repeated low-value formats that can produce “Not
  interested,” mute, block, report, or spam-like signals;
- use original, non-duplicative posts and trustworthy links;
- maintain account security and transparent pseudonymity so compromised,
  impersonation, or authenticity enforcement does not become a reach risk;
- keep the three-post weekly starting cadence. X's ten-post monthly threshold
  governs access to a transparency report, not distribution;
- treat that cadence as a learning baseline, not a one-post-per-day rule. An
  additional same-day original must independently earn publication through a
  distinct audience problem, current evidence, non-duplicative format, privacy
  clearance, and a reason the lesson is more useful now than later;
- use the account's observed audience quality, post results, and eventual
  `Under the Hood` label report before diagnosing visibility or changing the
  strategy.

Published ranking parameters are interpreted only at a pinned source commit.
Candidate retrieval, predicted-action scoring, parameter defaults, author and
network adjustments, reranking, visibility filtering, and transparency-report
eligibility must remain separate. Coefficients are not interchangeable action
values: dividing one by another does not show that a share, reply, or report is
worth a fixed number of likes. Coefficients and experiments can change, and
some anti-gaming and deployment details are not public.

  or personal Git identity in public;
- no fake biography, fake person, impersonation, or AI-generated human avatar;
- no “day N,” indie-hacker, AI-founder, or vibe-coder identity;
- no generic tools lists, copied prompts, or framework-news feed;
- no raw terminal, browser, notification, analytics, customer, commit, or file
  screenshot without privacy review;
- no unsupported customer, revenue, ranking, conversion, resolution, or cost
  claim;
- no engagement groups, bought followers, follow/unfollow, duplicate replies,
  automated engagement, or disguised promotion;
- no identical cross-posting between platforms;
- no publishing merely to satisfy a quota;
- no assumption that Premium, verification, hashtags, or a particular time
  guarantees distribution.
- no treating an engagement coefficient, freshness filter, author boost, or
  transparency-tool threshold as a guaranteed reach tactic.
- no converting coefficient ratios into likes-equivalent engagement targets.
