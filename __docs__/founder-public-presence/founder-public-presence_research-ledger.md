## Live X Profile Review — August 12, 2026

### Method and limits

The founder explicitly authorized read-only research through their logged-in X
session. Codex reviewed public profile positioning and the currently visible
recent-post sample for eight accounts. It also checked visible reply samples for
`@shadcn` and `@arvidkahl`.

No follow, like, repost, bookmark, reply, message, profile edit, or other
external action was performed. The founder's personal account details are not
part of this research record.

Visible samples are directional snapshots, not full-history studies or proof
that a cadence caused growth. Profiles, counts, posts, and handles can change.

### Cross-account conclusion

The common useful mechanism is not maximum frequency. The strongest accounts
make three things immediately legible:

1. what territory they repeatedly cover;
2. what artifact, source, product, or decision proves the claim;
3. why the next post belongs to the same identity.

For Proof & State, the best composite is:

- ByteByteGo-level profile clarity;
- shadcn-style artifact-led technical proof;
- Simon Willison-style receipts and follow-up notes;
- Jason Fried-style product boundaries;
- Arvid Kahl-style substantive questions and community participation.

The revenue-dashboard, extreme-volume, personal-lifestyle, and controversy-led
mechanisms visible on `@levelsio` and `@marclou` do not fit the pseudonymous,
production-grade Proof & State strategy.

`@nexxeln` is a useful peer-account candidate, but the five-post visible sample
does not satisfy the ten-post admission rule. Keep it in the candidate pool and
complete a larger review before adding it to a permanent private list.

## Research Rules

- Prefer current platform and community primary sources for policy and feature
  claims.
- Creator self-reports may establish what they say they did, not general causal
  proof.
- Record audience size, history, product-market timing, and other advantages
  before using a creator launch as a comparison.
- Do not copy protected expression, post wording, visual identity, or persona.
- Add a new source only when it changes a decision, adds a useful tested
  pattern, or closes a material uncertainty.
- Preserve rejected advice and why it was rejected.
