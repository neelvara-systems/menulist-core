# Founder Public Presence Metrics

**Status:** Initial baseline
**Last Updated:** August 12, 2026

## Governing Outcome

The account should create repeated recognition and useful relationships among
serious builders. Metrics diagnose whether that is happening; they do not
replace judgment.

## First Thirty-Day Baseline

- Twelve original X posts: three per week for four weeks.
- Approximately ten to fifteen thoughtful X replies per week.
- Three to five useful Reddit comments per week during the first two weeks.
- At most one native Reddit post during weeks three and four.
- Zero public original-identity, personal-account, product-correlation, Git,
  screenshot, voice, or metadata leaks.
- Zero spam behavior, disguised promotion, unsupported proof, or removed posts
  caused by a rule that should have been checked.

These are operating bounds, not growth or follower guarantees.

## Qualitative Review

Record short answers weekly:

1. Which post attracted the most relevant people?
2. Which post was understood differently from its intended lesson?
3. Did any conversation reveal a real Answerlattice workload or MenuList
   referral path?
4. Which topic felt generic, forced, or too promotional?
5. Which recurring question deserves a durable technical asset?
6. What should be kept, changed, or stopped next week?
7. Did any post, reply, profile field, image, account connection, or metadata
   create an identity-correlation risk?

## Meaningful Signals

- the same relevant people return to discuss later posts;
- a founder can accurately explain the account promise;
- a builder applies or saves a production pattern;
- a qualified Answerlattice founder or studio brings real support questions;
- a relevant person refers a MenuList owner or partner;
- a discussion produces a stronger product or content decision;
- a useful post becomes a durable resource without exaggerating its result.

## Weak Or Misleading Signals

- one large impression count from an unrelated topic;
- followers outside the intended audience;
- generic praise without understanding;
- engagement produced by controversy unrelated to the work;
- direct traffic with no qualified action;
- high posting volume that reduces product work or quality;
- creator monetization income treated as product-distribution proof.

## Adjustment Rules

- Do not change positioning from one post.
- Any identity leak pauses publishing until the source, copies, and account
  connections are reviewed.
- After twelve X posts, compare the three content pillars using relevant replies,
  saves, repeat people, profile-to-relevant-follow behavior, and qualified
  conversations.
- Increase cadence only when the content ledger has strong real material and the
  weekly workflow remains sustainable.
- Reduce or stop a format when it repeatedly attracts the wrong audience,
  requires artificial performance, or produces weak insight despite material
  founder time.
- Keep product evidence honest: an example is not a customer outcome, local
  verification is not hosted proof, and a conversation is not adoption.
