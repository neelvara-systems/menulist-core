# Proof & State Platform Playbook

**Status:** Active
**Last Updated:** August 12, 2026

## Channel Architecture

One lesson may travel across platforms only after it is reshaped for the native
context. Copy-paste distribution is not the strategy.

| Channel | Current role | Native unit | Launch decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | Discovery, short technical writing, and repeated relationships | concise post, proof card, focused reply | Start |
| Reddit | Trust through complete answers inside communities | direct comment or self-contained technical post | Start comments-first |
| Instagram | Visual discovery for diagrams and faceless proof artifacts | carousel, proof card, short silent/captioned reel | Reserve; activate after four proven visuals |
| Facebook | Broad creator distribution but stronger identity/transparency risk | Page post, Reel, group participation | Do not launch now |
| LinkedIn | Professional distribution tied closely to personal identity | personal or company post | Do not launch under this alias |
| YouTube | Durable technical education and search | faceless screen-led walkthrough | Admit only after repeated long-form demand |

## X Playbook

### What to publish

- Monday: one production mechanism.
- Wednesday: one audit finding, corrected assumption, or product boundary.
- Friday: one proof card, sanitized diagram, or short screen artifact.

Use 8:00 PM Asia/Kolkata for the first twelve posts as a controlled baseline,
not an algorithm claim. Change only after the account's own evidence.

### What to watch

Build three private lists:

1. **Method exemplars:** shadcn, Simon Willison, Jason Fried, ByteByteGo,
   Visualize Value, and 0xngmi. Study artifacts, explanations, visual grammar,
   disclosures, and replies—not tone or outcomes.
2. **Topic practitioners:** people currently discussing production AI,
   authorization, Firebase, RAG correctness, support systems, reliability, and
   product boundaries.
3. **Peer accounts:** small serious builders whose questions and repeated work
   overlap the intended audience.

Add an account only after reading at least ten recent posts. Remove it when it
becomes mostly hype, engagement bait, generic news, or unrelated controversy.

### When to reply

Reply only when at least one is possible:

- add a mechanism the post omitted;
- state a real trade-off or failure boundary;
- provide a concise example from verified work;
- ask a question that materially advances the subject;
- correct a consequential claim with a primary source.

Do not reply with praise-only filler, product links, “DM me,” contrarian bait,
copied phrasing, or a summary of the original post.

### Reply shapes

```text
Mechanism: The part I would make explicit is [boundary]. Otherwise [failure].
```

```text
Trade-off: This works while [condition]. Once [condition changes], [risk].
```

```text
Experience: We hit a similar issue. The fix was not [obvious tactic]; it was [general mechanism]. The limitation is [boundary].
```

```text
Question: How are you handling [specific failure path]? That is where this pattern usually changes for me.
```

## Reddit Playbook

Reddit reputation is account history inside communities, not follower growth.

### Initial communities

Start with no more than two communities at a time:

- `r/SaaS` for product, founder, onboarding, support, and reliability questions;
- one current technical community selected from `r/Firebase`, `r/nextjs`, or
  `r/webdev` based on the actual subject that week.

Read site-wide and community rules immediately before every contribution.
Rules and enforcement change.

### First-month behavior

- three useful comments per week;
- no product name, link, solicitation, feedback request, or concealed plug;
- no native post until a question repeatedly appears and the account has a
  useful participation history;
- no X thread expanded mechanically into a Reddit post;
- if a product is ever mentioned, disclose affiliation in the same comment.

At review time, `r/SaaS` limited self-promotion—including names, links, and
comment plugs—to once per 60 days and treated alternate accounts as the same
promoter. PresenceOS does not use that allowance in the first month.

### Native answer structure

```text
Direct answer
-> relevant context
-> implementation or decision
-> observed result or current evidence
-> limitation
-> disclosure, only if relevant
```

## Instagram Admission Gate

Reserve the handle only if the founder accepts Meta's private registration
requirements. Do not activate the feed until all are true:

- four original Proof & State visual posts have already been tested on X;
- a reusable privacy-safe proof-card template exists;
- at least one native five-to-seven-slide carousel is ready;
- the account can remain valuable without face or natural voice;
- the Meta account is not linked publicly to personal Facebook or Instagram.

Instagram receives original visual adaptations, not screenshots of X posts.
Primary forms are diagrams, carousels, and short caption-led screen artifacts.

## Facebook Decision

Do not create a fake personal Facebook profile. Facebook requires Page managers
to use an authentic private profile, and Page Transparency may show manager
country, manager counts, or confirmed owner information. This does not fit the
zero-public-identity launch.

Facebook may be reconsidered only through a specific privacy review. Audience
size or creator monetization is not enough to override the identity boundary.

## Pattern Review Method

When studying a successful account, record:

- what repeated subject made it recognizable;
- what artifact or proof supported trust;
- how it replied and participated;
- which platform-native format it used;
- its prior audience, reputation, product timing, and other advantages;
- what can transfer to Proof & State and what cannot.

Never infer that cadence, hashtags, verification, or one viral format caused
the account's outcome.
