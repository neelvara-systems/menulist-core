## Daily Operating Rhythm

| Time | Action | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8:30 AM | Read the scheduled Codex Presence Brief | 10 minutes; choose at most one action |
| 1:00 PM | Scan the three X lists and save at most three relevant posts | 15 minutes; no feed wandering |
| 8:00 PM | Publish or complete the day's reply/community action | 20 minutes |
| 9:00 PM | Record URLs, useful responses, and one observation | 5 minutes |
| 9:30 PM | Read the automated accountability check and reply `DONE`, `BLOCKED`, or `SKIP` | 2 minutes; one-line evidence/status only |

## Missed-Action Recovery

- A due action without a founder result becomes `unconfirmed`, not failed.
- Carry forward only the highest-value unfinished action.
- Do not double tomorrow's posts, replies, or community actions to catch up.
- If blocked, the next brief gives the smallest safe unblock step.
- If the day is intentionally skipped, record why and resume the normal cadence
  unless the reason changes the plan.
- The canonical queue and evidence protocol live in the
  [daily progress tracker](./founder-public-presence_progress-tracker.md).

## Reply Selection

At the 1:00 PM scan, save only posts that meet one of these themes:

- authorization, multi-tenancy, billing, or entitlement truth;
- production failures in AI-assisted code;
- Firebase cost, cache, data, or serverless boundaries;
- RAG, support correctness, provenance, or documentation drift;
- product boundaries, defaults, removed complexity, or failure recovery.

At 8:00 PM, choose the best two. If neither deserves a substantive response,
post zero replies and record `no useful reply target`.

## First-Month Restrictions

- no product names or product links;
- no direct messages for acquisition;
- no asking people to follow, repost, comment, or “check out” anything;
- no Reddit self-promotion allowance;
- no Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or YouTube publishing;
- no Premium or verification purchase for hoped-for reach;
- no strategy change from one high or low result.

## Day Thirty Review

Review:

- which pillar attracted relevant repeat people;
- which posts earned saves, thoughtful replies, or useful disagreement;
- which reply themes started real conversations;
- whether the pseudonym remained intact;
- whether the work remained sustainable;
- whether four visuals justify the Instagram admission review;
- whether a product-connection audit is now worth doing.

The decision is keep, narrow, change one controlled variable, or stop a format.
It is never “post more everywhere” by default.
