## Quick Summary

Ready-to-send messages with your menu link, address, and business hours. Copy and paste into WhatsApp, SMS, or any messaging app. No typing needed.

## Getting Started

No setup needed. The messages appear automatically on your "Use MenuList" page after you publish your menu. They use your store information to fill in the details.

## How to Use

### Send a Menu Message

1. Open the **Use MenuList** page
2. Scroll to **Customer Messages**
3. Find the message you need (e.g., "Send Menu" or "Menu + Location")
4. Tap **Copy Message** to copy the text
5. Open WhatsApp and paste the message
6. Send!

### Send via WhatsApp Directly

1. Find the message you want
2. Tap **Send via WhatsApp**
3. WhatsApp opens with the message already filled in
4. Choose the contact and send

## Available Messages

| Message | What It Includes |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Send Menu** | Greeting + menu link |
| **Menu + Location** | Menu link + address + today's hours |
| **Quick Reply** | Just the menu link (minimal) |
| **Business Info** | Name + menu link + address + phone + hours |
| **Share with Staff** | Menu link + note for your team |
| **Staff Daily Replies** | Link, address, and today's hours for your team |

## Tips

- Use "Quick Reply" during busy hours for the fastest response
- Share the "Share with Staff" message with your team
- Share "Staff Daily Replies" when counter or phone staff need the same details for the day
- The menu link in every message opens your latest published menu
