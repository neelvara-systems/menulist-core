# Customer Communication Kit — Marketing & Sales

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** March 15, 2026
> **Audience:** Internal sales/marketing team

---

## Elevator Pitch

Customer asks "Send menu" — you tap once, message is ready, paste and send. No typing, no searching for links, no copy-pasting addresses. Ready messages with your menu link, location, and hours — every time.

## Feature Narrative

Restaurant owners answer the same questions 20-50 times daily: "Send menu", "Where are you?", "Are you open?" Each response requires manually typing the same information. Customer Communication Kit generates polished, ready-to-send messages that combine the menu link with the business address, hours, and contact info. One tap to copy, one tap to send via WhatsApp.

## Sales Talking Points

- "Customer asks for menu on WhatsApp? One tap — message is ready with your link, address, and hours."
- "No more typing the same reply 30 times a day."
- "Your staff can use the same messages — everyone sends a consistent, professional response."
- "One staff handoff covers today's link, address, and hours."
- "Works on your phone during busy service — just copy and paste."

## Approved Language

- ✅ "Ready messages" / "Pre-filled responses" / "Quick replies"
- ✅ "Copy and paste" / "One-tap sharing"
- ❌ Never say "automated messaging" / "chatbot" / "AI responses"
- ❌ Never say "marketing messages" / "broadcast" / "campaign"
