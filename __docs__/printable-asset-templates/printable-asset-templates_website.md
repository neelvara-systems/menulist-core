## Problem Statement

Owners often print QR codes, table cards, and paper menus in a hurry. If those files look plain or mismatched, the business feels less prepared.

## Solution Statement

MenuList creates finished print files from the current approved menu. The owner chooses a style and downloads the file.

## How It Works

1. Open **Assets**.
2. Pick the file and style.
3. Download the print-ready file.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to design the card myself?**  
**A:** No. The templates are already prepared.

**Q: Can I use my logo and color?**  
**A:** Yes. MenuList uses the business identity already saved in the store.

**Q: Will the QR still scan?**  
**A:** Yes. The QR area stays dark on white for reliable scanning.

**Q: Can Premium remove MenuList branding?**  
**A:** Yes, visible MenuList attribution follows the existing Premium branding policy.
