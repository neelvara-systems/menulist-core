## How It Works

1. Open **Assets**.
2. Choose one theme for the menu or business.
3. Pick an asset and preview the generated output in that theme.
4. Add optional Gift Certificate or Invitation details before download, or leave those fields ready for handwriting.
5. Optionally adjust supported desktop assets from the finished template.
6. Download the print-ready file as PDF or image where supported, or share/save it from a supported phone. Business Card front/back images are delivered together as one ZIP; Complete Menu Kit keeps the same theme across its files.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to design the card myself?**  
**A:** No. The templates are already prepared.

**Q: Can I change the words or layout if needed?**
**A:** Supported desktop assets can be adjusted from the prepared template. The QR link stays protected.

**Q: Can I fill in a gift certificate or invitation before printing?**
**A:** Yes. Add only the details you want printed, or leave any field blank to write it by hand later. These drafts stay in the current browser workflow and are not saved as business records. Gift Certificates do not track balance or redemption, and Invitations do not collect RSVPs or register guests.

**Q: Can I use my logo and color?**  
**A:** Yes. MenuList uses the business identity already saved in the store.

**Q: Will the QR still scan?**  
**A:** Yes. The QR area stays dark on white for reliable scanning.

**Q: Can Premium remove MenuList branding?**  
**A:** Yes, visible MenuList attribution follows the existing Premium branding policy.
