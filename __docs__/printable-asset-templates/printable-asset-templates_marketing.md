# Printable Asset Templates - Marketing Notes

> **Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

## Elevator Pitch

MenuList gives every business ready-to-print menu and QR action files that match its brand, without starting from a blank design tool.

## Feature Narrative

Small business owners do not think of table cards, counter stickers, business cards, ID cards, flyers, gift vouchers, postcards, posters, and printed menus as "assets". They think of them as the face of the business. A poor QR card, rushed flyer, or weak business card makes the business look unfinished even when the digital menu is correct.

Printable Asset Templates turns those files into one coordinated brand set. The owner chooses one parent theme for the menu or business, then picks the file to download. Every print menu, card, poster, sticker, and Menu Kit file inherits that theme automatically. When a Gift Certificate or Invitation needs personal details, the owner can type only the fields they want printed and leave the rest as handwriting lines. These are print-design capabilities, not certificate balance/redemption or event RSVP/registration systems. When a practical layout adjustment is needed, supported desktop assets open from the finished template in a governed editor with the QR link protected. Store name, logo, color, menu link, feedback link, and MenuList branding policy are handled automatically.

The strongest QR story is not decorative QR art. It is a branded customer action point: a table card, counter sign, package insert, flyer, business card, or poster that says exactly what the customer should scan for.

## Pitch Deck Outline

1. **Printed Files Carry Trust** - Customers see table and counter files before they interact with staff.
2. **Owners Should Not Need Design Work** - Most SMB owners do not have time or skill for file prep.
3. **Assets Screen** - One workspace for table, counter, entrance, feedback, and paper menu files.
4. **One Parent Theme** - Real choice without mismatched per-asset styles or duplicate output options.
5. **Business-Relevant Catalog** - Thirty-four themes work everywhere; business context orders the most relevant directions first, while five food-specific systems and eight visibly industry-specific systems stay protected. Salon/Makeup Studio and Spa/Spa Resort each receive five recommended light systems.
6. **Current Menu Source** - QR points back to the current approved menu.
7. **Customer Action Point** - Each QR file has one plain action such as menu, order, feedback, booking, offer, or reorder.
8. **Brand Reuse** - Store color, logo, and name are applied automatically around the scan-safe QR.
9. **Premium Detail** - Premium stores can remove visible MenuList attribution through the existing policy.
10. **Low Operating Cost** - Files generate in the browser with no generated Storage upload.

The current Assets workspace uses the existing paid-subscription admission boundary. All 47 themes remain renderer-supported across every asset. Every admitted business receives all 34 common themes, its category-aware recommendation order, and any admitted food-category or exact-type family. Do not market theme counts as a higher-plan differentiator; restricted visibility is applicability, not an upsell. Visible MenuList attribution removal follows its existing plan policy separately.
