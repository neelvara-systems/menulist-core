# Printable Asset Templates - Help Documentation

> **Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

## Quick Summary

Assets gives you downloadable files for tables, counters, entrances, feedback, campaigns, business cards, ID cards, postcards, product tags, and paper menus. Choose one theme, choose the file, and download.

## Getting Started

### Before You Start

- Your menu should be published.
- Your store name should be correct.
- Add your logo and brand color in Business Profile for a better result.
- Add phone, email, and address details in the business profile before downloading business cards or ID cards.

## Open Assets

### Desktop

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Click **Assets** in the left menu.
3. Select the project if you have more than one menu.

### Mobile

1. Open the MenuList mobile app.
2. Tap **More**.
3. Tap **Assets**.

## Download a File

1. On desktop, choose **Place in your business**, **Promote & share**, or **Business identity**. On mobile, choose the file from the Assets list.
2. Select the file type, such as **Table Tent**, **Flyer**, or **Gift Certificate**.
3. Review the large preview. The current Brand Kit theme is already applied.
4. Click **Download PDF** or **Download image**, or choose **Preview & edit** for the detailed workflow. For **Business Card**, image delivery saves the front and back PNG files together in one ZIP.

For **Flyer**, you can add a campaign headline and optional offer, details, validity, and terms before downloading. Tap **Update preview** to see the supplied copy. A visible status tells you when the preview has unapplied changes. Leave the headline empty when you only need a clean brand flyer with your business link. MenuList does not invent an offer, deadline, discount, or terms for you. The campaign fields remain in the current open screen, so finish the download or customization before leaving it.

For **Gift Certificate**, enter any recipient, sender, personal message, value, valid-until date, or certificate number you want printed. This produces the printable design only; MenuList does not track balance, redemption, or certificate validity. For **Invitation**, enter any occasion, date, time, or location you want printed. It does not collect RSVPs or register guests. Tap **Update preview** to check the result. You can leave any field empty when you prefer to write it by hand after printing. These details stay only in the current open Assets screen and reset when you change projects or leave the workflow.

Download, Share/Save, and Customize first refresh the preview from the current fields. If preview generation fails, use **Retry preview**; output actions remain unavailable until the preview succeeds. While MenuList is generating a preview or file, wait for it to finish before closing the workflow or starting another action.

On a supported phone, use **Share or save PDF/image** to open the system share sheet. A Business Card image share uses one ZIP containing both sides. If native file sharing is unavailable, MenuList saves the file to downloads instead.

## Choose an Asset Theme

Choose one theme to keep your print menu, table cards, stickers, posters, and the rest of the Complete Menu Kit visually consistent.

- On desktop, click **Change brand look** in the Brand Kit section. On mobile, open the theme control in Assets.
- Use **Recommended** for the shortest relevant list, or **All themes** and search when you want to explore every theme available to the business.
- On desktop, click a theme once to inspect Print Menu, Table Tent, Feedback QR, Entrance Poster, Gift Certificate, and Business Card together. This only selects a preview; it does not apply the theme. Click the same selected theme again to return to the current look.
- Choose **Apply to this menu** for a menu-specific theme.
- Choose **Apply to all menus** when the same visual direction should be the baseline for the business.
- Choose **Return to business theme** to remove a menu-specific override.

The badge tells you what is active: **Menu theme**, **Business theme**, or **Recommended**. Every compatible asset inherits that theme automatically.

MenuList shows 34 versatile themes to every business. Your business type and category place the most relevant directions first, but they do not hide common choices. Food businesses also receive Craft Kitchen, Ember House, Coastal Table, Sunday Table, and Counter Rush. Salon and Makeup Studio receive five recommended light beauty themes; Spa and Spa Resort receive five recommended light treatment themes. Exact coffee, bakery, ice cream, and approved fitness types retain their dedicated literal themes too. An eligible saved menu or business theme takes priority over the recommendation. Unknown legacy business types receive the same 34-theme common catalog.

The selected theme applies to every asset. Individual asset screens do not offer a second theme picker. On desktop, **Customize design** may adjust content or layout while the asset remains part of the selected theme family.

## Adjust a File on Desktop

For Table Tent, Single Table / Counter Card, Counter Sticker, Entrance Poster, Feedback QR, Flyer, Gift Certificate, Business Card, Staff Name Badge, Invitation, Postcard, and Campaign Poster, click **Customize design** after opening a style. For Product Tag, open the saved menu item and choose **Product Tag**: MenuList prefills the real item details, opens the preview, and lets you edit the design or download image/PDF without retyping the item.

Use Background, Images, Text, Styles, and Brand Kit when you need to move text, shorten a phrase, add an owned image, or adjust the layout. The design opens with the complete asset visible and no protected layer selected. The QR link stays protected so the printed file still opens the right page. For Business Card, the front and back print sides stay protected while you adjust editable text.

MenuList keeps a recovery copy in this browser while you work. If you try to close a changed design, choose **Keep editing** or **Discard changes**. **Print PDF** and **Image** check the design first; fix the listed issues when possible, or repeat the same download action when the warning is intentional. Use **Save reusable design** only when you want the edited version to appear under Saved designs.

## Download Your Prepared Set

Open **Complete Menu Kit** and download the ZIP. MenuList applies the current menu or business theme to every supported file in the package.

All files still use your current store name, logo, color, and menu link. A theme changes the governed visual family only; it does not replace a reusable design that you edited and saved in the desktop editor.

## Which File Should I Use?

| File | Use It For |
| --- | --- |
| Print Menu | Paper menu or print shop handoff. |
| Table Tent | Tables where customers sit. |
| Single Table / Counter Card | Acrylic holders, counter stands, wall clips, or small display stands. |
| Counter Sticker | Billing counter, pickup counter, or reception desk. |
| Entrance Poster | Door, window, or host stand. |
| Feedback QR | Asking customers to leave private feedback. |
| Flyer | Offer handout, delivery insert, or local campaign card. |
| Gift Certificate | Printable voucher design; balance and redemption tracking are not included. |
| Business Card | Front and back contact card with your latest link. |
| Staff Name Badge | Select one active staff member. MenuList uses that staff record's name and valid role for the current business, plus a name-derived initials monogram. It does not use store-contact details or claim to be a verified photo credential. |
| Invitation | Enter any occasion, date, time, and location you want printed, or leave those fields ready for handwriting. MenuList does not add sample event details, a reply request, QR, unrelated link, RSVP collection, or guest registration. |
| Postcard | Every style uses the same premium layout with your exact headline, optional message, business identity, and business link; changing the parent theme changes its artwork, colours, and typography without changing your content. |
| Product Tag | Retail, bakery, pickup, or counter item tag, created from the saved item so its content and direct link stay truthful. |
| Campaign Poster | Offer poster for windows and counters. |
| Complete Menu Kit | Downloading everything together. |

For a current Today campaign, choose **Download poster to mark done**. MenuList opens the Campaign Poster in the selected menu theme, uses the saved campaign and item when available, and lets you preview, edit, or download it. The action is marked handled only after a file downloads. For a separate manual promotion, open **Campaign Poster** in Assets, enter a real headline and any optional details, then download it. MenuList will not export placeholder campaign claims.

## Troubleshooting

### My logo is not showing

Add your logo in Business Profile, then download again. If no logo is added, MenuList uses your business initials.

### The wrong menu is opening

Check the selected project at the top of the Assets screen.

### Feedback QR is unavailable

Turn on feedback first, then return to Assets.

### The file looks too plain

Try another style. Premium stores can also remove visible MenuList attribution.

## Tips

- Use matte paper for table cards and posters.
- Test the QR after printing.
- Keep the short link visible under the QR.
- Replace damaged cards quickly.
