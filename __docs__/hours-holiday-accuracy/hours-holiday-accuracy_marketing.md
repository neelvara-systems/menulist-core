# Working Hours — Marketing Boundary

**Status:** Approved claim boundary, not a campaign approval

**Last verified:** July 16, 2026

> Current boundary: Do not publish this as current public copy until a source-backed holiday/exception runtime exists.

> Current source-backed claim is limited to owner-set weekly working hours, public open/closed status, Today quick-hours edits, and time-slot presets. Holiday calendars and date-specific exception managers are not shipped.

## Safe Positioning

**One setup. Current status.**

MenuList shows an open or closed status from the regular hours the owner saves. The owner can update today's weekday timing or use Temporary Status for a one-off change. Category time slots can control when parts of a menu appear.

Use Temporary Status or today's hours when the regular week needs a short change.

## Safe Claims

- “Show customers your current open or closed status from saved weekly hours.”
- “Use Temporary Status when today is different.”
- “Keep breakfast, lunch, dinner, or late-night categories within the times you set.”
- “Overnight timings continue past midnight in your business timezone.”

## Claims to Reject

- Automatic holiday handling.
- Built-in India or global holiday calendars.
- Automatic date-specific exceptions.
- Google listing synchronization.
- Guarantees that external listings or every customer surface are synchronized.

## Owner Conversation

“Set your normal week in Business Settings. If today changes, use Today or Temporary Status. MenuList uses that saved source on its customer pages.”
