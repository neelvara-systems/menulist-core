# Working Hours — Marketing Boundary

**Status:** Approved claim boundary, not a campaign approval

**Last verified:** July 30, 2026

> Current source-backed claim includes owner-set weekly hours, exact-date special hours, public open/closed status, Today quick edits, and time-slot presets. Automatic holiday calendars are not shipped.

## Safe Positioning

**One setup. Current status.**

MenuList shows an open or closed status from the regular and special hours the owner saves. The owner can set a planned holiday date without changing the normal week, or use Temporary Status for an unplanned interruption. Category time slots can control when parts of a menu appear.

Use Special hours for a planned date. Use Temporary Status for a live interruption.

## Safe Claims

- “Show customers your current open or closed status from saved weekly hours.”
- “Add special hours for a holiday or one-day change without changing your regular week.”
- “Use Temporary Status when today is different.”
- “Keep breakfast, lunch, dinner, or late-night categories within the times you set.”
- “Overnight timings continue past midnight in your business timezone.”

## Claims to Reject

- Automatic holiday handling.
- Built-in India or global holiday calendars.
- Automatic holiday detection or closure.
- Google listing synchronization.
- Guarantees that external listings or every customer surface are synchronized.

## Owner Conversation

“Set your normal week once. Add a special date when a holiday or one day is different. MenuList uses that saved source on its customer pages.”
