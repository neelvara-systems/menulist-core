# Working Hours — Website Content

## Current Source Boundary

MenuList can show the current open/closed status from the regular weekly hours saved by the owner. Owners can update the current weekday from Today and use reusable time slots for category availability. No holiday-calendar or exception manager is shipped in the current runtime.

## Customers See If You're Open

Save your regular weekly hours once. Customers see the status before they visit. The status uses your business timezone and the hours you maintain.

### What is included

- Regular weekly hours.
- Overnight open/closed handling.
- Current open/closed status.
- Today weekday edit and Temporary Status for one-off changes.
- Reusable category time slots, including overnight windows.

### What is not included

- Automatic holiday closures.
- Date-specific special-hour calendars.
- Google Business Profile hours synchronization.

Do not publish claims that MenuList automatically handles holidays or keeps third-party listings synchronized.
