## Problem Statement
A negative review arrives. You're frustrated. You type a reply in the heat of the moment — "That's not true!" or worse. That reply is now permanent and public. Every future customer sees it. One emotional response can undo months of good work.

## Solution Statement
MenuList quietly watches for risky responses before they go public. If your reply contains emotional language, defensive phrases, or anything that could escalate the situation, the system pauses and suggests a calmer alternative. You still have full control — but with a safety net.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Prevents Emotional Replies
The system detects risky language before your reply goes public. No more "I didn't say that!" appearing under your business name forever.
> **Visual:** Reply being typed → system highlighting "risky phrase" → suggesting calmer alternative

### 2. Silent by Default
You won't even notice it's there — until it saves you from a costly mistake. No dashboards, no notifications, no analytics to check.
> **Visual:** Clean, empty interface with subtitle "Nothing to see here. That means it's working."

### 3. Your Reputation Stays Intact
Every prevented emotional reply is a future customer who sees professionalism instead of defensiveness.

## How It Works
1. **A review comes in** — The system monitors automatically
2. **You reply** — Type your response as usual
3. **Safety check** — If the reply is risky, you get a gentle pause and suggestion
4. **You decide** — Accept the suggestion or post your original. You're always in control.
