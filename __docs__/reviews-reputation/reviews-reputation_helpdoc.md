# Reviews & Reputation — Help Documentation

## Quick Summary
Reviews & Reputation is a silent protection layer that helps you avoid costly public mistakes when responding to customer reviews. It's designed to be invisible until you need it.

---

## Getting Started

### How It Works
- The system runs silently in the background
- When you write a reply to a review, it checks for risky language
- If something could escalate the situation, you get a gentle suggestion
- You always have the final say — the system never posts on your behalf

### What It Protects Against
- Emotional language ("That's not true!", "You're lying!")
- Defensive responses that look unprofessional publicly
- Accidental disclosure of private customer information
- Replies that could violate platform policies

---

## How-To Guides

### How to respond to a negative review safely
1. Read the review carefully
2. Wait at least 10 minutes before replying (our recommendation)
3. Write your response
4. If the system flags anything, review the suggestion
5. Accept the calmer version or adjust your original
6. Post when you're ready

### How to customize your reply tone
1. Go to **Settings** → **Reputation**
2. Choose your preferred reply tone (Professional, Warm, Neutral)
3. Add any phrases you want to always avoid
4. Save

**Why:** This feature may not be active for your account yet. It's being rolled out gradually.
**Fix:** Contact support to check availability.

## Tips
- 💡 Never reply to reviews when you're upset — wait at least 10 minutes
- 💡 Keep replies short and professional
- 💡 Thank the reviewer, even for negative reviews
- 💡 Take the conversation offline — "Please contact us at [number] so we can make this right"
- 💡 The best defense is consistent quality, not clever replies
