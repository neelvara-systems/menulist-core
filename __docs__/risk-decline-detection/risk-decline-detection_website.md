## Problem Statement

Most business declines happen slowly. Fewer returning customers. Lower engagement. Weakening trust. By the time it shows in revenue, the damage is months old. No tool gives business owners this early warning.

## Solution Statement

MenuList combines trust, loyalty, and engagement signals into one business health indicator. If patterns start declining, you'll know early enough to act. One word on your dashboard: Stable, Watch, or At Risk.

## Feature Benefits

1. **Early Warning** — Detect decline weeks before it hits your bottom line
2. **One Word** — "Stable", "Watch", or "At Risk." No complex dashboards.
3. **Automatic** — Computed weekly from real visitor behavior
4. **Actionable** — Early enough to investigate and course-correct

## FAQ

**Q: Can MenuList predict my revenue?**  
A: No. MenuList observes visitor behavior patterns. It detects shifts, not predicts outcomes. Early awareness, not forecasting.

**Q: What should I do if I see "At Risk"?**  
A: Check your reviews, service quality, menu accuracy, and competitive landscape. The signal tells you something is shifting — you decide what to investigate.
