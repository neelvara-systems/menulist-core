## Quick Summary

MenuList monitors your overall business health by combining trust, loyalty, and engagement signals. If patterns start declining, you'll see an early warning on your dashboard.

---

## Understanding the Signal

| Signal | What It Means | What To Do |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Stable** | All health signals are normal | Nothing — business is healthy |
| **Watch** | One signal is weakening | Check reviews, service quality, competition |
| **At Risk** | Multiple signals declining | Investigate immediately — something is shifting |

## When It Appears

This signal requires both Trust and Loyalty signals to be active (at least 4 weeks of visitor data each). It appears automatically when those prerequisites are met.

## FAQ

**Q: "At Risk" — should I panic?**  
A: No. This is early awareness, not an alarm. It means patterns are shifting. Investigate calmly — check reviews, menu accuracy, and recent changes.

**Q: How often does it update?**  
A: Once per week, automatically.
