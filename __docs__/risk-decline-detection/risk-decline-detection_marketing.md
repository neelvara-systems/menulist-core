## Elevator Pitch

"Businesses don't collapse suddenly. They decline slowly — and owners notice too late. MenuList quietly watches for early signs of decline and tells you before it becomes a revenue problem. One word: Stable, Watch, or At Risk."

## Feature Narrative

The most dangerous threat to any business isn't a sudden crisis — it's the slow, silent decline that goes unnoticed for months. Fewer returning customers, weakening trust, declining engagement — by the time owners see it in their revenue, the damage is deep. MenuList provides an early warning signal based on real customer behavior patterns. Not analytics. Not forecasting. Just calm awareness.
