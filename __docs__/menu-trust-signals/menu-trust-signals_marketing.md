# Menu Trust Signals — Marketing & Sales

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** March 15, 2026
> **Audience:** Internal sales/marketing team

---

## Elevator Pitch

When customers scan a QR and see your menu, they instantly know it's official and up to date. Subtle trust signals — "Official Menu" badge and "Updated today" indicator — build confidence before customers even start browsing.

## Feature Narrative

Digital menus often look like random web pages. Customers can't tell if prices are current or if the menu is actually maintained. MenuList adds quiet trust signals that answer these doubts immediately: this is the official menu, it was updated recently, and it belongs to this restaurant. Customers browse with confidence and order faster.

## Sales Talking Points

- "Your digital menu looks official — customers immediately trust it."
- "Unlike PDF menus or Google photos, your MenuList menu shows it was updated today."
- "Customers see your restaurant's name, logo, and 'Official Menu' badge — they know this is the real menu."

## Approved Language

- ✅ "Official menu" / "Trusted menu" / "Up-to-date menu"
- ✅ "Customer confidence" / "Professional presentation"
- ❌ Never say "verified" (implies third-party verification process)
- ❌ Never say "certified" or "authenticated"
