# Menu Trust Signals — Marketing & Sales

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** June 3, 2026
> **Audience:** Internal sales/marketing team

---

## Elevator Pitch

When customers scan a QR and see your menu, they can quickly tell it belongs to the right business and was updated recently. Subtle trust signals — location, open/closed status, menu type, and exact update date — build confidence before customers even start browsing.

## Feature Narrative

Digital menus often look like random web pages. Customers can't tell if prices are current or if the menu is actually maintained. MenuList adds quiet trust signals that answer these doubts immediately: this page is tied to the business location, it shows current operating status, and it was updated recently. Customers browse with confidence and order faster.

## Sales Talking Points

- "Customers can see the location, status, and update date before they browse."
- "Unlike PDF menus or Google photos, your MenuList menu can show when it was updated."
- "The menu uses factual signals instead of promotional badges."

## Approved Language

- ✅ "Current menu" / "Updated menu" / "Location-aware menu"
- ✅ "Customer confidence" / "Professional presentation" / "Factual trust signals"
- ❌ Never say "official" as a customer-facing badge claim
- ❌ Never say "verified" (implies third-party verification process)
- ❌ Never say "certified" or "authenticated"
