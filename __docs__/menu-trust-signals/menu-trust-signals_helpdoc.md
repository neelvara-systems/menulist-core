> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** June 3, 2026
> **Audience:** Business owners (customers)

## Quick Summary

When customers view your digital menu, they see subtle trust signals such as your location, open/closed status, menu type, and recent update date. These factual details help customers know the menu is current without adding extra clutter.

## How It Works

Trust signals appear automatically on your published menu. No setup needed.

When customers scan your QR code or open your menu link, they see:
- **Your business name and logo** at the top
- **Location** when your business area or city is available
- **Open/closed status** when working hours are configured
- **Menu, Service List, or Product Catalog** based on your business type
- **"Updated today"** or an exact date like **"Updated Mar 12"** when the menu was published recently

## What Customers See

| What | When It Shows |
|------|-------------|
| Location | If your area or city is saved |
| Open/closed status | If working hours are configured |
| Offering label | If the menu page is published |
| "Updated today" | If you published the menu today |
| "Updated Mar 12" | If you published within the last 30 days |

If your menu has not been updated in over 30 days, the freshness date is hidden so the page does not overstate freshness.

## Tips

- Publish your menu regularly to keep the "Updated today" signal active
- Keep working hours current so open/closed status stays reliable
- Add your area or city so customers can recognize the right location
