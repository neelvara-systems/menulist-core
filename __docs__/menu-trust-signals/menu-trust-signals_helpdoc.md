# Menu Trust Signals — Help Documentation

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** March 15, 2026
> **Audience:** Business owners (customers)

---

## Quick Summary

When customers view your digital menu, they see subtle trust signals — "Official Menu" badge and "Updated today" indicator — that tell them your menu is real, maintained, and current.

## How It Works

Trust signals appear automatically on your published menu. No setup needed.

When customers scan your QR code or open your menu link, they see:
- **Your business name and logo** at the top
- **"Official Menu"** badge (or "Official Services" for non-food businesses)
- **"Updated today"** or **"Updated this week"** indicator showing freshness

## What Customers See

| What | When It Shows |
|------|-------------|
| "Official Menu" badge | Always (when menu is published) |
| "Updated today" | If you published or edited your menu today |
| "Updated this week" | If you published within the last 7 days |
| "Updated recently" | If you published within the last 30 days |

If your menu hasn't been updated in over 30 days, the freshness indicator is hidden — but the "Official Menu" badge still shows.

## Tips

- Publish your menu regularly to keep the "Updated today" signal active
- Even small edits (like marking an item sold out) refresh the update indicator
- The more frequently you update, the more customers trust your menu
