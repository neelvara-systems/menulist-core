# MenuList Activation Concierge - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal positioning
**Created:** June 24, 2026

## Positioning

Activation Concierge is not a new public product name. It is the internal name for making MenuList activation practical for owners.

Approved public framing:

```txt
Create one current customer link and place it where customers already look.
```

Short outreach framing:

```txt
One current menu link for QR, WhatsApp, Google/Profile, Instagram, and repeat customers. You review it before publishing.
```

Partner framing:

```txt
Help your restaurant clients replace scattered menu images, PDFs, and outdated links with one current customer-ready menu link.
```

Founder/content framing:

```txt
Most restaurants do not need a bigger marketing stack first. They need one current menu customers can open, share, and trust.
```

## Why It Matters

SignalDesk can prepare distribution work, but MenuList wins only when a business gets activated:

- current list source exists;
- owner reviews the preview;
- customer link is published;
- at least two customer surfaces use the same link;
- proof can be created from real owner-confirmed use.

## Approved Claims

Use only when true for the specific flow:

- owner reviews before publishing;
- one customer link can be used across QR, WhatsApp, Google/Profile, Instagram, website, print, and staff replies;
- current link can replace old screenshots, PDFs, and scattered menu links;
- starter activation tracks whether the link is actually used on customer surfaces.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not say:

- guaranteed sales;
- automatic Google update;
- automatic Instagram/WhatsApp posting;
- official WhatsApp/Google/Instagram partnership;
- AI will handle your marketing;
- we scraped your restaurant;
- QR creates retention by itself;
- MenuList replaces POS/ordering systems.
