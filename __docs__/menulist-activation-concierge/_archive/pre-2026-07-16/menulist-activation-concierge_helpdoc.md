# MenuList Activation Concierge - Owner Help Doc

**Status:** Owner-facing help draft
**Created:** June 24, 2026

## Get Your Customer Link Live

MenuList helps you turn your current menu, service list, catalog, or price list into one customer link.

You review the link before publishing. After it is live, use the same link where customers already look.

## Steps

1. Upload your current list.

   You can use a clear photo, image file, PDF where supported, or menu link where the page can be read.

2. Review the preview.

   Check names, prices, sections, phone number, address, and customer actions.

3. Approve when it is correct.

   MenuList publishes the customer link only after approval.

4. Use the link on two customer surfaces.

   Start with two of these:

   - WhatsApp;
   - QR;
   - Instagram;
   - Google/Profile;
   - website or link page;
   - print material;
   - staff replies.

5. Keep using the same link.

   When the list changes, update MenuList instead of sending old screenshots or PDFs.

## What Counts As Setup Complete

Setup is complete when your customer link is live and used on two customer surfaces within seven days.

Examples:

- WhatsApp + QR;
- Instagram + QR;
- Google/Profile + WhatsApp;
- Website + print material.

## What MenuList Does Not Do

- MenuList does not automatically post to Google, Instagram, or WhatsApp.
- MenuList does not guarantee more sales.
- MenuList does not change external platforms unless you or your team place the link there.
- MenuList does not publish unreviewed menu details.

## If Something Looks Wrong

Do not publish until the preview is correct.

Send corrections or update the menu from your workspace.
