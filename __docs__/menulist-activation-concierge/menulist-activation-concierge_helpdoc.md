# Starter Sharing Steps - Help

**Status:** Local source complete

Setup access asks for two different practical sharing or placement actions.

MenuList can record when you copy the official link, download a QR/Menu Kit, complete device sharing, or open WhatsApp sharing. For Google, Apple, Bing, Instagram, or a WhatsApp profile, use Search & Discovery to confirm that you placed the link.

The label tells you how MenuList knows:

- **MenuList recorded:** an action completed inside MenuList.
- **Owner confirmed:** you marked an external placement yourself.

If you remove an external placement confirmation, it stops counting. These steps do not mean MenuList verified the third-party page or that customers used the link. MenuList does not automatically post to external platforms and does not guarantee traffic or sales.
