## Feature Mention

- **Title:** Cleaner item photos
- **Body:** Add an item photo with a simple frame. MenuList prepares the image for the menu automatically.

## FAQ

- **Q:** Does the owner need to crop photos manually?
  **A:** No. MenuList prepares item photos for the menu after the photo is added.

- **Q:** Can owners still upload a saved photo?
  **A:** Yes. The existing upload option remains available.

- **Q:** Is this required for every item?
  **A:** No. Owners can still publish with missing photos.
