# Item Photo Capture Assist Help Documentation

## Quick Summary

You can take an item photo while adding images to a menu item. MenuList prepares the saved image for the menu.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- A menu project.
- At least one menu item.
- Camera permission if you want to take a new photo.

## How To Take An Item Photo

1. Open **Menu**.
2. Open the item that needs a photo.
3. Choose **Add Images** or **Edit Images**.
4. Open the upload tab.
5. Select **Top-down** or **Closer**.
6. Tap **Start Camera**.
7. Place the item inside the frame.
8. Tap **Use Photo**.
9. Tap **Upload Image**.

> Screenshot: Item image upload modal with camera guide.

## How To Upload A Saved Photo

1. Open the same item image screen.
2. Use the upload box below the camera guide.
3. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
4. Tap **Upload Image**.

> Screenshot: Existing file upload box below camera guide.

## If The Camera Does Not Open

1. Check browser camera permission.
2. Use the saved-photo upload option.
3. Try again from a mobile browser if the desktop has no camera.

## Tips

- Place the dish near good light.
- Keep extra items out of the frame.
- Keep the item inside the guide.
- Hold the phone steady before tapping **Use Photo**.
