# Branded QR Action Templates Marketing

**Status:** Internal
**Last Updated:** June 26, 2026

## One-Liner

Turn each printed QR into a branded customer action point.

## Positioning

Branded QR Action Templates make MenuList print files feel like part of the business, not a generic QR sticker.

The value is not prettier QR codes. The value is a finished physical prompt with a clear customer action: view the current menu, order, leave feedback, book, join, reorder, or claim an offer.

## Approved Talking Points

- "Each QR file says what customers should do next."
- "Your business name, color, logo, short link, and QR stay together."
- "The QR stays scan-safe."
- "The desktop editor includes guided QR action cards for common business jobs."
- "Standard QR files keep opening the live MenuList page."
- "Campaign QR tests are separate and measured only when enabled."

## Avoid

- "AI QR art"
- "Decorative QR"
- "Guaranteed scan lift"
- "Guaranteed sales lift"
- "Secure QR"
- "Verified QR"
- "No spam QR"
- "Official WhatsApp QR"

## SMB Examples

| Business | Action Template |
| --- | --- |
| Cafe | Scan to order again, scan for today's menu, scan for feedback. |
| Salon | Scan to book, scan for services, scan for a gift certificate. |
| Retail counter | Scan for product info, scan for loyalty, scan for offer. |
| Clinic/service business | Scan for service list, scan to request appointment, scan for directions. |
| Caterer/event business | Scan for event menu, scan for quote request, scan for booking. |

## Pitch Boundary

Do not publish conversion-lift claims until QR WhatsApp Experiments or another measured runtime proves them with real store data.
