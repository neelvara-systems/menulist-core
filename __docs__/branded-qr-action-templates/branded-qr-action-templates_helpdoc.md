# Branded QR Action Templates Help Doc Draft

**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** June 26, 2026

## What It Means

Some printed files are not just QR codes. They tell the customer what to do:

- view the current menu or services;
- order;
- leave feedback;
- book;
- join a loyalty list;
- scan an offer;
- reorder later.

MenuList keeps the QR easy to scan and places your business name, color, logo, short link, and instruction around it.

## In The Editor

On desktop, choose the QRCode tab and pick an action style such as Menu, Feedback, Order, Offer, Book, or Loyalty. Use Add action card for a finished scan prompt. Use Add plain QR only when you need a simple QR without surrounding copy.

## Best Practices

- Use one action per file.
- Put the benefit near the QR.
- Keep the QR area clean.
- Test the QR after printing.
- Keep the short link visible when possible.
- Replace damaged or covered QR cards.

## What Not To Do

- Do not cover the QR with a logo or photo.
- Do not use low-contrast colors inside the QR.
- Do not print vague copy like "scan me" when you can say what the customer gets.
- Do not use a WhatsApp offer QR unless the campaign is set up for that purpose.

## Good Examples

| Use | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Table | Scan for today's menu |
| Counter | Scan to order |
| Receipt insert | Scan to reorder |
| Exit | Scan to leave feedback |
| Business card | Scan to view services |
| Flyer | Scan to claim this offer |

## Normal Menu QR

Your normal menu QR opens your live MenuList page directly. When you update the menu, the printed QR still opens the latest version.
