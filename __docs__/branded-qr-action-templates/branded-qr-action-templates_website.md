# Branded QR Action Templates Website Guidance

**Status:** Internal
**Last Updated:** June 25, 2026

## Website Decision

Use this concept to strengthen the Print Kit/Assets page, not to create a separate public QR-code product page.

The public phrase should be practical:

```text
Branded QR files for every customer action.
```

## Where It Fits

| Website Surface | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | Keep compact `Print files` output. Do not over-index on QR. |
| Print Kit feature page | Add a section showing table/counter/packaging/flyer/business-card action examples. |
| WhatsApp page | Only mention tracked QR campaigns after QR WhatsApp Experiments runtime exists. |
| Help/resources | Explain scan-safe printing, QR placement, and short link trust cues. |

## Good Copy

- "Print files that tell customers exactly what to scan for."
- "Menu, order, feedback, booking, offer, and reorder QR files from the same current business page."
- "Brand around the QR. Keep the QR itself easy to scan."

## Avoid

- "Artistic QR generator"
- "Create unlimited QR art"
- "Increase scans by 40%"
- "Official QR"
- "Secure QR"

## Visual Direction

Use images like printed table stands, packaging stickers, flyers, business cards, and counter cards. Avoid screenshots of distorted artistic QR codes as the main public proof.
