## Quick Summary

Special menus let you temporarily show a different menu to your customers — for festivals, events, seasons, or any special occasion. Your regular menu stays safe and comes back automatically when the special period ends.

---

## Getting Started

### What You Need

- A MenuList account with an active menu
- Your regular menu already set up

### When to Use This

- Diwali, Christmas, Eid, or other festival menus
- Sunday brunch or weekend specials
- Event nights (live music, match day)
- Seasonal items (mango season, winter specials)
- Wedding or festive service packages (salons, spas)

## How to Create a Special Menu

### Step 1: Open your menu list

Go to your dashboard and look at your menus. You'll see a **"Create Special Menu"** button.

`[Screenshot: Dashboard with "Create Special Menu" button]`

### Step 2: Give it a name

Type a name for your special menu. Examples:

- "Diwali Sweets Menu"
- "Sunday Brunch"
- "IPL Night Specials"
- "Wedding Packages"

`[Screenshot: Name input field]`

### Step 3: Choose how it appears

You'll see two options:

- **Replace my regular menu** — Customers see only the special menu during the period
- **Add as special section** — Special items appear alongside your regular menu

Pick what makes sense for your occasion.

`[Screenshot: Mode selection]`

> **Note:** Some business types only see the "Add as special section" option. This is normal — the system picks what works best for your type of business.

### Step 4: Set the dates

Choose when your special menu should start and when it should end.

- **Starts:** Pick a date and time
- **Ends:** Pick a date and time

Your special menu will appear automatically at the start time and disappear at the end time.

`[Screenshot: Date/time picker]`

### Step 5: Edit your special menu

Your special menu starts as a copy of your current regular menu. Now you can:

- **Remove** items you don't want in the special menu
- **Add** new festival or special items
- **Change** prices for the special period
- **Rearrange** categories

This uses the same editor you already use for your regular menu.

`[Screenshot: Editor with special menu items]`

### Step 6: Save

That's it! Your special menu is scheduled. You'll see it in your menu list with a "Scheduled" tag.

## Managing Your Special Menus

### Viewing Status

Your menu list shows the status of each special menu:

| Status        | What It Means                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Scheduled** | Waiting to start at the date you set |
| **Active**    | Currently showing to customers       |
| **Ended**     | Period is over, regular menu is back |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it before it started   |

### Editing a Scheduled Menu

You can edit a special menu before it becomes active. Just open it from your menu list and make changes.

### Ending a Special Menu Early

If you need to end a special menu before its scheduled end time:

1. Go to your menu list
2. Find the active special menu
3. Tap **"End Now"**
4. Your regular menu comes back immediately

### Cancelling a Scheduled Menu

If you changed your mind about a scheduled menu:

1. Go to your menu list
2. Find the scheduled special menu
3. Tap **"Cancel"**
4. The special menu won't activate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will my regular menu be affected?

**No.** Your regular menu stays exactly as it is. Special menus are completely separate. When the special period ends, your regular menu is back — exactly as you left it.

### Can I have multiple special menus?

You can **schedule** multiple special menus for different dates. For example, schedule your Diwali menu for October and your Christmas menu for December. But only one can be **active** at a time.

### What if my special menu dates overlap with another?

The system won't allow overlapping schedules. You'll need to adjust the dates so they don't conflict.

### Do QR codes and screens also show the special menu?

**Yes for MenuList-controlled live surfaces.** Your public page, QR code link, and configured digital screens follow the active menu through their supported refresh paths. Downloaded or printed PDFs should be regenerated or replaced after changes, and connected provider systems need separate integration evidence.

### What happens if I forget about a special menu?

Nothing bad. It ends automatically at the time you set. Your regular menu comes back on its own. That's the whole point.

### Can I reuse a special menu next year?

Special menus stay in your list after they end. You can create a new one for next year using the same approach.

### Does this cost extra?

No. Special menu switching is included in your MenuList plan.

## Tips

- **Prepare ahead:** Create your festival menu a week early. Schedule it, and forget about it.
- **Start from your current menu:** Your special menu starts as a copy — just add/remove what you need instead of building from scratch.
- **Check the dates:** Make sure your end date is correct so your regular menu comes back at the right time.
- **One at a time:** Only one special menu can be active. Plan your calendar so dates don't overlap.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                                    | Solution                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "I can't create a special menu"            | Make sure you have at least one regular menu first                                       |
| "Dates are overlapping"                    | Check your other scheduled menus and adjust dates                                        |
| "Special menu didn't appear"               | Check the start time — it might not have arrived yet                                     |
| "My regular menu is different than before" | This shouldn't happen. Contact support — regular menu is never modified by special menus |
