"Your menu changes during festivals, events, and seasons. With MenuList, schedule a separate Diwali menu, one-time Sunday brunch, or seasonal special — then let the regular menu return automatically."

---

## Feature Narrative

### The Problem (Owner's Voice)

> "Every Diwali, I spend hours editing my menu. I add sweets, change prices, add gift boxes. Then after 5 days, I have to undo everything. Last year I forgot to remove the Diwali combos for 3 weeks. Customers kept asking for items I didn't have anymore."

### The Solution

MenuList now lets you create a separate special menu for any occasion. Set when it starts and ends. The system handles everything — your regular menu stays untouched and comes back automatically.

### The Outcome

- Regular menu never gets messy
- Festivals, events, seasons handled cleanly
- Automatic switch-back (no forgetting)
- Same editor you already know
- Works on your public page, QR code link, and configured screens through supported refresh paths

## Language Governance Compliance

### Approved Language

- "Special menu" (not "dynamic menu" or "smart menu")
- "Schedule" (not "automate" or "AI-powered scheduling")
- "Switch" (not "deploy" or "push")
- "Appears" / "Shows up" (not "activates" or "triggers")
- "Your regular menu comes back" (not "auto-reverts" or "system deactivates")

### Forbidden Language

- ❌ "AI-powered menu switching"
- ❌ "Dynamic menu engine"
- ❌ "Smart scheduling"
- ❌ "Campaign" or "promotion" (this is NOT marketing tool)
- ❌ "Deploy your festival menu"
- ❌ "Menu automation platform"

## Product-Boundary Positioning

Keep claims tied to current MenuList source evidence: public menu/QR link, OBP menu entry, and configured Digital Screen paths follow the active-menu resolver and refresh contract. Do not claim POS synchronization, downloaded-file replacement, recurring schedules, instant switching, or competitor limitations without separate current evidence.
