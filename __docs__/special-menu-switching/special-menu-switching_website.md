## Feature Benefits (3-5 Outcomes)

### 1. Your regular menu never gets messy

Create a separate special menu for any occasion. Your daily menu stays exactly where it is — untouched, safe, ready to come back.

### 2. Set it today, it appears when you want

Schedule your Diwali menu weeks in advance. MenuList checks the boundary every two minutes, so the live menu normally changes within a few minutes of the time you set.

### 3. Never forget to switch back

When the special period ends, your regular menu returns automatically. No manual work. No "I forgot to remove the Diwali combos."

### 4. Works where your MenuList menu appears

Your QR code link, public page, and configured digital screens follow the scheduled menu through their supported refresh paths. Downloaded or printed menus should be regenerated or replaced after changes.

### 5. Same editor you already know

No new tool to learn. Create your special menu the same way you create your regular menu. Add items, set prices, arrange categories.

## How It Works (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Create your special menu

Click "Create Special Menu". Replace mode starts from your current menu; Add as special section starts with empty temporary rows. Add the festival items, seasonal dishes, or event specials you need.

### Step 2: Set the dates

Choose when it starts and when it ends. Tomorrow, next week, or next month — you decide.

### Step 3: That's it

MenuList handles the scheduled switch. Your special menu appears on the public link, OBP menu entry, and configured screens through their supported refresh paths, and your regular menu comes back automatically when it's over.

## FAQ

### Can I have multiple special menus scheduled?

Yes. You can prepare separate one-time Diwali, Christmas, and New Year schedules in advance. Only one can be active at a time. Recurring schedules are not part of the current flow.

### What happens to my regular menu?

Nothing. Your regular menu stays exactly as it is. When a special menu is active, customers see the special menu instead (or alongside it, depending on your choice). When it ends, your regular menu is back.

### Can I end a special menu early?

Yes. One tap to end it immediately. Your regular menu comes back right away.

### Do I need to learn a new editor?

No. You create special menus using the exact same editor you use for your regular menu. Same interface, same features.

### Does it work on QR codes, screens, and my public page?

Yes for MenuList-controlled live surfaces: your QR code link/public page, OBP menu entry, and configured screens follow the active menu through their supported refresh paths. Downloaded PDFs, printed copies, and connected provider systems need replacement, regeneration, or integration evidence.
