# MyCodex Founder Console Website Boundary

## Public impact

None.

The console is private, noindex, nofollow, noarchive, and available only through the owner application's authenticated internal path. It has no public landing page, sitemap entry, public navigation, customer help article, pricing plan, or sales claim.

MenuList and Answerlattice public websites must not mention internal platform controls, customer counts, provider health, costs, schedulers, or founder operations.
