# Owner Installed App Help

You can install MenuList from a supported browser and open it from your home screen.

- The installed app opens on **Today**.
- If your connection drops, you can keep reviewing the open screen. Saving and online actions may not finish until you reconnect.
- MenuList does not silently queue or replay changes made offline.
- Reconnecting does not refresh the app automatically.
- When a new version is ready, choose **Refresh now** when you are not editing, or dismiss the message and update later in the same session.

If the generic offline screen appears, reconnect and choose **Try again**.
