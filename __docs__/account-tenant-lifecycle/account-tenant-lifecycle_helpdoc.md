# Account And Tenant Lifecycle Help

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** from the desktop profile menu or mobile More screen. MenuList
ends the signed session and clears the previous account's active browser state.
Language, theme, consent, and install preferences can remain on the device.

## Remove staff

An authorized owner can remove a staff member from one store. If it is their
last assigned store, their account is deactivated and existing sessions are
revoked.

## Privacy request

Email `support@menulist.ai` from the account email where possible. Include the
business name, account identifier, and whether the request is for access,
correction, portability, or deletion. Support verifies identity and authority
and identifies any subscription, shared-access, billing, dispute, security, or
legal-retention constraint before acting.
