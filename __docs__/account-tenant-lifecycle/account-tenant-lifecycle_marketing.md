# Account And Tenant Lifecycle Marketing

Approved public statements:

- Owners can request access, correction, portability, or deletion where
  applicable.
- MenuList verifies identity and business authority before acting.
- Billing, dispute, security, shared-business access, and legal retention can
  limit or delay account closure or deletion.

Do not advertise instant deletion, instant full-account export, guaranteed
erasure of legally retained records, or an owner self-service control that the
runtime does not provide.
