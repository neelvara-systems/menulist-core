# UGC Script Studio - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

UGC Script Studio helps a local business or agency prepare local creator test briefs from real campaign facts.

## Buyer Promise

- A clear brief instead of vague instructions.
- Role-specific scripts for owner, staff, local creator, or agency handoff.
- Creator-fit checks and small local test plans before repeating spend.
- Claim checks before the brief leaves the workspace.
- Exportable scripts and shot lists.

## Proof Points

- Script, hook, shot list, CTA, and disclosure notes in one pack.
- Creator/audience-fit checklist, flat-fee boundary, and result prompt in the same handoff.
- Source-linked claims.
- Owner approval log.
- Agency handoff support.

## Avoid

- Do not imply CampaignCue supplies creators.
- Do not imply CampaignCue manages creator deals, contracts, payments, or performance.
- Do not imply legal clearance for regulated claims.
- Do not frame fake customer scripts as acceptable.
