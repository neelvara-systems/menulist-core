# UGC Script Studio - Help Doc

## What This Does

UGC Script Studio creates a script and brief for campaign content that a staff member, creator, or agency can produce.

## How To Use It

1. Open a campaign pack.
2. Choose the creator role.
3. Choose the channel and CTA.
4. Create the brief.
5. Review the script, shot list, talking points, and do/don't notes.
6. Approve or regenerate the brief.
7. Export or send it to the agency workspace.

## Important Rules

- Do not ask someone to pretend to be a real customer.
- Do not use review quotes unless they are approved and allowed for use.
- Do not make result claims unless the business can support them.
- Add disclosure language when the content is sponsored or paid.
