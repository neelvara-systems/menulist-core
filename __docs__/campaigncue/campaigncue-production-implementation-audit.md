# CampaignCue Production Implementation Audit

## Current Verdict

CampaignCue now has a production-safe export/download-first runtime in the shared repo:

- Public product shell: implementation_done, audit_done, docs_aligned, prod_ready. Public CampaignCue website files live under `src/app/sites/campaigncue`.
- Protected workspace app: implementation_done, audit_done, docs_aligned, blocked_for_production until the CampaignCue Firebase project is provisioned/deployed. Owner workspace files live under `src/app/(campaigncue)/campaigncue/app`, while `/__campaigncue/app` locally and `/app` on the CampaignCue product domain rewrite to `/campaigncue/app`. Product-domain `/api/*` traffic passes through before CampaignCue public-site rewrites, so `campaigncue.ai/api/campaigncue/*` reaches the protected API layer. When the external Firebase blocker is hit, protected APIs now return `503` with `CAMPAIGNCUE_FIREBASE_UNAVAILABLE`, and the app shows an owner-safe setup-not-ready state instead of a generic failure or Firebase instructions.
- Owner workspace screens now exist for Home, Business Details, Inputs, Export and Download, Settings, Campaign Ideas, Campaign Packs, Creative/Social, Video/Reels, UGC Scripts, WhatsApp, Google Local, Ads, Checks, Calendar, Assets, Results, Agency, Locations, and Plan/Access posture. Navigation is grouped as Start, Campaigns, Channels, and Operations, and the home screen includes a first-use checklist for business details, today's input, pack creation, and manual posting.
- The market-gap implementation pass is now in place: source facts, richer cue evidence, structured channel outputs, stronger vertical/channel trust checks, owner-first source visibility, guided manual handoff, owner-reported outcomes, agency/location safety signals, asset consent metadata, and in-app launch-readiness checks.
- Social account connection, direct provider publishing, WhatsApp direct send, ad spend mutation, paid AI generation, billing checkout, and rendered video provider calls remain intentionally absent from the active runtime. Provider posture is read-only future-layer information.

## Remaining Risks

- Firebase rules/indexes/storage deployment and authenticated owner write-flow testing are blocked by project access/provisioning: `campaigncue-qa` returned HTTP 403 project not found or permission denied. Until the dedicated CampaignCue Firebase project and Admin credentials exist, local tests cannot complete real signed-in workspace bootstrap, business-details saves, source input saves, campaign creation, asset registration, manual schedule writes, approval writes, or outcome writes against CampaignCue Firestore.
- Product-domain auth mapping needs a final launch decision for `campaigncue.ai/app` sign-in behavior.
- Rendered PNG/JPG creative generation and MP4 video assembly are not active; the current runtime provides campaign text, creative briefs, reel briefs, and manual text downloads only.
- Credit reservation/capture/refund, billing checkout, 30-day provider asset generation, and paid media/video providers remain disabled until billing and provider cost controls are configured.
- Multi-location location records are implemented; external group campaign automation and provider/location sync remain blocked until a separate future provider layer exists.
- Direct provider publishing, WhatsApp direct send, ad spend mutation, billing checkout, paid AI generation, and rendered video remain intentionally blocked.
