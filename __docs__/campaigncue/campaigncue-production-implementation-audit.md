# CampaignCue Production Implementation Audit

## Current Verdict

CampaignCue now has a production-safe manual/export-first runtime in the shared repo:

- Public product shell: implementation_done, audit_done, docs_aligned, prod_ready. Public CampaignCue website files live under `src/app/sites/campaigncue`.
- Protected workspace app: implementation_done, audit_done, docs_aligned, blocked_for_production until the CampaignCue Firebase project is provisioned/deployed. Owner workspace files live under `src/app/(campaigncue)/campaigncue/app`, while `/__campaigncue/app` locally and `/app` on the CampaignCue product domain rewrite to `/campaigncue/app`. When the external Firebase blocker is hit, protected APIs now return `503` with `CAMPAIGNCUE_FIREBASE_UNAVAILABLE`, and the app shows an owner-safe setup-not-ready state instead of a generic failure or Firebase instructions.
- Owner workspace screens now exist for Home, Business Details, Inputs, Posting Connections, Settings, Campaign Ideas, Campaign Packs, Creative/Social, Video/Reels, UGC Scripts, WhatsApp, Google Local, Ads, Checks, Calendar, Assets, Results, Agency, Locations, and Plan/Access posture. Navigation is grouped as Start, Campaigns, Channels, and Operations, and the home screen includes a first-use checklist for business details, today's input, pack creation, and manual posting.
- Direct provider publishing, WhatsApp direct send, ad spend mutation, paid AI generation, billing checkout, and rendered video provider calls remain intentionally blocked by flags and provider posture.

Production readiness depends on external environment setup:

- `CAMPAIGNCUE_FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_CAMPAIGNCUE_FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID`
- `CAMPAIGNCUE_FIREBASE_CLIENT_EMAIL`
- `CAMPAIGNCUE_FIREBASE_PRIVATE_KEY` or `CAMPAIGNCUE_GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS`
- Firebase deploy using `firebase-campaigncue.json`
- Vercel/domain mapping for `campaigncue.ai`

## Remaining Risks

- Firebase rules/indexes/storage deployment is blocked by project access/provisioning: `campaigncue-qa` returned HTTP 403 project not found or permission denied.
- Product-domain auth mapping needs a final launch decision for `campaigncue.ai/app` sign-in behavior.
- Rendered PNG/JPG creative generation and MP4 video assembly are not active; the current runtime provides copy, creative briefs, reel briefs, and manual text downloads only.
- Credit reservation/capture/refund, billing checkout, 30-day provider asset generation, and paid media/video providers remain disabled until billing and provider cost controls are configured.
- Multi-location location records are implemented; external group campaign automation and provider/location sync remain blocked until provider setup exists.
- Direct provider publishing, WhatsApp direct send, ad spend mutation, billing checkout, paid AI generation, and rendered video remain intentionally blocked.
