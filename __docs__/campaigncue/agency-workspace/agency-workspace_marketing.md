# Agency Workspace - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Agency Workspace helps local-marketing teams create, approve, and report on campaign work across multiple clients.

## Buyer Promise

- Move faster across clients without mixing facts.
- Get owner approval on the exact output version.
- Reuse campaign structures safely.
- Share reports that show what happened and what comes next.

## Proof Points

- Multi-client dashboard.
- Approval requests.
- Reusable templates.
- Client report snapshots.

## Avoid

- Do not position CampaignCue as a full agency operating system.
- Do not imply client data is shared across workspaces.
- Do not promise automated agency billing unless implemented.
