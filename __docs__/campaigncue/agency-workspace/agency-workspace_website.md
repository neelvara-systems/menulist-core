# Agency Workspace - Website Content

## Section Goal

Explain agency use without making CampaignCue sound like a CRM or full agency management suite.

## Approved Public Copy

> Manage campaign work across local-business clients with separated workspaces, approval requests, reusable templates, and client-ready reports.

## Supporting Points

- Client workspaces stay separated.
- Campaign templates do not carry client facts across accounts.
- Owners can approve or comment on exact output versions.
- Reports connect campaign outputs to results and next cues.

## Do Not Say

- Do not promise full CRM replacement.
- Do not imply client data can be blended.
- Do not promise agency invoicing unless implemented.
