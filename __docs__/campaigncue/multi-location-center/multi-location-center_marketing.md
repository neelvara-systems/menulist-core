# Multi-Location Center - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Prepare one campaign idea for several branches without mixing up branch contacts or approvals.

## Owner Value

- Reuse the campaign intent instead of rebuilding each branch pack.
- Apply each branch's area and contact details.
- Review trust and approval separately per branch.
- Export and record results independently.
- Keep manual delivery in the owner's control.

## Proof We Can State

- One to eight selected active branches per batch.
- Branch contact overrides with confirmed business-detail fallback.
- Independent campaign and trust records.
- Branch/global freshness recheck before public use.
- Assigned-location enforcement for local managers.
- No extra multi-location collection or realtime listener.

## Avoid

- Do not say CampaignCue syncs every branch with external providers.
- Do not promise one approval publishes everywhere.
- Do not claim branch-level revenue attribution.
- Do not imply all branches share prices, hours, stock, services, or terms.
