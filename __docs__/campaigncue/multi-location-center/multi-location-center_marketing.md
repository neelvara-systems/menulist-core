# Multi-Location Center - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Multi-Location Center helps growing local businesses run campaigns across branches without losing local accuracy.

## Buyer Promise

- One campaign idea can be localized for many branches.
- Local facts remain correct.
- Approvals and publishing status are visible per location.
- Results can be compared by branch or group.

## Proof Points

- Location-specific facts.
- Group campaign planning.
- Local approval state.
- Location analytics rollup.

## Avoid

- Do not imply all branches share the same details.
- Do not promise one-click publishing everywhere without connected channels.
- Do not blur location-specific customer promises.
