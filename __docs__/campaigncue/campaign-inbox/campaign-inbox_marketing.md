# Campaign Inbox - Marketing Boundary

## Approved Description

Campaign Inbox gives owners one short place to add what changed, review the detected details, and make current facts available to the Daily Campaign Desk.

## Approved Claims

- Review several current campaign details together.
- Keep canonical business details in one protected place.
- Save selected facts in one confirmation.
- Recompute recommendations from owner-approved source inputs.
- No social account connection is required.

## Rejected Claims

- `CampaignCue understands everything you say.`
- `CampaignCue automatically updates your business.`
- `One-minute guaranteed campaign creation.`
- `AI knows which facts are correct.`
- `Post everywhere from one message.`

## Positioning

The differentiator is not conversational novelty. It is a low-effort, review-first route from an owner update to auditable business source facts. Models may later help produce candidates, but rules, validation, and owner confirmation remain authoritative.
