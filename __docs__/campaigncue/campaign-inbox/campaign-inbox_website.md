# Campaign Inbox - Website Boundary

## Current Decision

Campaign Inbox is an owner-workspace capability. The initial implementation does not require a new public feature page, homepage section, or navigation item.

The public website may mention it only after runtime validation, using this bounded statement:

> Add what changed, review the details, and let CampaignCue prepare recommendations from the facts you approve.

Do not describe it as autonomous, always accurate, model-powered, or connected to social accounts.

## Future Proof Requirement

Before adding a website proof point, capture a real product screenshot showing:

1. the owner draft;
2. the candidate review;
3. the protected Business Details route; and
4. the resulting Daily Desk update.

No fabricated performance metric or time-saved claim may be published.
