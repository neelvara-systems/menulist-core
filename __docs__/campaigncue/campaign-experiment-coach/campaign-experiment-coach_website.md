# Campaign Experiment Coach - Website Boundary

This capability can appear on the CampaignCue website only as a supporting part of the result-learning loop, not as a headline A/B testing or optimization claim.

Approved copy:

> Try one clear change, keep the rest stable, and record what happened.

Public copy must not imply automatic distribution, statistical testing, prediction, provider experiments, or guaranteed results. Product screenshots should show owner approval and owner-reported evidence states.
