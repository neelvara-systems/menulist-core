# Campaign Experiment Coach - Marketing Boundary

## Approved Description

CampaignCue helps owners try one controlled campaign change and record what happened.

## Do Not Claim

- automatic A/B testing;
- statistically proven optimization;
- predicted engagement, bookings, revenue, or growth;
- autonomous campaign strategy;
- provider-side experiment management; or
- guaranteed improvement.

Owner-reported history is directional evidence, not causal proof.
