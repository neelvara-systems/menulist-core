# Asset Library - Website Content

## Section Goal

Explain that CampaignCue stores and reuses campaign assets responsibly.

## Approved Public Copy

> Keep source files, approved creative, channel variants, rights notes, and campaign usage history in one library.

## Supporting Points

- Organizes assets by campaign, channel, type, status, and location.
- Keeps source and permission notes visible.
- Lets approved assets feed future campaigns.
- Separates drafts, approved assets, exports, and blocked assets.

## Do Not Say

- Do not claim automatic rights clearance.
- Do not promise unlimited storage.
- Do not say it replaces a full enterprise DAM.
