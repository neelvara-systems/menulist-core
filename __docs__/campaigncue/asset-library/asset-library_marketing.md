# Asset Library - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Asset Library keeps a business campaign-ready by organizing approved images, generated variants, source files, and exported assets.

## Buyer Promise

- Reuse the best approved campaign assets.
- See where an asset came from.
- Keep channel variants organized.
- Avoid recreating the same material repeatedly.

## Proof Points

- Source and rights metadata.
- Campaign usage history.
- Approval state.
- Channel filters.

## Avoid

- Do not position it as an enterprise asset-management platform.
- Do not imply rights clearance is automatic.
- Do not imply every uploaded asset is safe to use publicly.
