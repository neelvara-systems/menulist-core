# Creative Trust Center - Mobile Support

## Mobile Admission

Creative Trust Center is mandatory on mobile because owners may approve campaign outputs from phone.

## Mobile Requirements

- Show trust state near every export/publish action.
- Use clear finding cards with source and recommended fix.
- Keep fix, regenerate, acknowledge, and approve actions large.
- Avoid dense compliance tables.
- Show blocked state before any channel handoff.

## Mobile Non-Goals

- Full rule editor.
- Bulk acknowledgement management.
- Legal-policy administration.

## Acceptance

- Owner can understand why a campaign is blocked from mobile.
- Owner can navigate from finding to the campaign output that needs fixing.
