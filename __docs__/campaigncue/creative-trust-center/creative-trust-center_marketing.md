# Creative Trust Center - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Creative Trust Center helps businesses review campaign content before it reaches customers or platforms.

## Buyer Promise

- See risky claims before publishing.
- Know which business facts support the output.
- Keep owner approval in the workflow.
- Reduce avoidable platform and customer-trust mistakes.

## Proof Points

- Source-linked findings.
- Channel-specific checks.
- Version-specific approval.
- Clear warning and blocked states.

## Avoid

- Do not call it legal approval.
- Do not promise platform acceptance.
- Do not imply the product certifies all media rights.
