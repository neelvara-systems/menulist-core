# Creative Trust Center - Website Content

## Section Goal

Explain trust checks as a core product differentiator, not as legal or platform approval.

## Approved Public Copy

> Review campaign claims, offers, dates, locations, source facts, and channel risks before content is exported or scheduled.

## Supporting Points

- Flags unsupported prices, dates, urgency, result claims, and review-like language.
- Shows which source facts support the campaign.
- Applies checks per channel and output version.
- Keeps owner approval before handoff.

## Do Not Say

- Do not promise legal compliance.
- Do not guarantee platform approval.
- Do not imply every risk can be detected automatically.
