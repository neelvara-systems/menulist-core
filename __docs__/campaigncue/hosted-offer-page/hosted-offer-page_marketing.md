# Hosted Offer Page And QR - Marketing Boundary

## Supported

- "Give one checked campaign a simple mobile page and QR."
- "Use the same approved destination across manual handoffs."
- "Unpublish when the campaign is over."

## Prohibited

- "Full landing-page builder."
- "Tracks every conversion."
- "Automatically publishes or boosts your offer."
- "Permanent public page for facts that can expire."
