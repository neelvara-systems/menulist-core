# Hosted Offer Page And QR - Website Boundary

The public CampaignCue website may describe a small, fact-checked campaign destination and downloadable QR as part of a Campaign Pack. It must state that the owner explicitly publishes it and that social posting remains manual.

Individual offer pages are runtime campaign outputs, not marketing-site pages. They are `noindex`, have no sitemap entry, and must not inherit generic website claims, lead forms, or visitor tracking.
