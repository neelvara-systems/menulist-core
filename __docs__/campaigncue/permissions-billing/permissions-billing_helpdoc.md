# Permissions Billing - Help Doc

## What This Does

Permissions Billing controls who can do what in a CampaignCue workspace and how credit usage is tracked.

Current CampaignCue shows role, member, billing, and feature-flag posture. Billing checkout, credit purchase, usage ledger capture, paid generation, rendered video, direct send, and direct publishing are disabled.

## Common Roles

- `Owner`: full workspace control.
- `Admin`: manage users and campaigns.
- `Marketer`: create and edit campaign packs.
- `Reviewer`: review and approve assigned outputs.
- `Local manager`: manage assigned locations.
- `Billing admin`: view plans, invoices, and credits.

## Credits

Credit estimates apply only to future paid generation or rendering actions. The current export/download-first runtime does not charge credits or write usage ledger entries.

## Spend

Campaign generation is separate from ad-platform or channel spend. Spend-changing actions require explicit approval and the right role.
