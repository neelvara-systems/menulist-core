# Permissions Billing - Website Content

## Section Goal

Explain permissions and usage transparency without committing to final pricing details.

## Approved Public Copy

> Keep campaign roles, approvals, credit estimates, and usage records clear across owners, agencies, and locations.

## Supporting Points

- Controls who can create, approve, export, hand off, and manage billing posture.
- Shows billing and credit posture now; estimated credits require paid generation or rendering to be enabled.
- Separates campaign generation from ad or channel spend.
- Supports agency and multi-location access patterns.

## Do Not Say

- Do not list unapproved pricing.
- Do not imply CampaignCue pays ad spend.
- Do not promise unlimited generation.
