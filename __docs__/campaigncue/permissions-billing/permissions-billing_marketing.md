# Permissions Billing - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Permissions Billing makes CampaignCue practical for owners, agencies, and multi-location teams by keeping roles, credits, and approvals clear.

## Buyer Promise

- Know who can create, approve, publish, and manage billing.
- See generation credits before use.
- Keep spend-changing actions approval-gated.
- Support agency-paid and client-paid workspace models.

## Proof Points

- Role-based permissions.
- Credit estimates.
- Usage ledger.
- Spend approval gates.

## Avoid

- Do not publish final plan names or prices before pricing is approved.
- Do not imply ad-platform spend is included unless a plan explicitly includes it.
- Do not hide credit use behind vague wording.
