# Design Cue - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Design Cue is the plain-language design assistant for local-business campaign assets.

It is not positioned as a professional design suite. It is positioned as the owner-safe way to make a campaign asset clearer, more local, and ready to export.

## Differentiation

Most AI design tools optimize for creating more designs. Design Cue optimizes for making the current business-backed asset safe and usable.

## Market Pattern Read

Current editor products are moving toward prompt-based and context-aware editing:

- Google Stitch positions natural language as a way to create, iterate, critique, and export design work from an AI-native canvas: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/stitch-ai-ui-design/
- Adobe Express AI Assistant describes prompt-based create/edit flows that keep selected parts intact, with an explicit toggle between manual editing and prompt assistance: https://helpx.adobe.com/express/web/ai-assistant/adobe-express-ai-assistant-overview.html
- Figma Make emphasizes selecting part of a design, prompting changes, then directly editing or moving output into editable design layers: https://www.figma.com/make/

CampaignCue should not copy the broad UI/app-builder scope. The local product opportunity is narrower: help SMB owners revise campaign assets without design vocabulary while preserving business facts, export-only delivery, and Firebase cost discipline.

Key points:

- business facts stay protected
- owner reviews every change
- editable layers stay editable
- export/download remains the active delivery path
- deterministic commands avoid unnecessary AI cost

## Internal Messaging

Use:

- “Ask for the change you want.”
- “Review before applying.”
- “Facts stay protected.”
- “Export when ready.”

Avoid:

- “fully autonomous designer”
- “auto-post”
- “guaranteed viral”
- “hands-free ad creation”
