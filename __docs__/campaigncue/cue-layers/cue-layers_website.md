## Problem Statement

Small businesses already have useful campaign images, but changing one date, offer, phone number, or branch name often means rebuilding the whole design. That slows down daily marketing and makes old assets hard to reuse.

## Solution Statement

CueLayers prepares a flat image for editing inside CampaignCue. It separates safe parts into layers, keeps uncertain parts as image fallback, and lets the owner download the final result for manual posting.

## FAQ

### Does CueLayers recover my original design file?

No. A flat image does not contain guaranteed original design layers. CueLayers creates a safe editable version where possible and keeps the original as fallback.

### Can I upload images from my phone?

Yes, supported image uploads should work from mobile. Full layer editing is easier on desktop.

### Will CampaignCue post the image for me?

No. CampaignCue's active flow is export and download. You decide where to post the final asset.

### What happens if the image cannot be safely separated?

CampaignCue keeps it as a flat image and may add safe editable overlays. It will not pretend that broken layers are ready.

### Can important text change by mistake?

CueLayers checks important text and keeps it as image fallback when it cannot safely make it editable.
