# Pattern Cue - Marketing Notes

## Internal Positioning

**See a useful format? Turn it into an original, fact-checked local-business brief.**

Pattern Cue is not a viral-content remixer. It removes blank-page planning while protecting business facts, source rights, consent, and originality.

## Proof Points

- Starts from one owner-chosen example.
- Produces three original business-specific hooks.
- Feeds the existing reel and creator pack.
- Keeps source wording and media out of durable CampaignCue truth.
- No account surveillance, direct posting, AI avatars, or reach guarantees.

## Forbidden Claims

- Go viral
- Copy any winning video
- Spy on competitors
- Guaranteed reach or sales
- Automatic posting
- AI customer or authentic synthetic testimonial
