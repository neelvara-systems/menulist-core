# Local Visibility Action Center - Marketing

## Approved Positioning

CampaignCue turns saved business truth into a short local visibility checklist: fix missing details, refresh stale campaign material, prepare a checked Google handoff, and use real approved images.

## Allowed Claims

- evidence-backed actions from saved CampaignCue information
- manual Google/local handoff preparation
- expired-detail and readiness checks
- no extra setup screen or blank prompt

## Prohibited Claims

- live Google profile audit
- automatic profile updates
- search ranking improvement or guarantee
- competitor monitoring
- review generation or direct solicitation
