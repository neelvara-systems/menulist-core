# Local Visibility Action Center - Owner Guide

Open **Visibility** to see what needs attention.

- **Do now** means a saved business detail or destination is missing.
- **Review** means a dated detail or Campaign Pack may be stale or unsafe to reuse.
- **Ready** means CampaignCue has enough saved evidence for a manual handoff.

Open an action to see why it appears, the steps to complete it, and what it unlocks. CampaignCue does not inspect or change your external profiles. Verify the destination, then copy or download the checked handoff material and update the owner-managed profile yourself.
