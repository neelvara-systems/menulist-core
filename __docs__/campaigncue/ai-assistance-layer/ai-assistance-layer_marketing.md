# CampaignCue AI Assistance Layer - Marketing Notes

## Internal Positioning

This is not a generic AI chat surface.

CampaignCue uses AI where it removes owner work:

- turn rough notes into usable facts,
- ask the next missing detail,
- draft channel copy from approved facts,
- explain trust checks,
- learn from simple result notes,
- guide useful real-photo tasks.

## Public Boundary

Allowed copy:

- "CampaignCue helps turn business facts into ready campaign steps."
- "AI helps with drafts and explanations after facts are checked."
- "The owner stays in control of facts, exports, and posting."

Avoid:

- "AI autopilot."
- "AI knows what to promote."
- "One-click posting."
- "Automatic growth."
- "Perfect campaign strategy."
