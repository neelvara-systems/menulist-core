# CampaignCue AI Assistance Layer - Help

## What is the AI assistance plan?

It is a short list of ways CampaignCue can reduce campaign work after it has checked the business facts.

It may show:

- what input is missing,
- what copy can be drafted,
- what trust check needs review,
- what result should be recorded,
- what photo would help.

## Does AI decide what to promote?

No. CampaignCue recommends campaigns from saved business facts, recipes, readiness, timing, assets, and result memory. AI is only a helper for drafts, explanations, and coaching.

## Does CampaignCue post for me?

No. The current runtime prepares the campaign pack for manual download, copy, and use. You still post, send, print, or share from your own channel.

## Does this cost extra Firebase reads?

No. The plan is built from the Daily Desk data already loaded on the page.
