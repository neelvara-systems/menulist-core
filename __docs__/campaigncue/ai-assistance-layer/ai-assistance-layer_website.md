# CampaignCue AI Assistance Layer - Website Copy Boundary

## Website Role

The website may mention this as a support layer inside Daily Campaign Desk and Campaign Pack, not as the main product category.

Recommended wording:

> CampaignCue can help turn rough business inputs into checked campaign steps, draft channel copy from approved facts, explain what needs review, and learn from owner-reported results.

## Must Say

- CampaignCue recommends from business facts, recipes, readiness, and memory.
- AI helps after facts are checked.
- Export and posting remain owner-controlled.

## Must Not Say

- Direct posting is available.
- The model decides the campaign.
- The model verifies legal or commercial truth.
- CampaignCue reads private customer conversations.
- Provider accounts are connected by default.
