# Video Reel Studio - Website Content

## Section Goal

Show that CampaignCue can help local businesses create short-video campaign packs without requiring a full video-production workflow.

## Approved Public Copy

> Create reel briefs with hooks, shot lists, captions, overlays, and calls to action that stay tied to your real offer or service.

## Supporting Points

- Works as a practical shoot brief even when rendered video is not enabled.
- Keeps campaign facts, dates, and CTA visible.
- Supports short video, story, and ad-video formats.
- Owner approval stays in the workflow.

## Do Not Say

- Do not promise viral videos.
- Do not guarantee platform publishing.
- Do not claim third-party music or likeness rights are included.
