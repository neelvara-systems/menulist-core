# Video Reel Studio - Help Doc

## What This Does

Video Reel Studio creates a short-video campaign pack from an approved campaign cue.

## What You Get

- Opening hook.
- Shot list.
- Scene timing.
- Text overlays.
- Caption.
- CTA.
- Optional render, when enabled.

## How To Use It

1. Open a campaign pack.
2. Choose a video channel.
3. Review the item, service, offer, location, and CTA.
4. Create the reel brief.
5. Review each scene.
6. Create a render if available, or use the manual shoot brief.
7. Approve and export.

## Practical Notes

Use real footage from the business when possible. Do not use customer faces, staff likenesses, music, or third-party footage unless the business has permission.
