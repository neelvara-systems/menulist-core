# Campaign Pack Output System — Marketing Notes

## Positioning

CampaignCue does not stop at a post or caption. It gives the local business owner a campaign pack: what to promote, why, what to copy, where to use it, what was checked, and what to record later.

## Internal Message

Know what to promote. Get the pack. Check the facts. Use it manually. Record what happened.

## Differentiator

Most creative tools output content. CampaignCue outputs a verified local campaign packet with delivery instructions and result memory.

## Do Not Claim

- direct social publishing
- direct WhatsApp sending
- automated ad spend
- hosted offer pages
- guaranteed sales, bookings, ranking, or ROI
