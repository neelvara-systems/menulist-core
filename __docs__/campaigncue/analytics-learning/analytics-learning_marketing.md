# Analytics Learning - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Analytics Learning helps local businesses understand campaign outcomes and decide what to do next.

## Buyer Promise

- See campaign results in plain language.
- Know which metrics came from which source.
- Turn performance signals into next campaign cues.
- Give agencies client-ready reporting material.

## Proof Points

- Channel-specific result capture.
- Metric-source labels.
- Next-cue feedback loop.
- Agency report snapshots.

## Avoid

- Do not promise perfect attribution.
- Do not claim guaranteed revenue lift.
- Do not hide manual or estimated metrics.
