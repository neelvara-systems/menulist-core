# Analytics Learning - Website Content

## Section Goal

Explain the outcome loop: campaign runs, results are captured, the next cue improves.

## Approved Public Copy

> Track campaign outcomes by source, see what worked, and turn results into the next campaign cue.

## Supporting Points

- Captures observed CampaignCue actions now; publish status, replies, clicks, and imported metrics require connected providers.
- Labels where each metric came from.
- Creates practical follow-up cues.
- Supports client-ready reporting for agencies.

## Do Not Say

- Do not promise perfect attribution.
- Do not promise revenue guarantees.
- Do not imply all metrics are available from every channel.
