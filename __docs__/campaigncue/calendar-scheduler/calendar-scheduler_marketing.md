# Calendar Scheduler - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Calendar Scheduler gives a local business one place to see upcoming campaigns, approved posts, manual tasks, and follow-up work.

## Buyer Promise

- Know what is planned.
- See what still needs approval.
- Keep manual posting tasks visible.
- Review results after campaigns run.

## Proof Points

- Channel-aware calendar.
- Approval and trust status on scheduled items.
- Manual task fallback.
- Result follow-up cues.

## Avoid

- Do not call it a full project-management system.
- Do not promise every channel can auto-publish.
- Do not hide manual tasks behind automation language.
