# Calendar Scheduler - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Calendar Scheduler gives a local business one place to save and review manual campaign reminders without implying that CampaignCue posts on the owner's behalf.

## Buyer Promise

- Know what is planned.
- Keep manual posting tasks visible.
- See when a task is due in the business timezone.
- Review results after campaigns run.

## Proof Points

- Explicit owner-selected local date/time.
- Trust, freshness, and agency-approval gate before reminder creation.
- Manual task type and optional staff/owner label.
- Derived due state and Campaign Rhythm follow-up.

## Avoid

- Do not call it a full project-management system.
- Do not call a reminder a scheduled post.
- Do not promise any channel can auto-publish.
- Do not hide manual tasks behind automation language.
