# Opportunity Engine — Marketing

## Pitch

CampaignCue shows the next useful campaign cue instead of asking owners to invent a post.

## Differentiation

Generic tools ask what to create. Opportunity Engine uses business context to show what is ready, missing, stale, or worth promoting.

## Use Cases

- Restaurant: top viewed item becomes a weekend WhatsApp and Google pack.
- Salon: open weekend slots become a booking push.
- Agency: clients missing weekly content are grouped for production.
- Multi-location: outlets ready for a campaign can move while blocked outlets wait.
