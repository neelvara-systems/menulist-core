# Opportunity Engine — Help Documentation

## Quick Summary

Campaign cues show what CampaignCue can prepare next. A cue may come from a menu item, service, offer, missing photo, source update, calendar date, or previous campaign usage.

## Use A Cue

1. Open **Home**.
2. Read the top campaign cue.
3. Click **Create campaign**.
4. Review the prefilled campaign.
5. Generate the campaign pack.

## Dismiss A Cue

1. Open the cue menu.
2. Click **Dismiss**.
3. Choose a reason if shown.

## Troubleshooting

### No cues are shown

Add more business data, upload assets, connect a source, or create a campaign manually.
