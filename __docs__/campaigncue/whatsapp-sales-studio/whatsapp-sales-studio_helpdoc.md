# WhatsApp Sales Studio - Help Doc

## What This Does

WhatsApp Sales Studio helps prepare WhatsApp campaign messages from a campaign cue.

## Before You Use It

Make sure the business has permission to message the audience. Do not import or message people who did not agree to receive marketing messages from the business.

## How To Use It

1. Open a campaign pack.
2. Choose WhatsApp as a channel.
3. Review the offer, service, CTA, and intended audience.
4. Confirm consent posture.
5. Create message variants.
6. Review trust checks.
7. Download/export, submit as a template, or send only where direct sending is configured.

## Good Campaigns

- Clear reason for the message.
- One CTA.
- Simple opt-out handling.
- No misleading urgency.
- No claims that the business cannot support.
