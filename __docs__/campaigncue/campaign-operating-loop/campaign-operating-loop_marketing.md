# Campaign Operating Loop - Marketing

## Positioning

CampaignCue does not only prepare content. It checks whether the business can use the campaign now.

## Owner Promise

Tell CampaignCue what the business can handle today. It checks stock, capacity, promotion limits, current facts, and past owner-reported results before it prepares the manual-use pack.

## Differentiators

- A quick business pulse instead of a blank prompt.
- Commercial limits that can stop an unsafe discount or unavailable item.
- A truth receipt that expires and is rechecked before public use.
- Review and return-customer packs without a customer contact database.
- Owner-managed local presence handoff across the profiles the business already uses.
- Protected-fact language handoff without claiming automatic translation.
- Staff tasks and simple owner-reported campaign receipts.
- One-variable learning instead of uncontrolled campaign changes.
- A practical campaign rhythm: approve, use, record, safely reuse, and schedule the next manual task.
- Safe pack reuse that rebuilds from current truth instead of recycling stale copy.
- A visible readiness score based on facts, trust, freshness, approval, and handoff completeness.

## Approved Claims

- "CampaignCue checks current business facts before public use."
- "CampaignCue can block a pack when stock, capacity, or promotion rules do not allow it."
- "Results are owner-reported unless a future authorized source says otherwise."
- "CampaignCue prepares files and handoff instructions for manual use."
- "CampaignCue can rebuild a useful previous pack from current checked facts."
- "Pack readiness measures whether a campaign is safe and complete, not whether it will go viral."

## Claims Not Allowed

- CampaignCue posts automatically.
- CampaignCue imports or messages customer lists.
- CampaignCue guarantees sales, reviews, bookings, reach, or ranking.
- CampaignCue automatically translates approved campaigns.
- CampaignCue always knows the best campaign.
- CampaignCue predicts engagement, reach, the perfect posting time, or guaranteed performance.
- CampaignCue automatically recycles or republishes old content.
