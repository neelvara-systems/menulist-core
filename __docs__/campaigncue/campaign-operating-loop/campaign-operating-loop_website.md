# Campaign Operating Loop - Website

## Public Story

**Headline:** Know what is safe to promote today.

**Supporting copy:** Add a quick stock-and-capacity pulse. CampaignCue checks current facts and promotion limits, prepares the manual-use pack, and remembers the owner-reported result.

## Public Proof Points

- Quiet now, stock ready, capacity available.
- Maximum discount and do-not-promote rules checked.
- Truth receipt rechecked before download or manual scheduling.
- Owner-managed customer destinations packaged without account connection.
- Staff handoff and next one-variable test included.
- Campaign rhythm shows whether to approve, use, record, safely reuse, or schedule next.
- Useful previous packs can be rebuilt from current checked facts.
- Readiness measures facts, trust, freshness, approval, and handoff completeness, not predicted engagement.

## Boundary Copy

CampaignCue prepares, checks, copies, downloads, and schedules manual work. It does not connect social accounts, send WhatsApp messages, import customer contacts, publish posts, or spend ad budget.

## Implemented Website Alignment

- CampaignCue homepage hero and workflow map mention current operating context and commercial limits.
- Campaign Pack Room mentions freshness, owner-managed destinations, and staff handoff.
- Daily Campaign Desk and Campaign Pack Studio feature records include the owner pulse, truth receipt, and commercial safety.
- Small-business use-case proof includes stock and capacity.
