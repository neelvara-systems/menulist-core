# Campaign Operating Loop - Help

## Quick Summary

CampaignCue checks what your business can handle now before it prepares a campaign. You can also save promotion limits, customer destinations, staff tasks, and what happened after using a pack.

## Update Today's Business Situation

1. Open **Daily desk**.
2. Find **Owner pulse**.
3. Choose whether the business is running normally, quiet, busy, or closed.
4. Choose current capacity and stock.
5. Add a local moment or short note when useful.
6. Set **Keep this pulse until** for time-sensitive information.
7. Select **Save pulse**.

If the pulse expires, CampaignCue asks you to confirm it again before relying on it.

## Set Promotion Safety Rules

1. Open **Business**.
2. Find **Commercial safety**.
3. Choose whether promotions and discounts are allowed.
4. Add a maximum discount or minimum promoted price when needed.
5. Add items, services, offers, or claims under **Do not promote**.
6. Save the business details.

CampaignCue blocks a pack when a current offer conflicts with these rules.

## Save Customer Destinations

1. Open **Shown to customers**.
2. Add only the owner-managed profile links the business uses.
3. Add the exact customer review destination before preparing a review request.
4. Select **Save destinations**.

CampaignCue packages these links for manual use. It does not connect to or update those accounts.

## Prepare A Review Or Return-Customer Pack

1. Open **Shown to customers**.
2. Select **Prepare review request** or **Prepare return-customer pack**.
3. Answer any missing-input card.
4. Do not paste customer names, phone numbers, emails, or contact lists into CampaignCue.
5. Download or copy the completed pack and use it through the owner's existing workflow.

Review requests must ask for an honest review. Do not offer a reward for a positive review.

## Record A Campaign Receipt

1. Open **Results**.
2. Choose what happened.
3. Add the time used when known.
4. Add optional replies, calls, bookings, orders, walk-ins, or link clicks.
5. Select the one thing tested.
6. Select **Record result**.

The counts are owner-entered and are not treated as provider-verified attribution.

## Follow The Campaign Rhythm

1. Open **Daily desk**.
2. Find **Campaign rhythm**.
3. Complete the first action shown: approve a pack, record a result, open a due manual task, reuse a useful pack, or prepare the next pack.
4. Open **Calendar** to review manual reminders.

CampaignCue does not predict the perfect posting time and does not post automatically.

## Reuse A Useful Pack Safely

1. Find a pack marked **Useful result** or use the Campaign Rhythm reuse suggestion.
2. Select **Reuse safely**.
3. Review any current missing fact, trust warning, date, price, stock, rights, or approval requirement.
4. Use the newly created pack.

CampaignCue rebuilds the same campaign recipe from current business truth. It does not copy the old approval, result, source hash, or stale output text.

## Resolve Approval

1. Open **Approvals and handoff**.
2. Request approval when the owner/client/local manager must review the pack.
3. An owner, admin, reviewer, or local manager selects **Approve** or **Reject**.
4. Add a short reason when rejecting.

Pending or rejected approval blocks download, export, manual scheduling, and mark-used actions. Approval does not bypass trust checks.

## Why A Pack Is Blocked

- Business facts changed after the pack was created.
- The pack expired.
- The business is closed or capacity is full.
- Stock is unavailable.
- A discount exceeds the saved limit.
- An item matches the do-not-promote list.
- A protected business fact or rights check is blocked.
- Owner/client approval is required, waiting, or rejected.

Confirm the current details and create a fresh pack. CampaignCue does not silently reuse an outdated pack.
