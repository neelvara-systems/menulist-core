# Creative Studio - Mobile Support

## Mobile Admission

Creative Studio is a mobile-relevant owner workflow because owners often approve and share campaign assets from a phone.

## Mobile Requirements

- Show one asset at a time with channel tabs.
- Keep approve, export, download, and regenerate actions at large touch sizes.
- Show trust status before the export action.
- Let owners copy captions and download images without desktop-only controls.
- Allow light editor work such as drawer search, Brand Kit quick picks, business text insertion, page switching, selected-layer quick actions, preview, and download when the editor is opened on a narrow viewport.
- Keep selected-layer quick actions large and wrapped when the editor is opened on a narrow viewport.
- Keep the contextual toolbar and page strip scrollable or wrapped with 44px targets on narrow screens.
- Avoid dense multi-column design controls on mobile.

## Mobile Non-Goals

- Full layout editing on mobile.
- Multi-asset drag-and-drop boards.
- Precision canvas toolbar workflows as the primary mobile path.
- Advanced crop tooling beyond simple channel previews.

## Acceptance

- Owner can review an Instagram/WhatsApp/Google asset, approve it, and download/export the prepared text from a phone.
- Credit and trust status remain visible before generation and export.
