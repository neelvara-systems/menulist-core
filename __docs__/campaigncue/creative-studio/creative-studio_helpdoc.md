# Creative Studio - Help Doc

## What This Does

Creative Studio creates static campaign assets from an approved campaign cue.

## How To Use It

1. Open a campaign pack.
2. Choose the channels you want to create for.
3. Review the selected item, service, offer, location, and CTA.
4. Check the credit estimate.
5. Create the assets.
6. Review the trust result.
7. Export or send the asset to the selected channel workflow.

## Good Inputs

- A clear offer or item/service to promote.
- A real date or time window when needed.
- One CTA, such as call, book, order, visit, or reply.
- Brand colors or a sample style if available.

## When To Regenerate

Regenerate only the part that is wrong: caption, headline, image prompt, crop, or channel version. Regenerating the whole pack should not be required for a small correction.
