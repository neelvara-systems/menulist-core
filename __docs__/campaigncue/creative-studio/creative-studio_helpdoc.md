# Creative Studio - Help Doc

## What This Does

Creative Studio creates static campaign assets from an approved campaign cue.

## How To Use It

1. Open a campaign pack.
2. Choose the channels you want to create for.
3. Review the selected item, service, offer, location, and CTA.
4. Check the credit estimate.
5. Create the assets.
6. Review the trust result.
7. Export or send the asset to the selected channel workflow.
8. In the editor, use AI Tools first for ready-to-share checks and missing business details, then for editable caption options, selected-text variants, brand checks, and export checklists.
9. Select a layer on the canvas to use the top contextual toolbar and floating quick-action bar for edit, color, style, position, duplicate, delete, group, distribute, or more controls.
10. Use drawer search, ready-made text templates, Brand Kit, business text, and page controls to refine the asset without leaving the editor.

## Good Inputs

- A clear offer or item/service to promote.
- A real date or time window when needed.
- One CTA, such as call, book, order, visit, or reply.
- Brand colors or a sample style if available.

## When To Regenerate

Regenerate only the part that is wrong: caption, headline, image prompt, crop, or channel version. Regenerating the whole pack should not be required for a small correction.

## AI Tools In The Editor

- AI Tools create editable text suggestions and review notes.
- Start with "Check if ready to share" or "Add missing business details" when you are unsure.
- They do not post, send, connect accounts, or spend ad budget.
- Background removal is not active yet; use image edits or CueLayers upload for reusable image work.
- The contextual toolbar, floating selected-layer bar, drawer search, ready-made text templates, Brand Kit, business text, and page controls only edit the open design in the browser. They do not publish, save, or spend anything by themselves.
- Downloads use the active page. JSON keeps the full page list.
- Always review business facts, prices, dates, and image rights before using the exported asset.
