# Campaign Pack Template Registry - Marketing Notes

## Elevator Pitch

CampaignCue gives local businesses a small set of ready campaign pack starters for their business type, then checks current facts before anything is used.

## Narrative

Most design tools ask the owner to browse a large template library. CampaignCue should not do that. It should start from the owner's business category, current campaign cue, and missing facts, then offer a focused pack that can be trusted.

The value is not template volume. The value is:

- the pack fits the business type,
- the offer facts are checked,
- the handoff fields are ready,
- the owner can reuse what worked,
- stale facts are blocked before export.

CampaignCue can offer output choices, but they must be framed as business-use choices:

- WhatsApp sales pack,
- source-to-channel pack,
- booking push pack,
- Google local update,
- Instagram post and story,
- poster or flyer,
- staff share pack,
- ad handoff pack,
- reuse old poster or image,
- custom size.

This is intentionally different from generic design tools that ask owners to choose from many formats before they know the campaign.

## Differentiation

| Generic creative tools | CampaignCue |
| --- | --- |
| Large template marketplace | Small curated category catalogs |
| Visual design first | Campaign pack and business result first |
| Owner brings the idea | Daily Desk suggests the useful action |
| Template text may be stale | Facts are rehydrated and checked |
| One design output | WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, print, staff, and result prompt |
| Search across many designs | Search within relevant business category and output intent |

## Sales Talking Points

### "Is this just templates?"

No. The template is only the starting point. CampaignCue still checks price, date, phone, location, CTA, photo rights, and delivery fields before the owner uses the pack.

### "Why not add thousands of templates?"

Local business owners do not need more browsing. They need the right campaign pack quickly. CampaignCue uses curated templates by business category and event tags.

### "Can we reuse what worked?"

Yes. Owners can save a campaign pack for reuse, and CampaignCue refreshes current facts when it is opened again.

### "Does this post for me?"

No. CampaignCue prepares download/copy handoff packs. Direct posting is not active in the current runtime.

## Pitch Deck Outline

1. Problem: SMB owners do not have time to browse templates.
2. Better approach: Start from business category and campaign cue.
3. Product: Curated pack templates with fact checks.
4. Output: WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, print, staff handoff, ad handoff, old-asset reuse, and result prompt.
5. Trust: Price, date, contact, and offer fields are protected.
6. Reuse: Save packs that worked and update them safely.
7. Cost discipline: One category catalog read, Storage payloads only when opened.
8. Close: Less browsing, more ready-to-use campaigns.
