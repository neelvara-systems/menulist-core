## Problem Statement

Most template libraries make owners browse too much. A restaurant owner, salon owner, or local service team needs a campaign that fits today's work, not hundreds of unrelated designs.

## Solution Statement

CampaignCue shows focused campaign pack starters for the saved business category. Each pack can include channel copy, print notes, staff handoff, trust checks, and result prompts.

## How It Works

1. **Pick a useful pack** - CampaignCue shows pack starters for the business category.
2. **Add missing details** - The owner confirms only the facts needed for that campaign.
3. **Use the checked pack** - Download, copy, print, share manually, and record what happened.

## FAQ

### Is this a template marketplace?

No. CampaignCue keeps a small curated set of campaign pack starters for local business use cases.

### Does the template include my current business details?

CampaignCue refreshes current facts before use. If a price, date, phone, or offer is missing, it asks before the pack is ready.

### Can I save my own campaign pack?

Yes. A completed pack can be saved for reuse, then refreshed with current facts the next time it is opened.

### Does CampaignCue post directly to social media?

No. The active product prepares download and copy handoff packs for manual use.
