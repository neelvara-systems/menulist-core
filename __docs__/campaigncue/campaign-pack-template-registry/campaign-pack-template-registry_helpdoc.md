# Campaign Pack Template Registry - Help Documentation

## Quick Summary

Campaign pack templates help you start from a useful campaign shape for your business type. CampaignCue still checks your current price, date, contact details, photos, and delivery fields before you use the pack.

## Getting Started

CampaignCue chooses templates from your business category, such as food, service, retail, health, or professional services.

You do not need to choose a technical category. CampaignCue uses your saved business details.

## How To Use A Template

1. Open CampaignCue.
2. Go to the Daily Campaign Desk or Campaign Pack screen.
3. Choose a suggested pack or open templates.
4. Pick a template that matches your goal, event, or channel.
5. Add any missing details CampaignCue asks for.
6. Review the checks.
7. Download or copy the campaign pack.

Screenshot slot: Template cards on the Campaign Pack screen.

## How To Find Festival Or Event Templates

1. Open templates.
2. Choose an event tag, such as Diwali, Christmas, New Year, birthday, or anniversary.
3. Choose the pack that fits your campaign.
4. Confirm the offer, date, contact, and photo details.

CampaignCue will not use an old price, phone number, or offer date without checking it.

## How To Save A Pack For Reuse

1. Open a campaign pack you want to use again.
2. Choose **Save as reusable pack**.
3. Give it a clear name.
4. Save it.

Next time, CampaignCue will refresh the business details before you use it.

## Why Some Details Are Missing

CampaignCue may ask for details before the pack is ready.

Common examples:

- offer price,
- end date,
- WhatsApp number,
- booking link,
- one product or service photo,
- photo rights or consent.

This protects your business from sharing the wrong information.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot see a template I expected

The template list is based on your saved business category. Check your business details if the category looks wrong.

### The pack says a detail is missing

Add the requested detail before using the pack. CampaignCue does this to avoid wrong prices, dates, phone numbers, or claims.

### My saved pack uses old information

Saved packs refresh current business facts before use. If an old value is no longer confirmed, CampaignCue will ask you to confirm it again.

### The old poster is not fully editable

CampaignCue keeps the original safe. Some text or image parts may stay as an image if they cannot be checked confidently.

## Tips

- Save packs that worked well.
- Use event tags for festival campaigns.
- Add real photos when possible.
- Record what happened after using a pack so CampaignCue can suggest better options next time.
