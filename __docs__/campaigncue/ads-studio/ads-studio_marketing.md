# Ads Studio - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Ads Studio helps local businesses and agencies prepare ad-ready campaign packs from source-linked offers and services.

## Buyer Promise

- Ad copy, creative, audience notes, budget guidance, and link tracking in one place.
- Policy checks before handoff.
- Manual export for agencies or platform entry.
- Owner-controlled manual setup; connected publishing is not active.

## Proof Points

- Source-linked ad pack.
- Budget approval guard.
- UTM support.
- Manual owner or agency handoff.

## Avoid

- Do not promise ad approval or lower ad costs.
- Do not imply CampaignCue manages spend without approval.
- Do not claim policy review is a legal guarantee.
- Do not advertise Meta reporting, account connection, MCP access, or provider analytics before the connector is implemented and verified.
