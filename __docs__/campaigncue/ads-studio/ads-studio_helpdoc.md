# Ads Studio - Help Doc

## What This Does

Ads Studio prepares a local ad pack from a campaign cue.

## What The Pack Includes

- Headlines.
- Primary text.
- Creative variants.
- CTA.
- Audience notes.
- Budget suggestion.
- Destination and UTM tracking.
- Policy risk notes.

## How To Use It

1. Open a campaign pack.
2. Choose ad channels.
3. Review offer, service, location, destination, and CTA.
4. Create the ad pack.
5. Review policy notes and budget guidance.
6. Approve the pack.
7. Export the pack and enter it manually in Meta Ads, Google Ads, or your agency workflow.

## Important

CampaignCue can help prepare ads, but the ad platform may still review, reject, limit, or pause ads based on its own rules.

CampaignCue does not currently connect an ad account, import Meta reporting, create or edit ads, change a budget, or start spend. Meta reporting and diagnostics are recorded as a future read-first option only.
