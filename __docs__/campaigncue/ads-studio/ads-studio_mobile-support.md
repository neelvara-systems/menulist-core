# Ads Studio - Mobile Support

## Mobile Admission

Ads Studio is mobile-relevant for review and approval, but not for deep ad account setup.

## Mobile Requirements

- Show copy variants, creative preview, CTA, budget, destination, and policy risk in a review flow.
- Keep approve/export actions large and explicit.
- Separate "approve pack" from any spend-changing action.
- Make manual export available from phone.
- Keep policy notes readable before handoff.

## Mobile Non-Goals

- Full ad account setup.
- Bulk campaign editing.
- Complex audience builder controls.

## Acceptance

- Owner can review and approve an ad pack on mobile.
- Owner cannot accidentally start ad spend from mobile generation or preview.
