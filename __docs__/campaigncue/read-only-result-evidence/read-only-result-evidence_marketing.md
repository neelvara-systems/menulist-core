# Read-Only Result Evidence Marketing Boundary

## Allowed description

CampaignCue lets owners keep a small, clearly labelled snapshot of selected totals copied from reports they already use.

## Owner value

- keeps campaign context in one place;
- avoids a mandatory provider connection;
- distinguishes report scope and date range;
- preserves the difference between provider totals and owner-observed business results.

## Prohibited claims

Do not say:

- CampaignCue connects to provider accounts;
- CampaignCue imports or syncs performance automatically;
- the numbers prove campaign attribution;
- CampaignCue optimizes spend or predicts return;
- CampaignCue learns from these totals automatically;
- the feature is a complete analytics platform.

Preferred copy:

> Keep a small snapshot from a report you can see. CampaignCue labels the source and date window and keeps it separate from owner-reported results.
