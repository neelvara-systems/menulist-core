# Campaign Studio — Marketing

## Pitch

One campaign brief becomes a complete pack for the channels the business uses.

## Value

The owner chooses the goal. CampaignCue prepares the campaign pack, keeps source details visible, and makes copy/download/manual handoff/approval actions clear.

## Approved Terms

- Campaign pack
- Campaign brief
- Ready outputs
- Needs review
- Manual mode

## Avoid

- Design canvas
- Prompt engineering
- Unlimited content
- Viral campaign
