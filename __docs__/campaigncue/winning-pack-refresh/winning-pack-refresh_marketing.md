# Winning Pack Refresh - Marketing Boundary

## Supported

- “Rebuild a useful campaign pattern from current checked facts.”
- “Keep a compact history of where the refreshed pack came from.”
- “Review seasonal or local context entered by the owner.”

## Prohibited

- “Automatically rerun your winning campaigns.”
- “Guaranteed winning creative.”
- “CampaignCue predicts what will convert.”
- “Old offers are always safe to repeat.”
