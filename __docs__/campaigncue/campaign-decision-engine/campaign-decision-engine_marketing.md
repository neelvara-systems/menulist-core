# Campaign Decision Engine - Marketing Notes

## Positioning

CampaignCue does not ask owners to start with a blank prompt. The Campaign Decision Engine turns real business facts, campaign recipes, timing, available assets, channel readiness, trust risk, owner effort, repetition, and result memory into one explainable recommendation.

The product promise is not that AI knows the best marketing strategy. The promise is that CampaignCue recommends the safest useful campaign from the facts it has, explains the reasoning, asks only for missing inputs, and feeds a complete Campaign Pack.

## Owner Promise

Know what to promote, why it is useful now, what is missing, and whether it is safe to prepare.

## Differentiators

- Recommendations come from facts, recipes, gates, scoring, and result memory, not an AI guess.
- Every cue explains why this, why now, missing inputs, trust risk, and ready outputs.
- CampaignCue can say "not enough facts" or "do not promote this yet" instead of generating unsafe content.
- The recommendation feeds a Campaign Pack, not a single post or random design.
- Manual export, copy, and download remain the delivery boundary until explicit posting integrations are added later.

## Do Not Claim

- Do not say AI decides the owner's marketing.
- Do not say CampaignCue guarantees growth, sales, bookings, or ranking.
- Do not imply direct social posting, WhatsApp sending, Google publishing, account connection, or ad-spend control in the active runtime.
- Do not say the model decides facts, prices, offers, dates, contacts, claims, safety, or spend.

## Approved Owner Language

- "Recommended because these facts are ready."
- "Needs one input before the pack can be prepared."
- "Blocked until this fact is confirmed."
- "Ready to prepare."
- "Use this campaign manually."
