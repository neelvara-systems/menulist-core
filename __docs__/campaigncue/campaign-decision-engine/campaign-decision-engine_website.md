# Campaign Decision Engine - Website Content

## Website Role

Use this as internal website-copy guidance for the CampaignCue public site and product pages. Public copy must stay owner-facing and must not expose scoring formulas, provider names, implementation details, or internal model language.

## Section Headline

Know what to promote today.

## Supporting Copy

CampaignCue ranks practical campaign ideas from your saved business facts, timing, available assets, channel readiness, trust checks, and past results. If a price, date, photo, link, or contact detail is missing, it asks for that detail before preparing the campaign pack.

## Proof Points

- Shows why a campaign is recommended.
- Shows why now is a useful time.
- Lists missing inputs before generation.
- Marks recommendations as ready, needs review, or blocked.
- Feeds WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, Instagram, print, staff, and ad-handoff pack outputs.
- Keeps direct posting, direct sending, account connections, and ad spend outside the active runtime.

## Public Copy Guardrails

- Say "recommends the safest useful campaign from your facts."
- Say "asks for missing details before preparing the pack."
- Say "checks business facts before you use the campaign."
- Do not say "AI decides your marketing."
- Do not imply direct social publishing, WhatsApp sending, Google posting, or automatic ad spend.
- Do not claim CampaignCue can see or use data that has not been connected, entered, imported, or confirmed by the owner.

## Suggested Page Placement

- CampaignCue homepage: use this under the main "Daily Campaign Desk" story.
- Product page: use this before Creative Studio, Design Cue, and CueLayers so the editor feels secondary to the campaign decision workflow.
- Help or trust page: link this to the Business Brain and Creative Trust Center explanation.
