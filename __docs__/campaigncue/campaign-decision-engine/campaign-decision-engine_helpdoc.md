# Campaign Decision Engine - Help Doc

## What It Does

CampaignCue recommends a campaign action from the facts saved in your workspace. It looks at your business type, available inputs, usable assets, channel readiness, trust checks, recent campaign history, and recorded results.

## What You See

- A recommended campaign.
- Why CampaignCue recommends it.
- Why now is a useful time.
- Details that are missing.
- Outputs that can be prepared.
- A trust state: Ready, Needs review, or Blocked.

## When It Needs Input

If a price, date, photo right, location, phone number, WhatsApp number, booking link, offer term, or destination is missing, CampaignCue asks for that detail before final use.

Each missing input should explain what it unlocks. For example, confirming a price can unlock the WhatsApp offer image, Google offer draft, and counter poster.

## What It Does Not Do

CampaignCue does not invent prices, dates, discounts, locations, phone numbers, booking links, or business claims. It also does not post to social channels, send WhatsApp messages, publish to Google, connect social accounts, or start ad spend in the active runtime.

## If A Recommendation Is Blocked

Open the missing input or trust item, fix the detail, then prepare or use the campaign pack again. If the information is not available, use a safer maintenance action such as adding a photo, confirming business details, recording a result, or preparing an evergreen update.

## Owner Checklist

- Check why the recommendation was shown.
- Answer required missing inputs.
- Review trust warnings before export.
- Use the prepared campaign pack manually.
- Record what happened after use.
