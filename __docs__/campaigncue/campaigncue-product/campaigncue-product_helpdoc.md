# CampaignCue Product — Help Documentation

## Quick Summary

CampaignCue creates campaign packs from your business data. You can use it for WhatsApp, social posts, Google updates, short videos, ad handoffs, approvals, and calendar planning.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Business name and category.
- WhatsApp or contact number.
- Menu items or salon services.
- Photos or videos, if available.
- Offer details, if the campaign includes a price or discount.

### First-Time Setup

1. Choose whether you are setting up one business, an agency workspace, or multiple locations.
2. Select the business type: restaurant, cafe, salon, spa, barbershop, retail, service, or other.
3. Use the Home checklist to confirm business details, add today's input, create a pack, and use it manually.
4. Add data from the signed-in business profile and Inputs such as menu links, booking links, offers, events, or uploaded file notes.
5. Add brand details such as logo, colors, writing style, and preferred language.
6. Review the first campaign ideas.

## Common Tasks

### Create A Campaign Pack

1. Open **Ideas** or **Packs**.
2. Choose an idea or create a pack from saved business details.
3. Review the selected channels.
4. Create the pack.
5. Review the outputs and Checks.
6. Copy, download, export, schedule a manual task, mark as used, or send for approval.

### Use Manual Mode

1. Open the ready output.
2. Click **Copy**, **Download**, or **Export**.
3. Post it manually in WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, or your ad account.
4. Mark it as posted when done.

### Fix A Trust Issue

1. Open the campaign marked **Needs review**.
2. Read the issue.
3. Choose the safe fix, such as use source price, mark as offer, add expiry, attach real review, or confirm consent.
4. Save the fix.

## Troubleshooting

### My source input did not update

Use the current business details or add a manual source input. Provider setup requests are not part of the active export/download runtime.

### A campaign is blocked

Blocked campaigns usually contain missing consent, fake testimonial wording, unavailable item data, wrong location details, or unsupported claims. Fix the listed issues before using the campaign pack.

### Credits Look High

The current export/download-first runtime does not charge credits. Credit estimates only become active when paid generation, rendered video, billing, or provider actions are enabled in a separate future layer.
