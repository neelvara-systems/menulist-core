# Business Brain — Marketing

## Pitch

CampaignCue learns the business once, then uses that context every time a campaign is created.

## Value

Owners should not start from a blank prompt. Business Brain keeps the menu, services, prices, photos, offers, tone, language, and contact details ready so campaign work starts from facts.

## Talking Points

| Point | Message |
| --- | --- |
| For restaurants | Menu items, prices, photos, and public links can drive menu-based campaigns. |
| For salons | Services, packages, booking CTA, and consent-aware assets drive booking campaigns. |
| For agencies | Client setup becomes reusable, so weekly packs are faster. |
| For multi-location | Shared brand defaults and local details stay separate. |

## Avoid

- "AI knows your business"
- "Automatic sales growth"
- "One-click viral content"
- "MenuList edits your campaigns"
