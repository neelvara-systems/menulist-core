# Business Brain — Help Documentation

## Quick Summary

Business Brain is the saved profile CampaignCue uses for campaigns. It keeps business details, menu or services, photos, offers, brand details, language, and contact information together.

## First-Time Setup

1. Open **Business setup**.
2. Choose the business type.
3. Add business name, locality, logo, and contact number.
4. Add menu items or services.
5. Add photos or continue with text-first campaigns.
6. Choose the main language and tone.

## Common Tasks

### Update A Price Or Service

1. Open the business profile.
2. Select **Menu items** or **Services**.
3. Edit the item or service.
4. Save the change.
5. Refresh any older campaigns before reuse.

### Add A WhatsApp Number

1. Open **Contact details**.
2. Add the WhatsApp number.
3. Save.
4. New WhatsApp campaign packs will use it as the main CTA.

## Troubleshooting

### CampaignCue says business data is incomplete

Add the missing item listed on the readiness card, such as WhatsApp number, price, photo, service list, or location.
