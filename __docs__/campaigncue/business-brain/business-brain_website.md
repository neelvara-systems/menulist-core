## FAQ

**Q:** Do I need MenuList?
**A:** No. Restaurants can connect MenuList, upload a menu, use a website link, or add details manually. Salons, retail shops, local services, fitness studios, clinics, and other local businesses can add details directly.

**Q:** Can I change business details later?
**A:** Yes. CampaignCue keeps source and confirmation history so old campaigns can be checked before reuse.
