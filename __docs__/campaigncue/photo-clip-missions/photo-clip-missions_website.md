# Photo And Clip Missions - Website Boundary

Photo and Clip Missions are an owner-workspace capability, not a standalone public tool. Public CampaignCue content may explain that CampaignCue asks for useful real-business visuals and keeps rights review visible.

The public website must not imply:

- automatic camera or gallery access;
- automatic consent verification;
- direct social publishing;
- guaranteed campaign results;
- a generic stock-media or creator marketplace.

A future website proof point should demonstrate the complete owner outcome: a recipe asks for one real photo, the owner uploads it privately, the campaign pack becomes more ready, and the same asset remains reusable.
