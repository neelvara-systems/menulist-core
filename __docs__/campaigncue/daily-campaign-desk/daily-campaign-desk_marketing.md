# Daily Campaign Desk — Marketing Notes

## Positioning

Most design tools start with a blank canvas, a template search, or a prompt box. Daily Campaign Desk starts with the business owner's next useful campaign action.

## Message

CampaignCue helps local businesses turn today's offer, service, item, or opening into a usable campaign pack without asking the owner to plan every channel.

Public positioning should stay close to:

> Your daily campaign desk for local business.
>
> Know what to promote. Get the pack. Share it with confidence.

## Differentiators

- Starts from business truth, not a blank canvas.
- Shows missing details before copy is used.
- Produces one pack that can be used across WhatsApp, Google, social, print, and staff handoff.
- Supports restaurant, salon, retail, local-service, fitness, clinic, and generic local-business workflows without making the owner start from a blank canvas.
- Treats the editor as optional last-mile work through Design Cue and CueLayers, not as the main product.
- Keeps direct posting and spend out of the day-one product.
- Remembers owner-reported results through quick options or short notes.
- Turns approval, due reminders, result capture, and safe current-fact reuse into one clear Campaign Rhythm.
- Shows pack readiness without pretending to predict reach, engagement, or ROI.

## Sales Boundary

Do not claim automatic social posting, connected scheduling, ad creation, or provider metrics. The active product prepares and downloads source-backed campaign packs.

Do not call Campaign Rhythm an autopilot or Pack readiness a performance score.
