# Campaign Memory 2.0 - Website Boundary

## Current Decision

Campaign Memory is an owner-workspace capability. It does not require a separate public feature page during the initial implementation.

After authenticated product evidence exists, the main website may use:

> Record what happened and let CampaignCue remember which campaign patterns were useful, with owner-reported confidence kept visible.

## Do Not Say

- Do not claim provider analytics are connected.
- Do not claim causal attribution, guaranteed performance, ROI, or prediction.
- Do not imply customer-level tracking.
- Do not describe owner-reported counts as independently verified results.

## Proof Requirement

Public proof needs a real product screenshot showing the result receipt, the confidence label, and the next-action explanation. Fabricated metrics or synthetic business outcomes are not acceptable proof.
