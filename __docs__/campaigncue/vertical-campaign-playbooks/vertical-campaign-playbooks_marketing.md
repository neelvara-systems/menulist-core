# Vertical Campaign Playbooks - Marketing Boundary

## Supported

- "Campaign actions shaped for common local-business jobs."
- "Recommendations use checked facts and business-specific guardrails."
- "A small, curated action library instead of a generic template catalog."

## Prohibited

- "CampaignCue knows the perfect strategy for every industry."
- "Guaranteed bookings, sales, health outcomes, or reach."
- "Automatic campaigns based on guessed stock, capacity, holidays, or offers."
