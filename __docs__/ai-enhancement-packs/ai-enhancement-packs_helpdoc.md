## What are AI Enhancement Packs?

AI Enhancement Packs give your menu additional AI features — image generation, description enhancements, and language translations.

Every MenuList plan includes AI features for your menu. Enhancement Packs are available when your business needs more.

---

## When do I need one?

You don't need to think about it. If your menu needs more AI enhancements, MenuList will let you know with a simple option to purchase a pack.

Common situations where an Enhancement Pack is useful:

- **Large menu** — Generating images for 100+ items
- **Multiple languages** — Adding 3 or more languages to your menu
- **Frequent updates** — Refreshing descriptions or images regularly
- **Multiple outlets** — Running AI features across several stores

---

## How do I buy one?

1. When your menu needs more AI enhancements, you'll see an option: "Get more AI enhancements for your menu"
2. Click the option
3. Complete the payment
4. Your AI features are available after payment is confirmed

No setup. No activation steps. It just works.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Does my plan already include AI features?"

Yes. Every MenuList plan includes AI features for typical menu needs. Enhancement Packs are for businesses that need more.

### "Can I buy more than one pack?"

Yes. Purchase additional packs whenever your menu needs them.

### "Does a pack expire?"

No. Enhancement Packs never expire.

### "Does one pack work for all my stores?"

Each store manages its own AI enhancements. Buy an Enhancement Pack for any store that needs more.

### "What happens after I buy a pack?"

Your AI features are available after payment is confirmed. Continue using MenuList as usual.

### "Can I get a refund?"

Refunds are handled per our terms of service. Contact support for assistance.

### "How do I know if I need a pack?"

You don't need to monitor anything. If your menu needs more AI enhancements, MenuList will let you know.
