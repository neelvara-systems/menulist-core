## FAQ Section

### "What's included in my plan?"

> Every MenuList plan includes AI features for your menu — image generation, descriptions, and translations. For typical menu sizes and update frequencies, your plan covers everything.

### "When do I need an Enhancement Pack?"

> If your menu needs more AI enhancements than your plan covers, MenuList will let you know. You can purchase an Enhancement Pack and continue after payment is confirmed.

### "How much does an Enhancement Pack cost?"

> AI Enhancement Pack pricing must come from the approved pricing source before publication. Do not publish this draft with TBD price placeholders.

### "Does an Enhancement Pack expire?"

> No. Enhancement Packs never expire. Use them whenever your menu needs more.

### "Does one pack cover all my stores?"

> Each store manages its own AI enhancements. Buy an Enhancement Pack for any store that needs more.

### "Can I buy more than one pack?"

> Yes. Purchase additional packs whenever your menu needs more AI enhancements.

### "What AI features does the pack include?"

> Enhancement Packs cover all AI features — image generation, description enhancements, and language translations. Everything your menu needs.
