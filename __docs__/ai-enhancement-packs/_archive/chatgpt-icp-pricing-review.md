## Topics Covered

1. **ICP Alignment Stress-Test** — Validated against non-technical SMB behavior. 80/15/5 segmentation. Cognitive load test, emotional test, multi-outlet test.
2. **Yearly Subscription Margin Simulation** — Pro yearly ₹14,990 vs ~₹1,320 AI cost/year = 91% margin even at heavy usage.
3. **Pricing Psychology** — SMBs compare to designers/agencies (₹8k–₹25k), not AI tools. ₹3k pack = "extremely cheap" by comparison.
4. **Unit Cost Validation (5 credits/image)** — Sweet spot analysis: 3 = overused, 5 = balanced, 7+ = restrictive.
5. **India vs Global Pricing** — One internal economy, multiple external price layers. Same units globally. PPP gap ₹2,999 vs $39–49.
6. **Margin Protection** — High margin is correct for SaaS (supports costs, churn, scaling, AI price hikes). Never reduce price — adjust internal unit costs.
