## Positioning Statement

## Objection Handling Guide

### "My subscription already includes AI — why do I pay more?"

> "Your subscription includes AI features for typical menu needs. Enhancement Packs are for businesses that run more AI operations — like generating images for 200+ items or adding multiple languages. Most subscribers never need one."

### "How do I know if I need a pack?"

> "You don't need to think about it. If your menu needs more AI enhancements, the system will let you know. It's simple — buy a pack and continue."

### "Can I see how much AI I've used?"

> "Your AI features are handled automatically. If you need more, the system makes it easy to get an Enhancement Pack."

### "Why can't I just pay per image?"

> "Enhancement Packs are simpler. One purchase covers all AI features — images, descriptions, translations. No per-item math."

### "My competitor says they have unlimited AI."

> "We focus on quality and reliability. Your menu gets consistent AI enhancements. Enhancement Packs ensure your menu is always covered."

### "What if I buy a pack and don't use it all?"

> "Enhancement Packs don't expire. Use them whenever you need — this month, next quarter, next year."

### "Is it one pack per store?"

> "Yes — each store manages its own AI enhancements. Buy an Enhancement Pack for any store that needs more."

### Real-World Competitive Frame (Internal — For Sales Team)

> **Source:** ChatGPT pricing psychology review (Feb 10, 2026)

We are NOT competing with AI tools (Midjourney, ChatGPT, Canva). We are competing with the SMB's current alternatives:

| What They'd Pay Without Us | Typical Cost (India)     |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Food photoshoot            | ₹8k–₹25k per shoot       |
| Designer menu setup        | ₹3k–₹15k                 |
| Professional translation   | ₹500–₹2,000 per language |
| Menu redesign              | ₹5k–₹20k                 |

**Sales angle:** If a restaurant spends ₹3k–₹6k/year on Enhancement Packs, they save ₹15k+ in designer and photographer costs. Position packs as **extremely cheap compared to the alternative**, never as an add-on cost.

### Indian SMB Buying Psychology (Internal — For Pricing Decisions)

> **Source:** ChatGPT ICP stress-test (Feb 10, 2026)

**They pay easily for:** Things that earn money, save time, improve business image.

**They resist:** Confusing pricing, recurring microcharges, per-use billing, surprise bills.

**Our model avoids all resistance patterns:**

- Subscription = predictable monthly cost (like rent)
- Pack = occasional one-time purchase (like printer ink or WhatsApp recharge)
- No meter, no countdown, no per-action billing

**Pack pricing comfort zone (India):** ₹1,500–₹3,000 is "occasional boost" territory. Above ₹5,000, small SMBs hesitate.

### India vs Global — Sales Positioning

**Same product globally. Only price differs.**

| Market    | Pack Price | Why It Works                                         |
| --------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| India     | ₹2,999     | Within SMB comfort zone, saves ₹15k+ vs alternatives |
| US/Global | $39–49     | Trivial vs $200+ photoshoot or $300+ menu design     |

**Never:** Offer different features by region. Never suggest global users get "more" AI. The system is identical — only the price tag changes.

**Hidden advantage:** As global user base grows, global revenue subsidizes Indian pricing. We can keep India affordable while maintaining strong overall margins.
