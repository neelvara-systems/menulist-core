## Quick Summary

MenuList shows whether customers are returning to your business consistently. One word: Strong, Stable, or Weak. Based on aggregate visitor patterns, not individual tracking.

---

## Understanding the Signal

| Signal | What It Means | What To Do |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Strong** | Customers are coming back regularly | Keep doing what you're doing |
| **Stable** | Normal return patterns | Business as usual |
| **Weak** | Fewer customers returning than before | Check service, menu quality, and customer experience |

## When It Appears

The loyalty signal shows on your dashboard after 4+ weeks of regular visitors. Before that, it stays hidden.

## FAQ

**Q: This isn't a loyalty program?**  
A: Correct. No points, no rewards, no customer sign-ups. Just a signal about overall return patterns.

**Q: What can I do if loyalty is "Weak"?**  
A: Think about recent changes — new competition, price increases, menu changes, service quality. The signal tells you there's a shift; you decide what to investigate.
