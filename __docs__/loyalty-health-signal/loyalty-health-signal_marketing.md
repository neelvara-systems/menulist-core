## Elevator Pitch

"77% of restaurant customers never come back. But most owners don't know their return rate. MenuList watches visitor patterns and tells you — in one word — whether customers are coming back or drifting away."

## Feature Narrative

The hardest truth for a business owner: customers came once but didn't return. It hits ego, quality, confidence, and survival all at once. But owners have no reliable way to know. MenuList gives a simple, calm loyalty signal based on real visitor patterns. Not a CRM. Not a loyalty program. Just awareness.
