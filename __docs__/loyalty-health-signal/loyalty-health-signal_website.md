## Problem Statement

77% of restaurant customers never return after their first visit. But business owners have no way to know their return rate without expensive CRM systems or loyalty programs that most customers ignore.

## Solution Statement

MenuList watches aggregate visitor patterns on your public page and tells you whether customers are returning consistently. One word: Strong, Stable, or Weak. No dashboards. No customer tracking. Just a calm awareness signal.

## Feature Benefits

1. **Know Your Return Rate** — Without CRM or loyalty programs
2. **Privacy-Safe** — No individual customer tracking
3. **Automatic** — Appears when you have enough visitors
4. **Actionable** — Weak signal prompts self-investigation

## FAQ

**Q: Is this a loyalty program?**  
A: No. There are no points, no rewards, no customer accounts. It's simply an awareness signal about return visit patterns.

**Q: How does it know if customers return?**  
A: By analyzing aggregate visitor patterns — total visits vs unique visitors over time. No individual tracking.
