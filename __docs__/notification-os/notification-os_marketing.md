# NotificationOS — Marketing Guidance

> **Status:** Internal positioning only; not approved as a current public feature claim
> **Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## Positioning

NotificationOS is internal reliability infrastructure that helps important account messages reach an owner through the channels they have actually made available.

## Owner Outcome

- Important account updates are not tied to one sign-in method.
- Owners can use email, WhatsApp, or both when eligible.
- The same event is communicated consistently across channels.
- Consent and channel availability are respected.

## Approved Internal Language

- “Important account updates through your available channels.”
- “Choose email, WhatsApp, or both.”
- “WhatsApp notifications require your permission.”
- “Your sign-in method does not silently decide your notification settings.”

## Prohibited Claims

- “Guaranteed delivery.”
- “Every message arrives instantly.”
- “WhatsApp enabled automatically for phone users.”
- “All products support every channel.”
- “NotificationOS is live” before release certification.
- “AI-powered”, “smart”, “dynamic” or “intelligent”.

## Product Narrative Boundary

This should normally appear as a trust/supporting capability inside onboarding, billing or account-management content, not as a standalone MenuList headline. Provider names and infrastructure details do not belong in owner-facing marketing.
