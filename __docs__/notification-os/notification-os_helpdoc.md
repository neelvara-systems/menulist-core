# NotificationOS — Owner Help Draft

> **Status:** Draft for future release; not proof that settings or channels are currently available
> **Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## Quick Summary

After this feature is certified for your product, you can choose eligible important account updates by email, WhatsApp, or both.

## Choose Your Channels

1. Open **More** and select **Notification settings**.
2. Check which email address and phone number are verified.
3. Choose **Email**, **WhatsApp**, or **Both** where available.
4. If you choose WhatsApp, review the permission text and select **Allow WhatsApp notifications**.
5. Save your changes.

Notification settings are available to the account owner. Store staff do not see this owner-level control. The screen shows masked verified sign-in contacts so public business contact fields are not mistaken for notification destinations.

> Signing in with a WhatsApp code does not automatically allow account notifications.

## Common Questions

### I signed up with my phone. Why is email unavailable?

Your account can work without a real email address. Add and verify an email before choosing email notifications. MenuList’s internal sign-in address is never used for delivery.

### I signed up with Google. Can I receive WhatsApp updates?

Yes, after this capability is available: add and verify your WhatsApp number, then allow WhatsApp notifications.

### Can I select both?

Yes for eligible notifications. Some messages may still use one channel because of consent, provider rules or the type of update.

### I stopped WhatsApp notifications. Will login codes stop too?

Notification permission and authentication are separate. The product must explain the authentication consequence before any change that affects login-code delivery.

### Why did only one of two channels receive an update?

One channel may be missing, suppressed, not permitted, unavailable or rejected. Your account action is not blocked by notification delivery.
