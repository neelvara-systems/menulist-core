# EmailOS — Internal Marketing and Sales Notes

> **Audience:** Founder, sales and product marketing
> **Public feature:** No
> **Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## Internal One-Liner

Product email has one governed delivery standard with product-specific isolation and observable outcomes.

## Customer Value

EmailOS is infrastructure, not a separately sold feature. Its value appears through reliable account messages, clearer delivery recovery and fewer repeated sends to invalid addresses.

## Approved Proof Points

Use only after provider certification proves them:

- Product email uses authenticated product domains.
- Permanent delivery failures stop repeated attempts.
- Important delivery outcomes are recorded for operational recovery.
- Email content has both HTML and plain-text forms.

## Claims That Are Not Approved

- Guaranteed inbox delivery
- Zero spam placement
- Perfect email reliability
- Dedicated IP delivery before the plan and volume qualify
- Real-time customer engagement analytics
- One shared customer database across products

## Sales Talking Points

| Question | Approved response |
| --- | --- |
| Do you send account emails? | Active products can send bounded account and operational messages after their delivery provider is certified. |
| Can emails be duplicated? | Product claims and provider idempotency reduce duplicate delivery; ambiguous failures are not automatically sent through another provider. |
| What happens after a hard bounce? | The address is blocked for that product until the delivery issue is resolved and the suppression is reviewed. |
| Is this a marketing automation system? | No. EmailOS is internal transactional delivery infrastructure. |

## Packaging

EmailOS is included in product operations. Resend subscription cost is an infrastructure cost, not a separately priced customer add-on. SignalDesk outreach remains separately governed and does not inherit transactional-email activation.
