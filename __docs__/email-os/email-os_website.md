# EmailOS — Website Content Boundary

> **Public landing page:** Not admitted
> **Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## Decision

EmailOS does not receive a standalone public page, navigation item, CTA or SEO route. Customers buy the product outcome, not the delivery provider or internal messaging architecture.

## Permitted Trust-Layer Copy

After production certification, a product trust or security page may state:

> Account emails use authenticated product domains and monitored delivery infrastructure.

If suppression handling is production-certified, it may also state:

> Permanent delivery failures are stopped instead of being repeatedly retried.

## Prohibited Copy

- “Guaranteed delivery”
- “Always reaches your inbox”
- “Spam-proof”
- “Dedicated sending infrastructure” unless a dedicated IP is active and evidenced
- Provider logos presented as a product benefit without approval
- Resend pricing, plan or quota claims on product marketing pages

## SEO Decision

No page title, meta description, target keyword or public sitemap entry is created. EmailOS remains internal infrastructure.
