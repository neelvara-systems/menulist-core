# Accessibility Help

MenuList follows your browser and device accessibility settings.

- You can zoom or enlarge the page using normal browser and device controls.
- Keyboard users can reveal a **Skip to main content** link at the start of owner and website pages.
- Focused actions have a visible outline.
- Reduced-motion preferences minimize non-essential movement.
- Mobile actions keep larger touch areas even when the icon itself is small.

These behaviors do not change your menu, billing, subscription, or saved business information.
