# Continuous Menu Intelligence — Support Boundary

**Last verified:** July 16, 2026

CMI is private background infrastructure. There is no owner-facing setup, dashboard, confidence score, mobile screen, toggle, notification, or daily task.

Support should not tell owners that:

- CMI changes their menu order or visibility;
- a changed Featured choice proves CMI is learning;
- CMI knows completed orders, sales, inventory, ratings, or reviews;
- intelligence improves after a promised number of days; or
- they need to contact support to control a private score.

For questions about the customer Featured section, use `../decision-intelligence/decision-intelligence_helpdoc.md`. Owners control that section through the shared desktop/mobile Featured section settings; those controls do not edit CMI state.

Historical support copy is retained at `_archive/pre-2026-07-16/continuous-menu-intelligence_helpdoc.md`.
