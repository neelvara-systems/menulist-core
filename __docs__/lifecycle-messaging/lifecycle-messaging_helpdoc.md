# How Notifications Work — Help Guide

**Feature:** System Notifications  
**Audience:** Store Owners  
**Last Updated:** Feb 20, 2026

---

## Quick Summary

MenuList sends you important emails about your account — payment confirmations, billing reminders, and your menu going live. These are system messages only. We never send marketing or promotional emails.

---

## What Emails Will I Receive?

| Email | When | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| **Menu is Live** | After your first menu is published | Gives you your permanent public link |
| **Payment Confirmed** | After successful subscription payment | Confirms payment and shows next billing date |
| **Payment Issue** | If a payment fails | So you can update your payment method |
| **Renewal Reminder** | 3 days before your subscription renews | So you can ensure sufficient funds |
| **Credits Added** | After purchasing a credit pack | Confirms credits added to your balance |

---

## Where Are Emails Sent?

Emails go to the contact email address on your account. This is the email you used when setting up your store.

---

## Can I Change the Email Address?

You can update your notification email in **Business Settings → Notifications**.

---

## Troubleshooting

### I'm not receiving emails
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Search for emails from `system@menulist.ai`
3. Make sure your email address is correct in Business Settings
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### I received a payment failure email
1. Check your bank account or card balance
2. Update your payment method if needed
3. Your menu stays live during the grace period — no rush

---

## Tips

- Keep your contact email up to date to never miss important billing notifications
- Payment confirmations are useful for your business accounting records
- We only send essential system messages — typically 2-3 per month
