## Problem Statement

Running a business means you can't check dashboards every day. Important billing updates get buried. Payment failures go unnoticed. And suddenly your service is interrupted.

## Solution Statement

MenuList keeps you informed with essential system emails — payment confirmations, renewal reminders, and billing alerts. No marketing. No spam. Just the updates that matter.

---

## How It Works

1. **Your menu goes live** → You receive your permanent public link by email
2. **Payments process** → Instant confirmation with next billing date
3. **Something needs attention** → Clear notification with what to do next

## Feature Benefits

- **Stay informed without logging in** — Important updates come to your inbox
- **Prevent payment surprises** — Renewal reminders 3 days before billing
- **Keep records for accounting** — Payment confirmations with amounts and dates
- **No noise** — Only essential system messages, typically 2-3 per month

## FAQ

**Will I receive marketing emails?**
No. MenuList only sends essential system notifications about your account and billing. No newsletters, no promotions, no tips.

**How many emails will I receive?**
Typically 2-3 per month — payment confirmations and occasional reminders. Critical alerts only when something needs your attention.

**Can I choose where to receive notifications?**
Yes. You can set your preferred notification email in Business Settings.
