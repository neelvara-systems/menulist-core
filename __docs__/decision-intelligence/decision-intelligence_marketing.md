# Featured Choices — Marketing Boundary

**Last verified:** July 16, 2026
**Status:** Approved claim boundary; not current launch certification.

Release use still requires the active production-readiness audit, External Certification Runbook evidence, scoped scheduler deploy evidence, browser/mobile customer-menu QA, public-cache evidence, and production-host smoke.

## Approved positioning

Featured Choices can give customers a clearer starting point from the current approved menu. Owners can select an item when direct control matters, while the full menu remains available underneath.

Approved short copy:

> Show Featured, Quick, and Value choices from the current approved menu.

Approved supporting copy:

> When enough settled menu activity exists, MenuList can prepare a small Featured section. Owners can also choose what appears. Availability and the current menu always win.

## What can be claimed

- Choices come from the current approved menu.
- The normal menu remains unchanged and fully browsable.
- Owners can show, hide, select, or clear supported choices.
- Automatic choices require sufficient settled activity.
- Unavailable, hidden, out-of-time, repeated, stale, or otherwise ineligible items are skipped.
- MenuList may show less when there is not enough safe evidence.

## What cannot be claimed

- a guaranteed reduction in decision time;
- more orders, revenue, conversions, table turns, or repeat customers;
- most ordered, most booked, top rated, customer favorite, or best seller from this flow;
- POS, inventory, review, rating, purchase, or completed-order knowledge;
- that Featured Choices appear on every menu;
- that a selected item is guaranteed to appear; or
- `AI-powered`, `Smart recommendations`, algorithm, machine learning, or optimization language.

## Owner-facing language

Use:

- `Featured section`
- `Featured choice`, `Quick choice`, `Value choice`
- `MenuList chooses automatically`
- `Use automatic choice`
- `Your selected item is used whenever it is available.`

Do not explain score weights, confidence, customer profiling, or internal algorithms to owners.

## Customer-facing reasons

- Automatic popular candidates use the neutral reason `Popular choice` after per-item behavioral evidence passes.
- Quick choice uses an explicit item duration.
- Value choice uses the bounded current-menu price comparison.
- Owner-selected candidates use `Owner's choice`.
