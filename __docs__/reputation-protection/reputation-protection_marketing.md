## Elevator Pitch

"Bad reviews happen. What matters is how you respond. MenuList watches your Google reviews and helps you reply calmly and professionally — right from your dashboard. No panic. No missed reviews. Just protection."

## Feature Narrative

One bad review can ruin a business owner's entire week. They see it, panic, and either ignore it or type an angry reply that makes things worse. MenuList changes this. Reviews that need attention surface quietly inside MenuList, with a suggested professional reply ready to go. Owner approves, it posts. Reputation stays stable.

## Pitch Deck Outline

1. **Problem:** 88% of customers read reviews before visiting. One bad reply can go viral.
2. **Solution:** MenuList watches reviews and suggests calm, professional replies.
3. **How it works:** Reviews → Classification → Only important ones surface → AI suggests reply → Owner approves
4. **Protection:** No reviews missed. No emotional replies. Consistent brand tone.
5. **For chains:** All outlets in one inbox, same brand voice everywhere.
6. **ROI:** Positive reviews can lift revenue up to 8% (SocialPilot 2025).
7. **CTA:** Enable reputation protection when your Google Business Profile is connected.
