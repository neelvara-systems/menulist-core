## Problem Statement

88% of customers check reviews before choosing a business. One bad review without a response can cost you dozens of future customers. Most business owners either miss reviews, respond too late, or reply emotionally — making things worse.

## Solution Statement

MenuList monitors your Google reviews and quietly alerts you when something needs attention. It suggests a calm, professional reply — you review it, approve it, and it posts. Your reputation stays protected without the stress.

## Feature Benefits

1. **Never Miss a Review** — Important reviews surface in your dashboard automatically
2. **Reply Professionally** — Get a suggested reply that's calm, polite, and on-brand
3. **Stay Calm** — No panic, no emotional responses. Just protection.
4. **One Inbox for All Locations** — Chain owners see all outlet reviews in one place

## How It Works

1. **MenuList watches** — Google reviews are checked automatically
2. **Important ones surface** — Only reviews needing attention appear in your inbox
3. **Reply with confidence** — Review the suggested response, edit if needed, post with one tap

## FAQ

**Q: Does MenuList auto-reply to reviews?**  
A: No. MenuList suggests a reply, but you always review and approve before anything is posted.

**Q: What about positive reviews?**  
A: Positive reviews don't need action — MenuList only surfaces reviews that need your attention.

**Q: Which review platforms are supported?**  
A: Currently Google Business Profile. This is where most customers check first.
