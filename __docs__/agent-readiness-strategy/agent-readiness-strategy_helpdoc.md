## Quick Summary

MenuList publishes your owner-approved menu and business information in a structured format on your public MenuList pages. AI and search systems can read that public source when they crawl or open the page, but they decide what they show, cite, or refresh.

---

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- A published menu on MenuList
- Up-to-date business hours and contact info

### Is There Anything I Need to Set Up?

**No.** MenuList structures your published menu and business info automatically. There's nothing to configure, enable, or pay extra for. As long as your menu is published and your business info is current, your public MenuList page has structured data available for systems that crawl or open it.

---

## How It Works

### How AI Finds Your Business

1. You update your menu and business info on MenuList
2. MenuList automatically structures this into a format AI can read (called "schema.org")
3. When an AI or search system crawls or opens your MenuList page, it can read this structured data
4. The external system decides what to show, cite, or summarize

### What Information AI Can Read

From your MenuList page, AI assistants can see:
- Your business name and type
- Your full menu with item names, descriptions, and prices
- Dietary information (vegetarian, vegan options)
- Whether items are available right now
- Your business hours and whether you're currently open
- Your location and contact details

---

## How To Guides

### How to Make Sure AI Gets Your Info Right

1. Keep your **menu updated** — add new items, remove old ones, update prices
2. Keep your **business hours** current — especially holidays and special hours
3. Fill in your **business info** completely — address, phone, business type
4. Use your **MenuList link** everywhere — Google listing, Instagram bio, WhatsApp, QR codes
   > The more places your MenuList link appears, the more AI systems recognize it as your official page

### How to Check If AI Knows About Your Business

1. Open ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Perplexity
2. Ask: "What's on the menu at [your business name]?"
3. See if the answer includes accurate menu info
   > It may take a few weeks for AI systems to discover new pages. Keep your info updated and be patient.

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

### AI gave wrong information about my business
**Why this happens:** AI systems sometimes use cached or outdated data from other sources.
**How to fix it:**
1. Make sure your MenuList page has the correct information
2. Share your MenuList link on your Google Business Profile and social media — this helps AI prioritize your official data
3. Give it time — AI systems regularly re-read pages and update their information

### AI doesn't seem to know about my business at all
**Why this happens:** AI systems need time to discover new pages, especially for new businesses.
**How to fix it:**
1. Make sure your menu is published (not in draft)
2. Share your MenuList link publicly (Google listing, Instagram bio, QR codes)
3. Keep your menu and hours updated regularly — active pages get discovered faster

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep your menu items descriptive — "Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad" is better than "Salad #3"
- Add dietary tags to items (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) — AI uses these to answer specific customer questions
- Update your hours whenever they change — wrong hours damage trust with both customers and AI
- Use your MenuList link as your primary public link everywhere so external systems have one clear source to read
