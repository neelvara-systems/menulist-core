# WhatsAppOS — Marketing Guidance

> **Status:** Internal positioning only; not approved as a current public feature claim
> **Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## Positioning

WhatsAppOS is internal delivery infrastructure for approved, permission-based WhatsApp communication. Owners should experience timely, clear messages—not a provider or infrastructure product.

## Approved Internal Language

- “Official WhatsApp delivery through Meta.”
- “Permission-based account notifications.”
- “Login codes and account notifications use separate permission rules.”
- “Product-specific sender identity and message templates.”

## Prohibited Claims

- “Guaranteed WhatsApp delivery.”
- “Free unlimited WhatsApp messages.”
- “Phone sign-up automatically enables notifications.”
- “Read means the owner completed the action.”
- “Available across all products.”
- “AI-powered”, “smart”, “dynamic” or “intelligent”.

## Public Narrative

After certification, describe owner outcomes inside the relevant product flow: receive a login code, continue WhatsApp onboarding, or allow important account updates. Do not market WhatsAppOS itself as a standalone customer feature.
