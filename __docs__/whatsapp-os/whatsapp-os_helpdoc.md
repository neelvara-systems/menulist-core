# WhatsAppOS — Owner Help Draft

> **Status:** Draft for future certified product flows
> **Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

## Quick Summary

WhatsApp can be used for supported login codes, owner-started setup conversations, and eligible account updates. These uses have separate rules.

## Allow Account Notifications

1. Open **Notification settings**.
2. Add or confirm your WhatsApp number.
3. Complete verification if requested.
4. Read the permission message.
5. Select **Allow WhatsApp notifications** and save.

You can revoke this permission later. Login-code availability is explained separately because authentication and account-notification consent are not the same.

## Troubleshooting

### My code did not arrive

Wait for the resend timer, confirm the country code and number, then request one new code. Do not repeatedly request codes. Use the available alternate sign-in or support route if the issue continues.

### I allowed notifications but did not receive one

The update may not use WhatsApp, the template may be unavailable, or delivery may have failed. Check email if enabled. Your business action is not blocked by notification delivery.

### I no longer want account notifications on WhatsApp

Open **Notification settings**, turn off WhatsApp notifications and save. The change applies to later eligible account updates.

### I changed my phone number

Verify the new number before selecting it for notifications. The old number must not remain eligible after the change.

## Safety

- MenuList will not ask you to share your OTP with a person.
- Do not send passwords, card details or access tokens through support messages.
- Report an unexpected account message through the product’s current support route.
