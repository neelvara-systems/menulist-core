# Billing Taxation Owner Help

## Why MenuList asks for billing details

MenuList uses the billing address to calculate applicable tax and prepare the payment record. These details are private and do not change the public business page.

## GSTIN

Indian businesses can add a GSTIN. It must match the state selected in the billing address. A GSTIN is optional for customers who do not have one.

## Changing details

Contact billing support before the next checkout if the legal name, billing address, state, GSTIN, or tax ID changes. A completed payment keeps the snapshot used at that time.

## International checkout

International checkout is available only after MenuList's export and payment setup is enabled. Availability does not itself determine the customer's local tax obligations.
