> **Version:** 1.0
> **Last Updated:** March 15, 2026
> **Audience:** Business owners (customers)

---

## Quick Summary

Menu Quality Signals shows you simple suggestions to improve your menu — like adding descriptions or images to items that are missing them.

## How It Works

1. Open your **Dashboard**
2. Look at the **Menu Quality** card
3. You'll see signals like:
   - "5 items missing descriptions" → Tap **Generate** to create them automatically
   - "12 items missing images" → Tap **Generate** to create them
   - "All items have prices" ✓
4. When everything looks good, you'll see: **"Your menu looks great"**

## What Each Signal Means

| Signal | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| Items missing descriptions | Some items don't have descriptions. Customers browse faster when descriptions explain each dish. | Tap "Generate" to create descriptions automatically |
| Items missing images | Some items don't have images. Customers order more when they can see the food. | Tap "Generate" to create images |
| Items missing prices | Some items don't show a price. | Open the editor and add prices |
| Category has many items | One category has a lot of items. Splitting it may help customers browse. | Open the editor to reorganize |

If prices are turned off in menu design, price-related checks are hidden. No action needed.

## Tips

- Start with descriptions — they make the biggest difference for customers
- Images are generated automatically and look professional
- You can always edit generated content in the editor
