# Distribution Operating System - Internal Positioning

> Public marketing status: prohibited
> Internal name: DistributionOS
> Invocation: `$distribution-os`

## Internal Description

DistributionOS is the portfolio's repository-backed curator for marketing and distribution knowledge. It maintains one living Bible of what we believe and how we operate, admits only material improvements, and routes execution to the correct existing system.

## Value

- Durable ideas survive beyond one chat without creating an article diary.
- Repetitive, weak, or non-decision-useful material is intentionally not stored.
- Important manipulation patterns and guardrails become durable doctrine.
- Product boundaries are checked before implementation.
- Deferred ideas have named activation and revalidation gates.
- Prior research is retrieved before duplicate work.
- SignalDesk execution records stay separate from knowledge intake.

## Approved Internal Language

- repository-backed distribution knowledge system;
- selective external insight curation and routing;
- living Marketing and Distribution Bible;
- evidence-limited product verdicts;
- read-only retrieval and registry audit;
- one coordination tag for future inputs.

## Prohibited Language

Do not call DistributionOS:

- a public growth product;
- an autonomous marketing agent;
- an AI citation optimizer;
- a publishing or outreach engine;
- a guaranteed-growth system;
- a replacement for SignalDesk, CampaignCue, AssetOS, or human approval.

There is no public sales copy, pricing, signup, launch plan, logo, domain, or customer proposition for DistributionOS.
