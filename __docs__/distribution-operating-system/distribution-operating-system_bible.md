# Marketing And Distribution Bible

**Status:** Living internal source of truth
**Owner:** Founder with Codex curation
**Applies to:** MenuList, Answerlattice, CampaignCue, SignalDesk, GrowthOS/Growth Kits, and portfolio distribution decisions

## Purpose

This is the first document to read before marketing or distribution strategy,
content, SEO, launch, channel, partnership, paid-acquisition, AI-discovery, or
growth-system work.

It is a curated synthesis, not a diary of articles. External material earns a
place here only when it creates a durable advantage for our products, materially
improves an existing rule, establishes a reusable operating pattern, changes a
real decision or gate, or preserves an important guardrail. Repetition,
unsupported tactics, short-lived platform folklore, and ideas already covered
without improvement do not get stored.

Supporting source ledgers retain selected evidence and detailed validation when
provenance genuinely matters. SignalDesk retains execution and outcomes. This
Bible retains what we believe and how we operate.

## The Core Doctrine

### 1. Product truth comes before distribution

Distribution amplifies whatever the product and public surfaces already say.
Do not scale a message, page, video, partnership, or campaign until the product
identity, buyer, capability, limitation, proof, and next action are accurate.

### 2. Proof comes before volume

A real product walkthrough, permissioned customer result, verified workflow,
or honest decision aid is more valuable than a large generic content calendar.
One strong proof source may be transformed into several channel-native assets;
fabricated proof, generic filler, and content volume are not substitutes.

### 3. One motion needs one buyer problem and one promise

Every page, video, post, partner brief, or campaign must name:

- the exact buyer or user state;
- the problem they are trying to resolve;
- the product's truthful role;
- the proof or decision aid;
- the next qualified action.

Do not chase an audience that cannot buy or use the product merely because it
is easy to reach.

### 4. Qualified actions outrank vanity metrics

Followers, impressions, views, likes, and generic engagement are diagnostics.
Success is measured through qualified conversations, prepared inputs, private
previews, verified setups, activations, retained use, permissioned proof,
payments, or product-specific outcomes.

### 5. Evidence admits work; inspiration does not

An article can reveal a pattern, question, or test. It cannot by itself
authorize a feature, page family, integration, campaign, account, provider,
budget, or automated system. Admission requires current product fit, credible
evidence, an owner decision when needed, and a measurable outcome.

## Curating External Knowledge

When new material is shared, DistributionOS evaluates it against current repo
truth and this Bible.

### Admit into the Bible when the input

- adds a durable principle that applies beyond one post;
- materially improves or corrects an existing doctrine;
- supplies a reusable workflow, decision rule, or measurement pattern;
- exposes a meaningful risk, manipulation pattern, or failure mode;
- changes a current product decision, gate, priority, or operating boundary;
- provides credible evidence for an existing hypothesis.

### Do not store when the input is

- a repetition that adds no material nuance;
- an unsupported result, universal formula, or algorithm anecdote;
- a tactic that conflicts with product doctrine, platform integrity, privacy,
  disclosure, security, or founder authority;
- too product-irrelevant or too temporary to guide future work;
- a list of tools without a proven workflow need;
- interesting but not decision-useful.

When an input is useful, synthesize the lesson into the relevant section. Do
not append a chronological article summary. Add a supporting evidence-ledger
record only when later readers will need provenance, claim-by-claim validation,
or a product-specific decision trail.

## Audience, Positioning, And Messaging

- Lead with the product's durable category and customer problem, not a trendy
  technology label.
- Use customer language learned from permissioned conversations, support,
  sales, onboarding, and first-party behavior.
- Explain the transformation early and visibly.
- State important limitations and coexistence boundaries; narrow truthful
  positioning is stronger than implying replacement of a broader system.
- Keep one coherent promise across website, product, sales, support, video, and
  partner material.
- Never use guaranteed growth, ranking, revenue, conversion, or time-to-result
  claims without direct approved evidence.

## SEO And Canonical Page Admission

Use page families as an opportunity taxonomy, not a production quota. Useful
families include category, problem, use case, constraint, comparison,
alternative, integration, pricing, differentiated feature, and genuinely
different buyer/workflow segments.

A new page is admitted only when it has:

- a distinct reader and search intent;
- a materially different answer from existing canonical pages;
- current product capability and approved claims;
- credible demand evidence from queries, customers, sales/support language, or
  a strategically necessary category explanation;
- at least one useful decision asset such as a real screenshot, workflow,
  comparison matrix, migration/coexistence guide, example, checklist, or
  permissioned proof;
- a clear conversion path and internal-link home;
- a maintainable freshness and verification owner.

Strengthen, merge, or retire existing pages before adding a near-duplicate.
Reject keyword-swapped doorway pages, mass AI page production, restaurant-only
drift for MenuList, broad competitor-replacement implications, and separate
pages for every imagined AI/search fan-out query.

### Technical preflight

Before scaling pages, verify the production host, crawlability, response status,
canonical, rendered content, internal links, sitemap inclusion, unique title/H1,
mobile equivalence, indexability policy, and Search Console state. A sitemap is
a discovery aid, not proof of indexing.

AI tools may help research, structure, draft, localize, suggest links, and check
contradictions. They do not decide page admission, verify competitor facts,
resolve cannibalization, approve claims, or publish autonomously.

## AI Discovery And Attribution

- Make public product truth clear, consistent, crawlable where appropriate,
  evidence-backed, and explicit about boundaries.
- Use factual comparisons when they genuinely help a buyer decide. Never
  manipulate competitor descriptions or create citation bait detached from
  user value.
- Treat platform-source preferences, citation percentages, indexing delays,
  authority behavior, memory effects, and citation lifetimes as unstable claims
  requiring current evidence.
- First-party analytics may miss AI-assisted discovery. Preserve optional,
  closed-list self-reported discovery at meaningful onboarding/qualification
  points and compare it with observed referral data.
- Do not use prompt injection, agent-only persuasion, fake urgency, fabricated
  discounts, or hidden instructions intended to influence an AI agent.
- Do not grant an agent autonomous signup, billing, installation, publishing,
  business-truth, or account authority merely to gain distribution.

## Organic Content And Channel System

### Package before production

Before creating content, define the buyer, channel context, promise, title or
first frame, proof, useful takeaway, CTA, and measurement plan.

### Teach a real sub-problem

Content should be valuable even before the product CTA. Demonstrate the
product's natural role instead of hiding an unrelated pitch at the end.

### One proof source, native derivatives

A verified walkthrough may become a long video, short clips, founder lesson,
resource page, partner brief, or sales asset. Adapt structure and opening to the
channel rather than copying identical content everywhere.

### Treat platform rules as experiments

No universal posting cadence, hook formula, share/like weight, CTR threshold,
retention threshold, video length, posting time, or algorithm hack becomes
portfolio doctrine. Use current official guidance, small tests, qualified
outcomes, and stop conditions.

### Integrity

No fake region/device identity, bought or aged account, engagement group,
undisclosed affiliate amplification, artificial comments, impersonation,
fabricated founder/customer proof, or enforcement circumvention.

## Video And Demonstration

- Show real product behavior and accurate UI wherever possible.
- Build around one viewer problem and one clear product transformation.
- Make the opening immediately legible without relying on audio.
- Use native aspect ratios and channel-specific framing; do not treat one crop as
  sufficient for every surface.
- Treat narration, captions, music, sound effects, pacing, and transitions as a
  coordinated system.
- Prefer modular, reusable compositions and deterministic local production.
- Track qualified continuation and product action, not view count alone.
- Never invent results, users, testimonials, screens, or capabilities for a
  polished story.

Asset production belongs to the Website Asset Operating System or the approved
video workflow; this Bible governs why and how the asset is distributed.

## Paid Acquisition

Paid acquisition begins only after the product can measure the relevant funnel
from first touch through meaningful activation and payment/retention where
applicable.

### Search

- Start with high-intent exact and phrase themes when evidence and budget are
  limited; broader delivery requires controlled evidence.
- Match landing-page promise to query/ad promise.
- Add negatives and review real search terms.
- Do not treat one keyword tool or article as demand proof.

### Paid social

- Test a bounded set of materially different concepts, not cosmetic variants.
- Keep audience, geography, language, exclusions, budget, consent, attribution,
  and stop-loss rules explicit.
- Use human approval for creative, claims, audience, spend, and launch.

### Economics

Evaluate acquisition through qualified activation, realized gross-margin value,
churn, refunds, CAC, LTV, and payback—not clicks, leads, or a simple spend ratio
alone.

No autonomous marketing agent receives provider-send, campaign-launch, budget,
or optimization authority without a separate proven control contract.

## Permissioned Field Distribution And Partnerships

- Start with permissioned businesses or trusted introductions, not scraped or
  invented leads.
- Use a concrete diagnostic or private preview that solves a current problem.
- Ask for owner approval before publication, proof reuse, testimonial use, or
  partner attribution.
- Make sender identity, consent, opt-out/suppression, response ownership, and
  jurisdictional requirements explicit.
- Disclose affiliate, employee, gifted, paid, or materially connected promotion.
- Record complaints, revocations, and negative outcomes as seriously as wins.

For MenuList, the durable field loop is:

`permissioned business -> current-list consistency audit -> private preview -> owner approval -> public link -> two customer surfaces -> outcome receipt`

## Measurement And Learning

Every distribution motion needs:

- a starting hypothesis;
- the buyer and channel;
- a qualified action;
- a measurable activation/outcome;
- a time and budget/attention ceiling;
- stop, continue, and scale conditions;
- a place for negative evidence.

Use self-reported discovery alongside observable referral and cohort behavior
when dark attribution is plausible. Add dashboards or automation only when
manual evidence volume repeatedly blocks a decision.

## Revalidation Triggers

Recheck this Bible and its supporting evidence when:

- an active product changes identity, buyer, capability, pricing, or launch state;
- a deferred channel, integration, provider, page family, or paid test reaches
  its named gate;
- current primary platform guidance changes;
- first-party data contradicts a doctrine;
- a repeated field or workflow failure appears;
- a source introduces credible evidence that materially changes a decision.
