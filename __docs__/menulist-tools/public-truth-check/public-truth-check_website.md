## Problem Statement

Customers may find old menus, missing prices, unclear hours, or different links in different places.

The business may be active, but the public source is not clear.

---

## Solution Statement

Public Truth Check reviews the basic facts customers need and marks what is present, missing, unclear, or not checked.

MenuList then gives one practical next step: create or update the current customer link.

---

## Feature Benefits

### See The Missing Basics

The report checks business name, menu or service list, prices, hours, location, contact, customer actions, and the public link.

**Visual:** Simple report with green/neutral/missing rows.

### Works Beyond Restaurants

Use it for menus, service lists, packages, catalogs, rate cards, and price lists.

**Visual:** Source cards for restaurant menu, salon service list, clinic price list, and repair rate card.

### One Clear Next Step

The report does not create a new dashboard. It points to the next source fix.

**Visual:** Missing hours row linking to a source setup action.

### No Ranking Promise

The check is about clear public facts. Search engines and AI systems decide what they crawl, show, cite, or summarize.

**Visual:** Boundary note next to report footer.

---

## How It Works

1. **Add your source** - Enter your business name and current menu or service source.
2. **Read the report** - See what is present, missing, unclear, or not checked.
3. **Create one link** - Turn the approved source into one current customer link.

---

## FAQ

- **Q:** Does this check my ranking?
  **A:** No. It checks whether the public facts are clear.

- **Q:** Does this change anything on Google or Instagram?
  **A:** No. It prepares a clearer source and link.

- **Q:** Can I use this for a salon or service business?
  **A:** Yes. Use a service list, package list, rate card, or catalog.

- **Q:** Will MenuList fetch every page about my business?
  **A:** No. The check uses the source you provide and MenuList-owned data when available.

- **Q:** What happens after the report?
  **A:** You can create one current customer link from the approved source.
