# Hours Check - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal positioning
**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

---

## One-Line Pitch

Check whether customers can clearly see when the business is open, closed, or using special hours.

---

## Why SMB Owners Care

Hours are not a marketing trick. They are a public trust fact.

Customers need to know:

- when the business is open
- when it is closed
- whether festival, holiday, seasonal, or temporary hours apply
- what to do if the hours are unclear
- where the current business link lives

MenuList turns that into a simple report and routes the fix into one current customer link.

---

## Positioning

Use:

- "hours clarity"
- "current customer link"
- "regular hours and special hours"
- "checked entered fields only"
- "Google was not inspected"

Avoid:

- "AI visibility"
- "ranking opportunity"
- "traffic loss"
- "guaranteed visits"
- "verified Google hours"
- "automatic external updates"

---

## Sales Talking Points

| Owner concern | Calm response |
| --- | --- |
| "Customers already see my hours on Google." | This check does not inspect Google. It shows whether the hours facts you provide are clear enough to put on one current customer link. |
| "I only change hours during holidays." | That is exactly the gap. Special hours need a clear place before customers call, visit, book, or order. |
| "I have different locations." | V0 checks one source. Multi-location consistency belongs in V2 paid reporting after owner authentication. |
| "Will this update external profiles?" | No. V0 only reports entered facts and routes the fix into MenuList. |

---

## Packaging

| Lane | Position |
| --- | --- |
| V0 | Free public lead magnet |
| V1 | Included owner readiness check inside Business Health/Public Discovery |
| V2 | Paid recurring holiday readiness, history, multi-location, and agency reports |

Paid value must come from recurrence, history, branch consistency, or reporting. Do not sell a better one-time check.
