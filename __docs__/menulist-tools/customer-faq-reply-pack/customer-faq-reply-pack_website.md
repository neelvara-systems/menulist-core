# Customer FAQ Reply Pack - Website

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

**Public route:** `/tools/customer-faq-reply-pack`

---

## Page Promise

Create reusable FAQ and auto-reply text from repeated customer questions and owner-entered business facts.

## Required Public Boundaries

The page must say or imply:

- runs in the browser
- uses entered facts only
- no customer conversations are read
- no chatbot is created
- no automation is configured
- no message is sent
- links are not fetched
- reports are not stored unless the owner submits the follow-up form
- no AI answer is generated

## Discovery Copy

Use a source-truth frame:

```txt
Browser-local reply pack that turns owner-entered customer questions and business facts into reusable FAQ and auto-reply text.
```

Do not present the tool as a chatbot, inbox, campaign tool, support automation platform, or AI answer generator.
