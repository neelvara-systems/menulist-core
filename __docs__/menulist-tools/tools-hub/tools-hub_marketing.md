# Tools Hub - Marketing Notes

**Last Updated:** July 9, 2026

## Internal Positioning

MenuList Tools is the public acquisition layer around public business truth.

Recommended short positioning:

> Free checks that show whether your public business facts are clear, current, and easy for customers to act on.

## Audience

- SMB owner with scattered public information
- Restaurant, cafe, salon, clinic, shop, studio, repair, retail, or service-list business
- Local agency helping SMB clients clean up customer-facing source material
- Multi-location operator evaluating consistency gaps

## Approved Message

- Run a focused free check.
- See the exact public gap.
- Use MenuList to create or clean up one current customer link.

## Avoid

- "AI visibility score"
- "SEO audit"
- "Rank higher"
- "Get cited by AI"
- "We scanned your website"
- "We checked your Google profile"
- "We update external platforms for you"

## Conversion Path

```txt
Tools Hub
  -> specific free check
  -> gap report
  -> create/import MenuList customer link
  -> owner Business Health/Public Discovery readiness
  -> future paid recurrence/history/multi-location/agency reporting
```
