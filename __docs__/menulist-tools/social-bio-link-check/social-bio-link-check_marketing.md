# Social Bio Link Consistency Check - Marketing Notes

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

## Positioning

Social Bio Link Consistency Check is a small public tool for owners who are not sure whether customers see the same current link across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google, their website, QR codes, and printed materials.

Use it as a setup clarity conversation, not an SEO or social growth pitch.

## Owner Promise

```txt
Find where your public links may be scattered, then point customers to one current MenuList link.
```

## Good Fit

- restaurant with Instagram and QR codes
- salon using WhatsApp and Instagram
- clinic with Google profile and website links
- retail/catalog business with old campaign links
- agency cleaning up local SMB profiles
- multi-location owner preparing a profile-link checklist

## Do Not Say

- "We scanned your social profiles"
- "We checked your Google profile"
- "We updated your bio links"
- "This improves ranking"
- "This improves AI visibility"
- "This guarantees citations"

## Say Instead

- "This is an owner-confirmed checklist."
- "The report shows which placements you said already point to the current link."
- "MenuList gives you the one customer link to place in those public spots."
- "No external profile is inspected or changed by this free check."
