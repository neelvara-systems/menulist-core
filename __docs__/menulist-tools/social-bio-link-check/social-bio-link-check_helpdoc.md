# Social Bio Link Consistency Check - Help Doc Draft

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

## What This Tool Does

This tool helps you check whether your social bios, public profiles, website link, QR codes, and printed materials point customers to the same current customer link.

## What You Enter

- your business name
- your city or area
- your current customer link
- the places where you have already placed that link
- whether old links have been removed
- whether customers can clearly take the next action

## What The Report Means

- **Ready:** the link, placements, cleanup, and customer action look consistent from your answers.
- **Missing basics:** the current link, placement, or customer action is missing.
- **Needs cleanup:** the link exists, but old links or limited placements may still confuse customers.
- **Not checked:** external profiles and pages were not inspected.

## What This Tool Does Not Do

It does not open, fetch, inspect, verify, store, or update social profiles, websites, Google profiles, QR destinations, print materials, search results, or AI answers.

## Best Next Step

Create or review one current MenuList customer link, then use that same link in your public profiles, social bios, QR codes, print materials, and customer messages.
