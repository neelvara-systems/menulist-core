# One Customer Link Preview - Help Doc

One Customer Link Preview checks whether one public customer link contains the basics customers need.

## What You Enter

- business name
- city or area
- business type
- current customer link
- which facts are visible on that link

## What The Report Shows

- whether the link looks ready from your entered details
- which facts are missing or unclear
- what was checked
- what was not checked
- the next MenuList action

## What It Does Not Do

The tool does not open your link, scan your website, inspect Google, inspect social profiles, store the report, or update anything outside MenuList.

## Best Next Step

If the report shows missing basics, create one current MenuList customer link and use it from QR, Google, WhatsApp, social bios, and printed material.
