# One Customer Link Preview - Marketing Notes

**Public name:** One Customer Link Preview
**Internal positioning:** The simplest way to show why MenuList should become the business's current customer source.

## Owner-Safe Pitch

"Paste the link you currently share with customers and mark what they can clearly see. MenuList will show whether the link has the basics customers need before they call, visit, order, book, or ask a question."

## What It Helps Sell

- one current customer link
- Official Business Page readiness
- QR/share readiness
- cleanup from scattered PDFs, social bios, and screenshots
- setup help for non-technical SMB owners

## Do Not Say

- "We scanned your website"
- "We checked your Google ranking"
- "We improve your AI visibility"
- "We update your external profiles"
- "We guarantee citations"

## Best SMB Use Cases

- Owner has a Google profile but no clean customer link
- Owner shares a PDF or social bio as the main public source
- Agency needs a before/after setup report
- Multi-location owner wants consistent branch link checks later
