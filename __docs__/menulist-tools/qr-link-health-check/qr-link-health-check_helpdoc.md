# QR Link Health Check - Help Doc

**Status:** Implemented - V0 public route
**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026
**Audience:** Owners and support

---

## What It Does

QR Link Health Check helps you check the link your QR code opens.

It shows whether the entered link looks ready, missing, unclear, or needs review before customers use it.

---

## How To Use It

1. Scan your QR code with your phone camera.
2. Open the link from the QR.
3. Copy the URL.
4. Paste it into QR Link Health Check.
5. Select what customers can see and do from that link.
6. Run the check.

---

## What It Checks

- Whether a QR target URL was entered.
- Whether the URL uses a public HTTPS address.
- Whether the URL appears to be a MenuList customer link.
- Whether you marked the target as current.
- Whether customers can act from the target.
- Whether the printed QR context is clear.

---

## What It Does Not Check

- It does not decode QR images.
- It does not open the target page.
- It does not check Google, Instagram, WhatsApp, or other profiles.
- It does not track QR scans.
- It does not change external platforms.
- It does not promise search ranking, AI visibility, or more orders.

Local, private, insecure, and credentialed targets are treated as missing basics because customers should not receive QR codes that point to non-public or unsafe links.

---

## What To Do With The Report

If the report says the QR is missing basics or unclear, create one current MenuList customer link and use that link when replacing the QR target.

If the report says manual review is needed, review the current QR target before printing more signs, flyers, cards, or table materials.

---

## Data And Privacy

The report runs in your browser. MenuList does not store the report unless you submit the follow-up form and agree to be contacted.
