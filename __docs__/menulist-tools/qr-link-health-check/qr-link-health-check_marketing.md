# QR Link Health Check - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Implemented - V0 public browser-local checker
**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026
**Audience:** Founder, sales, support, website

---

## Positioning

QR Link Health Check is a simple front door into MenuList:

```txt
Check the URL your QR opens.
See what is missing.
Replace it with one current customer link.
```

The tool should feel practical, not technical. The owner should not need to know what a schema, crawler, scan log, or AI visibility score is.

---

## Primary Message

Your QR code should open one clear, current customer link.

---

## Support Points

- Works from the URL the owner already gets after scanning the QR.
- Does not need access to Google, Instagram, WhatsApp, or the owner's website.
- Shows whether the target is missing, unclear, external, or ready to replace with MenuList.
- Routes the fix to creating one current customer link.

---

## Good-Fit Segments

| Segment | Why it converts |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurants | Table/counter QR codes are common and owners understand QR failure quickly |
| Salons and clinics | Booking/contact QR codes often drift across old pages and profiles |
| Retail and service shops | Flyers, packaging, and counter cards often point to stale pages |
| Agencies/freelancers | A clear QR gap report helps sell setup/cleanup work |
| Multi-location SMBs | Old branch QR codes create branch-level inconsistency |

---

## Do Not Say

- "We scanned your QR."
- "We checked every scan."
- "We inspected your website."
- "We fixed your Google profile."
- "Improve your rankings."
- "Improve your AI visibility."
- "Guaranteed more orders."

---

## Lead Quality Signals

| Signal | Lead quality |
| --- | --- |
| Owner pastes an old PDF/menu URL | High |
| Owner marks replacement needed | High |
| Owner selects multi-location or branch context in free text/follow-up | Very high |
| Owner submits contact after report | High |
| Owner only opens the page without report | Low |

Do not store extra lead scoring state in V0. If needed, infer from consented contact text in existing lead review only.

---

## Sales Follow-Up Frame

Use calm, direct language:

```txt
Your QR target can be replaced with one current MenuList customer link.
```

Avoid fear-based language. The problem is setup clarity, not SEO panic.
