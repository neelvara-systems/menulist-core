# Photo Gap Check - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal positioning
**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

---

## One-Line Pitch

Check whether customers can recognize and trust the business from the visuals on its current public source.

---

## Why SMB Owners Care

Photos make a business feel real before a customer visits, books, orders, or messages.

Customers look for:

- logo or brand mark
- storefront, location, team, or work photo
- product, menu item, service, or catalogue photos
- cover image
- a current customer link where these visuals live

MenuList turns that into a simple report and routes the fix into one current customer link.

---

## Positioning

Use:

- "visual basics"
- "public photo gaps"
- "current customer link"
- "checked selected facts only"
- "images were not uploaded or analyzed"

Avoid:

- "AI photo score"
- "brand quality score"
- "ranking opportunity"
- "traffic loss"
- "verified Google photos"
- "automatic external updates"

---

## Sales Talking Points

| Owner concern | Calm response |
| --- | --- |
| "My photos are already on Instagram." | V0 does not inspect Instagram. It shows whether the visual basics you select are ready for one current customer link. |
| "Will this judge my photo quality?" | No. It checks coverage, not quality or style. |
| "Can I upload photos here?" | Not in V0. Upload and storage belong in the approved MenuList setup or owner page flow. |
| "Will this update my Google profile?" | No. It reports selected facts and routes the fix into MenuList. |

---

## Packaging

| Lane | Position |
| --- | --- |
| V0 | Free public lead magnet |
| V1 | Included owner readiness check inside OBP readiness, Business Health, or Public Discovery |
| V2 | Paid recurring visual coverage, history, multi-location, and agency reports |

Paid value must come from recurrence, history, branch consistency, or reporting. Do not sell a better one-time check.
