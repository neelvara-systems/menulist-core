# Google Profile Basics Checklist - Help Doc

Google Profile Basics Checklist shows whether the Google Business Profile facts you maintain are ready for customers and connected to one current MenuList customer link.

## What It Checks

- profile access is claimed or verified
- business name and area are clear
- category is set
- address or service area is clear
- hours are current
- contact and website/customer link are present
- menu/service/catalog/rate-card link is present
- order, booking, reservation, enquiry, or visit action is present where needed
- photos are present

## What It Does Not Check

- It does not open Google.
- It does not inspect Google Maps.
- It does not inspect Google Search.
- It does not update a Google Business Profile.
- It does not check rankings.
- It does not inspect reviews.

## Best Fix

Create one current MenuList customer link, then place that link in the profile fields the business already maintains.
