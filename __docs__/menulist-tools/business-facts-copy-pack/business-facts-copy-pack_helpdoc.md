# Business Facts Copy Pack - Help Doc

**Status:** Implemented V0 public browser-local tool
**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026

---

## What This Tool Does

Business Facts Copy Pack turns the details you enter into reusable copy for public profiles, WhatsApp, social bios, website snippets, staff replies, and customer link sharing.

## What You Enter

- business name
- city or area
- business type
- what customers can get
- short description
- hours
- location or service area
- phone or WhatsApp
- current customer link
- booking, order, or enquiry link
- preferred customer action

## What You Receive

- a short report showing what is present, missing, or unclear
- copy blocks you can copy one by one
- a full report you can copy or download
- a public report link you can share
- an optional follow-up form if you want MenuList to contact you

## What Is Not Checked

The tool does not open your website, Google profile, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, maps, or directories. It does not update external platforms. It does not use AI or search providers.

Entered links are checked only for local URL format.

## Where The Fix Leads

If the facts need one stable customer-facing home, use the next action to create a MenuList customer link.
