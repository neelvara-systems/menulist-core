# Business Facts Copy Pack - Internal Marketing Notes

**Status:** Implemented V0 public browser-local tool
**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026
**Audience:** Internal marketing, sales, support

---

## Positioning

Business Facts Copy Pack is a small public MenuList tool for owners who keep rewriting the same business details across public profiles, social bios, WhatsApp replies, staff messages, and website snippets.

The message:

```txt
Enter the facts once. Get copy blocks you can reuse. Publish one current customer link when the facts need a stable home.
```

## Use This Language

- "Turn entered business facts into reusable copy."
- "Copy for public profiles, WhatsApp, social bios, website snippets, staff replies, and customer links."
- "Checked entered facts only."
- "No external profile is opened."
- "Use one current MenuList customer link as the stable source."

## Do Not Say

- "We updated Google."
- "We checked your Instagram."
- "WhatsApp is fixed."
- "SEO score."
- "AI visibility."
- "Ranking improvement."
- "More orders."
- "Automated profile management."

## Best CTA

```txt
Create one current customer link
```

Secondary CTA:

```txt
Ask MenuList to set this up
```

## Lead Quality Signals

High-intent signals:

- owner enters a business description
- owner enters offer/menu/service summary
- current customer link is missing
- owner provides phone/WhatsApp/email with consent
- agency or multi-location wording appears in the follow-up message

V0 should not silently score or store these signals outside the existing consented contact path.
