# Shareable Tool Reports - Internal Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal positioning
**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026

---

## Positioning

MenuList Tools produce a useful report, not a vanity score.

Approved framing:

> Run a free check, share the report, and turn the gaps into a MenuList setup path.

Use this for public truth and setup gaps:

- QR link broken or unclear
- social profiles point to old links
- menu/service text is hard to understand
- prices or availability are unclear
- hours or holiday hours are missing
- photos are incomplete

---

## What To Avoid

Do not market this as:

- AI visibility scoring
- SEO ranking audit
- competitor spy tool
- reputation management
- social posting automation
- external profile monitoring
- guaranteed traffic or citations

---

## Conversion Logic

The report should make the next MenuList action obvious:

```txt
Gap in public source
-> one current customer link
-> owner-approved MenuList truth
-> optional paid recurring/multi-location/agency work later
```

V0 is not a paid wall. Paid packaging only makes sense when recurrence, saved history, multi-location control, agency export, or done-for-you repair is real.
