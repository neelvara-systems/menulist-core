# Menu Readability Check - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Implemented - V0 public browser-local checker
**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026
**Audience:** Founder, sales, support, website

---

## Positioning

Menu Readability Check is a simple source-cleanup front door into MenuList:

```txt
Paste the menu or service text.
See what customers may miss.
Turn it into one current customer link.
```

The tool should feel practical and owner-friendly. It should not sound like SEO, AI optimization, or copywriting software.

---

## Primary Message

Customers should understand what you sell, what it costs, and how to act.

---

## Support Points

- Works from pasted text the owner already has.
- Does not need access to Google, Instagram, WhatsApp, or the owner's website.
- Shows whether categories, items/services, prices, details, action, and link are clear.
- Routes the fix to creating one current customer link.

---

## Good-Fit Segments

| Segment | Why it converts |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurants and cafes | Menus often exist as long WhatsApp text, screenshots, PDFs, or old pages |
| Salons and clinics | Packages and rates need clearer grouping and action paths |
| Repair, rental, retail, and service shops | Service/rate lists are often unstructured and hard to scan |
| Agencies/freelancers | The report helps explain setup cleanup work without fear language |

---

## Do Not Say

- "We rewrote your menu."
- "We scanned your website."
- "We read your PDF."
- "Improve your rankings."
- "Improve your AI visibility."
- "Guaranteed more orders."

---

## Sales Follow-Up Frame

Use calm, direct language:

```txt
Your current list can become one clearer MenuList customer link.
```

Avoid fear-based language. The problem is clarity and setup, not marketing panic.
