# Maps Place Check - Help Doc

**Status:** Internal draft. Do not publish as a public help article until UI exists.

## What It Does

MenuList can check whether a business appears to match a public Google Maps place and show the details that may need review.

## What It Does Not Do

- It does not change your business details automatically.
- It does not change your Google profile.
- It does not update prices, menus, ratings, reviews, or availability.
- It does not guarantee Google will show changes immediately.

## Review Rule

If MenuList finds a difference, the owner or admin must confirm before any
MenuList business detail changes. Confirmation UI is not released while the
provider and duplicate-location safety gates remain pending.
