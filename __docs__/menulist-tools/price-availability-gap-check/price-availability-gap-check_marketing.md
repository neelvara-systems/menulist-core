# Price Availability Gap Check - Marketing Notes

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

**Audience:** Internal sales, founder-led outreach, partner conversations
**Public name:** Price Availability Gap Check

## Positioning

Price Availability Gap Check is a practical public truth tool:

> Customers should not have to ask what costs what, what is available, or how to get a quote.

It converts messy menu, service, catalog, package, and price-list sources into a simple gap report and then routes the owner toward one current MenuList customer link.

## Good Outreach Angle

Use plain SMB language:

- "Are your prices clear before customers message you?"
- "Can customers tell what is currently available?"
- "Are sold-out, seasonal, or quote-based items clear?"
- "Do customers have one current link for prices and actions?"

## Avoid

- AI visibility score
- SEO audit
- price ranking
- competitor pricing
- live inventory claim
- POS integration claim
- ordering-provider check claim

## Conversion Path

Free check -> gap report -> create/import/fix MenuList customer link -> owner Business Health readiness -> paid recurring/multi-location/agency reporting only when needed.
