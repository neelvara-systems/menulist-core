# WhatsApp Reply Pack - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal notes for implemented V0  
**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026

---

## Positioning

WhatsApp Reply Pack helps an owner prepare clearer replies from their own business facts.

Use it for SMBs that receive repeated WhatsApp questions about hours, menus, services, prices, delivery, pickup, booking, orders, or the current customer link.

## Approved Promise

Create reusable WhatsApp replies from owner-entered facts without sending messages or calling WhatsApp APIs.

## Do Not Say

- "We send WhatsApp messages for you"
- "We verified your WhatsApp account"
- "AI wrote your replies"
- "We checked your website"
- "We updated Google, Instagram, or WhatsApp"
- "This guarantees more orders"
- "This improves ranking or AI visibility"

## Conversion Path

```txt
WhatsApp reply gap
  -> reusable replies
  -> current customer link
  -> MenuList setup or follow-up
```

MenuList remains the current public source. WhatsApp remains a customer action surface.
