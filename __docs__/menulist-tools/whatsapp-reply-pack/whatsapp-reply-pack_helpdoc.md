# WhatsApp Reply Pack - Help Doc

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

WhatsApp Reply Pack creates reusable reply text from the facts you enter.

It can help you prepare replies for:

- greetings
- hours
- menu or service questions
- prices and payment
- orders or bookings
- delivery or pickup
- fallback replies
- your current customer link

It does not send messages, verify your WhatsApp number, open WhatsApp, inspect links, or update any external platform.

If your replies depend on current menu, service, price, or hours information, the next step is to create or fix one current customer link in MenuList.
