**Status:** Help evidence; not current support-publication approval
**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026

---

## What It Does

Customer Question Coverage Check helps you review whether common customer questions are already answered in your public information.

## What It Does Not Do

- It does not open or scan your website.
- It does not read customer chats.
- It does not call AI providers.
- It does not generate chatbot answers.
- It does not update external platforms.

## How To Use It

1. Enter your business details.
2. Answer the coverage questions.
3. Review the evidence shown in each row.
4. Copy or download the report.
5. Use the MenuList next action if you want help fixing missing public answers.
