# Customer Question Coverage Check - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal positioning evidence; not current launch certification
**Last Updated:** July 2, 2026

---

## Positioning

Use calm, bounded language:

- Check whether common customer questions are visibly answered.
- See what is clear and what is missing.
- Route missing answers back into MenuList's canonical business truth layer.

## Do Not Claim

- Guaranteed ranking.
- Guaranteed citation.
- Guaranteed AI visibility.
- Generated chatbot answers.
- Scanned website or chat logs.
- External platform updates.

## Safe One-Liner

Check whether your public information answers the questions customers ask before they visit.
