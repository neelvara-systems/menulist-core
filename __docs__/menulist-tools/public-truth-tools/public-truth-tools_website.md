## Problem Statement

Customers often find old menus, missing prices, unclear hours, broken QR links, or different business details on different pages. The owner may know the correct information, but the public version is scattered.

When the public source is unclear, customers hesitate or ask again.

---

## Solution Statement

MenuList Public Truth Tools check the basics that customers need: menu or service list, hours, location, contact, links, and photos.

The report shows what is present, what is missing, and what can be turned into one current customer link.

## How It Works

1. **Add the source** - Enter your public link or current menu/service list.
2. **Get the report** - MenuList marks the clear, missing, and unclear facts.
3. **Create one link** - Turn the approved source into one customer link.

Shared report links are browser-local self-reports, not digitally verified records. Readers must confirm owner-entered facts before changing MenuList truth.

## FAQ

- **Q:** Does this guarantee better ranking?
  **A:** No. The check only shows whether your public facts are clear and usable.

- **Q:** Does this update Google or Instagram?
  **A:** No. It prepares a clearer source and link. You choose where to place that link.

- **Q:** Is this only for restaurants?
  **A:** No. It also works for service lists, packages, catalogs, rate cards, and price lists.

- **Q:** Will the tool post anything for me?
  **A:** No. Public Truth Tools do not post to external platforms.
