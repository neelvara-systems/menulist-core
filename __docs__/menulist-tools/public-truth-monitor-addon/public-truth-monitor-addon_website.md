## Disallowed Copy

- "Improve AI visibility"
- "Rank higher on Google"
- "Fix your reputation"
- "Automate your social presence"
- "We update Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook for you"
- "Guaranteed bookings, calls, orders, or visits"
