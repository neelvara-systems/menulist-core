# Public Truth Monitor Add-On - Marketing

**Last Updated:** July 4, 2026
**Status:** Internal positioning only

---

## Positioning

Public Truth Monitor is paid follow-up work for businesses that need public facts to stay current after setup.

Use:

> Keep your public business facts reviewed across locations and reports.

Avoid:

- AI SEO
- ranking monitor
- visibility score
- reputation automation
- social engagement
- guaranteed traffic

## When To Offer

Offer only when one of these signals exists:

- multiple locations
- agency or freelancer managing clients
- repeated seasonal or holiday updates
- frequent menu/service/price changes
- owner asks for monthly report
- owner asks MenuList to help repair public source material

## Packaging

| Package | Buyer | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly Public Truth Report | Single serious owner | Saved monthly status and fix list |
| Multi-location Consistency Pack | Branch/franchise operator | Compare public readiness across locations |
| Agency Setup Report Pack | Agency/partner | Exportable client report |
| Managed Public Source Repair | Non-technical owner | Owner-approved setup/repair work |

## Boundary Language

Say:

- "We check the MenuList truth you approved."
- "The report shows what changed and what needs review."
- "Every gap maps to a MenuList fix path."

Do not say:

- "We improve Google ranking."
- "We make ChatGPT cite you."
- "We update external profiles automatically."
- "We post on your behalf."
