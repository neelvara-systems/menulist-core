# Public Truth Monitor Add-On - Help Doc

**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026
**Status:** Owner help draft for implemented paid runtime

---

## What It Is

Public Truth Monitor is a paid add-on for owners or partners who want saved reports for their MenuList public facts.

It can help review:

- business details
- customer link
- menu or service list
- prices and availability
- hours
- action links
- WhatsApp readiness
- photos
- QR/print readiness
- Google profile handoff status

## What It Does Not Do

It does not:

- update external websites or profiles automatically
- send WhatsApp messages
- post on social media
- promise search ranking
- promise AI citations
- replace owner approval

## Current Availability

Entitled owners can open Business Health and use Public truth history to save the latest report, keep a capped history, and download a text report.

The report uses MenuList store and menu facts only. It does not inspect external websites, social profiles, Google profiles, AI answers, search rankings, QR scans, or third-party platforms.
