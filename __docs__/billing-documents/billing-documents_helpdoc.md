# Billing Documents Owner Guide

> Status: Product guidance; issuance remains operator-gated
> Last updated: August 23, 2026

## Where are my invoices?

Open **Billing** and then **Billing history**. Use the document action beside a payment to download its MenuList tax invoice. Refund credit notes appear as separate credited entries.

When billing-document delivery is enabled, MenuList can send the PDF by email, WhatsApp, or both, based on the billing owner's notification setting. Each message also includes the private MenuList document link as a fallback. WhatsApp requires a verified number, explicit consent, and the approved document template. You must sign in to use the fallback link.

## Why might I also see a Razorpay receipt?

Razorpay processes the payment and may provide a provider receipt. The MenuList tax invoice or credit note is the accounting document generated from the billing details approved at checkout.

## Can I edit an issued invoice?

No. Issued documents preserve the original transaction. Contact support if billing details were wrong; any legally valid correction must use the reviewed credit-note/reissue process rather than overwriting history.

## Can staff download documents?

Owners and staff roles with subscription-management permission can access documents for the currently selected store. Other staff cannot. Billing documents are private account files; they are not public links and never appear on customer-facing menu surfaces.
