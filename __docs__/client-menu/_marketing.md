## Feature Narrative

### Why This Matters to SMB Owners

Restaurant owners don't have time to manage marketing. They're busy cooking, serving, and surviving.

**The old way:**

- Print new menus when prices change (expensive, slow)
- Post on Instagram hoping customers see it
- Answer "Is this available?" 50 times a day
- Watch customers stare at the menu for 2 minutes

**The MenuListAi way:**

- Update menu from your phone → customers see it instantly
- Sold out? One tap. Customers see it immediately.
- Popular items show up first → customers decide in 15 seconds
- Menu switches from breakfast to lunch automatically

**The result:** Customers order faster. Staff answers fewer questions. Owners do less work.

## Sales Talking Points

### Objection: "This is just a digital menu"

**Response:**

> "A digital menu shows items. MenuListAi decides which items to show first based on what's actually popular. It's the difference between a sign and a salesperson."

---

### Objection: "We already have a QR menu"

**Response:**

> "Does your QR menu update the moment you mark something sold out? Does it show customers what's popular today? Does it switch from breakfast to lunch automatically? That's what makes this different."

---

### Objection: "Our customers don't use phones"

**Response:**

> "If they have a phone, they can scan a QR. No app download, no login. And for customers who prefer paper, you can still have both. But 80% of customers now prefer scanning."

---

### Objection: "Will this really help our sales?"

**Response:**

> "Customers who see recommendations order faster and try new things. We don't promise specific numbers because every restaurant is different. But the pattern is clear: less confusion, more orders."

---

### Objection: "It's too expensive"

**Response:**

> "₹999/month is less than the cost of reprinting your menu once. And you'll never pay for reprinting again. Plus, your staff saves time not answering availability questions."

## Approved Language (From Strategy)

### Terms to Use

| Approved                       | Why                               |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| "Live menu"                    | Implies real-time, modern         |
| "Instant updates"              | Clear benefit                     |
| "Smart recommendations"        | Implies intelligence without "AI" |
| "Customers see it immediately" | Outcome-focused                   |
| "One tap"                      | Simplicity                        |
| "Runs itself"                  | Zero effort promise               |

### Terms to Avoid

| Forbidden                  | Why                          |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| ❌ "AI-powered"            | Sounds cold, intimidating    |
| ❌ "Machine learning"      | Tech jargon                  |
| ❌ "Algorithm"             | Scary word                   |
| ❌ "Recommendation engine" | Too complex                  |
| ❌ "Confidence scores"     | Internal metric              |
| ❌ "Real-time analytics"   | Owner doesn't want analytics |
| ❌ "Dashboard"             | Sounds like work             |
