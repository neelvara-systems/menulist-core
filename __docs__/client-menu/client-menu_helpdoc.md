# Client Menu (Digital Menu) — Help Documentation

## Quick Summary

Your digital menu is what customers see when they scan your QR code. It shows your menu items, prices, and categories from your latest published MenuList data, on any phone.

---

## Getting Started

> **Terminology:** In MenuList, your menus are called **"Projects"** in the dashboard. When this guide says "menu", we mean the project you see in the **Projects** section of your sidebar.

### Prerequisites

- A MenuList account with at least one store
- At least one menu project created (with items and categories)

### First-Time Setup

1. Go to **Projects** in your dashboard
2. Click **Create New Project** and give it a name (e.g., "Food Menu")
3. Upload your menu (photo, PDF, or enter items manually)
4. Wait for the system to extract your items — this takes about 30 seconds
5. Review and edit your items in the Editor
6. Click **Preview** to see how customers will see it
7. Your QR code is ready — print it and place it on tables!

> 💡 **Tip:** Your first project is automatically set as the default menu. Customers scanning your QR code will see this one.

## How-To Guides

### How to update a menu item price

1. Go to **Projects** → select your menu project
2. Find the item you want to change
3. Click on the price and type the new amount
4. Click **Save**
5. Customers see the new price within seconds

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Editor view with price field highlighted

### How to mark an item as sold out

1. Go to **Projects** → select your menu project
2. Find the item that is sold out
3. Toggle the **Available** switch to OFF
4. Click **Save**
5. The item will appear faded on the customer menu

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Available toggle on an item

### How to add a new menu item

1. Go to **Projects** → select your menu project
2. Click **Add Item** in the category where it belongs
3. Fill in: name, price, description (optional), image (optional)
4. Click **Save**
5. The new item appears on your customer menu immediately

### How to change your menu's look and feel

1. Go to **Projects** → select your menu project
2. Click **Preview** tab
3. Use the design controls to change:
   - **Colors** — accent color, background
   - **Layout** — vertical list or horizontal tabs
   - **Style** — mood/theme of the menu
4. Click **Save** when happy with the look

### How to set up your QR code

1. Your QR code is automatically created when you create a store
2. Your menu URL is: `yourstore.menulist.ai`
3. Print the QR code and place it where customers can scan
4. Anyone scanning it sees your live menu

> 💡 **Tip:** You can use your own domain (like `menu.yourrestaurant.com`) — ask support to set this up.

### How to have multiple menus (food, drinks, etc.)

1. Go to **Projects**
2. Click **Create New Project** for each menu (e.g., "Drinks", "Desserts")
3. Each menu gets its own URL: `yourstore.menulist.ai/drinks`
4. Set one as **Default** — this is what customers see when they scan without a specific link

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

### My menu is not showing the latest changes

**Why this happens:** Changes take a few seconds to appear. The system caches your menu for up to 60 seconds to keep it fast.
**How to fix it:**

1. Wait 60 seconds and refresh the page
2. If still not updated, check that you clicked **Save** in the editor
   > If this doesn't work, contact support — there may be a cache issue.

### Customers see "Menu Not Found"

**Why this happens:** Your store or project may not be active, or the QR code points to a wrong URL.
**How to fix it:**

1. Check that your store is set to **Active** in Store Settings
2. Check that your project is set to **Active** (not deleted)
3. Verify the QR code URL matches your store subdomain
4. If using a custom domain, check that domain verification is complete

### The menu loads slowly

**Why this happens:** Large images or slow internet on the customer's phone.
**How to fix it:**

1. Reduce image sizes in the editor (the system compresses automatically, but very large originals take longer)
2. The menu is optimized for mobile — it should load in under 2 seconds on a normal connection
3. The menu works offline after the first load

### Items are showing in the wrong order

**Why this happens:** Items display in the order they appear in your editor.
**How to fix it:**

1. Go to the Editor
2. Drag items to reorder them within their category
3. Click **Save**

## Tips & Best Practices

- 💡 Keep menu item names short (under 40 characters) — they read better on phones
- 💡 Add images to your top 5-10 items — items with images get more attention
- 💡 Use categories to group items (Starters, Main Course, Drinks) — customers find things faster
- 💡 Mark sold-out items instead of removing them — customers can see what you usually offer
- 💡 Check your menu on your own phone after making changes — see what customers see
