# MyCodex PWA Shell Help

## What Changed

MyCodex now leaves room for iPhone and iPad PWA safe areas:

- status bar
- notch
- home indicator
- landscape side insets

## What To Check

Open MyCodex from the iPhone home screen and confirm:

1. The top header is not hidden under the status bar.
2. The settings drawer title and close button are visible.
3. The navigation drawer title and search input are visible.
4. The scroll-to-top button and audio mini-player sit above the home indicator.

## Limits

This is visual safe-area handling. It does not make audio continue after the app is closed or the phone is locked.
