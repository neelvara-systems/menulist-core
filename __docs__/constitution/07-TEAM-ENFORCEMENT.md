# MenuList Team Enforcement & Founder Oath

**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** 🔒 BINDING — APPLIES TO ALL TEAM MEMBERS  
**Authority:** Maximum — Including Founder

---

## Core Truth

> **Infrastructure survives failure. It does not survive denial.**

Most systems fail because their creators can't stop helping.  
This document ensures MenuList is protected from its own team.

---

# PART 1: Founder Oath

## The Oath (Non-Negotiable)

I acknowledge that MenuList is not a product I continuously improve,  
but an autonomous system I am temporarily responsible for stewarding.

**I swear that:**

1. I will **not interfere** with MenuList's decisions once AutoMode is active, even when I disagree, feel uncomfortable, or see short-term loss.

2. I will **not tune, tweak, or "slightly adjust"** thresholds, logic, or behavior to satisfy customers, investors, advisors, or my own instincts.

3. I will **treat silence as success**, and boredom as proof of correctness.

4. I will **accept refusal** as a valid and often superior outcome to action.

5. I will **not demand explanations** from the system, nor force it to justify itself in human language.

6. I will **respect the audit checker** as final authority, even when it blocks progress.

7. I understand that **overriding the system once** permanently weakens MenuList's claim to autonomy.

8. I accept that **my role is not to be right** — but to get out of the way once the system earns authority.

# PART 2: Team Enforcement Clause

## Scope of Authority

No individual — including the founder — has unilateral authority to:

- Change decision logic
- Adjust confidence thresholds
- Add or remove telemetry
- Bypass refusal rules
- Override AutoMode constraints

**Authority belongs to the system law, not people.**

---

## Definition of a Violation

A violation is any action that:

- Alters MenuList behavior outside defined governance
- Responds to pressure instead of invariants
- Treats owner behavior as preference instead of resistance
- Makes the system "look smarter" at the cost of calmness

**Intent does not matter. Outcome does not matter. Only the invariant matters.**

---

## Mandatory Violation Handling

If a violation occurs:

1. It **must be logged immutably**
2. The violated invariant **must be named**
3. AutoMode **must be marked BREACHED** if applicable
4. **No retroactive justification** is allowed

> Silence or concealment is a greater breach than the violation itself.

---

## Protection Clause

Any team member who:

- Refuses to implement a violating request
- Blocks a "quick fix"
- Escalates a governance breach

**Is protected, not punished.**

> Retaliation against governance enforcement is grounds for removal.

---

## Removal Clause

Any individual who:

- Repeatedly violates system law
- Attempts to bypass the audit checker
- Treats MenuList as a conventional SaaS
- Optimizes for optics over invariants

Must be removed from decision-making authority. **This includes the founder.**

> Infrastructure does not survive ego.

# PART 3: Forbidden Promises

These statements may **NEVER** be made to customers:

| ❌ Forbidden Promise                           | Why It Kills Authority        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| "We can customize the recommendations for you" | Creates dual authority        |
| "You can control what appears on the screen"   | Undermines intelligence       |
| "We can add that feature for you"              | Promises non-existent roadmap |
| "The AI learns YOUR preferences"               | Wrong mental model            |
| "You can turn off the AI recommendations"      | Makes the core optional       |
| "We'll add a dashboard for that"               | Dashboard creep               |
| "We guarantee X% increase in sales"            | Unverifiable ROI promise      |

### Correct Response

> "MenuList works best when it runs quietly. The system learns from your customers, not your preferences."

# PART 4: Forbidden Actions

These actions may **NEVER** be taken — even under pressure:

| ❌ Forbidden Action                                      | Consequence               |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Manually override a recommendation for specific customer | Fire offense              |
| Promise a feature on the roadmap                         | Requires founder approval |
| Share internal confidence scores                         | Creates false precision   |
| Disable AI recommendations                               | Removes core value        |
| Commit to a timeline for any fix                         | Only founder commits      |

# PART 5: Escalation Rules

### When to Escalate to Founder

| Situation                                   | Escalate?            |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Customer asks for feature not on roadmap    | ✅ Yes               |
| Customer offers 10x price for custom work   | ✅ Yes               |
| Customer is enterprise/chain (5+ locations) | ✅ Yes               |
| Customer threatens public complaint         | ✅ Yes               |
| Customer found a real bug                   | ⚠️ Engineering first |
| Customer wants discount                     | ❌ No — answer is no |
| Customer wants explanation of AI            | ❌ No — use redirect |

### How to Escalate

1. Document the exact request
2. Document your redirect response
3. Document customer reaction
4. Send: "Request that exceeds my authority. Awaiting decision."

**Do NOT promise "I'll check with my team."**

This is not bureaucracy. This is **survival**.
