# Commercial Identity Help

## Who provides MenuList?

MenuList is operated by Neelvara Systems. Neelvara Systems is the current
operating trade name.

## Who appears on my billing document?

The applicable MenuList billing document shows the verified legal supplier
name, registered address, and tax details for that purchase.

## What does Razorpay do?

Razorpay processes checkout and payment-method details. MenuList records the
payment and prepares the applicable billing document. Contact MenuList billing
support for a duplicate, incorrect, or unrecognised charge.
