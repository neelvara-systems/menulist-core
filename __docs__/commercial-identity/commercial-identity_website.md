# Commercial Identity Website

## Placement

- Footer: one quiet operator sentence.
- Terms: product/operator boundary and billing supplier explanation.
- Privacy: operator identification and separate Razorpay processing boundary.
- Refund Policy: operator identification and payment/billing-record boundary.

## Copy Rules

The site does not publish an unverified registered entity name, address, GSTIN,
or legal suffix. The applicable issued billing document is the source for the
verified supplier identity of a purchase.

The disclosure must remain readable in light/dark themes and on mobile without
turning the footer or legal pages into a parent-company marketing surface.
