# Commercial Identity Marketing

## Approved Public Framing

- `MenuList is operated by Neelvara Systems.`
- MenuList remains the prominent product name and customer-facing identity.
- The operating disclosure stays quiet and factual in legal/footer surfaces.

## Prohibited Framing

- Neelvara Systems Private Limited
- Neelvara Systems LLP or OPC
- registered parent, holding company, or corporation
- Razorpay as the seller of MenuList
- one entity name substituted across product, operator, and supplier roles

Marketing pages should explain product value. They should not repeat the
operator disclosure in promotional sections or use it as a trust claim.
