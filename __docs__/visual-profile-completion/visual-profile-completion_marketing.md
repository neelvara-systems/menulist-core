# Visual Profile Completion Marketing Notes

## Positioning

MenuList helps the owner keep the public business profile complete by showing which visual pieces are missing.

## Approved Copy

Short line:

> MenuList shows when the public profile is missing a main photo, business photos, or a menu photo.

Owner benefit:

> Owners can fix the gap from the same Official Page screen they already use.

## Avoided Claims

Do not claim:

- AI chooses the best photo.
- MenuList automatically classifies every photo.
- MenuList posts to Google, Instagram, Facebook, or WhatsApp.
- MenuList improves ranking.
- MenuList creates campaigns from photos.
- MenuList replaces Canva or social media tools.

## Product Boundary

This is a MenuList public-profile completion feature. Campaign creation, social output selection, asset editing, and marketing exports belong outside this feature boundary.
