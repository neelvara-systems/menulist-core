## Suggested Section

MenuList can send required account notices by email, and by WhatsApp when the notice is urgent or started from a WhatsApp flow. Billing, menu live state, and setup notices use your business settings for dates, time, and currency.

| Benefit | Copy |

## FAQ

### Is this a marketing message system?

No. It is for required account and service notices.

### Will every email also be sent on WhatsApp?

No. Email is the main channel. WhatsApp is reserved for urgent or owner-started messages when consent and provider rules allow it.

### Can I test notifications?

The planned setup includes test sends so owners can confirm the right email or WhatsApp contact is working.
