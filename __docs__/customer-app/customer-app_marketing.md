## Feature Narrative

### The Problem

Restaurant owners know the pain:

- A customer loved the food, said "I'll be back"
- Next time they're hungry, they can't remember the restaurant's name
- They search "pizza near me" and find a competitor
- The original restaurant loses a repeat customer they earned

**The root cause:** No persistent presence on the customer's phone.

### The Solution

MenuList's **Customer App** creates automatic persistence:

1. Customer visits the menu (QR scan, link, Google)
2. After a few visits, we suggest: "Save this menu for faster access"
3. Customer adds to home screen — sees the restaurant's logo and name
4. Now the restaurant has a permanent icon on that customer's phone
5. One tap opens the live menu — no friction, no forgetting

### Why This Matters

For the **restaurant owner:**
- No additional work — it's automatic
- No additional cost — included with MenuList
- Increased repeat visits — reduced friction wins

For the **customer:**
- No app store download — instant
- No registration or account — just the menu
- Always current — syncs with live menu automatically

## Sales Talking Points

### Objection: "This is just a bookmark, right?"

**Response:**
> "No — it's stronger than a bookmark. A bookmark is buried in the browser. This is an app icon on their home screen, next to WhatsApp and Instagram. Customers see it every time they unlock their phone. That's presence, not a bookmark."

### Objection: "My customers aren't tech-savvy."

**Response:**
> "They don't need to be. We show them exactly what to do — 'Tap here, then tap Add.' If they can add a WhatsApp contact to their home screen, they can do this. And once it's done, using it is easier than anything — just tap the icon."

### Objection: "We already use Zomato/Swiggy for orders."

**Response:**
> "Those apps show you next to 100 other restaurants. Your Customer App shows only you. No competition, no commission, no algorithm deciding who sees your menu. Direct relationship with your customer."

### Objection: "Will this actually increase my sales?"

**Response:**
> "We don't promise revenue — we promise reduced friction. Every time a customer has to search, type, or remember, you risk losing them. The app removes all of that. Your repeat customers come back easier. That's the value."

### Objection: "I need to build a real app, not this web thing."

**Response:**
> "Real apps cost ₹5-10 lakh to build and ₹50,000/month to maintain. They need app store approval. Updates take days. This gives you 90% of the value — your icon on their phone, one-tap access — at zero cost and zero maintenance. Start here. Scale up if you outgrow it."

## Approved Language

### Terms to Use

| Approved Term | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| "Customer App" | Frames as product, not technology |
| "Your branded app" | Emphasizes ownership |
| "One tap away" | Simple benefit |
| "Home screen" | Universal concept |
| "No download needed" | Removes friction concern |
| "Automatically" | Reduces perceived effort |
| "Included" | No extra cost implication |

### Terms to Avoid (Forbidden)

| Forbidden Term | Why | Replacement |
|----------------|-----|-------------|
| "PWA" | Technical jargon | "App" or "Customer App" |
| "Progressive Web App" | Technical jargon | "Installable menu" or "Your app" |
| "Add to Home Screen" | Technical instruction | "Save to your phone" or "Get the app" |
| "Web app" | Weakens positioning | "App" or "Customer App" |
| "Browser-based" | Weakens positioning | "Works on any phone" |
| "AI-powered" | Violates Language Governance | Nothing, or "Automatic" |
| "Smart" | Violates Language Governance | Nothing, or "Simple" |
| "Optimizes" | Violates Language Governance | "Handles" |
| "Increases sales" | Unverifiable claim | "Reduces friction" |
| "Conversion" | SaaS jargon | "Repeat visits" |
