## FAQ

### Does Growth Kits post for me?

No. It prepares copy and materials you can review, copy, download, print, or publish yourself.

### Where does the content come from?

From your current MenuList menu, item details, store hours, images, and public link.

### Can I edit the text?

Yes. You can copy the draft and adjust it before posting or sending.

### Does it work on mobile?

Yes. You can copy and share kits from the mobile owner app.

### Can it reply to Google reviews?

Only if the guarded review reply option is enabled for your store. It uses review text you provide. Automatic review access is not part of the initial add-on.

### Will it increase orders?

It helps you keep local updates current and accurate. It does not promise or measure order growth.
