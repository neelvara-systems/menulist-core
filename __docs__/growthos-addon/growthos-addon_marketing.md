# GrowthOS Add-on - Marketing And Packaging Notes

**Status:** Internal sales and positioning notes
**Public name recommendation:** Growth Kits
**Product form:** Paid MenuList add-on

---

## 1. Positioning

GrowthOS should be sold as:

> Ready-to-use growth kits from the menu truth you already keep in MenuList.

The value is not "marketing software." The value is that MenuList already knows what the business can safely say today.

## 2. Customer Promise

Use this promise:

> MenuList prepares accurate messages, captions, and in-store prompts from your live menu so you can share them without starting from a blank page.

Do not promise:

- more orders
- higher revenue
- better ranking
- automatic posting
- social media management
- ad performance
- review growth

## 3. Competitive Frame

| Category | What they do | GrowthOS response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Pomelli | Generates on-brand campaigns from a website and editable assets. | GrowthOS does not start from a website profile. It starts from current MenuList menu truth and produces immediate local action. |
| Canva/Adobe | Broad creative production, design, editing, brand kits, and campaigns. | GrowthOS avoids design canvas workflows and returns finished, short outputs. |
| Social schedulers | Plan, schedule, and post across channels. | GrowthOS does not manage calendars or auto-post. It prepares accurate copy the owner can use now. |
| Local SEO tools | Profile listings, reviews, rankings, reports. | GrowthOS does not sell reports. It turns current business facts into update-ready material. |
| Agencies | Human strategy and managed execution. | GrowthOS can support managed MenuList clients but remains low-touch and truth-grounded. |

## 4. Sales Narrative

Problem:

Owners want to keep their local presence active but do not have time to write accurate updates every day.

Why MenuList:

MenuList already holds the current menu, hours, item details, images, and public link.

Add-on value:

Growth Kits turn those facts into short messages and prompts for WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, staff, and printed surfaces.

Close:

The owner still approves and posts manually. MenuList just removes the blank page and reduces mistakes.

## 5. Packaging

Recommended packages:

| Package | Who | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium included allowance | Higher-tier MenuList client | Limited monthly Growth Kits included as a premium benefit. |
| Growth Kits add-on | Active local business | More kit generations per month plus review reply drafts. |
| Managed Growth Kits | Multi-outlet or premium service client | Operator-assisted kit review and setup, still using MenuList truth as source. |

Do not sell GrowthOS to non-MenuList clients in the current plan.

## 6. Pricing Direction

Pricing should be tied to:

- monthly kit allowance
- image generation allowance if enabled
- review reply allowance
- managed service involvement
- store count

Do not price on:

- promised revenue lift
- social follower growth
- rank improvement
- number of posts automatically published

## 7. Public Language Rules

Use:

- Growth Kits
- ready to share
- from your current menu
- copy, download, print
- owner-approved
- accurate menu details

Avoid:

- AI-powered
- smart
- dynamic
- autonomous marketing
- growth hacking
- campaign optimizer
- ROI dashboard
- guaranteed results
- auto-posting

## 8. Sales Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I already use Canva." | Keep using Canva for design. Growth Kits are for quick, accurate menu-based messages and prompts. |
| "Can it post for me?" | Not in the approved version. It prepares drafts you can copy or publish yourself. |
| "Will this get more orders?" | It helps you keep local updates current. It does not promise or measure order lift. |
| "Can my staff use it?" | Yes, if their MenuList role allows access. Staff lines are one of the intended outputs. |
| "Can it reply to reviews?" | It can draft replies from text you provide. Automatic review ingestion depends on Google access and approval. |

## 9. Launch Readiness

Do not market publicly until:

- feature flag and entitlement gate exist
- owner-facing copy is reviewed
- mobile flow works
- cost model is verified
- no direct posting claim appears
- helpdoc is ready
- support team knows what it does not do
