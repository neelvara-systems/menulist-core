# Growth Kits - Helpdoc Candidate

**Status:** Candidate owner help article
**Feature:** GrowthOS Add-on
**Audience:** MenuList owners and staff with access

---

## What Growth Kits Does

Growth Kits prepares short messages and prompts from your current MenuList menu.

You can use a kit for:

- WhatsApp status or messages
- Instagram captions
- Google Business Profile update drafts
- staff recommendation lines
- printable counter or table prompts
- review reply drafts from text you provide

Growth Kits does not post anything automatically.

## Open Growth Kits

1. Open MenuList.
2. Select `Growth Kits`.
3. Choose the latest suggested kit or create a new one.

If you do not see Growth Kits, your store may not have the add-on enabled.

## Create A Kit

1. Select the action you want to prepare.
2. Review the menu item, hours, or store detail being used.
3. Select `Create kit`.
4. Copy, share, download, or print the output you want.

## Use A WhatsApp Message

1. Open the kit.
2. Select `Copy message`.
3. Paste it into WhatsApp.
4. Review it once before sending.

## Use A Google Update Draft

1. Open the kit.
2. Select `Copy Google update`.
3. Open your Google Business Profile.
4. Paste the draft into a new update.
5. Review and publish it from Google.

Google reviews posts before showing them publicly.

## Use A Staff Line

1. Open the kit.
2. Read the staff line.
3. Share it with your team for the day.

Example:

```txt
If someone asks for a quick snack, mention the paneer roll today.
```

## Use A Review Reply Draft

1. Open the review reply option.
2. Paste the review text.
3. Create a reply draft.
4. Read it before posting.
5. Copy it only if it sounds right for your business.

Do not post a reply if the kit says a public reply is not recommended.

## Old Menu Details Warning

If you see:

```txt
This kit may use old menu details.
```

Create the kit again before using it. This can happen when an item price, availability, hours, or public link changed after the kit was created.

## What Growth Kits Will Not Do

Growth Kits will not:

- publish posts for you
- send WhatsApp messages for you
- schedule social posts
- change your MenuList menu
- change your Google Business Profile
- promise more orders
- show revenue reports

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The item is wrong | Update the item in MenuList, then create the kit again. |
| The price is old | Update the menu price, then create the kit again. |
| The kit is too generic | Choose a more specific menu item or use an item with an image. |
| I cannot create a kit | Check whether Growth Kits is included in your plan or add-on. |
| I cannot use review replies | Review replies may be disabled for your store or require pasted review text. |

## Need Help

Contact support if a kit uses menu details that are already correct in MenuList but still appear wrong in the generated output.
