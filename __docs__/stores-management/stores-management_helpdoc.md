# Stores Management — Help Documentation

**Last Updated:** February 13, 2026

## Quick Summary

Stores represent your physical business locations. Each store has its own QR code, menu URL, and settings. If you have multiple locations, each one is a separate store.

> **Running a chain?** See [Multi-Outlet Consistency help](../multi-outlet-consistency/multi-outlet-consistency_helpdoc.md) for adding outlets and managing menus across locations. See [Multi-Chain Permissions help](../multi-chain-permissions/multi-chain-permissions_helpdoc.md) for controlling what each store can do.

## How-To Guides

### How to view your store settings

1. Go to **Settings** → **Store Settings** (or contact your admin)
2. View your store's: name, address, phone, working hours, subdomain

### How to update your store name and logo

1. Go to **Settings** → **Store Settings**
2. Find the **Basic Information** section
3. Update your store name, description, or business type
4. To change your logo, click the logo area and upload a new image
5. Click **Save**

### How to update your address and contact info

1. Go to **Settings** → **Store Settings**
2. Find the **Location Information** section for your address, city, state, and coordinates
3. Find the **Contact Person** section for your primary contact name, phone, and email
4. Click **Save**

### How to update your store's working hours

1. Go to **Settings** → **Store Settings**
2. Find the **Working Hours** section
3. Set hours for each day of the week
4. Click **Save**
5. Working hours appear on your customer menu and Google listing

### How to set up a custom domain

1. Contact MenuList support with your desired domain
2. We'll configure the DNS settings
3. Once verified, your menu appears at your custom domain
4. Your subdomain (yourstore.menulist.ai) still works as a fallback

### How to update your store's SEO settings

1. Go to **Settings** → **Store Settings**
2. Find the **SEO** section
3. Set: Meta Title, Meta Description, Keywords
4. Click **Save**
5. These appear when your menu is shared on social media or found in search

## Troubleshooting

### My store's QR code isn't working

**Fix:** Check that your store is set to **Active**. Inactive stores return "Menu Not Found."

### Customers see wrong store name

**Fix:** Update the store name in Store Settings and save. Changes reflect within 60 seconds.

- 💡 Add your phone number — it appears in Google search results
- 💡 Set accurate working hours — customers rely on them
