## Problem Statement

Multi-location restaurants struggle to keep menus consistent. Different prices at different locations, outdated hours, wrong phone numbers — it's chaos when managed manually.

## Solution Statement

MenuList gives each location its own store profile with dedicated settings, menus, and QR codes — all managed from one dashboard.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Each Location, Fully Independent

Every store has its own subdomain, working hours, contact info, and menus. Changes at one location don't affect others.

### 2. Centralized Control

See all your locations in one view. Switch between stores instantly. No logging in and out of different accounts.

### 3. Custom Domain Per Location

Each store can have its own branded URL — `downtown.yourrestaurant.com`, `airport.yourrestaurant.com`.
