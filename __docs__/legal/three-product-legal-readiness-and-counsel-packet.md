## 3. India Regulatory Baseline for Counsel Confirmation

This section is an issue list, not a legal conclusion.

- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 has phased commencement. India
  Code currently records commencement on November 13, 2025 for specified
  institutional and miscellaneous provisions, November 13, 2026 for specified
  consent-manager/Data Protection Board provisions, and May 13, 2027 for the
  principal processing obligations. Counsel must confirm the operative duties
  on each launch and contract date.
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 and their corrigendum were
  published by MeitY in November and December 2025. Counsel must map notice,
  consent, rights, grievance, security-safeguard, breach, child-data, retention,
  processor, and cross-border duties to the actual product roles and phased
  commencement.
- The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules,
  2020 may affect online sale of services, disclosures, pricing, refunds,
  grievance handling, and unfair trade practices. Counsel must decide the exact
  applicability to each self-serve product and customer type.
- Section 10A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 recognizes contracts
  formed through electronic means. Counsel must approve clickwrap placement,
  assent evidence, version retention, notice, and contracting authority.
- Counsel must separately consider tax/GST, payment-provider, marketing,
  employment, contractor, intellectual-property, and cross-border requirements.

Official reference points:

- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/22037)
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 — MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa)
- [Consumer Protection materials and E-Commerce Rules — Department of Consumer Affairs](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-and-rules/consumer-protection/consumer-protection)
- [Information Technology Act, 2000 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/18594)

and public media have different operational lifecycles. Eligible deletion or
anonymization remains subject to account, billing, security, dispute, and legal
requirements. Counsel must approve notice language and identify any required
fixed periods; engineering must not invent them.

Answerlattice is governed answer infrastructure for SaaS support. It is not a
helpdesk replacement, autonomous support agent, compliance product, or
unrestricted AI system.

raw answer/search history, selected notification/operational logs,
