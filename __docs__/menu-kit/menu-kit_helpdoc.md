## What is Menu Kit?

Menu Kit gives you everything you need to get customers scanning your menu — print files, social images, and a placement guide. All in one download.

---

## What's inside Menu Kit?

When you download Menu Kit, you get a ZIP file with ready-to-use files:

| File                              | What it is                                  | What to do with it                            |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Table Tent (A5 fold)**          | A card with QR on both sides (fold in half) | Print on A5, fold, place on each table        |
| **Counter Sticker (8×8 cm)**      | A square sticker with your QR code          | Print and stick near your billing counter     |
| **Entrance Poster (A4)**          | A large poster with QR for your entrance    | Print and place at your door or window        |
| **Delivery Bag Sticker (6×6 cm)** | A small sticker for delivery bags           | Print and stick on delivery bags/boxes        |
| **Takeaway Card (business card)** | A small card to drop in takeaway orders     | Print 50–100 cards, drop one per bag          |
| **Instagram Story**               | A ready image for Instagram                 | Post as a story, save as "Menu" highlight     |
| **WhatsApp Status**               | A ready image for WhatsApp                  | Post as your status                           |
| **Google Maps Image**             | A ready image for Google                    | Upload to your Google Business Profile photos |

All files already have your restaurant name, logo when available, brand color, and QR code. No editing needed.

## How to use the print files

### Table Tent (Dual-Sided)

1. Open the file `YourName_TableTent_A6.pdf`
2. Take it to any print shop
3. Ask for: "10 copies, A5 size, 300 GSM paper"
4. Fold each card in half along the dotted line
5. Place one tent on each table (center of table)

Both sides of the tent card show the same QR code — customers can scan from any direction.

**Cost:** Around ₹100–300 for 10 copies

### Entrance Poster

1. Open the file `YourName_EntrancePoster_A4.pdf`
2. Take it to any print shop
3. Ask for: "1 copy, A4 size, 200–300 GSM paper, matte finish"
4. Place it near your entrance or in a window

Customers can check your menu before they even walk in.

### Counter Sticker

1. Open the file `YourName_CounterSticker_8x8.png`
2. Take it to any print shop
3. Ask for: "1 matte sticker, 8×8 cm"
4. Stick it near your billing counter or payment machine

### Delivery Bag Sticker

1. Open the file `YourName_DeliveryBag_6x6.png`
2. Take it to any print shop
3. Ask for: "50–100 matte stickers, 6×6 cm"
4. Stick one on each delivery bag or box before sending out

Customers receiving deliveries can scan to view your menu and reorder.

### Takeaway Card

1. Open the file `YourName_TakeawayCard_85x55.png`
2. Take it to any print shop
3. Ask for: "50–100 cards, business card size (85×55 mm), 250–300 GSM"
4. Drop one card into each takeaway bag

Customers keep the card and can scan later to view your menu from home.

## Staff line

Tell your staff this one line:

> **"Menu? Please scan the QR on the table or at the counter."**

This helps customers use the QR instead of asking for a paper menu.

---

## Where to place QR (quick guide)

- **Tables:** 1 QR per table, center of table
- **Counter:** 1 sticker near the payment machine
- **Entrance:** Optional — 1 poster near the door
- **Delivery:** 1 sticker per delivery bag or box
- **Takeaway:** 1 card per takeaway bag

### Recommended sizes

- Table QR: 5×5 cm minimum
- Counter QR: 8×8 cm
- Poster QR: 12×12 cm

---

## Tips

- Print on **matte paper** (not glossy) — glossy causes glare and QR won't scan well
- Place QR where customers are **already holding their phone** (counter is best)
- The QR always shows your **latest menu** — no need to reprint when you update

---

## QR safety

Your QR codes point to your official menu on MenuList. A few things to keep in mind:

- **Check your QR periodically** — Make sure no one has placed a sticker over your QR code
- **Your QR always shows your name** — Customers can verify it points to your business
- **The link always shows menulist.ai** — If the link looks different, the QR may have been tampered with
- **Report suspicious QR changes** — Contact us if something looks wrong

This is rare, but good to be aware of.
