## Subheadline

---

## What You Get

- **Table card** — Print and place on each table
- **Counter sticker** — Print and stick near your billing counter
- **Instagram story** — Post to your Instagram, save as a "Menu" highlight
- **WhatsApp status** — Share with your customers and staff
- **Google Maps image** — Upload to your Google Business Profile
- **Placement guide** — Shows exactly where to place each QR code
- **Staff line** — One sentence your staff can say when customers ask for the menu

---

## How It Works

1. Open your menu project
2. Click **Share**
3. Click **Download Menu Kit**
4. A ZIP file downloads with all 9 asset files inside
5. Print the table card and sticker at any local print shop
6. Post the social images from your phone

Your menu name, logo when available, colors, and QR code are already included in every file. No editing needed.

---

## Why Menu Kit Matters

Most QR menu services give you a link. That link sits in a message, unseen.

Menu Kit gives you the files to put your menu where customers actually look — on the table, at the counter, on Instagram, on WhatsApp, on Google Maps.

The difference: customers scanning within 24 hours instead of never.
