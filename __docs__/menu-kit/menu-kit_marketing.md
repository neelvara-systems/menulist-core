## Positioning

Menu Kit is not a feature. It is a **deployment system** — the bridge between "menu is live" and "customers are scanning."

**One-line pitch:** "Your menu is live. Here's everything you need to get customers scanning in 24 hours."

---

## Value Proposition (Owner Perspective)

### Before Menu Kit
- Owner gets a menu link
- Owner wonders: "What do I print? What size? Where do I post? What do I tell staff?"
- Owner procrastinates → QR never gets placed → no scans → no value → churn

### After Menu Kit
- Owner downloads one ZIP file
- Takes files to any print shop → QR on tables in 30 minutes
- Posts Instagram story → online presence in 2 minutes
- Tells staff one line → adoption starts immediately
- Customers scanning within 24 hours

---

## Key Messages (Use These)

### For Sales Conversations

- "We don't just give you a link. We give you everything you need to deploy it."
- "Table tent, counter sticker, Instagram story, WhatsApp status — all ready. Just download and use."
- "Print 10 table tents. Place one on each table. Done."
- "Your staff needs one line: 'Menu? Scan the QR on the table.'"

### For Onboarding

- "Here's your Menu Kit. It has everything — print files, social images, placement guide."
- "Take the A6 file to any print shop. They'll know what to do."
- "Post the Instagram story now. Save it as a highlight called 'Menu'."

### Forbidden Language

- ❌ "AI-powered" / "smart" / "intelligent"
- ❌ "Customize your tent card"
- ❌ "Design your own"
- ❌ "Marketing toolkit"
- ❌ "Growth pack"

## Competitive Advantage

| What others do | What MenuList does |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Give a QR code image | Give a complete deployment pack (6 assets + guide + staff script) |
| Owner figures out what to print | Print-ready A6 PDF — take to any shop |
| Owner creates social posts manually | Social images pre-made (IG, WA, Google Maps) |
| No placement guidance | Placement guide with exact sizes and locations |
| No staff training | One-line staff script included |

## Use in Pilot Outreach

During pilot acquisition, Menu Kit is the "wow moment":

1. Extract menu from photos (5 minutes)
2. Show preview on owner's phone
3. Owner says yes → publish
4. Download Menu Kit → show all 6 assets
5. Owner realizes: "This is more than a QR code. This is a system."

**The handover feels premium.** That's the conversion trigger.
