# MenuList Marketing Distribution - Outreach Scripts

**Status:** Draft, consent-aware manual outreach scripts  
**Created:** June 22, 2026  
**Owner:** Codex drafts, founder approves before use  
**Related action:** MLD-A007  

---

## Purpose

This document gives the founder short manual outreach scripts for the pilot and launch period.

Do not use these for bulk automation. Email, WhatsApp, SMS, and call campaigns need sender setup, consent, suppression, opt-out handling, and legal review before scale.

---

## Outreach Rules

1. Use founder-led, low-volume, relevant outreach first.
2. Contact only businesses where the current-list problem is visible or relationship-based.
3. Ask for one next action at a time.
4. Stop when the owner says no or asks not to be contacted.
5. Do not scrape contacts into automated campaigns.
6. Do not claim ranking, revenue, traffic, Google refresh, AI citation, or guaranteed growth.

---

## Restaurant / Cafe / Bakery

```text
Hi [Name],

I noticed customers may need a current menu before visiting, ordering, or sharing your place.

I am launching MenuList. It turns the current menu a business already uses into one owner-approved customer link for QR, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile links, print, and repeat customers.

If you want to try it, send the current menu you want customers to see. We will prepare a review version before anything is treated as public.
```

## Salon / Spa / Barber / Studio

```text
Hi [Name],

I am looking for salons, spas, barbers, and studios where customers ask for services, packages, prices, or hours before booking or visiting.

MenuList turns that current service list into one owner-approved customer link that can sit behind WhatsApp, Instagram, QR, Google/profile links, print, or a booking/inquiry action.

If useful, send the current service list or rate card you want customers to see. We will prepare a review version first.
```

## Local Service / Rate Card

```text
Hi [Name],

Does your business have a package list, rate card, catalog, or service list customers ask for before calling or visiting?

I am testing MenuList for this exact use case: one approved public customer link for the current list, placed where customers already look.

If you want to test it, send the current list and I will route it through the review flow before publishing.
```

## Agency / Freelancer / Local Consultant

```text
Hi [Name],

I am building MenuList for SMBs whose menus, service lists, price lists, catalogs, or rate cards are scattered across PDFs, WhatsApp, Instagram, QR pages, and old links.

The product creates one owner-approved customer link that can be placed across QR, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile links, print, and repeat-customer access.

If you help local businesses with public information, websites, social pages, menus, or setup work, I would like to compare notes and see whether this fits your client workflow.
```

---

## Warm Introduction Ask

```text
Do you know one SMB owner who regularly sends customers a menu, service list, package list, price list, or catalog through WhatsApp, Instagram, PDFs, or screenshots?

I am looking for a few practical pilot cases for MenuList.
```

---

## First Follow-Up

```text
Quick follow-up on MenuList.

The first step is just the current list customers should see: menu, service list, package list, catalog, or rate card.

If it is not relevant now, no problem.
```

---

## Close / Stop Message

```text
No problem. I will close this for now.

If the current menu/service-list problem becomes relevant later, you can send the current list and we can review it then.
```

---

## Response Routing

| Reply | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Sends list/source | Mark source received; prepare preview path |
| Asks price | Explain pilot status and founder-approved pricing only |
| Asks "is this QR menu?" | Use Product Hunt reply bank |
| Asks about booking/POS | Clarify MenuList is not replacing booking/POS |
| Asks for agency terms | Route to partner brief |
| Says no | Stop |
| No reply | One follow-up only unless relationship context supports more |
