## Launch Position

Product Hunt should present MenuList as:

> One official customer link for menus, service lists, packages, catalogs, and price lists.

Campaign shorthand:

Do not present MenuList as:

- a QR menu maker;
- a restaurant website builder;
- a generic AI restaurant tool;
- an SEO/ranking tool;
- a booking/POS/delivery replacement;
- a marketing automation platform.

## Maker Comment Draft

Use this as a base. Founder should make it sound like their own voice before posting.

```text
Hi Product Hunt,

I built MenuList for a simple problem I kept seeing with small businesses: the owner knows the current menu, service list, package list, or price list, but customers keep finding old PDFs, screenshots, QR pages, WhatsApp images, Instagram highlights, and broken links.

MenuList turns the current list a business already uses into one owner-approved public customer link. That link can sit behind QR codes, WhatsApp replies, Instagram bio links, Google/menu links, print files, and repeat-customer shortcuts.

The important part for us is not just "make a menu page." It is keeping the customer-facing version tied to the source the business approves.

For launch, we are especially looking for feedback from:
- restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and cloud kitchens;
- salons, spas, barbers, and studios;
- local services with rate cards or packages;
- agencies or consultants who help SMB clients clean up public information.

We are using demo businesses in the gallery so no real customer data is exposed. Happy to answer questions about the flow, the claim boundaries, and where MenuList fits or does not fit.
```

## First Reply Bank

Use these for common Product Hunt questions. Keep responses factual and bounded.

| Question | Draft answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is this just a QR menu maker? | No. QR is one surface. MenuList is focused on one owner-approved customer link that can support QR, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/menu links, print files, and repeat-customer access. |
| Is it only for restaurants? | No. Food menus are one use case. The same current-list problem appears in salons, spas, barbers, pet grooming, auto detailing, event services, florists, classes, and other SMBs with customer-facing lists. |
| Does it update Google automatically? | No. MenuList can give the business a clearer official public link and checklist-style placement path, but external platforms decide what they crawl, show, or update. |
| Does it replace booking software? | No. MenuList can place a booking or inquiry action near the public list, but it does not replace a booking calendar, staff scheduling, deposits, reminders, POS, or marketplace workflows. |
| Does it guarantee SEO or AI citations? | No. MenuList helps create crawlable, structured, owner-approved public information. Search engines and AI systems decide what they crawl, index, show, cite, or summarize. |
| Are the gallery businesses real customers? | No. They are sample businesses using demo data only, so no customer data or unsupported proof is exposed. |
