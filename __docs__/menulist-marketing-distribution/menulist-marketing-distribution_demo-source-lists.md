This document provides safe fictional source-list material for the six core demo businesses in the MenuList demo universe.

Use it to create:

- source-before visuals;

- Product Hunt gallery frames;
- short video scripts;
- partner examples;
- future service-list SEO proof.

Do not present these as customer data. Use the public label where needed:

```text
Sample business. Demo data only.
```

---

## Source Data Rules

1. These businesses are fictional.
2. Phone numbers, emails, URLs, social handles, and addresses are placeholders.
3. Prices are demo values only and should not be used as market pricing advice.
4. Do not add reviews, ratings, customer counts, revenue, ranking, traffic, or sales-lift claims.
5. Do not imply MenuList replaces booking systems, POS, ecommerce, delivery apps, social media, or staff replies.
6. If this source material is converted into runtime demo data, mark the tenant/store internally as a demo/sample business.
