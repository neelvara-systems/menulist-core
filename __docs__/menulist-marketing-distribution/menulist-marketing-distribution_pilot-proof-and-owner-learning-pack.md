## Purpose

This pack turns an activated, permissioned MenuList pilot business into reusable evidence without inventing customer outcomes or exposing private information.

It covers:

- post-activation proof packet preparation;
- activation and day-30 owner interviews;
- evidence and permission review;
- the first invitation-only owner clinic;
- neutral independent-review requests;
- handoff to AssetOS and SignalDesk Content Distribution Rail after approval.

It does not authorize outreach, publication, review requests, provider account creation, or use of a real business without explicit permission.

## Proof Eligibility

A business becomes a proof candidate only when all required conditions pass.

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Owner authority | The business owner or authorized representative controls the source and public page |
| Published truth | The MenuList customer link is live and owner-approved |
| Activation | Two distinct customer surfaces were owner-confirmed within seven days |
| Permission | The owner selected the exact proof items MenuList may use |
| Claim safety | Every statement is factual, measured, or explicitly excluded |
| Privacy | No customer identities, payment identifiers, private messages, or unrelated owner data |
| Asset readiness | Source and after-state captures are readable, current, and redacted where required |

Payment is not required for a factual setup proof packet. Payment and day-30 continuity must be stated only when the maintained pilot board confirms them.

## Required Evidence

### 1. Before State

Use only owner-controlled or owner-supplied material:

- current PDF, image, screenshot, typed list, or owned page;
- the owner-confirmed problem, such as old versions or repeated screenshot sharing;
- capture date and source authority;
- redaction status.

Do not copy third-party reviews, customer messages, restricted platform content, or an unofficial menu as if the owner approved it.

### 2. MenuList State

Capture:

- owner-approved MenuList customer link;
- readable menu, service list, catalog, or price list;
- Official Business Page where applicable;
- current owner-approved action links;
- capture date.

### 3. Activation State

Capture two distinct owner-confirmed surfaces, for example:

- QR or print placement;
- WhatsApp owner/staff use;
- Instagram bio or expected reply;
- Google, Apple, Bing, or other profile placement;
- website or link page;
- counter card, table card, or customer handout.

Do not claim that an external platform refreshed, indexed, ranked, or verified the link unless provider evidence supports that exact statement.

### 4. Timing

Allowed timing fields:

- source received to preview ready;
- preview ready to owner approval;
- owner approval to publish;
- publish to second surface;
- day-30 review date.

Timing is operational evidence. It is not evidence of traffic, revenue, rankings, or customer growth.

### 5. Owner Voice

An owner quote must be:

- based on their own experience;
- shown to the owner in final context;
- approved word-for-word;
- free from claims MenuList cannot verify;
- removable if permission is revoked.

## Permission Request

Send only after the business activates and the owner has received value.

```text
Your MenuList customer link is now active on two customer surfaces.

May MenuList prepare a short before-and-after example for review? Nothing will be published unless you approve the exact wording and images.

You can allow any of these separately: business name, city/category, before source, MenuList page, placement screenshots, owner quote, logo/photo, or video/audio. You can also say no or withdraw approval later.
```

Record each item independently.

| Proof item | Permission state |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | No / pending / yes |
| City and category | No / pending / yes |
| Before source/capture | No / pending / yes |
| MenuList page/capture | No / pending / yes |
| Surface placement capture | No / pending / yes |
| Owner quote | No / pending / yes |
| Logo or owner-supplied photo | No / pending / yes |
| Video or audio | No / pending / yes |
| Measured performance result | No / pending / yes plus evidence review |

Silence is not permission. General product consent is not marketing-proof permission.

## Claim Classes

### Allowed Factual Claims

- owner supplied or confirmed the current source;
- owner reviewed before publication;
- one MenuList customer link was published;
- owner confirmed the link on two named customer surfaces;
- setup took the measured amount of time;
- the link remained active at the day-30 check when confirmed.

### Measured-Only Claims

These require a defined source, date range, baseline, calculation, and written owner approval:

- traffic or click changes;
- reduced repeated menu requests;
- conversion or inquiry changes;
- revenue or order changes;
- time or cost savings;
- search or profile interaction changes.

### Prohibited Claims

- guaranteed sales, customers, traffic, or rankings;
- automatic Google, Apple, Bing, WhatsApp, Instagram, or AI updates;
- official provider partnership without a real agreement;
- AI recommendation or citation guarantees;
- customer behavior inferred without measured evidence;
- competitor criticism presented as owner testimony;
- a fictional or generated quote presented as real.

## Activation Interview

Run within three days after two-surface activation. Target 15 minutes.

1. What source was easiest or hardest to provide?
2. What made the preview feel safe or risky?
3. Which correction mattered most before publication?
4. Why did you choose these two customer surfaces?
5. What part required founder or partner help?
6. What would make you stop using the link?
7. May MenuList prepare a private proof draft for your review?

Record concise answers. Do not record audio or video without separate permission.

## Day-30 Interview

Run no earlier than 30 days after activation. Target 15 minutes.

1. Is the MenuList link still active on both surfaces?
2. Did the menu, service list, prices, hours, or actions change?
3. Who kept the information current?
4. Did staff continue using the same link?
5. What caused any delay, stale information, or cancellation?
6. Would you recommend the workflow to another business owner? Why or why not?
7. If yes, would you like the short permissioned-introduction text? A positive answer does not authorize sharing another owner's contact details.
8. Is the proof draft still accurate, and do you approve the final version?

The answers to questions 6 and 7 are learning evidence, not automatic permission to publish a testimonial or contact another owner.

## Independent Review Request

Eligibility:

- the business is paid;
- day-30 review is complete;
- no unresolved support, billing, permission, or truth issue exists;
- a response owner is available for the review platform;
- the request is independent of owner-referral credits or discounts.

Approved neutral request:

```text
You have now used MenuList for at least 30 days. If you are comfortable sharing your experience, you may leave an honest review on [approved review platform].

Please describe what worked and what did not. A review is optional and does not affect your plan, credits, support, or referral rewards.
```

Do not ask for five stars, a positive review, specific wording, or removal of a negative review as a condition of support.

## First Owner Clinic

### Entry Gate

Run only after three independent businesses activate. Invite at most five owners. Participation is optional and is not a support requirement.

### Format

- 45 minutes;
- founder-led;
- invitation-only;
- no recording by default;
- no product demo presentation longer than five minutes;
- no roadmap promises or public testimonial requests during the group session.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| 0-5 minutes | Purpose, privacy, and no-recording reminder |
| 5-15 minutes | How each owner currently shares their menu/list |
| 15-25 minutes | What blocked source, preview, approval, or placement |
| 25-35 minutes | What stayed useful or became stale after activation |
| 35-42 minutes | Rank the top three recurring owner problems |
| 42-45 minutes | Confirm next owner check and close |

### Outputs

Produce one private founder note with:

- recurring problem;
- number of owners who raised it;
- exact owner wording without attribution unless permitted;
- current product/workflow coverage;
- decision: fix, clarify, observe, or reject;
- owner and due date for any approved action.

Do not create a community forum, public group, badge system, feed, or owner leaderboard from this clinic.

## Asset And Distribution Handoff

After owner and internal approval:

1. Freeze the approved evidence packet and permission scope.
2. Create the matching AssetOS brief and manifest under `packages/asset-factory/`.
3. Produce website/social/launch/documentation assets only from approved evidence.
4. Route channel copy through SignalDesk Content Distribution Rail when that internal workflow is used.
5. Keep all drafts approval-gated; do not auto-publish.
6. Record every public URL and the revocation/takedown owner.

One approved packet may produce:

- website case-study block;
- before/after carousel;
- short vertical video;
- partner one-page example;
- Product Hunt gallery frame;
- founder post;
- documentation screenshot.

Asset variations must not add claims or visual details absent from the approved packet.

## Revocation And Takedown

When permission is withdrawn:

1. mark the packet `revoked`;
2. stop new distribution immediately;
3. remove queued SignalDesk drafts;
4. identify every published URL and asset manifest;
5. remove or replace public assets under founder/legal direction;
6. preserve only the minimum private audit note required to show the request was handled;
7. confirm completion to the owner through the agreed channel.
