## Learning Questions

- What source list was easiest to provide?
- What made approval feel safe or risky?
- Which two surfaces did the owner choose and why?
- What blocked payment after publication?
- Would the owner keep the link current without founder help?

Day-30 state is one of `active`, `cancelled`, `not reached`, or `unknown`. A proof packet may include the approved source-before view, live customer link, two placement screenshots, time-to-publication, day-30 state, and owner-approved quote. It must not include revenue, ranking, traffic-lift, or customer-growth claims unless separately measured and approved.

An independent review request is allowed only after the owner has received value. Ask for an honest review, never a positive review. Do not condition MenuList credits, discounts, support, or referral rewards on the review.

## Exit Gate

Do not broaden acquisition until the founder reviews all ten rows, the proof-and-retention table, cancellation feedback, attributed public-loop claims, and partner consent quality. The board is operationally complete; recruitment remains owner-controlled because no real business data or outreach identity was supplied.
