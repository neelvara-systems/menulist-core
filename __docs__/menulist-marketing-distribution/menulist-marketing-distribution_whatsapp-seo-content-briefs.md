## Page Template

Every WhatsApp SEO page should use this structure:

1. Plain H1 that matches owner language.
2. One-sentence value proposition.
3. Problem section: scattered current list, old PDF, old screenshot, old QR destination.
4. Actual workflow: send list -> preview -> owner review -> official customer link -> use on surfaces.
5. What owners can send.
6. What MenuList does not do.
7. Demo proof or permissioned example.
8. Placement checklist: WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile link, QR, print, staff replies.
9. FAQ with no unsupported claims.
10. CTA: Send list on WhatsApp or upload current list.

---

## Required Claim Boundaries

Every page must avoid:

- official WhatsApp/Meta partner language;
- WhatsApp Catalog sync;
- automatic Google/Instagram/Yelp/Apple/LINE/Kakao/POS updates;
- reply-command approval claims;
- ranking, sales, revenue, or AI citation promises;
- "best QR menu" comparison bait;
- implying MenuList replaces WhatsApp Business.

Allowed:

- "MenuList gives your WhatsApp conversations one current customer link to point to."
- "Owner reviews before publishing."
- "Use the same approved link in WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile links, QR, print, website, and staff replies."
- "External platforms decide what they crawl, show, or update."
