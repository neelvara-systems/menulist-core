## Purpose

This document gives agencies, freelancers, and local consultants a simple way to understand where MenuList fits.

It is a draft. Do not send as a formal partner program until pricing, terms, support scope, and referral handling are approved.

---

## One-Line Partner Pitch

MenuList helps SMB clients turn their current menu, service list, package list, catalog, or price list into one owner-approved public customer link.

---

## Best-Fit Clients

| Client type | Fit signal |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurant, cafe, bakery, cloud kitchen | Menu/prices/specials are shared through PDFs, photos, WhatsApp, QR, or social posts |
| Salon, spa, barber, studio | Services, packages, prices, hours, and booking/inquiry actions are scattered |
| Pet grooming, auto detailing, classes, local services | Customers ask for rates or packages before calling or booking |
| Florist, boutique, catalog-lite SMB | Customers need a current public catalog or offer list |
| Multi-location SMB | Branches show inconsistent menus, services, prices, hours, or actions |

Not a good fit:

- no public list customers need;
- only wants booking/POS/ecommerce replacement;
- refuses owner review;
- wants guaranteed ranking, traffic, or revenue;
- needs a custom agency project rather than repeatable public-list setup.

---

## Partner Workflow

1. Identify client with current-list problem.
2. Collect the current menu, service list, package list, catalog, rate card, PDF, image, owned page, or typed list.
3. Route the client through MenuList intake or founder-assisted setup.
4. Owner reviews prepared public version.
5. Publish the customer link.
6. Place the link on at least two customer surfaces.
7. Record proof and next action.

---

## Customer Surfaces

Recommended first placements:

- QR card/table/counter material;
- WhatsApp reply/status/template;
- Instagram bio, story highlight, caption, or DM reply;
- Google Business Profile or public profile link where applicable;
- business website/link page;
- print material;
- staff reply script;
- repeat-customer shortcut.

Activation means:

```text
published MenuList customer link + two customer surfaces active within seven days
```

---

## What Partners Can Say

Use:

- "one approved customer link";
- "current menu, service list, package list, catalog, or price list";
- "owner reviews before publishing";
- "place the same link where customers already look";
- "QR is one surface, not the whole product."

Do not use:

- "guaranteed SEO";
- "rank higher";
- "automatic Google update";
- "replace your booking/POS";
- "AI will bring customers";
- "more revenue guaranteed."

## Partner Qualification Questions

Ask before offering anything formal:

1. Which SMB categories do you already serve?
2. How often do clients send menu/service/rate updates through WhatsApp, PDFs, images, or social posts?
3. Do your clients need a public link customers can trust?
4. Who will collect source material and corrections?
5. Who will place the public link on QR, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile links, websites, or print?
6. Do you need referral, reseller, or done-with-you setup terms?
