# Physical Distribution Partner Pilot

**Status:** Operating pack ready; partner recruitment not started  
**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## Objective

Test whether businesses already trusted by local SMB owners can introduce MenuList at the moment a menu, price list, sign, QR card, or customer handout is being prepared.

## First Partner Types

- Local print shops and digital printers
- Menu and food photographers
- Hospitality setup consultants
- QR/table-card and signage suppliers

## Ten-Business Test

Recruit at most two partners for one Bengaluru pod. Each partner may introduce up to five permissioned businesses. The founder owns approval, onboarding, and proof permission.

Partner handoff:

1. Ask whether the owner wants one current customer link for the list being printed or photographed.
2. Obtain permission before sharing owner contact details.
3. Send the owner to the approved `/create-menu` link or founder-operated intake path.
4. Record only source, permission, business category, funnel state, two-surface activation, paid status, and proof permission.
5. Stop contact after an opt-out or two unanswered manual follow-ups.

## Commercial Boundary

The pilot uses a pre-approved flat completion fee per activated business or no-fee learning partnership. Do not promise recurring revenue share, reseller rights, territory exclusivity, discounts, rankings, or customer growth without a separate legal and finance decision.

## Materials

- One-page partner brief
- One approved owner message
- Tagged `/create-menu?utm_source=physical_partner&utm_medium=partner_handoff&utm_campaign=bengaluru_pilot_2026` link assigned to the partner class, not an individual customer
- MenuList QR/table-card sample
- Pilot board

## Success and Stop Rules

Continue only if at least 4 of 10 permissioned businesses publish and add the link to two customer surfaces, at least 3 owners complete a learning interview, and no material consent complaint occurs. Stop on misleading partner claims, bulk unsolicited contact, unapproved price promises, or customer data being retained outside the approved board.
