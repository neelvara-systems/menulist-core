# MenuList Marketing Distribution - Strategy

**Status:** Active consultant strategy  
**Created:** June 22, 2026  
**Scope:** MenuList product marketing, launch, distribution, channels, assets, and measurement. Website campaign work is documented where shipped; backend messaging-onboarding runtime remains governed by `__docs__/messaging-onboarding/`.

## Consultant Position

MenuList should not be launched like a generic SaaS tool.

It should be launched like a visible public-truth deployment system for SMBs:

> A business sends the current list customers already see. MenuList turns it into one official public source. That source is placed where customers actually look.

The marketing job is to make that transformation obvious, believable, and repeatable.

## WhatsApp-First Moat Update

The priority India wedge is now:

> Send your current list on WhatsApp. MenuList turns it into one official customer link.

This does not replace the broad-SMB strategy. It makes the broad strategy easier to understand because restaurants, cafes, bakeries, salons, spas, barbers, local services, studios, and package businesses already use WhatsApp to send the latest list customers ask for.

The moat is not a WhatsApp button. The moat is a lower-friction path from existing owner behavior to MenuList public truth:

```text
current list in WhatsApp
-> MenuList preview
-> owner approval
-> official customer link
-> WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile link, QR, print, and staff replies
```

Guardrails:

- Do not claim automatic WhatsApp catalog sync.
- Do not claim official WhatsApp/Meta partnership.
- Do not claim reply-command approval such as `APPROVE` until implemented and security-reviewed.
- Do not bulk-message scraped businesses.
- Keep "Send list on WhatsApp" live only on the controlled `/whatsapp` test-number CTA until the production destination, intake ownership, compliance language, and tracking path exist.

The detailed operating plan is `menulist-marketing-distribution_whatsapp-first-hype-strategy.md`.

## Strategic Thesis

MenuList wins when a business publishes one official customer link and places it on at least two real customer surfaces.

MenuList should not choose one narrow industry as its identity. The first market is broader:

> customer-facing SMBs whose customers need a current menu, service list, price list, package list, catalog, rate card, or public offering list before taking action.

Restaurants and cafes are important, but they are not the whole market. Salons, spas, barbers, clinics, studios, caterers, local services, and retail counters are valid from day one when the current-list problem is real.

The early marketing system must therefore optimize for:

1. current list uploaded
2. preview prepared
3. owner approval
4. official public link published
5. two customer surfaces activated
6. paid plan, partner lead, or multi-location review

Everything else is secondary.

## Market Reality

The market is already cluttered. MenuList will be compared against QR menu tools, restaurant website builders, link-in-bio pages, generic menu makers, social profiles, WhatsApp/PDF habits, and local setup agencies.

The marketing response is not to claim every SMB workflow. The response is to own one practical job across many SMB categories:

> turn the current customer-facing list into one official customer link.

This lets MenuList stay broad without sounding generic.

## Launch Offer

### Founder-Led Pilot Offer

For the first controlled cohort:

- founder-assisted setup
- one official public source
- one QR/share kit

### Guardrails

- No unlimited free processing.
- No anonymous heavy usage.
- No private-only work without public proof.
- No public attribution removal during subsidized setup.
- No unsupported sales, ranking, or traffic claims.

### Activation Definition

A business is activated when:

> Published business + MenuList shared on two or more customer surfaces within seven days.

Customer surfaces:

- QR printed or downloaded
- WhatsApp link copied/sent
- Instagram bio/menu link placed
- Google Business Profile/menu link marked done
- Menu Kit downloaded
- public customer link scanned/visited
- screen or PDF output used

## Channel Strategy

### Product Hunt

Job:

- public launch event
- feedback source
- credibility asset
- partner/tech audience discovery

Success metrics:

- current-list uploads
- demo requests
- partner conversations
- multi-location reviews
- useful comments
- founder follow-up meetings

Not primary metrics:

- rank alone
- upvotes alone
- vanity comments

### LinkedIn

Job:

- founder credibility
- partner recruitment
- multi-location and agency visibility
- launch narrative

Content:

- founder learning posts
- before/after public-source posts
- pilot progress
- partner setup model
- "why official customer source matters" posts

### Instagram/Reels and YouTube Shorts

Job:

- visual transformation
- SMB owner comprehension
- paid creative source

Creative pattern:

1. show stale/scattered list
2. show prepared MenuList version
3. show public link on QR/WhatsApp/Google/Instagram
4. close with "Send your current list"

Creator and local-influencer tests are allowed only as proof/content experiments:

- use local food, salon, service, retail, or SMB creators only when their audience has likely owner/customer relevance;
- judge fit by baseline views, real comments, local intent, and natural category fit, not follower count;
- test three small creator/angle/audience combinations before repeating spend;
- track current-list uploads, previews approved, public links published, and two-surface activations instead of vanity views;
- do not build influencer workflow into MenuList or position MenuList as creator campaign software.

### X

Job:

- Product Hunt support
- builder/founder conversation
- tech audience feedback

Do not treat X as primary SMB acquisition.

### SEO and Content

Job:

- compounding discovery
- owner education
- durable proof pages

First clusters:

- digital menu vs PDF/photo
- QR menu for restaurants
- service menu for salons/spas
- WhatsApp menu link
- Google Business Profile menu link
- official business page
- multi-location menu consistency

Rules:

- use product screenshots and workflow proof
- avoid low-value programmatic pages
- avoid ranking and AI-search guarantees
- keep pages crawlable and structured

### Direct Outreach

Job:

- founder-led pilot
- local proof
- fast feedback

Allowed:

- warm intros
- manual local conversations
- partner-led intros
- opt-out email
- consented WhatsApp replies

Blocked:

- scraped WhatsApp blasts
- bulk SMS/calls
- automated cold sequences without suppression
- fear-based claims

### Partners

Job:

- repeatable setup channel
- local trust
- business-type access

Start with setup partners, not resellers:

- local agencies
- menu designers
- social media freelancers
- photographers
- POS/onboarding consultants
- small-business consultants

Partner promise:

> You bring the business and current list. MenuList prepares the official customer source and deployment kit.

### Paid Marketing

Job:

- accelerate proven funnel
- retarget warm intent
- test specific vertical offers

Start only when:

- tracking is ready
- preview cost is controlled
- follow-up exists
- two-surface activation is measurable
- stop-loss rules are defined

First paid order:

1. retargeting
2. Google Search for high-intent list/menu terms
3. Meta/Instagram vertical proof tests
4. LinkedIn partner and multi-location tests
5. Performance Max only after conversion data exists

## Measurement System

### Primary Metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Current lists uploaded | Shows intent |
| Previews prepared | Shows system proof |
| Owner approvals | Shows trust |
| Public links published | Shows activation step one |
| Two-surface activations | Shows distribution behavior |
| Paid plans | Shows monetization |
| Partner leads | Shows repeatable channel |
| Multi-location reviews | Shows higher-value demand |
| Cost per activated public business | Shows acquisition quality |

### Stop-Loss Rules

Stop or pause a channel when:

- it drives visitors but no uploads
- uploads do not become previews
- previews do not become approvals
- published links are not placed on customer surfaces
- cost per activated public business exceeds the current budget ceiling
- complaints, unsubscribes, spam reports, or channel policy risk rises

## First 90-Day Strategy

### Days 1-15 - Foundation

- finalize this folder
- lock the broad SMB category stance
- define multi-category demo universe
- prepare screenshot/video brief
- audit `/create-menu` and homepage CTA fit before pushing traffic
- create Product Hunt asset checklist
- create manual outreach and partner scripts

### Days 16-45 - Proof Pilot

- onboard 20-50 founder-led pilot businesses or demo equivalents across restaurants/cafes and salons/spas first, then adjacent list-driven SMBs as access allows
- publish official links
- push every pilot into two customer surfaces
- capture before/after proof
- record objections and follow-up patterns
- prepare Product Hunt page and launch posts

### Days 46-90 - Public Launch and Conversion

- run Product Hunt launch only when proof assets are ready
- follow up with every useful comment, upload, and partner lead
- publish first case-study style proof only when real permission exists
- test retargeting or search only after tracking and activation proof
- update docs after each execution batch
