## Page Job

The launch page should help Product Hunt visitors understand MenuList in under 10 seconds:

> SMBs already have a current list. MenuList turns it into one official customer link they can use across WhatsApp, QR, Instagram, Google/profile links, print, and staff replies.

The page should drive three useful actions:

1. send a current list;
2. view proof examples;
3. give Product Hunt feedback.

## Draft Copy

### Hero

**H1:** Forward any SMB list into one official customer link

**Subhead:** MenuList helps businesses turn the menu, service list, rate card, package list, catalog, or price list they already use into one owner-approved public link for WhatsApp, QR, Instagram, Google/profile links, print, and staff replies.

**Primary CTA:** Send list on WhatsApp  
**Secondary CTA:** View demo examples

### Proof Line

Use:

> Start from the list the business already sends customers. Review the prepared public version before it goes live.

Do not use:

> AI instantly publishes your WhatsApp menu.

### Offer Block

Default draft:

> Product Hunt launch offer: free first-list setup for the first approved pilot businesses. Paid plan starts only if you want to keep the same public link live and updated.

Founder must approve final offer before public use.

## FAQ

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is this just a QR menu maker? | No. QR is one surface. MenuList focuses on one owner-approved customer link that can support QR, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile links, print, and staff replies. |
| Is it only for restaurants? | No. Food menus are one use case. The same current-list problem appears in salons, spas, barbers, pet grooming, auto detailing, event services, florists, classes, and other SMBs with customer-facing lists. |
| Does it update Google or Instagram automatically? | No. MenuList gives the business a clearer official customer link and placement checklist. External platforms decide what they show, crawl, update, or summarize. |
| Does it sync WhatsApp Catalog? | No. MenuList complements WhatsApp by giving conversations one current customer link to point to. |
| Can I try it without a dashboard first? | Yes. The intended first action is sending or uploading the current list. The owner reviews before publishing. |

## Product Hunt Source Rules

Current Product Hunt source constraints reflected in this spec:

- direct URL; no shortened links or UTM-heavy submission URL;
- tagline should be short and clear;
- thumbnail should be square and crop-safe;
- 2+ gallery images required;
- YouTube video is supported if used;
- first maker comment should ask for feedback, not upvotes;
- scheduling is possible up to one month ahead.

Source: https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch
