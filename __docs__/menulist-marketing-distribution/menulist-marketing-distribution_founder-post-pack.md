## Post 1 - Why MenuList Exists

**Channel:** LinkedIn
**Timing:** 7-10 days before launch
**Goal:** Explain the problem without pitching too hard.

```text
Small businesses often know exactly what the current menu, service list, package list, or price list is.

Customers do not.

They find an old PDF, a screenshot in WhatsApp, a menu photo on Instagram, a QR page nobody updated, or a link that points to the wrong thing.

I am building MenuList around a simple idea:

The business should have one approved public customer link for the list customers need to see.

QR, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google/profile links, print, and repeat-customer access can all point back to that source.

Menus are one use case. Service lists, packages, catalogs, and rate cards have the same problem.

We are preparing the launch proof now.
```

## Post 2 - Not Only Restaurants

**Channel:** LinkedIn or X
**Timing:** 4-6 days before launch
**Goal:** Record broad-SMB stance.

```text
MenuList started with menus, but the problem is broader than restaurants.

A salon has a service list.
A spa has packages.
An auto detailer has rates.
A florist has a catalog.
A pet groomer has prices.
A cafe has a menu.

The common problem is not the industry.

The common problem is that customers need a current list before they call, visit, book, order, or share.

That is the launch focus: one official customer link for the current list the business already uses.
```

**Timing:** Before or after Product Hunt, depending on proof readiness
