# MenuList Marketing Distribution - Launch Week Content Calendar

**Status:** Draft launch calendar  
**Created:** June 22, 2026  
**Owner:** Codex drafts, founder schedules manually  
**Related action:** MLD-L004  

---

## Purpose

This calendar sequences the founder, Product Hunt, LinkedIn, X, Instagram/Reels, partner, and follow-up content around launch.

It does not schedule posts, upload assets, run ads, or send outbound messages.

---

## Calendar Rule

Do not publish proof-heavy posts until the screenshot and video assets are final.

Text-only founder context can go earlier. Public proof should wait for clean demo or permissioned customer visuals.

## Stop Rules

Pause launch-week content if:

- the intake route breaks;
- screenshot/video assets misrepresent product behavior;
- comments reveal confusing claims;
- upload/preview path fails for qualified owners;
- founder cannot respond to Product Hunt and qualified leads;
- external setup requirements are not ready.
