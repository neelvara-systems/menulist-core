# MenuList Marketing Distribution - Demo Universe

**Status:** Active source brief  
**Created:** June 22, 2026  
**Owner:** Codex drafts, founder approves before public use  
**Related actions:** MLD-A001, MLD-R006  
**Scope:** Fictional launch proof data, screenshot requirements, asset requirements, and claim boundaries for broad-SMB MenuList marketing.

---

## Purpose

MenuList should not rely on one restaurant demo.

The launch proof system must show that MenuList works for customer-facing SMBs with:

- menus
- service lists
- package lists
- rate cards
- catalog-style lists
- price lists
- multi-location list consistency

The shared proof line is:

```text
current list -> official customer link -> two customer surfaces
```

This document is the source brief for screenshots, Product Hunt assets, short videos, outreach examples, partner collateral, and future SEO/service-list pages.

---

## Default Data Decision

Use fictional demo businesses until real customers give written permission.

Every public asset using these examples must be treated as sample data. Do not imply the businesses are real customers, do not add testimonials, and do not invent outcomes.

Required label where the asset could be mistaken for a customer case:

```text
Sample business. Demo data only.
```

---

## Demo Universe Rules

1. Each demo must prove one specific list problem, not just decorate the website.
2. Each demo must show the same MenuList spine: source list, public customer link, and customer surfaces.
3. Do not show debug routes, local-only IDs, test tenant artifacts, private owner data, internal emails, billing data, or fake customer reviews.
4. Do not use ranking, revenue, conversion, AI citation, Google refresh, or traffic-growth claims.
5. Do not claim MenuList replaces booking, POS, delivery marketplaces, social media, websites, or agency work.
6. Food businesses remain important proof, but the first asset pack must also visibly include salon/spa, local service, package/rate-card, catalog-lite, and multi-location examples.

## Core Demo Details

### Local Table Cafe

**Business type:** Cafe / small restaurant  
**Source problem:** Old PDF menu, stale QR destination, WhatsApp screenshots, and changed prices.  
**Customer action:** Call, WhatsApp, directions, share.  
**Surfaces to show:** QR card, WhatsApp link, Google profile menu-link checklist, public menu page.  
**Example categories:** Breakfast, coffee, cold drinks, sandwiches, lunch bowls.  
**Example items:** Masala omelette, filter coffee, cold brew, paneer sandwich, millet bowl.  
**Proof line:** One current menu for QR, WhatsApp, and walk-in customers.

### Glow & Blade Studio

**Business type:** Salon / barber / spa hybrid  
**Source problem:** Service prices scattered across Instagram highlights, WhatsApp images, posters, and staff replies.  
**Customer action:** WhatsApp, call, directions, booking-link handoff if available.  
**Surfaces to show:** Instagram bio link, WhatsApp reply link, front-desk QR, Official Business Page.  
**Example categories:** Hair care, grooming, facials, packages, bridal prep.  
**Example items:** Haircut, beard shaping, glow facial, head spa, bridal trial package.  
**Proof line:** One official service list for Instagram, WhatsApp, Google, and QR.

### Spark Auto Detailing

**Business type:** Local service / vehicle detailing  
**Source problem:** Package prices and add-ons change, but old posters and chat replies keep circulating.  
**Customer action:** WhatsApp inquiry, call, directions.  
**Surfaces to show:** WhatsApp link, workshop counter QR, Instagram link, package-list public page.  
**Example categories:** Bike care, car wash, interior detailing, ceramic packages, add-ons.  
**Example items:** Bike foam wash, car interior clean, full detailing package, ceramic coating inquiry.  
**Proof line:** One current package list before customers message for rates.

### PawKind Grooming

**Business type:** Pet grooming  
**Source problem:** Size-based service prices are hard to explain repeatedly in chat and old PDFs.  
**Customer action:** WhatsApp inquiry, call, directions.  
**Surfaces to show:** Public service list, WhatsApp template reply, QR at reception, saved customer shortcut.  
**Example categories:** Small pets, medium pets, large pets, add-ons, care notes.  
**Example items:** Bath and blow dry, full grooming, nail trim, de-shedding add-on.  
**Proof line:** One service list customers can check before they ask for prices.

### Lens & Vows Studio

**Business type:** Photography / event service  
**Source problem:** Packages, starting prices, and add-ons are sent manually and become inconsistent.  
**Customer action:** WhatsApp inquiry, call, portfolio/booking handoff if available.  
**Surfaces to show:** Package public page, Instagram link, WhatsApp link, proposal QR.  
**Example categories:** Wedding, pre-wedding, event coverage, add-ons, albums.  
**Example items:** Pre-wedding shoot, half-day event, full-day wedding coverage, album add-on.  
**Proof line:** One package list before customers ask for a quote.

### BloomBox Florals

**Business type:** Florist / retail counter  
**Source problem:** Seasonal catalog changes and old product photos stay in chat, social posts, and PDFs.  
**Customer action:** WhatsApp order/inquiry, call, directions.  
**Surfaces to show:** Catalog public page, WhatsApp product inquiry, QR on counter, Instagram link.  
**Example categories:** Bouquets, event decor, gift boxes, seasonal picks, add-ons.  
**Example items:** Rose bouquet, birthday box, puja flowers, event table arrangement.  
**Proof line:** One current catalog link for seasonal products and quick inquiries.

## Screenshot Requirements

Screenshots must come from real MenuList routes where possible. Static mockups can be used only for source-before visuals, annotated diagrams, or asset planning.

Minimum screenshot set:

1. Upload/create-menu entry showing broad list-source language.
2. Prepared public customer link for Local Table Cafe.
3. Prepared public customer link for Glow & Blade Studio.
4. Official Business Page proof for Glow & Blade Studio or Urban Glow Group.
5. QR/Menu Kit proof for Local Table Cafe or Glow & Blade Studio.
6. WhatsApp/Instagram placement proof for Glow & Blade Studio.
7. Rate-card/package proof for Spark Auto Detailing.
8. Multi-location consistency proof for Urban Glow Group.

Screenshot rules:

- Use real product UI when the product can render it.
- Hide local IDs, debug state, private emails, billing, internal admin, and test-only labels.
- Prefer mobile-first public output screenshots.
- Use short annotations only when they help a first-time SMB owner understand the transformation.
- Do not use fake reviews, fake customer counts, fake traffic, fake revenue, or fake ranking positions.
