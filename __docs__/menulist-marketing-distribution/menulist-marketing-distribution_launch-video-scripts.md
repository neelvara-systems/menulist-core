## Current Positioning

Use this core line across MenuList launch and marketing videos:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

Use this market-facing expansion only as readiness/source-quality language:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

MenuList videos should not present the product as a QR menu app or generic AI restaurant software. The story is:

> Start from the list the business already has. Prepare a private customer version. Publish only after owner approval. Keep MenuList pages, QR, print files, customer actions, and owner-shared links tied to the approved version.
