## Executive Verdict

ChatGPT's 15-point structure is accepted as the working index for MenuList marketing and distribution.

The main correction is strategic discipline:

- Product Hunt is useful, but not the growth engine.
- Paid marketing is later, not first.
- Outreach must be consent-aware and carefully throttled.
- SEO and AI-search work must follow normal crawlable, useful content rules, not AEO/GEO hacks.
- MenuList must stay broader than restaurant-only without losing the current menu/offering-list wedge.
- Every channel should push owners toward a current-list upload and an official public link, not generic demos, vanity engagement, or content impressions.

## Point-by-Point Decisions

### Point 1 - Positioning and Core Message

**Accepted.** MenuList should not be framed as QR menu software or generic AI menu generation. The existing repo position is stronger: MenuList is a controlled public source for customer-facing business truth.

**Current operating line:** "MenuList runs your customer-facing menu and business information from one official source."

**Do not say publicly:** AI-powered menu generator, smart QR menu, restaurant website builder, marketing growth tool.

**Action:** Keep every asset grounded in "current list -> official public link -> customer surfaces stay correct."

### Point 2 - ICP and Buyer Pain Map

**Accepted with priority order.**

Primary launch ICP:

1. Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, cloud kitchens, dessert shops.
2. Salons, spas, barbers, clinics, service studios.
3. SMBs with package lists, rate cards, catalogs, or price lists.
4. Multi-location SMBs with consistency problems.
5. Setup partners: agencies, local marketers, designers, consultants, photographers, POS/onboarding providers.

The shared pain is not "they need a menu." The pain is that customers see stale, scattered, low-trust public information.

### Point 3 - Launch Offer and Pricing Hooks

**Accepted with cost guardrails.**

The launch offer should not be "free unlimited menu conversion." That attracts low-intent uploads and cost leakage.

The offer should be:

> Send your current list. We prepare one official customer version. You review before publishing.

Launch cohort guardrail:

- founder-assisted first setup is allowed
- cost-capped preview is allowed
- public attribution is required
- two customer surfaces are required for success
- stop subsidizing segments that do not publish

### Point 4 - Website Conversion Flow

**Accepted.**

The website should move users toward:

1. Upload current list.
2. See prepared preview.
3. Review official version.
4. Publish customer link.
5. Place link on QR, WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, screen, or Menu Kit.

**Risk:** Current `/create-menu` wording can look like a digital menu creator. Before heavy traffic, verify route copy and funnel behavior against current repo truth.

### Point 5 - Demo SMB Examples and Launch Visual Assets

**Accepted and upgraded.**

MenuList needs a demo universe:

- cafe menu
- bakery/catering list
- salon service list
- spa package list
- clinic/service rate card
- multi-location food or salon example

Each demo should show:

- before source: WhatsApp PDF, photo menu, old poster, messy price list
- MenuList source: clean owner-reviewed version
- public outputs: menu, Official Business Page, QR/Menu Kit, WhatsApp link, Google/Instagram placement

### Point 6 - Video Storytelling and Asset System

**Accepted.**

The strongest video story is operational, not inspirational:

1. Customer sees old/stale list.
2. Staff/owner keeps resending screenshots or PDFs.
3. MenuList creates one official customer link.
4. Same link goes to QR, WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, screen, and repeat customers.

Video formats:

- 10-15 second vertical proof clips
- 30-45 second founder demo
- 60-90 second Product Hunt launch walkthrough
- silent GIF/screen recording for website and Product Hunt gallery

### Point 7 - Product Hunt Launch Page

**Accepted as event, not engine.**

Product Hunt should be used for:

- public proof
- founder story
- feedback
- partner visibility
- credibility with builders and early adopters

It should not be measured mainly by rank. A Product Hunt launch fails if it creates attention but no current-list uploads, partner conversations, or public MenuList surfaces.

### Point 8 - Social Launch Content System

**Accepted with channel jobs.**

Channel roles:

- LinkedIn: founder/operator credibility and partner distribution.
- Instagram/Reels: visible before/after transformation for SMB owners.
- YouTube Shorts: reusable vertical demo proof and paid-test creative.
- X: Product Hunt/support/community conversation, not SMB acquisition core.
- WhatsApp: owner conversation and support surface, not bulk blast channel.

### Point 9 - Direct SMB Outreach System

**Accepted only under compliance and founder-led limits.**

The best outreach offer is concrete:

> Send the list customers already see. We will prepare one public version for review.

Allowed now:

- founder-led warm outreach
- partner-introduced outreach
- manual local pilot conversations
- email with opt-out and sender discipline
- WhatsApp replies or consented conversations

Blocked by default:

- scraped WhatsApp blasts
- bulk SMS/calls
- fake urgency
- "we found your menu wrong" fear copy
- unconsented automated sequences

### Point 10 - Partner and Reseller Strategy

**Accepted as setup-partner first.**

MenuList should not start with unmanaged reseller promises.

Start with:

- local marketing agencies
- menu designers
- food photographers
- POS/onboarding consultants
- website/social media freelancers
- small-business consultants

Partner offer:

> Bring us current-list businesses. We prepare the public source and deployment kit. You stay the setup partner.

### Point 11 - SEO and Content Marketing

**Accepted with correction.**

The right SEO strategy is useful, crawlable, specific content and structured public proof. Do not create low-quality programmatic pages or "AEO/GEO" hacks.

Content clusters:

- digital menu vs PDF/photo
- QR menu for restaurants
- service menu for salons/spas
- menu link for WhatsApp/Instagram/Google
- official business page for local businesses
- multi-location menu consistency
- how to replace stale public menu links

### Point 12 - Launch Calendar

**Accepted.**

The calendar must start before Product Hunt:

1. Strategy and proof asset preparation.
2. Demo universe and screenshot/video production.
3. Website/funnel readiness.
4. Founder-led pilot.
5. Product Hunt preparation.
6. Launch week.
7. Follow-up and conversion.
8. Paid tests only after proof.

Detailed sequencing lives in [execution plan](./menulist-marketing-distribution_execution-plan.md).

### Point 13 - Metrics Dashboard

**Accepted conceptually.**

Measure:

- current lists uploaded
- previews prepared
- approved previews
- public links published
- two-surface activations
- paid plan starts
- partner leads
- multi-location review requests
- cost per activated public business

Do not optimize for:

- impressions
- likes
- Product Hunt rank alone
- raw traffic
- cost per click without activation

### Point 14 - Post-Launch Follow-Up

**Accepted.**

Follow-up turns launch attention into proof. Every Product Hunt comment, demo request, upload, partner inquiry, and local pilot conversation needs an owner-readable next step.

Follow-up paths:

- uploaded current list -> prepare preview
- preview opened -> approval nudge
- approved -> publish + two-surface placement
- published -> paid plan or partner/referral path
- partner lead -> setup-partner call
- multi-location inquiry -> consistency review

### Point 15 - Paid Marketing

**Accepted as later accelerator.**

Paid marketing starts only after the funnel proves:

> Ad click -> current list uploaded -> preview prepared -> owner approval -> public link published -> two customer surfaces activated -> paid plan or qualified partner/multi-location lead.

First paid tests:

- retargeting to visitors and upload abandoners
- Google Search for high-intent "QR menu", "digital menu", "service menu", "menu link" terms
- Meta/Instagram vertical before-after proof
- partner/multi-location LinkedIn tests

Blocked first:

- broad awareness spend
- generic "AI menu" ads
- paid traffic to an unverified funnel
- Performance Max without conversion data

## Durable Marketing Doctrine From This Conversation

This conversation contains doctrine-level guidance for future marketing work:

- Marketing must make the product transformation visible, not invent hype.
- Distribution is only real when the public MenuList link is placed where customers actually look.
- Launch attention is not success unless it becomes current-list uploads and activated public surfaces.
- MenuList should sell official public source, not content generation.
- Channel scale is unsafe until measurement, consent, and follow-up systems are ready.

For now, this doctrine is recorded in this folder. If it becomes a cross-product rule, promote it into the constitution after founder approval.
