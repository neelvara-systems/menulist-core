## Getting Started

The Help Center is your one-stop destination for getting answers, submitting requests, and staying updated. You can find it in your dashboard navigation.

---

## Finding Answers

### Using the Search Bar
1. Open the Help Center from your dashboard menu
2. Type your question in the search bar at the top
3. The system finds the most relevant articles and generates a clear answer
4. Each answer shows which article it came from — click to read the full article

### Uploading a Screenshot
If you see something on screen that you need help with:
1. Click the image icon in the search bar
2. Upload your screenshot
3. Type your question about what you see
4. The system analyzes the image and finds relevant help articles

### Follow-Up Questions
After getting an answer:
- **Ask a Follow-up** — Continue the conversation about the same topic
- **New Question** — Start fresh with a different question

### Chat History
Your previous conversations are saved. Click the history icon to see past questions and answers.

## Browsing the Knowledge Base

1. Click "Knowledge Base" in the Help Center tabs
2. Browse categories on the left
3. Click a category to see its sections and articles
4. Click any article to read the full content
5. Use the "On This Page" sidebar to jump to specific sections within long articles

### Article Feedback
Found an article helpful? Click the thumbs up. Not helpful? Click thumbs down. Your feedback helps improve the documentation.

## Submitting a Support Ticket

When you need direct help from the team:

1. Click "Submit a Ticket" in the Help Center tabs
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Subject** — Brief description of your issue
   - **Category** — Select the most relevant category
   - **Priority** — How urgent is this?
     - *Not urgent* — Can wait a few days
     - *Important* — Needs attention soon
     - *Urgent* — Needs immediate attention
   - **Message** — Describe your issue in detail
   - **Attachments** — Upload screenshots or files if needed
3. Click Submit

### After Submitting
- You'll see your ticket in the "Running Tickets" section on the Help Center home page
- The ticket gets a short ID (like "A3F2B1") for easy reference
- You can track the status: Open → In Progress → Resolved → Closed
- Reply to add more information or respond to the support team

### Ticket Priorities and Response Times
| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Urgent | Within 2 hours | Within 1 day |
| Important | Within 8 hours | Within 3 days |
| Not urgent | Within 24 hours | Within 7 days |

- **Search first** — The AI search usually finds your answer faster than submitting a ticket
