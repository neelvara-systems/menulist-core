## Page: Product Ontology (canonica.app/product#ontology)

### Headline

**Your product, structured.**

### Subheadline

Canonica builds a knowledge graph of your product's features, integrations, and error codes. Support queries resolve through product concepts — not keyword guessing.

### Body

Most support AI searches documents. Canonica understands your product.

When you add documentation, Canonica automatically identifies the product concepts inside — features, workflows, integrations, error codes. These become entities in a structured knowledge graph.

When users ask questions, Canonica detects which product concepts they're referring to — even when they use informal language like "token" instead of "API Keys." Answers come from the right documentation, every time.

### Features List

- **Automatic entity extraction.** Write articles. Canonica identifies product concepts automatically. No manual tagging.
- **Alias resolution.** Users say "token." Your docs say "API Keys." Same concept. Resolved automatically.
- **Knowledge coverage.** See which product areas have strong documentation and which need more content.
- **Entity governance.** Rename, merge, deprecate entities as your product evolves. All references update automatically.
- **Version-aware.** Entities track product versions. Answers scope to the correct version window.

## Page: How It Works (canonica.app/how-it-works)

### Section: Entity-First Retrieval

**How Canonica answers questions:**

```
User question
    ↓
Detect product concepts (entities)
    ↓
Find documentation covering those concepts
    ↓
Generate grounded answer
```

Unlike search-based systems that match keywords to articles, Canonica routes queries through your product's knowledge structure. This produces consistent, accurate answers — even for ambiguous questions.
