## §2 — Positioning

### Category
Support Signal Intelligence (NOT product analytics)

### Competitive Positioning

| Competitor | What They Do | How Canonica Differs |
|------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Intercom Fin | ML-based topic clustering + CX Score | Canonica uses deterministic entity graph — auditable, reproducible, zero ML cost |
| Zendesk Intelligent Triage | Intent taxonomy + sentiment analysis | Canonica maps friction to actual product entities, not generic intents |
| Amplitude / Mixpanel | Product usage analytics | Canonica works from support signals only — no product instrumentation needed |
| Productboard | Feature request management | Canonica detects friction automatically — no manual tagging required |

### Key Differentiator
Other tools require product instrumentation or manual tagging. Canonica detects friction **automatically from support interactions** — the data already exists.

## §3 — Value Propositions

### For SaaS Founders
1. **See where your product fails users** — without building analytics dashboards
2. **Detect new confusion patterns early** — before they become major support burden
3. **Track if improvements work** — trend arrows show if friction is rising or falling
4. **Zero setup** — works from existing support signals. No product instrumentation needed.

### For Head of Support
1. **Prioritize knowledge gaps** — top friction entities ranked by severity
2. **Justify product changes** — data-backed friction reports for product team
3. **Measure impact** — see if documentation improvements reduce friction

## §4 — Sales Scripts (Following Language Governance)

### Opening
> "Canonica automatically detects which parts of your product confuse users. You don't need to set anything up — it works from your existing support conversations."

### Feature Explanation
> "Every week, you see a simple list of your top friction areas — ranked by severity. If something new is emerging, it's flagged automatically. If your improvements are working, the trend shows it."

### Objection Handling

**"We already have Amplitude/Mixpanel for this."**
> "Product analytics shows what users do. Canonica shows where they get stuck. These are different signals. Users don't file support tickets about features they understand."

**"How is this different from just reading tickets?"**
> "Reading tickets shows individual problems. Canonica shows patterns across hundreds of conversations — and ranks them by severity. You see the product-level signal, not individual noise."
