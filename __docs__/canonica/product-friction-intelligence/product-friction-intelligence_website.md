## §3 — Problem Section

### Heading
**Your support tickets already contain the answer.**

### Body
Every time a user asks "How do I connect Stripe?" or "Why isn't my webhook working?" — that's a signal. A signal that your product UI, documentation, or onboarding is failing.

Most SaaS teams collect these signals in ticketing tools. But they never see the pattern: **which product areas generate the most confusion.**

Canonica sees the pattern. Automatically.

---

## §4 — Solution Section

### Heading
**Friction intelligence, not analytics.**

### Three Cards

**1. Automatic Detection**
Support conversations are analyzed nightly. Friction signals are grouped by product entity — features, workflows, integrations, errors. No manual tagging required.

**2. Severity Ranking**
Each friction area receives a severity score based on query volume, escalation rate, and confidence failures. You see the highest-impact areas first.

**3. Trend Tracking**
Every entity shows a trend arrow — rising, stable, or improving. You know instantly if your product changes are reducing confusion or making it worse.

---

## §5 — How It Works Section

### Heading
**From support signals to product insight in 3 steps.**

**Step 1: Signals flow automatically**
Every support query, escalation, and negative feedback creates a signal. Canonica's entity graph maps each signal to the relevant product area.

**Step 2: Nightly aggregation**
Each night, signals are aggregated into friction metrics per entity. Scores, trends, and emerging patterns are computed automatically.

**Step 3: Weekly insight report**
Every week, an AI-generated summary tells you what changed, what's emerging, and where to focus. One page. Two minutes to read.
