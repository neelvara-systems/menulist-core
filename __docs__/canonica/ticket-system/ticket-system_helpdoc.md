## Submitting a Support Request

When you need help from the team, submit a support request directly from your dashboard.

### How to Submit

1. Open the **Help Center** from your dashboard menu
2. Click **Submit a Ticket**
3. Fill in the form:
   - **What is this about?** — Pick the category that best matches your issue
   - **How urgent is this?** — Select the urgency level
   - **What's happening?** — Write a short title for your issue
   - **Tell us more** — Describe the issue in detail — what happened, when it started, what you tried
4. **Add a screenshot** (optional) — Paste from clipboard or upload a file. Screenshots help resolve issues faster.
5. Click **Send Request**

Your request gets a short reference ID (like "A3F2B1") so you can track it.

---

## Tracking Your Requests

After submitting, your request appears in two places:

- **Help Center home page** — "Your Tickets" section shows your most recent active requests
- **Submit a Ticket tab** — Full history of all your requests on the right side

Each request shows:
- **Reference ID** — Short code for quick reference
- **Priority** — Color-coded icon showing urgency
- **Subject** — Your issue title
- **Category** — What type of issue
- **Status** — Current state (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)
- **Last updated** — How long ago something changed

A green **Live** badge means updates appear automatically — no need to refresh.

---

## Understanding Status

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Open** | Your request has been received and is waiting for attention |
| **In Progress** | The team is actively working on it |
| **Resolved** | The issue has been addressed |
| **Closed** | The request is complete |

---

## Response Times

| Urgency Level | Expected First Response | Expected Resolution |
|--------------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Urgent** | Within 2 hours | Within 1 day |
| **Important** | Within 8 hours | Within 3 days |
| **Not urgent** | Within 24 hours | Within 7 days |

---

## Continuing the Conversation

Click any request to open its details. You can:

1. **Read the full conversation** — See all messages between you and the support team
2. **Send a reply** — Type your message and click **Send Reply** (or press Ctrl+Enter)
3. **Close the request** — If your issue is resolved, click **Close Request**

Status changes appear automatically in the conversation as system messages.

---

## Tips

- **Be specific** — The more detail you provide, the faster we can help
- **Use screenshots** — A picture saves time for everyone
- **Check your existing requests first** — Your issue might already be in progress
- **One issue per request** — This helps us track and resolve each issue separately
