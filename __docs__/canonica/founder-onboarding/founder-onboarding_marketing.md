## 1. One-Liner

"Upload your docs. Your AI support works in 5 minutes."

---

## 2. Elevator Pitch

Most AI support tools fail because they start empty. Founders sign up, stare at a blank screen, and give up before uploading a single document. Canonica eliminates this entirely — upload your existing documentation and your AI support system starts answering customer questions within minutes. Behind the scenes, Canonica automatically extracts product concepts, generates structured answers, and builds your knowledge graph. You review and refine gradually, but your customers get help immediately.

## 3. Key Differentiators

| Competitor Approach | Canonica Approach |

| Empty KB → manual article writing | Upload docs → AI works immediately |
