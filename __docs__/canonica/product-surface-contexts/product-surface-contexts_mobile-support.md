# Product Surface Contexts Mobile Support

## Mobile Admission

- Frequency: moderate. Product owners may adjust surfaces during setup or release work.
- Speed: yes. Create/edit actions are simple text/select operations.
- Touch: yes. Controls use large fields, tags, and action buttons.
- Value: yes. Owners may need to verify context wiring from a phone.

## Mobile Decision

Supported in the Canonica dashboard with the same responsive component used on desktop.

## Mobile Requirements

- Cards stack vertically.
- Save/rebuild actions remain reachable.
- Form fields use full-width inputs.
- Tags and route patterns wrap without overflow.
- Preview content remains compact and readable.
