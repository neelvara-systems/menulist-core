# Product Surface Contexts Marketing

## Positioning

Canonica connects support knowledge to the exact product surface where users need it.

## Value

- Users get relevant help faster.
- Product owners connect articles, releases, and support signals once.
- Support teams can see which product areas create repeated friction.
- Help stays tied to product truth instead of loose labels.

## Short Copy

Connect every product page to the articles, release notes, and support signals that explain it.
