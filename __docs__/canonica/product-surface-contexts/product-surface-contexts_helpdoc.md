# Connect Help To Product Pages

Product Surfaces let you connect help content to the pages in your software.

## Add A Product Surface

1. Open Canonica.
2. Go to Product Surfaces.
3. Select New Surface.
4. Add a clear name, such as Billing / Invoices.
5. Add route patterns, such as `/billing` and `/billing/*`.
6. Add the feature, page, workflow, tags, and related entities.
7. Save.

## Link Content

When creating or editing an article, changelog, or ticket, select the related product surface.

Example:

- Billing article: Billing / Invoices
- Billing release note: Billing / Invoices
- Billing ticket: Billing / Invoices

## Refresh Related Content

Canonica refreshes the summary after linked content is saved. Use Rebuild Summary after larger imports or manual corrections. Installed widgets will then use the updated related content.
