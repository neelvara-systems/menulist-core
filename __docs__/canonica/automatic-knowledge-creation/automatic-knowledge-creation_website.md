## §3 — How It Works Section

### Step 1: Signals Reveal Gaps
Users submit tickets, give negative feedback, or escalate to humans. Canonica clusters these signals by product entity and detects which topics lack canonical answers.

### Step 2: Drafts Generated
When a knowledge gap is confirmed (5+ signals, no existing answer), Canonica generates a structured draft: title, summary, step-by-step instructions, warnings, prerequisites — all grounded in real user questions.

### Step 3: You Review and Publish
Review the draft in your governance dashboard. Edit if needed. Click approve. The answer becomes a live canonical response — deterministic, versioned, governed.

## §5 — Technical Credibility Section

### How Canonica Differs

**Entity-based intelligence, not keyword matching.**
Canonica doesn't cluster by text similarity. It clusters by product entity — the actual concepts in your product's ontology. This means "API key invalid," "authentication failed," and "token rejected" all map to the same entity: Authentication. More accurate. More explainable. Zero cost.

**Unified signal pipeline.**
Tickets, chat feedback, and escalations flow through one pipeline. Other tools separate these channels. Canonica sees the full picture.

**Governed by design.**
3-year architecture freeze. Deterministic retrieval. Append-only audit trail. Your knowledge base is infrastructure, not a chatbot feature.

## §7 — FAQ

**Q: Will AI drafts contain incorrect information?**
A: Drafts are structured skeletons grounded in actual support signals and your existing knowledge base. They never invent product features. And every draft requires your review before publishing — nothing goes live automatically.

**Q: How many drafts will I need to review?**
A: Typically 5-15 per month for a growing SaaS product. The system only generates drafts when it detects genuine knowledge gaps (5+ signals with no canonical answer).

**Q: Can I turn this off?**
A: Yes. Automatic Knowledge Creation is feature-flagged. When off, Canonica still detects knowledge gaps — it just won't generate drafts. You can write answers manually.

**Q: Does this replace my documentation team?**
A: No. This accelerates documentation work by generating first drafts. Your team adds domain expertise, screenshots, edge cases, and final polish. Think of it as a research assistant, not a writer.
