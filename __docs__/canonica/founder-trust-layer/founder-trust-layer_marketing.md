## §5 — Sales Objection Handling

**"We already track metrics in our analytics tool."**
→ Analytics tools show conversation volume and response times. The Trust Layer shows answer quality at the knowledge entity level. It tells you which product features have weak documentation — your analytics tool can't do that.

**"Our support volume is too low for this."**
→ The Trust Layer works from day one. Even with 10 queries/day, it identifies knowledge gaps and drift. Better to catch problems early when volume is small than discover them at scale.

**"We review conversations manually."**
→ Manual review doesn't scale. At 100+ conversations/day, you can't read them all. The Trust Layer automatically identifies the 5 areas where AI fails most, so you fix what matters.
