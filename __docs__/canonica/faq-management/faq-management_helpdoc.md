# FAQ Management Help Doc

## What FAQs Are For

Use FAQs for questions customers ask repeatedly when the answer is short.

Keep the detailed explanation in the linked article. This makes it easier to update one source when product behavior changes.

## When To Publish

Publish a FAQ when:

- the question is repeated,
- the answer is stable,
- the linked article proves the answer,
- the product surface is correct.

## When To Review

Review a FAQ when its linked article changes or when customers mark it not helpful.

## How To Connect A FAQ

1. Open Canonica Dashboard.
2. Go to Support Control > FAQs.
3. Select or create a FAQ.
4. Link the detailed article.
5. Add product surfaces or tags.
6. Set status to Published.
7. Save.
