---

## Positioning

Primary line:

**Teach Canonica your product once. Launch support that stays source-backed.**

Short line:

**Paste your product link. Canonica builds the first support layer.**

Long description:

Canonica intake helps first-time founders and solo SaaS builders turn product links, selected website pages, docs, files, policies, changelog entries, screenshots, transcripts, and support exports into source-backed support knowledge. Canonica finds what matters, flags conflicts, builds a product map, drafts help articles, FAQs, and approved answers, and asks the owner to approve only the important launch decisions.

## Claims To Make Only After Implementation

- Canonica can scan selected website/docs pages.
- Canonica can discover candidate product website pages without turning every discovered URL into a Firestore source document.
- Canonica can skip expensive reprocessing when selected website pages are unchanged.
- Canonica can import PDF, DOCX, Markdown, CSV/FAQ CSV, XLSX, PPTX, JSON/YAML, ZIP docs, screenshots/images, transcripts, and support exports.
- Canonica builds a product map before generating drafts.
- Canonica flags source conflicts and missing launch info.
- Canonica generates source-backed article, FAQ, approved-answer, and widget-suggestion drafts.
- Canonica shows topic readiness before launch.
- Canonica keeps source lineage on published content.

## Differentiation

Generic import tools:

- upload docs
- generate articles
- hope the answers are right

Canonica intake:

- collects sources
- ranks authority
- detects conflicts
- builds product map
- drafts multiple support outputs
- requires owner approval
- publishes to widget/help/FAQ/approved answers
- tracks readiness and source lineage

Sales boundary:

Only claim "ready for widget/search" when the approved content has completed the runtime refresh path: embeddings for article-backed search, published FAQs, active approved answers, refreshed page-aware surface summary, and cache/source-version invalidation.

## Launch Story

For founders shipping with AI:

AI can help build the app. Canonica helps support it correctly.

The first product moment should be:

1. Founder pastes the product URL.
2. Canonica finds support-worthy public pages.
3. Canonica shows a product map.
4. Canonica says: "7 decisions before launch."
5. Founder approves answers.
6. Widget and hosted help use the approved support knowledge.
