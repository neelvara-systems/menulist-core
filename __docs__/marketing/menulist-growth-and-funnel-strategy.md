# MenuList Growth and Funnel Strategy

**Status:** Current strategy note
**Created:** May 20, 2026
**Scope:** Marketing and funnel strategy only. Runtime implementation remains governed by feature docs and codebase truth.

## Decision

MenuList should use controlled hype: make the product transformation visible, subsidize the first aha moment, and then force distribution. The product should not market itself as an AI menu tool. It should be positioned as:

> Upload your current menu.
> Get one official customer link for QR, WhatsApp, Google, screens, and repeat customers.

Photo, PDF, link, and WhatsApp extraction are intake conveniences, not the durable advantage. Public positioning must lead with owner-approved source alignment: one current source the owner controls, reviewed before publication, then placed where customers already look.

## Funnel Position

The recommended funnel is:

1. Free owner account, no payment required.
2. Menu upload and cost-capped structured preview.
3. Owner review of the prepared source.
4. Publishing and distribution setup.
5. Paid plan for heavier value: QR kit depth, PDF exports, generated images, descriptions, translations, analytics, custom domain, multi-location, and ongoing operations.

Do not promote the funnel as "no account needed" or an anonymous free AI utility. The public promise should be:

> Free account to start. You review before publishing.

## Why This Is The Right Market Balance

Pay-first is too much friction for non-technical SMB owners because they need to know whether MenuList understands their real menu before paying. Full anonymous free processing is also risky because AI/server costs can attract low-intent uploads, competitors, and users who only want a free converted menu.

The correct middle ground is to subsidize proof, not unlimited usage. A first preview is acceptable acquisition cost if it moves the business toward a published public MenuList surface.

## Activation Metric

Upload is not success. Publish is not enough.

The primary activation metric should be:

> Published business plus MenuList shared on 2 or more customer surfaces within 7 days.

Tracked surfaces:

- QR downloaded or printed.
- WhatsApp/link copied.
- Instagram bio checklist completed.
- Google Business Profile marked done.
- Menu Kit downloaded.
- Customer page scanned.

This matches the actual lock-in behavior: one surface means trial, two surfaces means behavior replacement.

## Distribution Strategy

The first distribution engine is product-led distribution:

1. Business creates a free account and uploads current menu.
2. MenuList prepares owner-reviewed public source.
3. Business publishes and shares QR, OBP, link, customer view, screen, or PDF.
4. Customers and nearby owners see "Powered by MenuList".
5. New owners create their own free account and upload.

The second distribution engine is transformation demo content:

- Old WhatsApp PDF to clean customer menu.
- Paper menu photo to official business page.
- Wrong/old menu chaos to one current source.
- Owner updates once and customers see current version.

The third distribution engine is city density:

- Pick one tight market first.
- Onboard 50-100 businesses in one visible cluster.
- Let QR tables, WhatsApp shares, Instagram links, Google profile links, and attribution create repetition.

## Early Lead Subsidy Rule

It is acceptable to bear early lead cost when the cost creates public proof.

Acceptable early subsidies:

- First menu preview after free account creation.
- Founder-assisted first setup for a limited cohort.
- Basic publish path during launch experiments.
- Manual cleanup for high-intent pilot businesses.

Unacceptable subsidies:

- Unlimited anonymous extraction.
- Free heavy image generation for every visitor.
- Free custom setup with no public attribution.
- Private experiments that never publish or share.

Suggested control:

- Launch cohort: first 100-500 businesses.
- Require real business identity before official setup.
- Keep MenuList attribution visible.
- Require or strongly nudge 2 or more distribution surfaces.
- Stop subsidizing segments where cost exceeds expected conversion or public proof value.

## 90-Day Plan

### Days 1-15

- Tighten homepage and create-menu funnel copy around "current menu" and "official version customers can trust".
- Remove visible "no account needed" promise.
- Produce 10 transformation demo videos.
- Track free account to upload to preview to publish to 2-surface activation.

### Days 16-45

- Run a city pilot.
- Personally onboard 50-100 businesses.
- Offer first setup free or founder-assisted under clear limits.
- Push every published business into QR, WhatsApp, Instagram/Google, and Menu Kit distribution.

### Days 46-90

- Scale only the proof that converts.
- Use paid ads to boost winning demo videos, not generic digital-menu ads.
- Build case studies from businesses that shared on 2 or more surfaces.
- Watch "Powered by MenuList" signups as the core organic loop.

## Final Recommendation

The winning loop is:

> Subsidized draft -> owner publishes -> QR/WhatsApp/Google surfaces spread -> customers see Powered by MenuList -> nearby owners copy -> city density grows.

MenuList should spend early money only where it creates this loop. The product transformation is the hype. Public distribution is the moat.
