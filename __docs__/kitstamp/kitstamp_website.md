## Section: From Source To Approved Kit

KitStamp starts with source material: product photos, menu facts, raw descriptions, reviewer notes, or imported snapshots.

Each item becomes a content unit. KitStamp keeps drafts, edits, translations, and review decisions tied to that unit until the final kit is approved.

## Section: What A Kit Includes

A Final Content Kit can include:

- approved image files
- approved descriptions
- caption variants
- translated text
- alt text
- usage notes
- source summary
- approval metadata
- export manifest
- optional CSV/JSON handoff files

The kit is ready for another system or team to use. KitStamp does not publish it for you.

## Section: Repeatable Handoff Packages

Use built-in package formats so every approved kit arrives with predictable filenames, folders, notes, and manifests.

KitStamp can prepare structured handoff files for operators, agencies, developers, or catalog teams. It does not connect to those systems or update them directly.

## Section: Built For Review

Generated or edited content stays in draft until someone approves it.

Reviewers can compare source material with candidate output, request changes, leave notes, and approve only the items that are ready.

## Section: For Teams That Prepare Repeated Content

KitStamp is designed for:

- agencies preparing client assets
- restaurant and cafe groups preparing item content
- small catalog teams
- marketplace listing teams
- multi-location operators
- brands that need repeatable handoff

## Section: Clear Product Boundaries

KitStamp is not a design canvas, social scheduler, CMS, PIM, DAM, or ad platform.

It prepares reviewed content kits. Other tools publish, store, advertise, or measure them.

## Section: MenuList Source Support

MenuList customers can use KitStamp as a preparation workspace for menu-item content when source import is enabled.

MenuList remains the live menu truth. KitStamp works from copied source snapshots and exports approved kits.

KitStamp does not change MenuList prices, availability, hours, public menu pages, or live item data.

## FAQ

### Does KitStamp publish posts or update my website?

No. KitStamp prepares approved kits for handoff. Publishing happens in the tools you choose.

### Does KitStamp sync directly to Shopify, PIM, DAM, or catalog systems?

No. KitStamp prepares structured handoff packages. Direct sync is not part of the current product plan.

### Is KitStamp a design tool?

No. KitStamp is a readiness workspace. It keeps source facts, candidate content, review notes, and final exports together.

### Can KitStamp use MenuList data?

When enabled, MenuList can provide copied source snapshots. KitStamp does not change live MenuList menu truth.

### Can generated content be final without review?

No. A human approval step is required before a kit is final.

### What teams is KitStamp best for?

Teams that repeatedly prepare product, menu, catalog, or listing content and need a clear approval handoff.
