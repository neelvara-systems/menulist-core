# KitStamp - Marketing And Positioning Notes

**Status:** Internal planning notes
**Created:** May 31, 2026
**Audience:** Founder, product, sales, website copy review

---

## 1. Positioning

KitStamp is a content readiness workspace for teams that need product, menu, catalog, and listing content approved before handoff.

Short position:

> KitStamp turns scattered source material into approved Final Content Kits.

Do not position KitStamp as:

- a design suite
- a social scheduler
- a campaign platform
- an image API
- a CMS
- a PIM
- a DAM
- a MenuList feature

## 2. Market Landscape

| Segment | Strong players | What they own | KitStamp stance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General creation | Canva | canvas, brand, multi-format creation, team workflows | Do not compete. Integrate/export when useful. |
| Enterprise creative production | Adobe | enterprise APIs, custom models, batch production, professional creative ops | Do not compete. Stay practical and operator-led. |
| Product-photo automation | Photoroom | background, relighting, batch product images, e-commerce API | Do not compete as image-only. Add review, text, provenance, and kit export. |
| Publishing/scheduling | Buffer, Later, Meta tools, GBP tools | post scheduling and channel management | Stay export-only. |
| Product/catalog management | PIM/DAM/CMS vendors | master data and asset libraries | Stay project readiness and handoff. |

## 3. Core Differentiator

The differentiator is not generation.

The differentiator is controlled readiness:

- source snapshot
- item-level preparation
- human review
- approved files and text together
- export manifest
- provenance
- cost control
- no forced publishing workflow

## 4. Sales Narrative

Problem:

> Content work gets created in many tools, then the final handoff loses source facts, approval notes, filenames, translations, and usage context.

KitStamp answer:

> Keep each item in a review-ready workspace until its image, copy, translations, alt text, notes, and approval metadata are ready as one kit.

Founder-safe claim:

> KitStamp helps teams prepare approved content packages before they publish elsewhere.

Avoid claims:

- improves sales
- increases engagement
- replaces designers
- publishes automatically
- manages your entire content operation
- always creates accurate content

## 5. Best Initial Offers

| Offer | Buyer |
| --- | --- |
| Product Content Kit | E-commerce operators and catalog teams |
| Menu Item Content Kit | Restaurant groups, MenuList clients, food agencies |
| Marketplace Listing Kit | Listing operators and agencies |
| Agency Client Kit | Agencies preparing repeated client deliverables |

## 5a. First Wedge Decision

The first wedge should not be broad Shopify/PIM/DAM integration.

Preferred first wedges:

- Menu Item Content Kit for MenuList clients and food agencies
- Generic Client Handoff Kit for agencies
- Product Content Kit for small catalog teams
- Developer JSON Kit for technical handoff

Advanced adapters are sales qualifiers, not first-position marketing. The public promise should stay around approved handoff packages, not direct platform sync.

## 6. Packaging Direction

Candidate packages:

| Package | Included |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | one workspace, limited projects, manual review, limited exports |
| Operator | higher unit volume, generation credits, export kits, reviewer roles |
| Agency | multiple client workspaces, branded exports, higher batch limits |
| Credit packs | image, edit, translation, copy generation usage |

Pricing must be validated against provider and Storage cost before launch.

## 7. Sales Qualification

Strong fit if buyer says:

- "We prepare many product/menu/listing assets repeatedly."
- "Clients approve content before we publish."
- "Files and captions get lost in handoff."
- "Translations or alt text are part of the final package."
- "We need proof of what was approved."

Weak fit if buyer says:

- "I just need a design tool."
- "I want posts scheduled for me."
- "I need full catalog master data."
- "I already have enterprise creative ops."
- "I only need background removal."

## 8. Relationship To MenuList

MenuList can be a source and customer of KitStamp, not the owner of KitStamp.

For MenuList clients, KitStamp can package approved menu item content. The import path must copy snapshots and must not mutate MenuList menu truth.

Safe buyer language:

> Start from selected MenuList items, prepare approved item content, and export a source-backed kit without changing the live menu.

## 9. Public Language Guardrails

Use:

- content readiness
- approved content kits
- source facts
- review
- handoff
- export
- provenance

Avoid:

- AI-powered
- Smart
- Dynamic
- autopilot
- guaranteed growth
- campaign optimization
- publish everywhere

## 10. Launch Readiness

Do not market KitStamp until:

- product domain is confirmed
- public website exists behind product host
- billing and credit scope are implemented
- export kits work end to end
- human approval cannot be bypassed
- product data is separate from MenuList and Answerlattice
- support/help docs match actual implementation
- file-based adapters are described as handoff packages, not live sync

## 11. Rejected Market Claims

Do not claim:

- direct Shopify sync
- direct PIM/DAM sync
- guaranteed import acceptance
- automatic MenuList updates
- source fact correction without review
- external platform publishing
