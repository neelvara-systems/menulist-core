## Problem

Local businesses need to stay visible, current, and trusted, but the work keeps slipping:

- hours go stale
- offers expire
- customers ask the same questions
- reviews need replies
- posts never get written
- photos and links age quietly

## Solution

GrowthOS reads the approved business profile and creates a short weekly queue:

- fix stale public facts
- create a ready promo pack
- reply to reviews with care
- add missing customer answers
- turn real proof into public copy

The owner approves what is ready. Nothing goes public without approval.

## How It Works

1. Connect or create the business profile.
2. Review this week's ready actions.
3. Approve and export the pack.

## FAQ

### Is this part of MenuList?

GrowthOS uses MenuList business truth, but it is planned as a separate product surface if approved.

### Does it post automatically?

The first plan is approval and export only. Direct posting is not part of the initial approved scope.

### Can it create content from nothing?

No. It uses approved business facts, owner input, photos, offers, and verified proof.

### Will this change my public profile?

Not without approval. Any MenuList truth change must go through MenuList-owned write paths.
