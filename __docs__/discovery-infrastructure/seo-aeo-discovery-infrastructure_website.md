## Problem Statement

When customers search "best restaurant near me" or ask ChatGPT where to eat — businesses with clean, structured data get shown. Businesses with scattered info get skipped.

Most local businesses have wrong hours on Google, outdated menus online, and different prices on different platforms. Search engines and AI can't trust messy data. So they skip it.

---

## Solution Statement

MenuList automatically structures your business data — menu, hours, prices, location, contact — so search engines and AI can read it perfectly. When you update anything in MenuList, the internet reflects it. No SEO knowledge needed. No extra tools. No agencies.

## FAQ

- **Q:** Do I need to learn SEO?
  **A:** No. MenuList handles all the technical work. You just keep your business info updated.

- **Q:** How does this help with Google?
  **A:** MenuList adds structured data (Schema.org) to your business page. This helps Google understand your menu, hours, and location — so it shows accurate info in search results.

- **Q:** Does this work with AI search like ChatGPT?
  **A:** Yes. AI search prefers structured data. MenuList makes your business data AI-readable automatically.

- **Q:** Is this a separate product?
  **A:** No. It's built into every MenuList account. When you set up your menu and business info, the structured data is generated automatically.

- **Q:** How much does it cost?
  **A:** Included with every MenuList account. No extra charge.
