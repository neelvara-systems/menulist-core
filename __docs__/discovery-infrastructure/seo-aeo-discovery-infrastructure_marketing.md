## Feature Narrative

### The Problem

SMBs are invisible online — not because they don't exist, but because their data is:

- **Scattered** — different info on Google, Instagram, Zomato, their PDF menu
- **Outdated** — wrong hours, old menus, closed status still showing "open"
- **Unstructured** — search engines and AI can't read PDFs, photos, or Instagram posts
- **Inconsistent** — one price on Zomato, another on Google, another on their website

97% of customers search online before visiting a local business. 46% of all Google searches have local intent. 78% of mobile local searches lead to a purchase within 24 hours.

If a business isn't showing up correctly in these searches — they're losing customers every day without knowing it.

### The Solution

MenuList automatically structures every business's data for search engines and AI crawlers. When an owner sets up their menu, hours, and contact info, MenuList converts it into public pages, sitemap entries, and Schema.org JSON-LD that machines can understand.

No SEO knowledge needed. No marketing tools to learn. No agencies to hire.

The owner just runs their business. MenuList handles discovery.

### Why This Matters

The internet is shifting from "10 blue links" to answer engines and summarized results. When a customer asks "best pizza near me open now under ₹400", systems need structured, current sources. MenuList businesses publish that source by default. Most small businesses still rely on PDFs, screenshots, or stale aggregator listings.

This isn't about ranking higher. It's about existing correctly in the places customers actually look.

## Sales Talking Points

### When client says: "I don't need SEO"

> You don't need to DO SEO. MenuList publishes the technical parts automatically. When you update your menu or hours, your official public source and structured data update from the same place. You're not doing marketing; your business has one clean source to point customers and crawlers to.

### When client says: "How is this different from my Google listing?"

> Google shows what it can find about your business, which can become stale. MenuList is what your business says is true: accurate hours, latest menu, correct prices, and the official link you can place on your Google Business Profile.

### When client says: "I already have a website"

> Your website is designed for people. Google, AI crawlers, and maps also need structured data. MenuList provides that: name, hours, menu, location, pricing, and contact details in a format machines understand. It works alongside your website.

### When client says: "Can you show me the SEO dashboard?"

> There is no ranking dashboard. And that's the point. MenuList works silently by keeping your official public source structured and current. Search engines and AI platforms decide what to show.

### The Killer Closing Line

> "Your customers are searching for you right now. MenuList gives them, and the systems they use, one official place to find the right information."
