## FAQ (For Sales/Support)

### Q: "How does it decide what to put on the tent card?"

**Wrong Answer:** "We analyze customer behavior, click patterns, and..."

**Right Answer:** "MenuList only suggests items it's confident customers choose easily. If we're not sure, we don't suggest anything."

### Q: "Can I customize the text?"

**Wrong Answer:** "Not yet, but we're working on..."

**Right Answer:** "The text is designed to be neutral and effective. You can always add your own materials alongside it."

### Q: "How often should I reprint?"

**Wrong Answer:** "Check weekly for updates..."

**Right Answer:** "When you see a new one is ready, print it. Otherwise, the current one is still good."

### Q: "Will this increase sales?"

**Wrong Answer:** "On average, businesses see 15% increase..."

**Right Answer:** "It helps customers decide faster. Whether that means more orders depends on your business."
