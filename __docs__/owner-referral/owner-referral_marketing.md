# Owner Referral - Marketing Source

**Feature:** Owner Referral
**Owner-facing label:** Invite another business
**Status:** Internal source aligned to implemented disabled runtime; promotion not approved
**Last updated:** July 10, 2026
**Audience:** Founder, marketing, sales, support

---

## Positioning

Owner Referral is a private owner-to-owner introduction, not a public refer-and-earn campaign.

The rule is intentionally simple:

> When both MenuList businesses have paid subscriptions, MenuList adds credits to both businesses.

The referring business receives 100 credits. The referred business receives 50 credits. There is no referral limit and no post-payment usage requirement.

---

## Thirty-Second Explanation

Business owners already recommend working solutions to one another. MenuList gives a paid business one private link to share. When the referred business pays for its MenuList subscription and both subscription wallets are verified paid, MenuList immediately adds credits to both businesses.

No menu publishing, QR download, link sharing, distribution checklist, or 30-day waiting period is required.

---

## Owner Value

- One private link inside the existing Share surface.
- Native Share, WhatsApp, or Copy link.
- No contact import or MenuList-sent message.
- 100 credits after both subscriptions are paid.
- No referral cap, tier, ranking, or milestone.

## Referred-Business Value

- A clear introduction from another business.
- 50 credits after both subscriptions are paid.
- Credits support generated menu images, descriptions, translations, and edits.
- No post-payment action or waiting requirement.
- Normal non-referral setup remains available.

## MenuList Value

- Every issued reward is backed by two verified paid MenuList subscription wallets.
- The system reuses payment truth and the current Pack wallet.
- No activation scheduler, public-summary reads, or distribution-event system.
- Credit liability is simple to model per paid referred business.
