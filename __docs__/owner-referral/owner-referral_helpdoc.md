**Feature:** Owner Referral
**Status:** Help source aligned to implemented disabled runtime; publication pending release approval
**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

## Overview

A paid MenuList business can privately invite a business owner they know.

When both businesses have verified paid MenuList subscriptions:

- the referring business receives 100 credits;
- the referred business receives 50 credits.

Credits are added to Pack balance. At current rates, 100 credits can cover up to 20 generated menu images or 100 description rewrites; 50 credits can cover up to 10 generated menu images or 50 description rewrites.

There is no referral limit. Publishing, QR downloads, sharing, distribution actions, and waiting periods are not required.

---

## Send an Invitation

### Desktop

1. Open MenuList.
2. Open `Use MenuList`.
3. Select `Invite a business owner you know`.
4. Select Share, WhatsApp, or Copy link.
5. Send the editable message.

### Mobile

1. Open the Share tab.
2. Select `Invite a business owner you know`.
3. Select Share, WhatsApp, or Copy link.
4. Send the message.

MenuList does not import contacts, select recipients, or send the message for you.

Default message:

> We use MenuList to keep our menu and business information current from one place. I thought it could help your business too: [invite link]
>
> MenuList adds credits to both businesses after both MenuList subscriptions are paid.

## How the Referred Business Joins

1. Open the invitation.
2. Review the reward and privacy notice.
3. Select `Create my customer link`.
4. Create a new MenuList business or continue with an existing unpaid business.
5. Choose and complete the first paid MenuList subscription.

The referral must be attached before the referred business's first successful subscription payment. A business that already paid cannot add a referral retroactively.

Before capture, MenuList explains that the referring business can see the new business display name and a general referral status. Contact, plan, payment, and account-activity details remain private.

The normal non-referral setup path remains available.

---

## When Credits Are Added

MenuList adds both rewards when:

1. the referring business has a verified paid MenuList subscription wallet; and
2. the referred business has a verified paid MenuList subscription wallet.

If both are paid when the referred first payment is confirmed, credits are added immediately. If one side is not currently paid, the referral remains waiting without expiry and is checked again when a verified subscription becomes paid.

No claim form is needed.

MenuList does not require:

- menu or business-page publication;
- customer-link sharing;
- QR or Menu Kit download;
- distribution actions;
- 14-day or 30-day retention;
- a payment or qualification deadline after attribution;
- staying below a referral limit.

## Check Referral Status

Open `Invite a business owner you know` to view up to ten recent referrals.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Their payment pending | The referral is attached; the referred first subscription payment is not verified. |
| Credits added | 100 credits were added to the referrer and 50 to the referred business. |

The list shows the referred business display name and general status. It does not show contact details, plan, price, payment amount, method, or account activity.

---

## Reward Rules

- 100 credits for the referring business.
- 50 credits for the referred business.
- Both rewards issue together.
- Each referred business wallet issues once.
- There is no referral cap.
- There is no post-payment action or waiting requirement.
- Credits are not cash and cannot be transferred or sold.
- Monthly plan allowance does not change.
- Later cancellation, refund, or chargeback does not subtract already-issued Pack credits.

Plan tier, billing interval, business category, geography, ownership, reseller/agency involvement, onboarding source, shared phone, device, IP, or business name do not change eligibility when both distinct MenuList wallets are paid.

## Existing Businesses

### Existing Unpaid Business

The invitation can be attached before the business's first successful paid MenuList subscription. Continue to plans and complete payment normally.

### Existing Paid Business

The referral cannot be attached after the first paid subscription. The existing subscription continues normally without a retroactive reward.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot see `Invite a business owner you know`

The business may not be included in the current rollout. If the action is visible but opens an unavailable state, the paid subscription, billing authority, rollout configuration, or invitation service may not be ready. Contact MenuList Support if it should be available.

### The invitation is no longer available

Invitation links expire after 30 days for security. Ask the referring business for a new link. This link expiry is not a reward qualification deadline after attribution.

### The referred business paid, but status still says waiting

MenuList must verify both paid subscription wallets. Reopen the panel after payment verification completes. If the status remains waiting, contact Support without sending payment card, bank, password, sign-in code, or token details.

### I have already received many referral rewards

There is no referral limit. Each separately referred business can issue one reward pair after both subscriptions are paid.

### My Pack balance did not update

Close and reopen Billing after the status shows `Credits added`. If the balance is unchanged, contact Support with the business name and general referral status only.

### I sent the link to the wrong person

Opening the link does not create a referral. The recipient must explicitly capture it and bind it before their first paid subscription.

## Privacy

MenuList does not:

- import or store share recipients;
- expose email, phone, plan, price, payment amount, method, or account activity to the referrer;
- allow the referrer to manage the referred business;
- send raw referral tokens to analytics.

The referred business retains full account and public-information ownership.
