# Language and Time Display

**Audience:** MenuList owners
**Last updated:** July 18, 2026

MenuList can show the owner dashboard in your selected language and timezone.

In App Settings, choose:

- dashboard language;
- timezone;
- date format;
- 12-hour or 24-hour time.

These choices change how the dashboard looks. They do not translate or change menu names, descriptions, prices, or your public business details.

Business Locale Settings are separate. Use those when you need to change the currency, public-menu languages, or the timezone used for your business.

If a saved display preference is no longer valid, MenuList safely uses English and UTC until you choose another option. No menu data is changed.

Owner dashboard screens on desktop and mobile are populated across all registered language files, including Bodo and Kashmiri. Product names, URLs, codes, and technical identifiers may intentionally remain unchanged. If a future release introduces a missing key, MenuList safely falls back to English rather than breaking the screen.
