## 1. MARKET OVERVIEW

### The Problem

SMB owners (restaurants, cafes, salons, spas) face daily decision fatigue:

- "What should I promote today?"
- "Is this item still popular?"
- "Should I post something?"
- "What's working?"

**They don't lack tools. They lack decisions.**

### The Gap

| Existing Solutions | Why They Fail                     |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Menu Builders      | Stop at display; no intelligence  |
| POS Systems        | Complex, ops-first, not marketing |
| Social Tools       | Require creativity and time       |
| Marketing Agencies | Expensive, unscalable             |
| AI Content Tools   | Generic, untrusted                |

### The Opportunity

- 10M+ offline SMBs globally
- <5% use any marketing automation
- WhatsApp is the default channel
- Decision fatigue is the real blocker
