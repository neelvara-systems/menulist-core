# SurfaceOS - Website Boundary

**Status:** Publication blocked
**Last verified:** July 17, 2026

Do not create or expose:

- `src/app/sites/surfaceos`;
- a local `/__surfaceos` page;
- navigation, sitemap, robots, structured-data, pricing, waitlist, or help
  entries; or
- redirects that make `surfaceos.app` appear live.

The domain and internal paths are collision reservations only. A public website
requires an approved product, implemented truthful capabilities, a deployment
target, legal pages, analytics/privacy decisions, and host-level verification.
