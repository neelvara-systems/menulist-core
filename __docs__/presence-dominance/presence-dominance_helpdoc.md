## Quick Summary

Your MenuList account comes with an official business link — a clean page showing your menu, hours, and contact info. Share this one link everywhere instead of sending PDFs or photos.

---

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- A MenuList account with at least one published menu
- Your business info (name, phone, address) entered in Business Settings

### Your Official Link
Your link is: **yourbusiness.menulist.online**

Find it in your dashboard under Business Settings → Official Page.

---

## How-To Guides

### How to Copy Your Official Link
1. Open your MenuList dashboard
2. Go to Business Settings
3. Find the "Official Business Link" card
4. Tap "Copy Link"
5. Paste it anywhere — WhatsApp, Instagram, email

### How to Download Your QR Code
1. Open your MenuList dashboard
2. Go to Share → Physical Surfaces
3. Choose QR style (sticker, tent card, or plain QR)
4. Tap "Download"
5. Print and place on tables, counters, or packaging

### How to Set as Instagram Bio Link
1. Copy your official link from MenuList
2. Open Instagram → Edit Profile
3. Tap "Website"
4. Paste your MenuList link
5. Save — now everyone who visits your profile can see your menu

### How to Set as Google Menu Link
1. Copy your official link from MenuList
2. Open Google Business Profile (search "my business" on Google)
3. Go to Info → Menu link
4. Paste your MenuList link
5. Save — customers searching on Google can now see your real menu

### How to Share on WhatsApp
1. Copy your official link
2. Open WhatsApp → new message or status
3. Paste the link with a message like: "Check out our full menu here!"
4. Send — customers tap the link and see everything

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

**Q: My link shows "Menu coming soon"**  
A: You need to publish your menu first. Go to the Editor, add your items, and tap Publish.

**Q: I updated my menu but the link still shows old info**  
A: Changes appear within 60 seconds. Try refreshing the page. If it still shows old info after 2 minutes, contact support.

**Q: Can I change my link URL?**  
A: Your link is based on your business name. If you change your business name, the old link will redirect to the new one automatically.
