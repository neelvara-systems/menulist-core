## Problem Statement

Every day, business owners answer the same questions: "Send menu", "Are you open?", "What's your number?" They send PDFs, screenshots, Google Maps links — and customers still get confused. Outdated info leads to bad reviews and lost customers.

## Solution Statement

MenuList creates one official link for your business. It shows your menu, hours, location, and contact info from the owner-approved public source, then reflects approved changes through the current public refresh/cache path. Share it on WhatsApp, print it as QR, set it as your Instagram bio. One link. Current approved information.
