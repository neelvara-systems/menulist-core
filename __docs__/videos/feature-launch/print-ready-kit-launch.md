## A. Purpose

Show how supported MenuList templates carry the approved customer link into practical table, counter, packaging, poster, and printer-ready assets.

## B. Audience

Owners who need usable physical touchpoints without rebuilding QR and menu assets from a blank design file.

## C. Placement

- Print-ready feature page.
- LinkedIn and Instagram.
- YouTube Short.
- WhatsApp sales.
- Retargeting.
- Onboarding and sales demo.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 40 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 30 seconds.
- Paid cut: 15 seconds.
- Silent asset carousel: 8-10 seconds.

## E. Core Message

MenuList fills supported print templates with the business identity and approved customer access details for practical placement.

## F. Emotional Job

`I can get useful customer-link assets without designing everything from scratch.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** Publishing a menu does not place it at the table or counter.
2. **Problem:** Physical customer touchpoints become another design task.
3. **Product entry:** MenuList fills supported templates from the approved source.
4. **Proof:** Owner selects, adjusts, checks, and exports real assets.
5. **Outcome:** Physical placements point back to one current customer link.
6. **CTA:** Print from the approved source.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Animate data into the template from one approved source.
- Keep paper and card mockups frontal enough to read.
- Use one shallow perspective move; avoid spinning print collateral.
- Verify QR after all scale and perspective treatment.
- Stop the export rail for at least one second so the assets can be inspected.
- Use a real scan result as the final proof.
- Use clean, no-pill captions above the bottom platform-control safe zone.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use `One Link Motion v2` with modest energy.
- Add one paper-settle texture, one template-selection click, and one download confirmation.
- Add a soft scan cue before the customer page returns.
- Keep all SFX below the voice and avoid printer-machine novelty sounds.
- Let the music resolve cleanly under the final identity.

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Reduce design and distribution anxiety after the customer link is approved.
- **Primary proof:** An approved source fills a supported template, exports, and scans back to the current page.
- **Allowed:** Current supported templates, permitted adjustments, files, and printer handoffs.
- **Required:** Scan-safe QR, current output proof, and owner selection.
- **Blocked:** Blank-canvas design claims, universal print formats, automatic replacement of old physical assets, or unsupported printer fulfillment.
- **Release gate:** Revalidate each export type, render every shown asset, and scan-test every QR before publication.
