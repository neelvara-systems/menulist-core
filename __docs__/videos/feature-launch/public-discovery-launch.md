## A. Purpose

Explain that MenuList can provide customers and discovery systems with a clearer, current, owner-approved public source without promising rankings, citations, or recommendations.

## B. Audience

Owners concerned about inconsistent public business information across customer search and AI-era discovery behavior.

## C. Placement

- Public discovery feature page.
- Founder/brand thought leadership.
- LinkedIn and YouTube.
- Instagram Reel.
- WhatsApp sales.
- Awareness and retargeting ads.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 40 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 30 seconds.
- Paid cut: 15 seconds.
- Silent public-source loop: 8 seconds.

## E. Core Message

MenuList keeps an owner-approved public page and structured business facts clearer and more current for customers, search, and AI-era discovery systems to read.

## F. Emotional Job

`My business can provide one clearer current source without depending on ranking promises or AI magic.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** Customers and discovery systems need a current source.
2. **Problem:** Old PDFs and inconsistent public details create ambiguity.
3. **Product entry:** MenuList publishes the approved menu, business details, and owner-controlled copy.
4. **Proof:** Clear pages, structured facts, and discovery files are shown as readable sources.
5. **Outcome:** The owner controls public truth while systems have a better source to read.
6. **CTA:** Be clearer online. Keep one approved source.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Start with source ambiguity, then visually reduce it to one approved page.
- Keep product UI larger than abstract discovery diagrams.
- Use clean source lines, not network clouds or orbiting logos.
- Show one owner-approved edit as the control proof.
- Slow motion during the no-guarantee line to increase trust and readability.
- Avoid search-result mockups that imply placement or ranking.
- Keep captions no-pill and clear of page content.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use `One Link Motion v2` with a restrained, current technology feel.
- Add one soft consolidation cue as scattered sources resolve.
- Add one approval tone after the owner-controlled change.
- Use silence or reduced percussion under `There is no ranking or AI-answer guarantee.`
- Avoid futuristic AI sounds, typing code, or search-result pings.

## N. Design Checklist

- [ ] Public page and every shown business field exist in current runtime.
- [ ] Structured facts and discovery files are verified, not invented.
- [ ] The owner controls every public-changing field shown.
- [ ] No ranking, citation, recommendation, traffic, or visibility guarantee appears.
- [ ] No automatic external-platform update is implied.
- [ ] Search/AI readiness remains source-quality language.
- [ ] No third-party logo suggests a partnership or integration.
- [ ] Product UI remains the dominant visual proof.
- [ ] Final identity is plain and held.
- [ ] Encoded output passes claim, readability, and audio QA.

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Establish MenuList as a trustworthy public source in the AI/search era without entering ranking-hype territory.
- **Primary proof:** Current approved public page plus verified structured/discovery outputs and one owner-controlled update.
- **Allowed:** Clearer, current, structured, owner-approved source language.
- **Required:** Explicit no-ranking/no-AI-answer guarantee.
- **Blocked:** Guaranteed Google ranking, AI recommendation, citation, traffic, discovery, or external-platform synchronization.
- **Release gate:** Review current public fields, structured data, discovery files, public policy wording, and search/AI claims immediately before render.
