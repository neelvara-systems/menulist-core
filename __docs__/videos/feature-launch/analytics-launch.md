## A. Purpose

Show the calm, aggregate activity evidence available after publishing without inventing growth outcomes or implying customer surveillance.

## B. Audience

Owners who want to understand whether customers use the menu and which approved content or actions receive attention.

## C. Placement

- Analytics feature page.
- LinkedIn.
- YouTube and Instagram.
- WhatsApp sales.
- Retargeting.
- Sales demo and onboarding.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 45 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 30 seconds.
- Paid cut: 15 seconds.
- Silent dashboard loop: 8-10 seconds.

## E. Core Message

MenuList gives owners read-only, aggregate views of customer menu activity and useful actions without requiring customer names or emails.

## F. Emotional Job

`I can see whether the public menu is being used without turning it into a complicated analytics project.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** Publishing creates a new question: are customers using it?
2. **Problem:** Raw traffic and vanity charts do not help a busy owner.
3. **Product entry:** MenuList presents calm period summaries.
4. **Proof:** Visits, meaningful sessions, items, categories, searches, and actions are visible.
5. **Outcome:** The owner understands activity without customer profiling.
6. **CTA:** See the activity that matters.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Animate charts from zero only when the resulting values are real demo data.
- Keep chart motion under 900ms and stop long enough to inspect labels.
- Use data focus states, not floating metric cards.
- Move through one period and one source change only.
- Use no people icons, avatars, maps, or session-replay visuals.
- Keep labels and units visible through platform compression.
- Use no-pill captions outside chart labels.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use `One Link Motion v2` at lower density under data-heavy narration.
- Add one soft tick on period change and one settle tone on the aggregate view.
- Do not sonify every number or animate to casino-like count-up sounds.
- Let the music recover during the privacy line and final summary.
- Keep voice calm and explanatory, not performance-focused.

## N. Design Checklist

- [ ] Every metric and period label matches current runtime definitions.
- [ ] Demo values are fictional, coherent, and visibly identified in production records.
- [ ] No value is presented as a real MenuList customer result.
- [ ] No revenue, conversion, retention, or growth outcome is inferred.
- [ ] No customer identity, email, profiling, or cross-platform tracking appears.
- [ ] Read-only aggregate boundary is explicit.
- [ ] Charts remain legible at native 9:16 size.
- [ ] Data animation stops long enough to read.
- [ ] Final identity is clean and held.
- [ ] Encoded output passes layout, chart, audio, and claim QA.

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Demonstrate useful post-publish evidence without selling surveillance or vanity analytics.
- **Primary proof:** Current aggregate summaries answer a clear owner question using labeled demo data.
- **Allowed:** Exact implemented periods, metrics, actions, and source summaries.
- **Required:** Aggregate, read-only, no-name/no-email boundary.
- **Blocked:** ROI, sales lift, conversion lift, customer profiles, session replay, cross-platform attribution, or invented live metrics.
- **Release gate:** Revalidate every metric definition, period, source, and privacy statement against current analytics code and UI.
