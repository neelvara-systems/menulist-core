## A. Purpose

Show that Business Health translates current MenuList facts into a calm owner status and routes needed work to the correct owner surface.

## B. Audience

Owners who want a quick operating answer without opening multiple reports or interpreting technical health data.

## C. Placement

- Business Health feature page.
- Instagram Reel and YouTube Short.
- LinkedIn.
- WhatsApp sales.
- Onboarding.
- Product demo and retargeting.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 35 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 25 seconds.
- Paid cut: 15 seconds.
- Silent stable-state loop: 6-8 seconds.

## E. Core Message

Business Health summarizes current MenuList facts, says `No action needed` when stable, and points the owner to the right next step when attention is required.

## F. Emotional Job

`I can quickly know whether anything needs my attention.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** Owners should not need five reports to know what needs attention.
2. **Problem:** Menu, links, activity, locations, and freshness create scattered checks.
3. **Product entry:** Business Health reads the latest MenuList facts.
4. **Proof:** Stable state stays calm; attention state points to the correct workflow.
5. **Outcome:** The owner gets orientation without silent public changes.
6. **CTA:** Know what needs attention.

## I. Full Voiceover Script

> You should not open five reports to know what needs attention. Business Health checks the latest MenuList facts: menu state, links, activity, locations, and freshness. When the latest check is stable, it says No action needed. When something needs work, it points to AI Menu Manager or the right owner screen. It explains and hands off. It does not change the public menu by itself. Know what needs attention.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Treat the stable state as a meaningful outcome, not dead space.
- Use a soft vertical reveal for current facts.
- Use one restrained connection line for the attention handoff.
- Keep the customer page static when explaining no autonomous changes.
- Avoid gauges, spinning health rings, confetti, or animated percentages.
- Keep captions clear of status labels.
- Transition through shared geometry to avoid pale flicker.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use a calmer mix of `One Link Motion v2` with reduced high-frequency activity.
- Add one soft settle tone on `No action needed`.
- Add one subtle directional cue for the handoff.
- Do not use alarms for attention states.
- Pause briefly before `It does not change the public menu by itself.`

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Show daily owner relief through calm operational orientation.
- **Primary proof:** A real stable state and one real handoff from an attention state.
- **Allowed:** Current facts, current status language, and current owner-screen handoffs.
- **Required:** Explicit non-mutation boundary.
- **Blocked:** Autonomous correction, invented health score, uptime guarantee, or claim that every business fact is monitored externally.
- **Release gate:** Revalidate status derivation, exact labels, handoff routes, and current browser/mobile states before render.
