# Feature Launch - Menu Content Prep

**Status:** Script-ready; fresh runtime capture required before production
**Primary runtime rows:** `media_image_system`, `descriptions_translations`
**Campaign authority:** [Feature Launch Inventory](./feature-launch-inventory.md)
**Mandatory standards:** [Founder-Approved Production Standard](../videos_founder-approved-production-standard.md), [HyperFrames Operating Guide](../videos_hyperframes-operating-guide.md), and [Asset Intake And Readiness](../videos_asset-intake-and-readiness.md)

## A. Purpose

Show that MenuList can prepare useful menu content from the approved list without taking public control away from the owner.

## B. Audience

Restaurant, cafe, retail, and service-business owners who have item names and prices but incomplete descriptions, images, or customer-language content.

## C. Placement

- Feature page.
- LinkedIn product post.
- Instagram Reel and YouTube Short.
- WhatsApp sales follow-up.
- Retargeting ad.
- Onboarding and sales demo.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 40 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 30 seconds.
- Paid cut: 15 seconds.
- Silent UI loop: 8 seconds.

## E. Core Message

MenuList prepares descriptions, images, and customer-language content where available, while the owner reviews what customers will see.

## F. Emotional Job

`I can improve an incomplete menu without writing everything from scratch or surrendering control.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** A name and price may not answer the customer's question.
2. **Problem:** Useful details are missing and manual preparation takes time.
3. **Product entry:** MenuList prepares supported content from the current approved menu.
4. **Proof:** The prepared content stays private and reviewable.
5. **Outcome:** Customers get clearer details without creating a second list.
6. **CTA:** Start from your current list.

## I. Full Voiceover Script

> A name and price may not be enough. MenuList can prepare descriptions, images, and customer languages from the approved menu. Where your plan and credits allow, the prepared content stays private for review. Check the wording, image, translation, item, and price before publishing. Approved content gives customers more detail without creating another list to manage. Prepare the content. Keep the owner in control.

## K. UI Screens Required

- Current menu editor with an incomplete item.
- Description-preparation control and result.
- Image-preparation control and candidate.
- Customer-language preparation and review state.
- Private item review with item name and price visible.
- Owner approval action and receipt/state.
- Customer menu item after approval.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Keep one focal action active at a time.
- Reveal prepared fields sequentially at 300-450ms intervals.
- Use a restrained cursor only to explain owner review and approval.
- Use a soft focus ring, never neon glow.
- Match-cut the reviewed item into its approved customer state.
- Do not animate generated copy as if it appears autonomously in public.
- Use no-pill captions with the current spoken word in MenuList blue.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use the approved `One Link Motion v2` brand bed by default.
- Duck music under the calm Indian-English voiceover and recover it in pauses.
- Use one soft preparation texture, one review click, and one muted approval tone.
- Leave a short pause before `Keep the owner in control.`
- Do not add a sound for every field reveal.

## N. Design Checklist

- [ ] The starting item is visibly part of an approved menu.
- [ ] Exact current UI labels are used.
- [ ] Plan and credit availability is not hidden.
- [ ] Prepared content remains private until owner review.
- [ ] Item name, price, image, description, and language stay readable.
- [ ] No automatic publishing or guaranteed content-quality claim appears.
- [ ] No fake before/after performance metric appears.
- [ ] Frame zero contains the owner problem, not a long logo intro.
- [ ] Final frame contains symbol, name, tagline, and `menulist.ai` only.
- [ ] Encoded MP4 passes layout, flicker, black-frame, loudness, and final-hold QA.

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Reduce the belief that useful menu content requires a full manual rewrite.
- **Primary proof:** One incomplete approved item becomes a richer private review and then an approved public item.
- **Allowed:** MenuList can prepare supported descriptions, images, and language content where current plan, credits, and runtime allow.
- **Required:** Owner review and approval before public use.
- **Blocked:** Unlimited generation, automatic correctness, autonomous publishing, guaranteed translation accuracy, or universal availability.
- **Release gate:** Current feature flag, runtime capture, plan/credit wording, and approval path must be reverified immediately before render.
