## A. Purpose

Show that important daily MenuList work remains available from the owner's phone without positioning the mobile surface as a separate product.

## B. Audience

Busy, non-technical owners and staff who operate from a phone more often than a laptop.

## C. Placement

- Owner phone feature page.
- Instagram Reel and YouTube Short.
- LinkedIn.
- WhatsApp sales.
- Onboarding.
- Product demo and sales deck.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 35 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 25-30 seconds.
- Paid cut: 15 seconds.
- Silent phone workflow loop: 8 seconds.

## E. Core Message

MenuList keeps important menu, publishing, sharing, health, feedback, and business-status work available in the owner's phone flow.

## F. Emotional Job

`I can handle the important work from the phone I already carry.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** Daily menu work should not wait for a laptop.
2. **Problem:** Owners work from counters, kitchens, shops, and in transit.
3. **Product entry:** MenuList opens in the phone browser or saved shortcut.
4. **Proof:** One short mobile journey completes real owner actions.
5. **Outcome:** The phone remains part of the same approved-source system.
6. **CTA:** Run MenuList from the phone you carry.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Prefer full-height phone UI for vertical; use a light crop or frameless screen in landscape.
- Show one continuous owner journey instead of disconnected phone cards.
- Use real tap feedback and short, readable scrolls.
- Keep each screen stable for at least 700ms after navigation.
- Use a maximum of one cursor/tap marker at a time.
- Do not place captions over bottom navigation or platform controls.
- Avoid heavy phone borders, reflections, or neon glass treatment.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use `One Link Motion v2` with light percussion and voice-reactive ducking.
- Add taps only on the three key actions: update, approve, share.
- Use one muted approval tone after the public-changing action.
- Avoid a sound on every navigation item.
- Let the music lift slightly when owner and customer states align.

## N. Design Checklist

- [ ] Every destination shown is present in the current mobile shell.
- [ ] The phone flow uses current auth, tenant, and navigation behavior.
- [ ] Saved access is described as a web app/shortcut, not an app-store app.
- [ ] Owner approval remains visible before public change.
- [ ] No full desktop-parity claim is made without certification.
- [ ] Touch targets and copy are readable at native 9:16 size.
- [ ] Status bar, browser chrome, and demo identity contain no personal data.
- [ ] Motion follows a real workflow.
- [ ] Final identity is clean and held.
- [ ] Encoded output passes mobile-device and desktop playback QA.

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Remove the fear that MenuList adds laptop-only operational work.
- **Primary proof:** A real mobile owner flow completes one approved update and share action.
- **Allowed:** Current phone-browser, saved-web-app, shell, and owner-task behavior.
- **Required:** One continuous task path and the owner-approval boundary.
- **Blocked:** Native app-store claim, complete desktop parity, offline guarantee, or unsupported mobile destinations.
- **Release gate:** Re-run the current mobile-owner verifier and capture the exact current shell on a representative phone viewport.
