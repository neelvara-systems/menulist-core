# Feature Launch - QR Menu And Links

**Status:** Script-ready; current proof assets exist and require runtime revalidation
**Primary runtime rows:** `publish_share_export`, `customer_app_pwa`
**Campaign authority:** [Feature Launch Inventory](./feature-launch-inventory.md)
**Mandatory standards:** [Founder-Approved Production Standard](../videos_founder-approved-production-standard.md), [HyperFrames Operating Guide](../videos_hyperframes-operating-guide.md), and [Asset Intake And Readiness](../videos_asset-intake-and-readiness.md)

## A. Purpose

Show that QR, share links, and saved shortcuts are stable access paths to the same approved MenuList destination, not separate menu copies.

## B. Audience

Owners currently sharing PDFs, screenshots, changing URLs, or disconnected QR destinations.

## C. Placement

- QR feature page.
- Instagram Reel and YouTube Short.
- LinkedIn.
- WhatsApp sales.
- Paid retargeting.
- Sales deck and onboarding.

## D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 30 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 20 seconds.
- Paid cuts: 6 and 15 seconds.
- Silent QR loop: 6-8 seconds.

## E. Core Message

MenuList keeps QR, share-link, and saved-shortcut access tied to the same approved customer menu.

## F. Emotional Job

`I can place one stable destination and update the approved menu behind it.`

## G. Narrative Arc

1. **Hook:** A QR is only useful when its destination is current.
2. **Problem:** Separate files and links drift.
3. **Product entry:** MenuList creates owned access paths to one approved menu.
4. **Proof:** Scan, tap, and saved shortcut open the same current customer page.
5. **Outcome:** The owner places the link where customers need it and maintains one source.
6. **CTA:** One approved menu. Every owned link.

## L. Motion Design Direction

- Use a real scan animation only once.
- Draw connections from the approved link outward; never animate external platforms inward as data sources.
- Match page position and URL identity across scan, tap, and shortcut shots.
- Keep QR quiet, high-contrast, and distortion-free.
- Use thin but compression-safe connection lines.
- Use one visible cursor/tap per digital path.
- Keep captions outside the QR quiet zone.

## M. Sound Direction

- Use `One Link Motion v2` as the default bed.
- Add one light scan cue, one tap cue, and one subtle link-confirmation tone.
- Let the music rise slightly through the placement montage.
- Duck under voice and hold a clean final resolve.
- Do not use a loud scanner beep or a sound on every placement.

## N. Design Checklist

- [ ] QR resolves to a current, controlled MenuList demo page.
- [ ] QR quiet zone and contrast remain valid after rendering.
- [ ] Saved shortcut is not described as an app-store application.
- [ ] All paths visibly reach the same approved destination.
- [ ] External placements are described as owner placement, not automatic sync.
- [ ] No unsupported platform logo or catalog-update claim appears.
- [ ] Printed placement is not confused with automatic physical print replacement.
- [ ] URL and page identity remain readable.
- [ ] Final identity is uncluttered.
- [ ] Encoded master passes QR scan, mobile readability, and audio QA.

## Q. Conversion And Claim Contract

- **Conversion job:** Reframe QR as an access path to an approved source rather than the product category.
- **Primary proof:** QR, link tap, and saved shortcut reach one current MenuList page.
- **Allowed:** MenuList-generated QR/share paths and owner-placed use in supported contexts.
- **Required:** Stable destination, current menu, and owner-placement language.
- **Blocked:** Automatic updates to Instagram, WhatsApp catalogs, Google, delivery platforms, websites, or physical print already in circulation.
- **Release gate:** Test the rendered QR, destination URL, saved shortcut, and every named placement against current runtime.
