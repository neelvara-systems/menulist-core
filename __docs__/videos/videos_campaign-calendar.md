# Videos - Campaign Calendar

**Status:** Draft launch video calendar
**Created:** July 7, 2026
**Primary output:** Pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch video sequence

## Purpose

This calendar turns the video assets into a launch/distribution rhythm.

The campaign should build the problem first, then explain MenuList, then answer objections.

## AI-Era Funnel Overlay

The 2026 market angle should be used as a layer on top of the launch sequence:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use it only as readiness/source-quality language. Do not claim guaranteed Google ranking, ChatGPT recommendations, AI citations, traffic, or automatic external-platform updates.

Trust videos should repeat the control line:

```text
AI prepares. Owner approves. Customers see the current version.
```

| Funnel group | Videos | Viewer job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Old PDF Problem Reel, QR Stale Page Reel, One Link Everywhere Reel, Founder / Brand POV Video | My business information is more scattered than I thought. |
| Trust | Owner Approval Reel, AI Menu Manager Reel, Official Business Page Reel | MenuList will not publish random AI changes. I stay in control. |
| Conversion | 75-sec Launch Film, 30-sec Launch Cut, Product Demo, Photo/PDF Reel, Multi-location Reel | I can start with what I already have. |

## Pre-Launch: 10-14 Days Before Launch

Goal: make owners feel the scattered-list problem before showing the product.

| Order | Video | Channel fit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Your QR still works. The menu behind it may not. | Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn | Use QR scan and stale page visual |
| 2 | Old PDFs are still reaching customers. | Reels, Shorts | Pain hook, no product overload |
| 3 | WhatsApp price, Instagram price, printed price - which one is correct? | Reels, LinkedIn | Strong for restaurant and service-list owners |
| 4 | Founder/brand POV: why MenuList exists | LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube | Founder context, not feature dump |
| 5 | One approved customer link is coming | All channels | Teaser before launch film |

## Launch Week: Day 0 To Day 7

Goal: explain MenuList clearly and push owners toward customer-link creation.

| Day | Video | Main goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Main launch film | Explain the whole product story |
| Day 1 | 30-second launch cut | Repeat the core promise quickly |
| Day 2 | Product demo walkthrough | Show the real workflow |
| Day 3 | How it works short | Source -> preview -> approval -> customer link |
| Day 4 | AI Menu Manager short | Approval-card update story |
| Day 5 | Official Business Page / QR / print short | Show connected surfaces |
| Day 6 | Multi-location short | Show higher-value consistency |
| Day 7 | FAQ / objection handling short | Owner approval, no automatic external posting, existing-list setup |

## Post-Launch: Day 8 To Day 30

Goal: convert skeptical owners.

| Topic | Video angle | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic publishing | Will it publish automatically? | No. Owner approval first. |
| Existing menu | Can I upload my existing menu? | Yes. Photo, PDF, owned public link, or typed list. |
| After publishing | What happens after publishing? | QR, page, print, actions, activity, and feedback stay tied to the approved list. |
| Price changes | What if prices change? | Message in, approval card, receipt after supported update. |
| Multi-location | What about multiple outlets? | Master list with outlet-level flexibility. |
| External platforms | Does it update Google/Instagram/Zomato automatically? | No. Owners can place the approved link where customers look; direct external posting is not the promise. |

## Calendar Stop Rules

Do not continue broad posting if:

- product screenshots are still placeholders and not labeled;
- the CTA route is not final;
- owner comments show misunderstanding about automatic external updates;
- launch assets overpromise beyond live product truth;
- tracking cannot separate curiosity from approved customer-link activation.
