# MenuList Market, Format, And Script System

**Status:** Active founder-reviewed operating brief
**Created:** August 10, 2026
**Last cross-checked:** August 10, 2026
**Scope:** Current market interpretation, platform roles, canonical script decisions for the 12 core launch videos, and governing rules for the feature-launch extension

## Purpose

This document converts current video-market evidence into practical decisions for MenuList. It does not replace the 12 individual production handoffs. It controls how those handoffs are interpreted, adapted, tested, and updated when real assets arrive.

The feature-launch extension is governed by [videos_feature-videos.md](./videos_feature-videos.md) and the [Feature Launch Inventory](./feature-launch/feature-launch-inventory.md). Feature videos reuse this market, opening, motion, encoded-close, asset, and measurement contract; they do not create a second brand system.

The campaign is built around one proof chain:

```text
Use the menu or service list the owner already has
-> prepare a private customer-facing version
-> let the owner review and approve it
-> publish one approved customer link
-> keep verified MenuList surfaces tied to that approved source
```

The campaign must make MenuList easier to understand without reducing it to a QR menu maker. Setup relief is the entry point. Owner-approved public truth is the category.

## Market Map

### Restaurant And Menu Category

| Visible category | Typical promise | Common visual proof | MenuList response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| QR-menu tools | Scan a code and see a digital menu | QR scan, phone menu, dashboard editing | Do not compete on `QR menu` alone. Show that QR is one destination for an approved public source |
| POS and order/pay platforms | Scan, order, pay, increase throughput | Table QR, cart, payment, kitchen/POS routing | Do not imply MenuList is a POS or payment system. Show verified customer actions and existing links only |
| Restaurant growth suites | More traffic, orders, repeat customers, and rankings | Dashboards, testimonials, sales metrics, review logos | Do not copy unsupported growth claims. Compete on correctness, owner control, and lower repeated cleanup |
| Website/menu builders | Build a page and manually manage it | Templates, editors, customization controls | Lead with existing-source intake and private preview rather than another blank builder |
| Generic AI restaurant tools | AI does the work automatically | Robots, chat, glowing cards, autonomous changes | Show a normal owner message, a prepared card, explicit review, and approval before public impact |

### Current Opportunity

The market is crowded with `scan`, `order`, `grow`, `rank`, and `AI-powered` claims. MenuList should own a different problem:

```text
Customers keep finding different versions of the same business information.
MenuList gives the business one approved customer link.
```

The product should feel easy for a non-technical owner because it starts from the menu already on the owner's phone. That is the setup promise. The enduring value is that the customer-facing version remains owner-approved.

## Platform Jobs

### Feed-Native Video

YouTube describes Shorts as vertical, full-screen, sound-on, and immediately skippable. TikTok recommends stating the proposition in the first three seconds, establishing the hook in the first six seconds, using captions, and showing actual product use. The practical MenuList rule is:

```text
0:00-0:03  Recognizable owner problem or relief plus product context
0:03-0:06  First visible transformation or trust proof
0:06-end   One workflow, one conclusion, one companion action
```

Feed-native videos must be composed in `9:16`; they must not be center crops of landscape masters. Keep headline and product proof inside the maintained vertical safe area. The platform-provided clickable CTA belongs in the campaign configuration. The encoded MP4 closes with brand identity and a readable destination, not a fake interactive button.

### Website And YouTube Product Proof

Wistia's 2026 analysis places social-awareness video below one minute and educational/product demos in the one-to-five-minute range. The practical MenuList rule is:

- use `60-75s` for the complete category story;
- use `2:15-2:45` for the normal product demo and allow up to `3:00` only when every additional second proves a real step;
- make product UI readable rather than feed-fast;
- pair the embedded video with a real clickable `Create customer link` or `Start menu preview` action.

### LinkedIn And Founder Distribution

LinkedIn's analysis of more than 13,000 B2B video ads identifies recurring value in credible experts, conversational delivery, human presence, and mobile-native formats. For MenuList:

- the founder video should sound like a practical explanation, not a memorized advertisement;
- product cutaways must substantiate every product claim;
- founder, partner, investor, and multi-location distribution can use `16:9`, `4:5`, or native `9:16` according to placement;
- a local Indian-English voice is a trust choice, not a novelty effect.

### WhatsApp Sales Sharing

WhatsApp use is high intent but constrained by mobile viewing and file size. Use:

- `9:16` or mobile-legible `16:9` according to the conversation;
- `15-30s` proof cuts for first contact;
- the `2-3m` demo only after the owner asks how it works;
- a plain link in the message beside the video, rather than a button drawn inside the MP4.

## Script Rules

### Owner Language

Prefer:

- `The menu already on your phone is enough to start.`
- `Upload the photos or PDF you already have.`
- `No need to retype the whole menu before you begin.`
- `MenuList prepares a private customer preview.`
- `Review the items, prices, and business details.`
- `Nothing goes public until you approve.`
- `One approved customer link.`

Avoid:

- `No typing required.` as an absolute product claim;
- `Create your link in minutes.` until a current measured setup-time claim is approved;
- `We handle everything.` because owner review remains required;
- `Instant`, `automatic`, `effortless`, or `zero work` unless the exact behavior is visibly proved;
- `AI-powered`, `smart`, `revolutionary`, `10x`, `best`, or `guaranteed`;
- claims that MenuList updates Google, Instagram, WhatsApp catalogs, Zomato, Swiggy, delivery platforms, or any other unsupported external destination.

### Voiceover Density

Use an average planning rate of `2.2-2.5` spoken words per second, then time against the approved recorded voice. Leave room for UI comprehension and pauses.

| Runtime | Normal word target | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6s | 8-13 words | One hook or one proof, often text-led |
| 10-15s | 22-34 words | One pain and one conclusion |
| 20-30s | 35-68 words | One short workflow with room for a visible state change |
| 45-60s | 70-125 words | One feature or controlled product story with readable UI holds |
| 60-75s | 130-165 words | Complete launch narrative |
| 2:15-2:45 | 220-330 words | Practical product demonstration with cursor actions and inspection pauses |

Do not fill every second with narration. Approval, source transformation, and the final brand hold benefit from short pauses.

### Opening Contract

Every video must answer these questions quickly:

1. Is this about my current menu or service-list problem?
2. Is MenuList visible and relevant to the promised payoff?
3. Is there a real product action I have not already seen in a generic QR-menu ad?

The first poster-safe frame must remain useful when a phone gallery, social share, or muted autoplay displays it before motion begins.

### Motion Contract

- Motion explains state change: source -> preview, private -> approved, approved link -> supported surface.
- One focal movement at a time.
- A visible change may occur every `1.5-2.5s` in the opening without forcing a hard scene cut.
- Readable proof can hold `2-4s` when the UI needs inspection.
- Cursor movement is used only for real interaction, settles before a click, and never covers proof copy.
- No universal `1.7s` cut rule, constant stimulation, or automatic `10%` speed increase.

### Encoded Close

The normal final MP4 close contains:

```text
MenuList symbol
MenuList
One approved customer link
menulist.ai
```

The website, post, ad unit, or sales message supplies the clickable CTA. A specific video may use a text conclusion immediately before the final identity, but the final identity should not become a second sales slide.

## Testing And Measurement

Do not test several creative variables at once. Start with:

1. Freeze the audience, destination, body, audio, caption style, and CTA.
2. Test Hook A against Hook B.
3. Measure first-three-second hold, first-six-second hold, completion, qualified click, private-preview reached, and first approved publish where instrumentation exists.
4. Diagnose comprehension through comments, sales replies, and misunderstanding guard metrics.
5. Promote only versions that improve qualified downstream behavior without weakening claim accuracy.

Views are a distribution signal. The business outcome remains progress toward an approved customer link.

## Production Decision

All 12 videos remain required, but they are not one simultaneous render batch. The recommended order after real assets arrive is:

1. Photo/PDF To Customer Link reel as the fastest setup-proof test.
2. Owner Approval reel as the trust companion.
3. Thirty-second launch announcement using the validated setup and approval scenes.
4. Product demo from the verified continuous workflow.
5. Hero film from the now-proven modular scenes.
6. Pain, one-link, Official Business Page, AI Manager, multi-location, and founder assets according to launch timing and certification readiness.

This sequence reduces expensive rework because the hero reuses approved evidence rather than defining untested product scenes first.
