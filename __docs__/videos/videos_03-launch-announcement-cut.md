## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Market Role And Asset Gate

- **Market role:** Feed-native launch announcement and first paid-test candidate.
- **Opening contract:** State setup relief and show the existing-menu source in the first `0:03`.
- **First payoff:** Source photo/PDF begins becoming a private customer preview by `0:06`.
- **Asset gate:** Current signed-in intake, private preview, owner approval, and supported output captures.
- **Claim gate:** Do not use an unmeasured `in minutes` promise or an absolute `no typing` claim.
- **Native adaptations:** Produce independent `9:16`, `16:9`, and required `1:1` layouts with safe-zone QA.

## Video 3 - 30-Second Launch Announcement Cut

### A. Purpose

This fast cut introduces MenuList quickly for launch posts, paid ads, and social feeds.

### B. Audience

Cold and warm launch audiences, SMB owners, social viewers, paid-ad viewers, LinkedIn viewers.

### C. Placement

- launch post;
- Instagram Reel;
- YouTube Short;
- LinkedIn;
- paid ad;
- website secondary placement;
- WhatsApp sales.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 30 seconds.
- Reel cut: 15 seconds.
- Paid ad cut: 15-30 seconds.

### E. Core Message

MenuList turns your current menu or service list into one approved customer link.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should understand: "This solves the scattered menu problem without publishing before approval."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Your current menu is enough to start.
2. Product entry: Sign in, then upload existing photos or PDF.
3. Problem: Old PDFs, screenshots, and QR pages keep different versions circulating.
4. Proof: Review before publishing.
5. Outcome: QR, page, print, and customer links stay tied to the approved version.
6. Close: MenuList identity, tagline, and `menulist.ai`; companion CTA is `Create customer link`.

```text
MenuList. One approved customer link.
```

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use fast but readable transitions. One clear UI highlight per scene. Use an approved-link hub to connect outputs. Avoid clutter.

### M. Sound Direction

Music can be slightly more energetic than the hero film but still calm. Use quick tap and scan SFX. Voiceover should be direct and steady.

- 9:16 safe zones are respected.

- Funnel stage: setup relief -> product understanding.

- Guard metric: first approved publish quality and absence of automatic-external-update assumptions.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `launch_cut_v1_approved_link_30s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until the exact cut has deeper create-menu attribution and an eligible ledger row.
