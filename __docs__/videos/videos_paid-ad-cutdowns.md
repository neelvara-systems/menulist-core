# Videos - Paid Ad Cutdowns

**Status:** Draft ad scripts and guardrails
**Created:** July 7, 2026
**Primary output:** 6-second, 15-second, and 30-second paid ad cuts

## Purpose

Paid ad cutdowns are for controlled tests after the activation and tracking gates are ready.

Do not start broad paid spend before the upload -> preview -> approval -> publish -> two-surface activation chain can be measured.

## Ad Rules

1. One problem, one product action, one CTA.
2. Keep claims narrower than organic/founder content.
3. Do not use fake testimonials, fake metrics, fake revenue charts, or fake customer logos.
4. Do not claim automatic external-platform updates.
5. Do not claim sales lift, ranking lift, traffic lift, AI citations, or conversion improvement.
6. Use demo visuals or permissioned real proof.

## 6-Second Bumper

**Use:** Retargeting, YouTube bumper, high-frequency social cut

```text
Old PDFs. Wrong prices. Stale QR links. MenuList gives your business one approved customer link.
```

CTA:

```text
Create customer link
```

## 15-Second Ad

**Use:** Instagram/Facebook/YouTube Shorts launch test

```text
Customers still find old menus across WhatsApp, QR, Instagram, and print. MenuList turns your current menu into one approved customer link. Review before publishing. Keep QR, page, and print tied to the latest version.
```

CTA:

```text
Start menu preview
```

## 30-Second Ad

**Use:** Retargeting, cold-test explainer, LinkedIn/social feed

```text
Your menu changes. But old PDFs, screenshots, QR pages, and printed links can keep showing the wrong version. MenuList starts from your current menu or service list, prepares a private preview, and waits for your approval. Once published, the same approved list supports your customer link, QR, official page, print files, and sharing links. MenuList: one approved list, every customer link.
```

## Format Matrix

| Format | Primary use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:16 | Reels, Shorts, vertical ads | Default for paid social tests |
| 1:1 | Feed placements | Keep text centered and large |
| 16:9 | YouTube, LinkedIn, website retargeting | Use product screen plus simple caption blocks |

## Creative Variants

| Variant | Hook | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old PDF | Old PDFs are still reaching customers | Cold awareness |
| QR stale page | The QR works. The page behind it is stale | QR-aware owners |
| Price drift | WhatsApp price, Instagram price, printed price | Food and service-list businesses |
| Owner approval | Nothing goes live until you approve | Trust objection |
| One link | One approved customer link | Retargeting and brand recall |

## Paid Launch Gates

Do not run paid ads until:

- the CTA route is final;
- tracking is in place for click, preview start, preview prepared, approval, publish, and two-surface activation;
- final WhatsApp/campaign destination is approved if using WhatsApp traffic;
- demo visuals are approved;
- landing page copy matches the ad claim;
- founder has set budget ceiling and stop rules.

## Stop Rules

Pause or revise if:

- owners click but do not start a preview;
- owners start previews but do not approve;
- comments show misunderstanding such as "does this update Google automatically?";
- ad platform comments frame MenuList as only a QR menu app;
- acquisition cost is being measured before activation quality is proven.
