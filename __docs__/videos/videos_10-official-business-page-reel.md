## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 10 - Official Business Page Reel

### A. Purpose

This reel shows that MenuList is bigger than a menu page by placing menu/service list, business details, and actions in one official customer link.

### B. Audience

SMB owners, service businesses, restaurants/cafes, owners who currently rely on WhatsApp/Instagram/Google/profile links, setup partners.

### C. Placement

- Instagram Reel;
- YouTube Short;
- TikTok;
- LinkedIn;
- website feature page;
- WhatsApp sales;
- paid retargeting;
- sales deck.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 45-60 seconds.
- Reel cut: 30 seconds.
- 9:16 and 16:9 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

Your customer should not search five places to know what is current.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should understand: "MenuList can become my practical customer-facing page, not just a QR menu."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Customers need more than a menu.
2. Problem: Menu, hours, contact, and location are scattered.
3. Product entry: Official Business Page.
4. Proof: Name, list, hours, photos, directions, actions.
5. Outcome: QR/Instagram/WhatsApp can point to the same page.
6. CTA: Put one trusted customer link online.

### I. Full Voiceover Script

```text
Customers do not only need your menu.

They need the correct business details around it.

MenuList gives your business one official page for menu, hours, location, photos, contact, and customer actions.

Customers can read the current list and act from the same page.

Use the same page in your QR, Instagram bio, packaging, WhatsApp replies, or website.

Because it stays tied to the approved list, public details are easier to keep aligned.
```

### J. On-Screen Text List

1. Menu is only one part.
2. Hours. Contact. Location. Photos.
3. One official customer link.
4. Menu. Hours. Actions.
5. Same link. Same approved list.
6. Tied to the approved list.
7. Put one trusted customer link online.

### K. UI Screens Required

### L. Motion Design Direction

Start cluttered, then simplify into one page. Use tap indicators on actions. Use a hub-and-spoke motion from the Official Business Page to owner-placed surfaces.

### M. Sound Direction

Use calm music and light tap SFX. Voiceover should be steady. Avoid dramatic reveal sounds.

### N. Design Checklist

- Shows business details, not only menu.
- Shows current list and actions on the same page.
- External channels are owner placements.
- No ranking or discovery guarantee appears.
- CTA is practical.

### P. CTA

Put one trusted customer link online.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: product understanding.
- Belief change: `MenuList gives customers the current list and the business details/actions around it in one approved place.`
- Proof moment: current menu/service list, hours, contact, location, photos, and supported actions appear on one Official Business Page.
- Linked action: `Create customer link` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: first approved customer link per qualified referred session.
- Guard metric: MenuList is not interpreted as automatic external-profile management or a generic website builder.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `official_page_v1_one_current_place_45s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until final UI proof and deeper create-menu attribution are approved.
