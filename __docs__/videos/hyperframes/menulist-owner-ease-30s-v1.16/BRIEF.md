# MenuList Owner Ease v1.16 Brief

## Objective

Make a busy, non-technical SMB owner understand within the first seconds that MenuList starts from the menu photos or PDF they already have.

## Single Message

Already have a menu? Start there.

## Audience Tension

The owner assumes a digital menu product will require typing the menu again, learning software, or rebuilding business information.

## Demonstrated Payoff

Menu photos or a PDF become a private customer preview. The owner reviews it before anything becomes public. One approved customer link then supports verified MenuList outputs.

## Distribution

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, mobile WhatsApp sales sharing, and vertical paid placements.

## Retention Requirements

1. No logo-only intro.
2. Brand, hook, and product proof are visible at encoded frame zero.
3. The opening product action is understandable without audio.
4. Something meaningful changes every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds in the first six seconds.
5. Motion must show intake, preparation, approval, or propagation. Decorative motion does not count.
6. Static holds remain long enough to read and are not shortened by a blanket speed-up.

## Frozen Inputs

- `v1.14` narration and word timings.
- `v1.14` One Link Motion audio master.
- Existing MenuList product claims and proof order.
- Original logo geometry and final logo animation.
- Final lockup: MenuList, One approved customer link, menulist.ai.
- Linked destination: `https://menulist.ai/create-menu`.

## Exclusions

- No AI hype or autonomous-change implication.
- No unsupported external-platform synchronization.
- No fake metrics, rankings, testimonials, ratings, logos, or growth outcomes.
- No dark device frames, neon styling, repeated blue patterns, or decorative floating cards.
- No claim that virality, retention, or conversion is guaranteed.
