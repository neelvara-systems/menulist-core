# MenuList Owner Ease 30s v1.7

**Status:** Founder-review horizontal pass
**Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, 30.037 seconds
**Audience:** Busy, non-technical SMB owners who already have a menu or service list

This is a fresh production pass based on the approved Owner Ease storyboard and the current MenuList video workflow. It preserves the frozen `v1.0` narration and music while applying the newer MenuList motion language, live-site typography, claim controls, transition QA, and immutable versioning rules.

## Story

1. MenuList is for busy owners, not technical setup.
2. The owner starts with menu photos or a PDF already on the phone.
3. MenuList prepares a private customer preview without full menu retyping.
4. The owner reviews items, prices, and business details.
5. Nothing becomes public before owner approval.
6. One approved customer link supports verified MenuList outputs.
7. The workflow resolves into one concrete CTA and a held MenuList close.

## Production Decisions

- Inter only, zero letter spacing, restrained MenuList website gradient.
- Plain original MenuList identity at encoded frame zero and the final frame.
- Frozen two-path symbol draw and left-to-right wordmark fill.
- Quiet bottom-right identity during product scenes.
- UI-led proof with no stock footage, heavy device frames, neon treatment, or repeated background pattern.
- Masked scene handoffs retain proof while preventing blank flashes and double-exposed headlines.
- No automatic external-platform update, ranking, AI-answer placement, growth, testimonial, or invented-metric claim.

## Audio
