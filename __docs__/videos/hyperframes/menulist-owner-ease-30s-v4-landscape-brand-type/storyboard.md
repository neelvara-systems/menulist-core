## Guardrails

- Do not show internal scene labels or production metadata in any public frame.
- Do not add lower-third summary cards that duplicate the readable headline, body, or proof UI.
- Retain only unique necessary callouts, accessibility captions when required, and the quiet bottom-right MenuList identity watermark.

- No AI hype.
- No automatic external-platform update claims.
- No ranking, traffic, sales, or recommendation promises.
- Keep BGM lower than voice.
- Keep final frame visually useful, not decorative empty space.
- Keep encoded frame zero useful for phone and social sharing previews; the brand lockup must be visible before animation begins.
- Use cursor and tap animations only to clarify owner actions.
- Keep glass styling restrained; avoid neon, blue glow, and futuristic AI visuals.
- Use reference videos only as high-level motion-card inspiration, not as direct visual replicas.
