# Owner Ease 30s v1.14 Conversion Contract

## Job

Move a busy, non-technical SMB owner from `I would need to rebuild or type my menu` to `I can start with the menu photos or PDF I already have and approve the customer version before it is public`.

## Audience

Restaurant, cafe, and service-business owners who already have a usable menu or service list but do not want another technical setup project.

## Single Message

Start from what you already have, review the private customer version, and put one approved customer link online.

## Proof Order

1. Existing menu photos and PDF.
2. No full menu retyping.
3. Private customer preview.
4. Owner review and approval.
5. One approved customer link.
6. Verified MenuList outputs: QR, official page, print files, sharing, and customer actions.

## CTA

- Display close: `MenuList` / `One approved customer link` / `menulist.ai`
- Destination for linked placements: `https://menulist.ai/create-menu`
- Campaign identity: `owner_ease_v1_14_centered_url_30s_16x9`

The MP4 contains no button-shaped CTA. Platform buttons, post copy, and linked placements carry the conversion action.

## Primary Metric

First approved customer link per qualified referred session.

## Guard Metrics

- Private preview reached per referred session when that event becomes available.
- Owner approval completed per referred session when that event becomes available.
- Category misunderstanding or unsupported-integration questions.

Use `Not available` until product or platform instrumentation provides real values. Do not infer or invent campaign performance.

## Distribution Gate

Founder-review render only. Paid or public distribution remains blocked until final founder approval, destination verification, current service-terms review, and campaign attribution readiness.
