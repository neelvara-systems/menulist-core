Most business owners do not wake up thinking about menu software.

They just want customers to see the correct menu, correct prices, correct hours, and the right way to contact them.

But in real life, public information spreads everywhere: QR codes, WhatsApp, Instagram, old PDFs, print, and staff phones.

That is why MenuList is built around one approved customer link.

Start with the list your business already has. Review the version customers will see. Publish it after approval.

Then keep QR, page, print, and customer actions tied to that approved version.

When something changes, MenuList should reduce repeated cleanup, not create another dashboard to manage.

In the AI era, scattered public information becomes a bigger problem. MenuList keeps the business source clear, current, and owner approved.

MenuList is for owners who want customers to see the current version, even when no one is watching.
