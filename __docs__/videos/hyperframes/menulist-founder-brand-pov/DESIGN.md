# MenuList Founder POV - HyperFrames Design

## Style Prompt

Founder-led MenuList positioning video without relying on founder footage yet. The visual language should feel sincere, operational, and owner-first: warm editorial canvas, large founder-style quote cards, practical product cutaways, phone-first customer surfaces, and controlled movement. The story is public business truth, not QR-menu software and not AI hype.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F7F3EA` warm off-white
- Ink: `#17201C` deep green-black
- Muted text: `#66736B`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft surface: `#ECE5D8`
- MenuList green: `#0F7A5A`
- Action amber: `#D98B2B`
- Problem red: `#B64B3A`
- Line: `#D8D0C2`

## Typography

- Headline: `MenuList Serif`, backed by local Georgia, 800-900 weight
- Body/UI: `MenuList Sans`, backed by local Avenir Next, 350-700 weight
- Data/labels: `MenuList Mono`, backed by local Menlo, 500-700 weight

Use large editorial typography for founder POV statements and smaller, dense typography only inside product/UI mockups.

## Motion

- Pacing: calm, human, and deliberate.
- Scene changes: soft paper wipes, product slides, and gentle fades.
- Entrances: 0.35-0.7s, mostly y-motion and opacity.
- Product proof: subtle tap rings, outlines, and approval-state checks.
- Captions: large, sentence-case, two lines maximum.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use robots, AI brains, blue glow, or futuristic AI imagery.
- Do not make MenuList look like a generic SaaS dashboard.
- Do not show unsupported external-platform updates.
- Do not claim ranking, traffic, sales lift, or AI recommendations.
- Do not use fake testimonials, fake customer logos, or fake metrics.
