# MenuList Owner Ease 30s V2 - Storyboard

**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Aspect:** 16:9, 1920 x 1080  
**Core message:** Upload what you already have. Review before publishing. Put one approved customer link online.

| Time | Beat | Visual direction | Voiceover | On-screen text | QA frame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-4.2 | Non-technical owner hook | Brand bug, large headline, phone gallery and PDF mockup | MenuList is built for busy owners, not technical setup. | Do not type your menu again. | 1.2s |
| 4.2-9.6 | Existing source to preview | Source cards and quick workflow strip | Take photos of the menu you already have, or upload a PDF. MenuList turns it into a private customer preview. | Photos in. Preview prepared. | 6.0s |
| 9.6-15.8 | Trust and owner approval | Private preview panel, not public state, approval rows, approve button | You do not type the full menu again. You review the items, prices, and business details. Nothing goes public until you approve. | You stay in control. | 12.4s |
| 15.8-23.2 | Approved link outcome | Approved link hub with QR, page, print, WhatsApp, actions | Once approved, one customer link powers your QR, page, print files, WhatsApp sharing, and customer actions. | One approved customer link. | 18.8s |
| 23.2-30.0 | Final CTA | Logo, large CTA, right-side outcome panel | MenuList. Upload what you have. Put one approved customer link online. | Put one approved customer link online. | 26.0s |

## Guardrails

- No AI hype.
- No automatic external-platform update claims.
- No ranking, traffic, sales, or recommendation promises.
- Keep BGM lower than voice.
- Keep final frame visually useful, not decorative empty space.
