# MenuList Launch Announcement - HyperFrames Design

## Style Prompt

Calm operational product video for MenuList. The visual language should feel like a trusted SMB owner tool: light canvas, readable product UI, phone/laptop mockups, practical before/after contrast, and restrained motion. The product truth is the hero. Avoid AI hype, neon SaaS styling, robot imagery, revenue charts, fake testimonials, and external-platform automation visuals.

This project uses the real MenuList website theme and app icon, not the earlier temporary green draft theme.

- Warm accent: `#DE77A1`

- Success green: `#059669`

## Typography

- Headline: `MenuList Serif`, backed by local Georgia, 800-900 weight
- Body/UI: `MenuList Sans`, backed by local Avenir Next, 350-700 weight
- Data/labels: `MenuList Mono`, backed by local Menlo, 500-700 weight

The serif headline gives founder-level authority; the sans/mono layers keep product UI and operational proof clear.

## Motion

- Pacing: calm and deliberate.
- Transitions: cream paper wipes and UI slides, no glitch effects.
- Entrances: short 0.35-0.7s movements with varied easing.
- Highlights: soft outlines, tap rings, small check states.
- Captions: large, sentence-case, two lines maximum.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use gradients as the main visual idea.
- Do not use robots, AI brains, blue glow, or futuristic dashboards.
- Do not show Google, Instagram, Zomato, Swiggy, or delivery platforms as if MenuList updates them.
- Do not use sales lift, ranking, traffic, or AI recommendation claims.
- Do not make the UI feel like a generic SaaS dashboard.
