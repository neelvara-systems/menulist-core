## Video direction

- Use the canonical MenuList `frame.md`, Inter everywhere, zero letter spacing, and one website-gradient decision phrase per scene.
- Keep the canvas pale and operational, with no repeated blue pattern, neon treatment, or full-screen decorative typography.
- Use one focal movement at a time. Motion explains intake, preparation, approval, or propagation.
- Use overlapping refocus transitions so neither side of a cut becomes blank or flickers.
- Keep the quiet in-scene MenuList identity bottom-right; use the large two-column lockup only on the opening and final slates.
- Keep captions centered above the lower safe area, no pill, two lines maximum, with muted upcoming words, blue current word, and dark completed words.
- Keep the retained Tara narration and visual timing unchanged. Use the documented Outlet Control comparison master with deeper speech carving, voice-reactive ducking, and a controlled post-narration lift.
- Claims remain limited to photo/PDF intake, private preview, owner approval, and verified MenuList outputs.
