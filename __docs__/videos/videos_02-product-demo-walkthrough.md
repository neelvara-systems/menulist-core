## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 2 - 2-3 Minute Product Demo Walkthrough

### A. Purpose

This video shows how MenuList actually works. It is the practical proof video for owners who need to see the workflow before trusting the product.

### B. Audience

SMB owners, sales prospects, onboarding users, setup partners, founder-led sales conversations, support/success reviewers.

### C. Placement

- website;
- YouTube;
- WhatsApp sales;
- onboarding;
- product demo;
- sales deck;
- founder follow-up messages;
- sales calls.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 2-3 minutes.
- Sales cut: 90 seconds.
- Onboarding cut: 60-90 seconds.
- Social proof cut: 45 seconds.

### E. Core Message

MenuList starts from your current list, prepares a private preview, and publishes the approved customer link.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "This is practical, I can start from what I already have, and I can review before customers see it."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Most businesses already have a menu or list.
2. Problem: Customers keep finding different versions.
3. Product entry: Upload photo/PDF, paste owned link, or type list.
4. Proof: Preview, review, approval, customer link, QR, page, customer browsing.
5. Outcome: Updates stay tied to approval and public surfaces.
6. CTA: Start menu preview.

### I. Full Voiceover Script

```text
Most businesses already have a menu or service list.

The problem is that customers keep finding different versions: old PDFs, screenshots, QR pages, social links, and printed copies.

MenuList starts from what you already have: menu photos, a PDF, an existing public link you own, or a service list.

No typing is required to start. MenuList prepares the customer-facing version for owner review.

MenuList prepares the customer-facing version for owner review.

You check items, prices, categories, and business details before anything goes live.

After approval, the public customer link goes live.

The same approved list can support your QR menu, official business page, print files, and sharing links.

Customers can search, jump sections, switch language when available, and use actions like call, WhatsApp, directions, booking, or ordering links.

When prices, availability, or notes change, MenuList prepares the update and waits for approval before important work goes live.

After publishing, owners can keep the approved list current and see useful activity or private customer reports where available.

Start with your current list. Review the customer version. Put one trusted customer link online.
```

### K. UI Screens Required

- upload photo/PDF screen;
- private preview screen;
- owner approval screen;
- customer menu page;
- Official Business Page;
- QR/table card mockup;
- print file mockup;
- AI Menu Manager approval card;
- Business Health / `No action needed` state;
- activity/feedback screen only if accurate and necessary.

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use screen recording as the backbone. Use cursor highlights and tap indicators. Use subtle zooms only to make UI readable. Use chapter title cards between sections if the demo feels too dense. Keep motion slower than social reels.

### M. Sound Direction

Music should stay underneath the voiceover. Use light UI click sounds for approve, link copy, QR scan, and navigation. Pause slightly before "Nothing goes live" or "Owner approval" moments.

### N. Design Checklist

- All UI is legible in 16:9.
- The approval step is not skipped.
- Demo data is clean and fictional or permissioned.
- External platform references are owner-placement only.
- Activity and feedback are shown only if accurate.
- The final CTA matches the active funnel.

### O. Versioning Requirements

- 16:9 master with VO and captions.
- 16:9 clean no-caption version.
- 9:16 short demo cut.
- 1:1 social crop.
- 60-90 second onboarding cut.
- 45-second sales follow-up cut.
- Thumbnail frame.

### P. CTA

Start menu preview.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: evaluation.
- Belief change: `I can follow this workflow with my current menu and review the result before publishing.`
- Proof moment: complete upload/import -> structured preview -> approval -> public link -> controlled update path.
- Linked action: `Start menu preview` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: private preview reached and first approved publish per referred demo session.
- Guard metric: completion of source intake without increased abandonment or approval confusion.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `demo_v1_upload_to_publish_180s_16x9`.
- Paid eligibility: demo is primarily website/sales/onboarding; any paid cutdown requires its own brief and ledger row.
