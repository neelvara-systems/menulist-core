# Videos - Short-Form Reels And Shorts

**Status:** Draft scripts and content bank
**Created:** July 7, 2026
**Primary output:** 8-30 second vertical videos

**Mandatory production standard:** [MenuList Founder-Approved Video Production Standard](./videos_founder-approved-production-standard.md).

## Purpose

Short-form videos create repeated awareness without making MenuList sound repetitive.

They should rotate through pain, trust, setup, product proof, and objections while always returning to one approved customer link.

## Format Rules

- Default format: 9:16.
- Keep each video understandable without sound.
- Put the hook in the first 2 seconds.
- Keep on-screen text large enough for phone viewing.
- Use product visuals, not generic restaurant stock footage.
- Use demo/sample labels when showing fictional public pages.

## Funnel Categories

| Category | Goal | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain | Make the scattered-list problem obvious | The QR works. The page behind it is stale. |
| Setup | Show the first action is easy | Start from a photo or PDF. |
| Trust | Show owner approval and no silent publishing | Nothing goes live until you approve. |
| Product proof | Show the connected customer link | QR, page, print, and actions point to one approved list. |
| Objection | Answer a worry directly | Will it publish automatically? No. |
| Brand | Reinforce doctrine | Your public list should come from your business. |

## Serialized Short-Form Series

Use short-form video as repeated product education, not random viral posting.

| Series | Purpose | Example hooks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menu Mistakes | Show real owner pain | Your QR works. The page behind it is stale. |
| Fix In 20 Seconds | Show one practical product action | Photo menu to customer link. |
| Owner Control | Build trust around approval | Nothing goes public until you approve. |
| AI Without Risk | Explain AI safely | AI prepares. Owner approves. |

AI-era hooks must stay factual:

- Use: One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
- Use: AI prepares. Owner approves. Customers see the current version.
- Use: Make your business easy to understand for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
- Avoid: MenuList gets you ranked in ChatGPT.
- Avoid: MenuList guarantees AI recommendations.

Treat every search and AI-era hook as readiness/source-quality language, not a ranking or recommendation claim.

## Reel Script - Old PDF Problem

**Duration:** 12-15 seconds
**Format:** 9:16
**Goal:** Awareness

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2s | Customer opens old PDF | Text: Your old menu PDF is still selling for you. Badly. |
| 2-6s | Old price shown | Customers keep finding screenshots and files no one remembers to update. |
| 6-11s | Current MenuList customer link opens | MenuList gives your business one approved customer link instead. |
| 11-15s | Link and CTA | Update the list. Keep the link. |

Final text:

```text
One approved list. Every customer link.
```

## Reuse Rules

- A 75-second launch film should produce 6-8 reels.
- The 2-3 minute demo should produce 3-5 short clips.
- Feature videos should produce 1 hook cut and 1 objection cut each.
- Do not run the same hook repeatedly; rotate pain, setup, trust, proof, and objection.
