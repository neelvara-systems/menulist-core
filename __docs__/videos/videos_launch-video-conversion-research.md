# MenuList Launch Video Conversion Research

**Status:** Founder-reviewed operating standard  
**Created:** July 11, 2026  
**Scope:** How MenuList launch videos should create, measure, and improve qualified customer-link starts in the AI era

## Founder Verdict

A successful MenuList launch video is not one film that directly creates sales by itself.

It is the first part of a conversion system:

```text
Recognize the problem
-> Believe setup is easy
-> Trust owner control
-> See the product work
-> Start a customer link
-> Reach private preview
-> Approve and publish
```

The strongest conversion idea for MenuList is:

```text
Upload the menu photos or PDF already on your phone.
MenuList prepares the customer version.
You review it before publishing.
```

This keeps the public-business-truth positioning intact while removing the largest likely objection for a non-technical SMB owner: "I do not have time to type and configure another system."

The video should not optimize for views alone. It should optimize for qualified owners who understand the workflow and continue into `/create-menu`.

## What Video Can Change

### 1. Problem Recognition

Short pain-led videos make an owner recognize that the problem is not the QR code or PDF format by itself. The problem is that old public versions continue answering customer questions.

Desired viewer thought:

```text
My menu and business details are more scattered than I realized.
```

### 2. Setup Confidence

Showing photo/PDF upload before any dashboard reduces the perceived setup cost.

Desired viewer thought:

```text
I can start with what is already on my phone. I do not need to retype everything.
```

### 3. Trust

Private preview and owner approval reduce fear of incorrect or uncontrolled public changes.

Desired viewer thought:

```text
MenuList helps with the work, but I control what customers see.
```

### 4. Product Understanding

The launch film gives the market category. The practical demo proves the workflow. Reels repeat one memorable benefit at a time.

Desired viewer thought:

```text
This is one approved public source, not another QR menu maker.
```

### 5. Action

One concrete CTA moves the viewer to the next owner action:

```text
Create customer link
```

Do not ask the same viewer to create a link, book a demo, contact sales, follow the page, and watch another video in one asset.

## What The Evidence Does Not Prove

Do not publish any of these claims:

- A MenuList launch video will increase conversion by a fixed percentage.
- Video viewers will become paying customers.
- A polished video is enough without a clear landing page and product flow.
- AI-generated visuals improve conversion.
- A high completion rate means the viewer understood MenuList.
- Platform-attributed conversions were caused only by the video.
- A distribution algorithm has no choice but to push a high-retention video.
- `70%` is a universal high-performance retention target.
- Viewers who pass `30s` remain because of sunk-cost fallacy.
- A cut every `1.7s` improves comprehension or conversion for every audience.
- Speeding every final video by `10%` improves business results.

## July 2026 Viral-Launch Article Review

The July 16, 2026 article `The Psychology of Viral Launch Videos` is useful as a production-discipline input, not as a scientific or platform guarantee.

### Adopt

- earn the first seconds instead of using a long logo-only introduction;
- keep the thumbnail, caption, opening frame, and hook promise consistent;
- introduce the product and its value early;
- show the product rather than describing it with generic sales adjectives;
- storyboard every line against visible product proof;
- make motion explain the workflow;
- iterate and review the complete stitch before publishing;
- use retention curves to diagnose where the story loses attention.

### Adapt For MenuList

- preserve the poster-safe frame-zero identity while combining it with the owner hook and real source/product evidence;
- target one meaningful visual event every `1.5-2.5s` in the first `6s`, then allow readable `2-4s` proof holds;
- make the first aha the existing-menu-to-private-preview transformation;
- use owner approval as the trust payoff;
- use one-link propagation only for verified MenuList outputs;
- keep one focal movement at a time and prefer stillness over decorative breathing or drifting;
- test native `9:16` hooks without changing the body, voice, music, CTA, destination, or audience.

### Reject

- `Virality is not luck` or `the algorithm has no choice` as operating claims;
- retention as the only recommendation or business-success signal;
- unsourced universal performance benchmarks;
- sunk-cost manipulation as a design objective;
- mandatory hyperactive cutting or constant stimulation;
- automatic final-video speed increases;
- using views or completion as a substitute for approved customer-link outcomes.

Official platform guidance supports early promise delivery and product visibility, but it also describes recommendation and performance as multi-signal systems:

- [YouTube recommendation performance guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650?hl=en)
- [YouTube audience retention guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en)
- [Meta awareness video guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-objectives/awareness)
- [Meta Reels creative guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- [Think with Google YouTube effectiveness guide](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/5679/YT_Effectiveness_Guide.pdf)

Research on editing supports using cuts and change to manage attention, but it also shows that discontinuous or excessive editing can divert attention from narrative understanding. The MenuList standard therefore optimizes meaningful progression rather than cut count:

- [Cinematographic continuity edits and viewer attention](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2023.1173704/full)
- [Attention and sunk-cost sensitivity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33692720/)

The external numbers justify testing and measurement. They do not justify a MenuList performance promise.

## AI-Era Conversion Principle

AI has lowered the cost of producing average-looking video. That makes polish alone less differentiated.

MenuList should win through verified specificity:

- show the actual owner problem;
- show an existing menu photo or PDF;
- show a private preview;
- show the approval decision;
- show the customer link live;
- use a human-reviewed Indian-English voice or founder voice;
- keep UI and claims visibly credible;
- use AI as a production assistant and controlled product assistant, not as the story's hero.

The relevant AI line is:

```text
AI prepares. Owner approves. Customers see the current version.
```

The relevant launch line is:

```text
Upload what you already have. Create one approved customer link in minutes.
```

## Measurement Model

### North-Star Conversion

For video campaigns, the meaningful business result is:

```text
Owner-approved customer links published per qualified video-referred session.
```

This is more useful than raw views, likes, or create-menu clicks.

### Measurement Ladder

| Level | Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Impressions, unique reach | The asset was delivered |
| Attention | 3-second hold, 5/10-second engaged view, 25% completion | The hook earned initial attention |
| Understanding | 50% and 75% completion, replay, sound-on rate where available | The owner stayed for product proof |
| Intent | Landing visit, `create_customer_link_clicked`, pricing visit, WhatsApp CTA | The owner requested a next step |
| Product progress | Source selected, upload completed, private preview reached | Setup fear was actually overcome |
| Trust progress | Review completed, owner claim completed | The owner accepted the governed workflow |
| Business conversion | First approved customer link published | The video-referred journey produced the intended product outcome |
| Quality | Link remains live, owner returns, no immediate abandonment | The conversion was useful rather than accidental |

### Existing Repo Evidence

The public website already records the broad `create_customer_link_clicked` event for internal links to `/create-menu` through `WebsiteMarketingClickTracker`.

The current gap is deeper attribution. The public create-menu flow does not currently expose a marketing measurement chain for:

- source selected;
- upload submitted;
- private preview reached;
- owner claim completed;
- first approved customer link published.

Before paid launch, add a bounded, consent-aware attribution design that carries campaign context through these milestones without storing raw sensitive menu content or creating a new high-cost analytics path.

This research document does not authorize that runtime change. It records the measurement requirement for a separate implementation review.

## A/B Test Plan

Test one meaningful variable at a time.

| Test | Variant A | Variant B | Decision metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | Do not type your menu again | Already have a menu? Start there | Qualified create-link starts per landing session |
| Pain framing | Old PDF still reaching customers | One price in five public places | 3-second hold plus qualified create-link rate |
| Proof order | Upload -> preview -> approval | Approval -> upload -> preview | 50% completion plus private-preview rate |
| CTA | Create customer link | Start menu preview | Private-preview rate, not click rate alone |
| Voice | Indian-English human-reviewed voice | Founder-recorded voice | Completion plus qualified conversion |
| Destination | Homepage | Direct `/create-menu` entry | Approved publish rate per referred session |

Rules:

- Define the primary metric before the test starts.
- Keep audience, placement, budget, and run window comparable.
- Use native platform A/B tools when available.
- Do not call a winner from view count alone.
- Do not call a winner before the planned sample is reached.
- Calculate the sample from the baseline conversion rate and minimum detectable effect; until MenuList has a baseline, results are directional.
- Reject a variant that increases clicks but lowers preview, claim, or publish quality.

## Launch Measurement Sequence

### Baseline Phase

Run the current Owner Ease V4 organically and in founder/WhatsApp distribution first.

Collect:

- platform retention curve;
- CTA clicks;
- landing sessions;
- create-menu starts;
- qualitative owner questions;
- common abandonment point.

### Controlled Paid Test

After milestone attribution is ready:

1. Test two hooks in native 9:16.
2. Keep the same body, CTA, audience, budget, and destination.
3. Optimize to the deepest reliable product event, not video views.
4. Retarget engaged viewers with Owner Approval or the 2-3 minute demo.
5. Compare approved-link outcomes and cost per qualified start.

### Website Test

Test placement, not just creative:

- hero-adjacent video with static CTA visible;
- setup section video immediately before `/create-menu` CTA;
- no autoplay audio;
- poster frame states the owner benefit;
- the CTA remains available without requiring the viewer to finish the video.

### Sales And WhatsApp Use

Use the 30-second Owner Ease cut for the first explanation.

Send the 2-3 minute demo only after the owner asks how the process works. Use a distinct `sales_share` UTM so manually assisted conversions are not mixed with organic social performance.

## Conversion Review Scorecard

Review every public cut against this scorecard.

| Question | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Does the first frame identify one owner problem or relief? | Understandable without prior MenuList knowledge |
| Is the product visible early? | Existing menu source or MenuList UI appears in the opening proof sequence |
| Does it reduce setup fear? | Upload from existing photo/PDF is explicit |
| Is owner control visible? | Private preview or approval is shown, not only narrated |
| Is the category clear? | The result is an approved customer link, not only a QR code |
| Is the CTA singular? | One action, one destination |
| Is the cut native to its platform? | Correct aspect, safe zones, captions, audio mix, readable UI |
| Can we identify the exact version? | Unique video id and UTM content value |
| Can we measure beyond views? | CTA and deeper product milestones are available or the asset remains organic-only |
| Are claims factual? | No ranking, revenue, traffic, or automatic external-platform promises |

## Founder Decisions

1. Keep the current Owner Ease angle as the first conversion test.
2. Produce a native 9:16 version before paid distribution.
3. Create one alternate hook while keeping the body and CTA unchanged.
4. Use founder/human-reviewed Indian-English delivery as the trust default.
5. Keep AI behind the production process and inside the controlled approval story.
6. Add deeper consent-aware create-menu milestone attribution before paid scale.
7. Judge success by approved customer-link outcomes, not views alone.

## Final Principle

```text
The launch video should make starting feel smaller than the owner's current pain.
```

For MenuList, conversion comes from showing that one existing menu photo can become one reviewed customer link without retyping, uncontrolled publishing, or repeated cleanup.
