# Videos - Future AI Search Ready Video

**Status:** Future concept, not part of the initial 12-video launch pack
**Created:** July 7, 2026
**Related strategy:** [AI-era market strategy layer](./videos_ai-era-market-strategy.md)

## Purpose

This is a future awareness video concept for the AI-era discovery angle. It should not be produced until the first 12-video launch pack is complete and the team has approved search/AI wording.

## Working Title

```text
Is your business ready for AI search?
```

## Duration

30-45 seconds.

## Hook

```text
AI cannot trust an old PDF, a wrong price, and three different menus.
```

## Core Message

MenuList keeps business information clean, current, and structured so customers and discovery systems have a better source to read.

Campaign line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

This is readiness/source-quality language, not a visibility guarantee.

## Safe CTA

```text
Put one trusted customer link online.
```

## Narrative Arc

1. Customer asks search or an AI tool for a local business with a current menu.
2. Business has old PDF, stale QR page, Instagram screenshot, and inconsistent prices.
3. MenuList starts from the approved menu/service list.
4. MenuList creates one approved customer link.
5. Official page shows current menu, hours, actions, photos, and directions.
6. CTA: put one trusted customer link online.

## Claim Boundaries

Do not say:

- MenuList ranks you in AI search.
- MenuList guarantees AI recommendations.
- MenuList makes Google rank you.
- MenuList automatically updates external platforms.

Do say:

- clean, current, structured source;
- better source to read;
- one approved customer link;
- AI prepares. Owner approves. Customers see the current version;
- ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.

## Production Gate

Do not produce this until:

- founder approves AI-era discovery wording;
- product and SEO docs agree with the claim boundary;
- no external ranking or AI recommendation guarantee is implied;
- demo source and page visuals are available.
