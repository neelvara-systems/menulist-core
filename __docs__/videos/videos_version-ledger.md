## Versioning Contract

Founder-review video passes use semantic review IDs such as `v1.0`, `v1.1`, `v1.2`, and `v1.3`.

- A version ID identifies a delivered founder-review pass, not an internal render attempt.
- Internal fixes made before a pass is delivered do not consume another version number.
- After delivery, any material visual, copy, timing, audio, workflow, or export change receives the next minor version.
- Frozen or accepted passes are never overwritten. A founder may explicitly remove a rejected pass to reclaim storage; in that case its purpose, decisions, verification figures, hashes, and removal date remain here even though source and binary files are deleted.
- Project folders and final MP4 filenames include the founder-review version ID.
- Every delivered pass records its purpose, change set, audio, output path, verification, status, and relationship to earlier passes here.

These IDs track the founder-review production sequence. They do not imply that every pass is the same campaign asset or that a later workflow proof supersedes a frozen video with a different purpose.
