# Videos - Product Demo Video

**Status:** Draft script and capture plan
**Created:** July 7, 2026
**Primary output:** 2-3 minute product demo walkthrough

## Purpose

The product demo proves how MenuList works after the launch video creates interest.

It should be clear enough for sales calls, onboarding, YouTube, and the website. It must show the real workflow rather than only brand promise.

## Demo Promise

```text
Start with your current list. Review the customer version. Put one trusted customer link online.
```

## Structure

| Time | Section | Voiceover / screen direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-15s | Problem | Most businesses already have a menu or service list. The problem is that customers keep finding different versions: old PDFs, screenshots, QR pages, social links, and printed copies. |
| 15-35s | Start | MenuList starts from what you already have: a photo, PDF, existing public link you own, or typed list. Show the setup/import flow. |
| 35-60s | Review | MenuList prepares the menu structure for owner review. Show items, prices, categories, and business details before anything goes live. |
| 60-90s | Publish | After approval, the public customer link goes live. The same approved list can support the QR menu, Official Business Page, print files, and sharing links. |
| 90-120s | Customer experience | Show search, section jumps, language selection where available, business identity, open status, prices, item details, and actions like call, WhatsApp, directions, booking, or ordering links. |
| 120-150s | Updates | When prices, availability, or notes change, the owner can use the dashboard or AI Menu Manager. MenuList prepares the card and waits for approval before important work goes live. |
| 150-170s | Activity and feedback | After publishing, owners can see useful activity and collect private customer reports for wrong prices, missing items, or outdated details. |
| 170-180s | CTA | Start with your current list. Review the customer version. Put one trusted customer link online. |

## Screen Sequence

1. Source list intake
   - photo upload;
   - PDF upload;
   - owned public link input;
   - typed list fallback.
2. Private preview
   - items;
   - categories;
   - prices;
   - business details;
   - not-public-yet state.
3. Owner approval
   - review screen;
   - publish confirmation;
   - customer link generated.
4. Customer view
   - mobile customer page;
   - search;
   - sections;
   - language selector when available;
   - actions near the list.
5. Connected assets
   - QR menu;
   - Official Business Page;
   - print files;
   - owner-shared message/link.
6. Ongoing updates
   - AI Menu Manager card;
   - manual dashboard update;
   - receipt after supported update.
7. Operational proof
   - Business Health stable state;
   - activity;
   - feedback.

## Demo Account Requirements

Use fictional or approved demo data only.

Minimum demo businesses:

- cafe/restaurant menu;
- salon/spa service list;
- package/rate-card local service.

Demo data must include:

- at least 5 categories or sections for the food example;
- at least 8 service-list entries for the salon example;
- at least 4 package/rate-card entries for the local-service example;
- one price change;
- one sold-out/unavailable example;
- one business-hours or contact action example;
- one QR or print output example.

## Narration Rules

Use:

- "private preview";
- "owner approval";
- "customer link";
- "approved list";
- "current menu or service list";
- "same link";
- "No action needed."

Avoid:

- "AI automatically fixes everything";
- "best QR menu";
- "instant growth";
- "publish everywhere";
- "syncs all platforms";
- "guaranteed search visibility."

## Capture Checklist

Before recording:

- demo account is clean;
- no real customer data appears;
- browser zoom is standard;
- notifications are hidden;
- any test labels are either hidden or intentionally shown as sample/demo labels;
- source files are fictional or approved;
- all visible CTAs point to the active funnel;
- AI Menu Manager unsupported-platform boundary is not contradicted.

After recording:

- check captions fit on mobile;
- check no internal IDs, secrets, tenant IDs, debug banners, or console errors appear;
- check all external-platform mentions describe owner placement, not automatic sync;
- export 16:9 master and a short 9:16 cut if useful.
