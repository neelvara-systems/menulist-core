## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 6 - Photo/PDF To Customer Link Reel

### A. Purpose

This reel makes setup feel easy by showing that the owner can start from what they already have.

### B. Audience

Owners with paper menus, PDFs, screenshots, rate cards, service lists, catalogs, or existing public menu links.

### C. Placement

- Instagram Reel;
- YouTube Short;
- TikTok;
- launch post;
- WhatsApp sales;
- onboarding;
- paid ad.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 20-25 seconds.
- 15-second cut.
- 9:16 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

Already have a menu or service list? Start there and turn it into a customer link.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "I do not need to rebuild everything manually."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Already have a menu? Start there.
2. Problem: Owners think setup means manual entry.
3. Product entry: Photo, PDF, owned link, typed list.
4. Proof: Private preview appears.
5. Outcome: Customer link goes live after review.
6. CTA: Create customer link.

### I. Full Voiceover Script

```text
Already have a menu? Start there.

Take a photo, upload a PDF, paste your existing menu link, or type your list.

MenuList prepares the customer-facing version.

You review before publishing.

Then put one trusted customer link online.
```

### J. On-Screen Text List

1. Already have a menu? Start there.
2. Photo. PDF. Link. Typed list.
3. Customer-facing version prepared.
4. Review before publishing.
5. Create your customer link.

### K. UI Screens Required

- upload photo screen;
- upload PDF screen;
- paste existing owned link screen;
- typed list input;
- private preview;
- review screen;
- customer link live screen.

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use card-based transitions from source types into one customer link. Use gentle highlight around selected source option. Use phone-first visuals.

### M. Sound Direction

Use light upload and tap SFX. Music should feel easy and practical. Voiceover should remove friction, not oversell.

### N. Design Checklist

- All four starting sources appear.
- Private preview appears before public link.
- Review before publishing is clear.
- No claim says manual review is skipped.
- CTA is visible for at least 2 seconds.

### O. Versioning Requirements

- 9:16 captioned.
- 9:16 clean.
- 1:1 feed crop.
- 15-second cut.
- With VO.
- Without VO.
- Thumbnail frame.

### P. CTA
