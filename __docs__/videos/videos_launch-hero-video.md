## Purpose

The launch / hero video explains MenuList in one clean story.

It should make a non-technical SMB owner understand the problem and the product without framing MenuList as a QR app, a website builder, or AI hype.

## Core Angle

```text
One approved list. Every customer link.
```

The video must move through this story:

1. Public menu/service-list truth is scattered.
2. Old versions keep reaching customers.
3. MenuList starts from the list the business already has.
4. MenuList prepares a private customer-facing version.
5. Owner approval happens before publishing.
6. The approved list powers the customer link, QR, Official Business Page, print, and sharing links.
7. Updates stay approval-first.
8. The public list stays current without repeated cleanup.

## Script 1 - Main Product Launch Film

**Duration:** 75 seconds
**Format:** Native 16:9 master plus separately composed 9:16 adaptation
**Use:** Website hero, YouTube, LinkedIn, launch post, sales deck, investor deck
**Title:** One approved customer link.

The final hook, 15-scene timing plan, 139-word narration, subtitle schedule, witness-item continuity system, transition map, sound direction, capture list, and encoded close are locked in [Video 1 - 75-Second Product Launch / Hero Film](./videos_01-product-launch-hero-film.md). Do not revive earlier versions of the hero script from this summary.

The final flagship story is:

```text
existing menu or service-list source
-> private customer preview
-> owner review and approval
-> one approved customer link
-> verified MenuList customer surfaces

```

Business Health remains a dedicated feature-video story. It is intentionally not inserted into the flagship ending because it interrupts the causal source-to-approved-link demonstration.

## Script 3 - Founder / Brand POV Video

**Duration:** 60-90 seconds
**Format:** Talking head plus product shots
**Use:** LinkedIn, Instagram, website About, founder post

```text
Most business owners do not wake up thinking about menu software.

They just want customers to see the correct menu, correct prices, correct hours, and the right way to contact them.

But in real life, the menu spreads everywhere: WhatsApp, Instagram, QR codes, old PDFs, Google, printed cards, and staff phones.

That is why MenuList is built around one approved customer link.

Start with the list you already have. Review the version customers will see. Publish it once. Then keep QR, page, print, and customer actions tied to that approved version.

When something changes, MenuList should reduce repeated work, not create another dashboard to manage.

MenuList is for owners who want their public menu and business details to stay correct, even when no one is watching.
```

CTA:

```text
Create your customer link.
```

## Shot Requirements

Required:

- owner phone with WhatsApp/menu screenshots;
- old PDF or menu image;
- stale QR page example;
- MenuList source input screen;
- private preview;
- approval step;
- public customer link;
- QR and print assets;
- Official Business Page;
- AI Menu Manager approval card;
- Business Health `No action needed` state.

Optional:

- founder talking-head intro;
- real counter/table QR context;
- packaging sticker or table-card closeup;
- multi-location dashboard flash.

## Claim Boundaries

Do not include:

- automatic Google/Instagram/Zomato/Swiggy update claims;
- ranking, traffic, revenue, conversion, or sales-lift claims;
- fake testimonials;
- unpermissioned customer proof;
- "AI-powered restaurant software" framing.

## Production Notes

- Keep the first 5 seconds visual and problem-led.
- Show product screens within the first 20 seconds.
- Use captions because many social viewers watch without sound.
- Keep vertical cut readable at 320px and 390px mobile widths.
- Use `Sample business. Demo data only.` on fictional proof assets.
