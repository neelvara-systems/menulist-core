## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 12 - Founder / Brand POV Video

### A. Purpose

This video gives the launch a founder-led explanation of why MenuList exists and why the product avoids hype.

### B. Audience

LinkedIn viewers, founder network, early customers, partners, investors, skeptical owners, launch reviewers.

### C. Placement

- LinkedIn;
- Instagram;
- YouTube;
- website About;
- founder launch post;
- WhatsApp sales;
- sales deck opener.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 60-90 seconds.
- LinkedIn cut: 60-75 seconds.
- Reel cut: 30-45 seconds.
- Quote cut: 15 seconds.

### E. Core Message

MenuList exists so customers can see the correct public list and business details from one approved customer link.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "This product understands real owner friction and is built to reduce repeated cleanup."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Owners do not need another dashboard.
2. Problem: Customers need correct menu, prices, hours, and contact details.
3. Product entry: Owners should not have to type the whole menu into another tool.
4. Proof: Upload menu photos, review a prepared customer version, then create one approved customer link.
5. Outcome: Less repeated cleanup, more public consistency.
6. CTA: Start from your current list.

### I. Full Voiceover Script

```text
Most business owners do not wake up thinking about menu software.

They just want customers to see the correct menu, correct prices, correct hours, and the right way to contact them.

But in real life, the menu spreads everywhere: WhatsApp, Instagram, QR codes, old PDFs, Google, printed cards, and staff phones.

That is why MenuList is built around one approved customer link.

Owners also should not have to type the whole menu into another tool.

With MenuList, start by uploading photos of the menu or service list you already have. MenuList prepares the customer version for review.

Review the version customers will see. Publish it after approval. Then keep QR, page, print, and customer actions tied to that approved version.

When something changes, MenuList should reduce repeated work, not create another dashboard to manage.

MenuList is for owners who want their public menu and business details to stay correct, even when no one is watching.
```

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use talking-head as anchor and product cutaways as proof. Avoid heavy motion graphics. Use lower thirds sparingly. Keep product cutaways slow and readable.

### M. Sound Direction

Music should be sincere, quiet, and grounded. Voiceover should sound like a founder explaining a real problem, not a commercial announcer. Let pauses breathe after "one approved customer link" and "not create another dashboard."

### N. Design Checklist

- Founder delivery is calm and practical.
- Product cutaways support every claim.
- No hype language.
- No fake outcome claims.
- CTA is simple.
- B-roll does not distract from the message.
