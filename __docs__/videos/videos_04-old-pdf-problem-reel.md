## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 4 - Old PDF Problem Reel

### A. Purpose

This pain-point reel makes owners recognize that old PDF files and screenshots keep selling for them after they stop being current.

### B. Audience

Restaurant owners, cafe owners, service-list businesses, salon/spa owners, any business still sharing PDF or image menus.

### C. Placement

- Instagram Reel;
- YouTube Short;
- TikTok;
- paid ad;
- WhatsApp sales;
- launch post reply asset.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 12-15 seconds.
- 6-second hook cut.
- 9:16 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

Old menu files keep reaching customers, but MenuList gives the business one approved customer link.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "My old menu file may still be causing problems."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Your old menu PDF is still selling for you. Badly.
2. Problem: Customer sees old item/price.
3. Product entry: MenuList approved customer link.
4. Proof: Current customer page opens.
5. Outcome: Update the list, keep the link.
6. CTA: Create customer link.

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use sharp before/after contrast. Keep the PDF looking static and dated. Let the MenuList page feel current through a simple page load and clean UI highlight.

### M. Sound Direction

Use a small paper/page-open sound at the start, a soft warning tick on wrong price, and a clean link-open sound when MenuList appears. Voiceover should sound matter-of-fact.

### N. Design Checklist

- Hook text is readable in first 2 seconds.
- Old PDF is clearly fictional or sample.
- MenuList page appears before 0:10.
- CTA is not hidden by platform controls.
- No claim about automatic external updates.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: problem awareness.
- Belief change: `The file I still share may be answering customers with an old version.`
- Proof moment: old PDF/wrong item becomes one current approved customer link.
- Linked action: `Create customer link` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: qualified create-link starts per referred session.
- Guard metric: owner comments show problem recognition rather than blame or a PDF-converter-only interpretation.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `old_pdf_v1_still_reaching_15s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until the rendered asset, destination, baseline, and deeper milestone measurement are approved.
