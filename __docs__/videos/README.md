**Status:** Active planning folder
**Created:** July 7, 2026
**Owner:** Founder with Codex acting as MenuList marketing/distribution support
**Scope:** MenuList launch, marketing, distribution, onboarding, and sales video preparation.

## Purpose

This folder is the canonical home for MenuList video preparation.

It splits the launch video system by video type so scripts, shot lists, claims, production gates, and launch timing do not stay buried inside one large marketing note.

## Source Of Truth

Use the current MenuList product and website story:

> One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.

MenuList launch videos must not sell the product as only a QR menu app or as generic AI restaurant software. The correct story is:

> Start from the menu or service list the business already has. Prepare a private customer-facing version. Publish only after owner approval. Keep MenuList pages, QR, print files, customer actions, and owner-shared links tied to the approved version.

## Video System Layers

| Layer | Purpose | Best duration | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch / hero video | Explain MenuList in one clean story | 60-75 seconds | Website hero, YouTube, LinkedIn, sales deck, investor deck |
| Product demo video | Show how the workflow actually works | 2-3 minutes | Sales calls, onboarding, YouTube, website demo section |
| Feature videos | Explain one capability at a time | 30-60 seconds each | Feature pages, retargeting, LinkedIn, WhatsApp sales |
| Short-form reels / shorts | Fast awareness and retargeting | 8-30 seconds | Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, paid social |
| Paid ad cutdowns | Convert one sharp problem into one action | 6, 15, and 30 seconds | Retargeting, paid social, launch tests |
| Reusable motion graphics | Make one product truth, brand moment, or CTA reusable | 3-10 seconds | Openers, transitions, overlays, website, sales deck, social modules |

The reusable motion-graphics layer is governed by the [MenuList Motion-Graphics Layer](./videos_hyperframes-operating-guide.md#menulist-motion-graphics-layer). It supplies short modules to the 12 campaign videos; it does not replace their full narrative structure.

## Core Message

Use this line consistently:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

Use this market-facing expansion carefully when the context is search, social sharing, or AI-era discovery:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

This is readiness/source-quality language. It must not become a promise of Google ranking, AI recommendations, AI citations, traffic, revenue, conversion, or automatic external-platform updates.

Supporting lines:

- Start from the menu or service list you already have.
- Upload existing menu photos or a PDF.
- No typing required to start.
- Create your customer link in minutes.
- Review the customer-facing version before publishing.
- QR, page, print, and customer actions stay tied to the approved list.
- Menu updates wait for owner approval.
- No more old PDFs, screenshots, or scattered links.

## Claim Boundaries

Allowed:

- Owner-approved customer link.
- Private preview before publishing.
- Photo, PDF, owned public link, service list, or rate card as starting sources; typed entry is only a fallback, not the primary launch message.
- Easy setup language such as upload current menu photos, no typing required to start, and create a customer link in minutes.
- QR, Official Business Page, print files, customer actions, owner phone view, activity, feedback, and Business Health tied to the approved MenuList list.
- AI Menu Manager prepares cards and waits for approval before important supported work.
- Multi-location master list plus outlet-level flexibility.

Avoid:

- AI-powered restaurant software.
- Grow your sales 10x.
- Automatically updates Google, Instagram, Zomato, Swiggy, Facebook, WhatsApp catalog, or delivery platforms.
- Best QR menu builder.
- Fully automated menu updates.
- Guaranteed search visibility, ranking, citations, traffic, revenue, conversion, or sales lift.
- Fake testimonials, fake metrics, or unpermissioned customer logos.
