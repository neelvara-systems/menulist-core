## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 1 - 75-Second Product Launch / Hero Film

### A. Purpose

This is the main brand/product video. It explains MenuList in one polished story for launch, website, sales, investor review, and social distribution.

### B. Audience

SMB owners, restaurant/cafe owners, service business owners, multi-location operators, setup partners, early reviewers, investors, and launch audiences.

### C. Placement

- website;
- launch post;
- LinkedIn;
- YouTube;
- WhatsApp sales;
- sales deck;
- investor/sales presentation;
- founder launch thread.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 75 seconds.
- Vertical cut: 45-60 seconds.
- Paid ad cut: 30 seconds.
- Reel cut: 15 seconds.

### E. Core Message

MenuList turns the list your business already has into one approved customer link.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "My public menu is scattered, and one approved customer link would reduce the cleanup."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Your menu may be in five places.
2. Problem: Customers still find the old version.
3. Product entry: Start from the list you already have.
4. Proof: Private preview, owner approval, customer link, QR, page, print, actions.
5. Outcome: Public surfaces stay tied to the approved list.
6. CTA: Create customer link.

### I. Full Voiceover Script

```text
Your menu may be in five places.

And when prices, items, or hours change, customers may still find the old version.

Upload photos of the menu or service list you already have. No typing required to start.

MenuList prepares a private customer preview in minutes. Nothing goes public until you approve it.

Once approved, the same list powers your customer link, QR, official business page, print files, and sharing links.

Customers see the current menu, prices, business details, and actions in one clean place.

When something changes, MenuList prepares the update and waits for owner approval.

MenuList helps your public list stay current without repeated manual cleanup.

MenuList. One approved customer link for your business.
```

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use calm push-ins, split-screen before/after, soft UI highlights, and connected-line motion from approved customer link to surfaces. Use tap indicators for mobile approval and customer actions. Avoid robotic AI animation, heavy dashboard transitions, and particle effects.

### M. Sound Direction

Music should be warm, steady, and low-hype. Voiceover should be calm and confident. Use light SFX for QR scan, tap approval, and link copy. Leave a short moment of quiet under `No action needed` to make the calm state feel intentional.

### N. Design Checklist

- Product appears before 0:20.
- Owner approval is visually clear.
- No external-platform auto-sync claim appears.
- Captions are readable in 9:16.
- Demo data is fictional or permissioned.
- CTA is concrete.
- The film does not feel like a generic SaaS dashboard montage.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: product understanding -> high-intent conversion.
- Belief change: `MenuList turns what I already have into one approved customer link without taking control away from me.`
- Proof moment: existing source -> private preview -> owner approval -> customer link across MenuList QR/page/print/actions.
- Linked action: `Create customer link` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: first approved customer link per qualified video-referred session.
- Guard metric: private-preview rate and absence of QR-only/external-auto-update misunderstanding.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `hero_v1_approved_link_75s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until final master approval and deeper create-menu attribution are ready.
