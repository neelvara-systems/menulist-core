## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 8 - One Link Everywhere Reel

### A. Purpose

This reel shows how the approved customer link becomes the practical replacement for scattered menu files.

### B. Audience

Owners using WhatsApp, Instagram bio, QR codes, table cards, packaging, website links, and staff-shared menu files.

### C. Placement

- YouTube Short;

### D. Recommended Duration

- 10-second cut.
- 9:16 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

One approved customer link can sit wherever customers look.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "I can stop sending files and place one trusted link."

3. Product entry: One approved customer link.

6. CTA: Put one trusted customer link online.

Use a hub-and-spoke motion from the approved customer link to owner-placed surfaces. Use clear labels. Do not animate as if MenuList pushes updates into external platforms.

### M. Sound Direction

Use a clean rhythmic montage with light link-copy and tap sounds. Voiceover should be short and confident.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: product understanding.
- Belief change: `I can stop sending separate menu files and place one approved customer link where customers already look.`
- Proof moment: one MenuList link appears across owner-placed QR, WhatsApp, Instagram bio, packaging, and website placements.
- Linked action: `Create customer link` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: first approved customer link per qualified referred session.
- Guard metric: viewers understand owner placement, not automatic external-platform synchronization.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `one_link_v1_everywhere_20s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until final placement visuals and deeper attribution are approved.
