## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 9 - AI Menu Manager Reel

### A. Purpose

This reel introduces AI Menu Manager safely as approval-based menu work, not uncontrolled automation.

### B. Audience

Owners curious about message-based updates, owners worried about AI, existing users, sales prospects, short-form viewers.

### C. Placement

- Instagram Reel;
- YouTube Short;
- TikTok;
- LinkedIn;
- website feature support;
- WhatsApp sales;
- onboarding;
- paid retargeting.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 20-30 seconds.
- 15-second cut.
- 9:16 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

AI prepares the update card; the owner approves before important work goes live.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should understand: "I can say the change normally, but MenuList will not silently change the public menu."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: Menu changed? Say it normally.
2. Problem: Owners do not want to hunt through controls for simple changes.
3. Product entry: Owner message.
4. Proof: Approval card with customer-facing impact.
5. Outcome: Approved update reaches the customer page.
6. CTA: Review before publishing.

### K. UI Screens Required

- owner approval button;
- receipt screen;
- customer page after approved update.

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use message-to-card transformation. Use no robots, no AI brain graphics, no autonomous magic. Highlight the approval button and receipt. Use a simple before/after public page only after approval.

### M. Sound Direction

Use soft typing/tap SFX. Keep music restrained. Voiceover should sound operational, not excited.

### N. Design Checklist

- The owner message is visible.
- Approval card is visible before update.
- Customer page updates only after approval.
- No external platform update claim.
- No robot/AI visual appears.
- The line `AI prepares. Owner approves.` is included.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: trust.
- Belief change: `I can use AI assistance without losing control of what customers see.`
- Proof moment: normal owner message -> prepared approval card -> owner review -> approval -> current customer page.
- Display close: `AI prepares. Owner approves.`
- Linked action: companion `Create customer link` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: approval/claim progress per qualified referred session.
- Guard metric: no autonomous AI, robot-assistant, or external-platform-update misunderstanding.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `ai_manager_v1_owner_approves_25s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until exact supported behavior and deeper approval attribution are approved.
