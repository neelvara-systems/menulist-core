## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 11 - Multi-Location Reel

### A. Purpose

This reel explains how MenuList can support consistency across outlets without making every branch maintain a separate public list from scratch.

### B. Audience

Multi-location restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, salons, service chains, franchised operators, HQ managers, founders with multiple outlets.

### C. Placement

- LinkedIn;
- website;
- sales deck;
- WhatsApp sales;
- product demo;
- paid retargeting;
- YouTube Short;
- Instagram Reel.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 45-60 seconds.
- 30-second cut.
- 9:16 and 16:9 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

One master list can keep outlets consistent while allowing controlled local flexibility.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should feel: "This can reduce branch-by-branch correction without removing local control."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: One menu is easy; five outlets become five versions.
2. Problem: Names, descriptions, prices, and availability drift.
3. Product entry: One approved master list.
4. Proof: Linked outlets, governed local price/status variation.
5. Outcome: Less branch-by-branch correction.
6. CTA: One master list. Controlled outlet flexibility.

### I. Full Voiceover Script

```text
One menu is easy. Five outlets can become five different versions.

MenuList lets you start from one approved master menu.

Linked outlets can follow the approved list while local changes stay controlled.

HQ keeps names and descriptions consistent.

Outlets adjust only what they are allowed to adjust.

Less branch-by-branch coordination. More public consistency.
```

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use grid-to-master consolidation. Use line connections from master list to outlets. Use split-screen for master value and local override. Avoid implying uncontrolled automatic branch updates.

### M. Sound Direction

Use steady music with slightly more operational rhythm. Use light connection sounds when outlets align. Voiceover should sound practical.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: high-intent evaluation for multi-location operators.
- Belief change: `HQ can keep shared public truth consistent while approved outlet differences remain controlled.`
- Proof moment: one master list -> linked outlets -> permitted local price/status variation -> outlet-specific customer pages.
- Display close: `One master list. Controlled outlet flexibility.`
- Linked action: companion `Create customer link` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: qualified multi-location create-link starts and first approved publish.
- Guard metric: no implication that outlet changes propagate automatically without governance or approval.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `multi_location_v1_controlled_flexibility_45s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until the multi-location demo state, audience, and deeper product milestones are approved.
