## Shared Positioning

MenuList must be shown as public-business truth infrastructure for SMBs, not as a standalone QR menu app, generic AI restaurant software, or flashy SaaS dashboard.

Core line:

```text
One approved customer link for your menu, services, and business details.
```

AI-era market line:

```text
One approved customer link - ready for customers, search, and AI-era discovery.
```

Use this as readiness/source-quality language only. Do not turn it into a ranking, traffic, AI recommendation, or external-platform update claim.

Required boundaries:

- Show owner approval before public customer-facing changes.
- Keep external surfaces framed as owner-placed links, not automatic external-platform updates.
- Avoid fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake customer logos, ranking claims, growth claims, and AI magic visuals.
- Use fictional or permissioned demo data only.

## Video 5 - QR Stale Page Reel

### A. Purpose

This reel separates the QR code from the page behind it and shows why MenuList matters after the scan.

### B. Audience

Owners with existing QR menus, table QR cards, packaging QR, counter QR, or printed links.

### C. Placement

- Instagram Reel;
- YouTube Short;
- TikTok;
- paid ad;
- WhatsApp sales;
- QR/print feature page support.

### D. Recommended Duration

- Master: 10-15 seconds.
- 6-second hook cut.
- 9:16 required.
- 1:1 optional.

### E. Core Message

The QR code is not the problem; the page behind it needs to stay tied to the approved current list.

### F. Emotional Job

The viewer should understand: "My QR can still scan but show the wrong thing."

### G. Narrative Arc

1. Hook: The QR code is not the problem.
2. Problem: The page behind it is stale.
3. Product entry: MenuList customer link.
4. Proof: Same QR opens current approved list.
5. Outcome: Same QR. Current menu.
6. CTA: Start menu preview.

### L. Motion Design Direction

Use a subtle QR scan line, not a flashy sci-fi scan. Use same-frame transition from stale page to current approved page. Keep QR readable and scan-safe.

### M. Sound Direction

Use one scan beep, one soft page-load sound, and steady music. Keep the voiceover concise.

### N. Design Checklist

- QR remains visibly the same.
- Stale page is clear but not messy.
- Current page shows MenuList product.
- No claim that MenuList updates external QR locations automatically.
- Text remains inside safe zones.

### Q. Conversion Contract

- Funnel stage: problem awareness -> setup relief.
- Belief change: `The QR can stay useful when its destination is tied to the current approved list.`
- Proof moment: same QR opens the updated MenuList customer page after owner approval.
- Linked action: `Start menu preview` -> `/create-menu`.
- Primary metric: private preview reached per qualified referred session.
- Guard metric: no claim or viewer interpretation that MenuList repairs every existing external QR destination automatically.
- Asset/UTM pattern: `qr_stale_v1_same_qr_current_menu_15s_<aspect>`.
- Paid eligibility: blocked until the exact QR scenario and deeper product attribution are approved.
