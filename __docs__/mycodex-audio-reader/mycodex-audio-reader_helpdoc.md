# MyCodex Audio Reader Help

## Use Read-Aloud

1. Open any MyCodex document.
2. Tap the read-page button in the header.
3. Use Pause, Resume, or Stop from Settings or the mini-player.

## Play Favorites

1. Star the documents you want to revisit.
2. Open Favorites from the sidebar or settings.
3. Tap Play all to read starred documents one by one.
4. Tap Stop when you want playback to end.

## Play Read Later

1. Open a document and add it to Read Later from Settings.
2. Open Queue from the bottom navigation or sidebar.
3. Tap Play queue to read queued documents one by one.
4. Clear or remove items when the temporary list is done.

## Adjust Voice

- Pick an India voice from the India voice selector when your browser/device provides one.
- Adjust Speed between `0.75x` and `1.5x`.
- Turn off Follow while reading if you do not want the page to scroll automatically.
- Keep screen awake can be enabled for foreground reading when the browser supports it.

## Notes

- This uses the browser/device voice system.
- MyCodex hides unrelated voices. If no India voice is installed, it uses the device default.
- No audio file is saved.
- No paid AI or cloud TTS route is used by MyCodex.
- Favorites playback reads local MyCodex Markdown through the protected same-origin document route.
- Keep screen awake does not create locked-screen or app-closed playback.
