# 📜 Digital Menu Output Constitution

## Part III — Mood × Layout Mapping (Controlled Expression System)

**Goal:**
Allow _expression without chaos_ by separating **WHAT changes** from **WHAT never changes**.

Owners choose a **Mood**.
The system chooses **everything else**.

---

## The Unbreakable Split

### 🔒 Never Changes (System-Owned)

- Typography scale
- Information hierarchy
- Price placement
- Accessibility contrast
- Navigation behavior
- Performance budget
- Interaction rules

### 🎭 Allowed to Change (Mood-Controlled)

- Color palette (within contrast limits)
- Image treatment (presence, framing, tone)
- Spacing density (relaxed vs compact)
- Header tone (bold vs calm)
- Micro-emphasis (badges, highlights — not animations)

---

## 1️⃣ The Mood System (Finite, Opinionated)

There are **exactly 5 moods**.
No custom moods. No sliders. No “creative freedom.”

Each mood is **cross-vertical safe**.

---

### 🟢 M1 — Clean & Calm

**Default / Clinics / Cafes / Spas**

**Visual Feel**

- Light background
- Soft accent color
- Generous whitespace
- Minimal imagery

**Used When**

- Trust > appetite
- Morning/daytime businesses
- Clinics, salons, wellness

**System Enforced**

- No dark backgrounds
- No image-dominant sections
- No aggressive highlights

> This is the “nothing feels wrong” mode.

---

### 🟠 M2 — Warm & Inviting

**Family restaurants / Casual dining**

**Visual Feel**

- Warm neutrals
- Food-forward imagery (limited)
- Comfortable spacing
- Friendly tone

**Used When**

- Comfort > excitement
- Broad audience
- Repeats & families

**System Enforced**

- Images capped per section
- Prices visible near name when item price display is enabled
- No hero-image takeovers

> This sells food without shouting.

---

### 🔵 M3 — Premium & Minimal

**Fine dining / Boutique cafes / Luxury salons**

**Visual Feel**

- High contrast
- Elegant typography
- Sparse imagery
- Confident spacing

**Used When**

- Perceived value matters
- Smaller menus
- Higher price points

**System Enforced**

- Images optional, never dominant
- No gimmick pricing
- No excessive modifiers

> Quiet confidence, not decoration.

---

### 🟣 M4 — Bold & Energetic

**Clubs / Bars / Night venues**

**Visual Feel**

- Dark mode allowed
- Strong accent colors
- Tighter spacing
- High visual contrast

**Used When**

- Night-time energy
- Drinks-first menus
- Fast scanning

**System Enforced**

- Text contrast auto-corrected
- No background videos
- Motion limited to state changes only

> Energy without unreadability.

---

### ⚪ M5 — Utility & Fast

**QSRs / Food courts / High volume**

**Visual Feel**

- Dense layout
- Minimal images
- Prominent prices when item price display is enabled
- Ultra-fast scanning

**Used When**

- Speed > brand
- Repeat ordering
- Peak-hour throughput

**System Enforced**

- List-first or tabbed scanning
- Compact spacing
- No decorative elements

> This is a machine. And that’s okay.

---

## 2️⃣ Layout System (Orthogonal to Mood)

Mood controls _feel_.
Layout controls _behavior_.

Owners pick **one layout**.

---

### L1 — List (Default)

- Text-first stacked items
- Fast scanning with clear item hierarchy
- Best for most menus

---

### L2 — Card

- Image-top item cards
- Balanced visual presentation
- Image quota capped per category

---

### L3 — Grid

- Two-column visual scan where space allows
- Image-forward but still text-first in decision hierarchy
- No Pinterest-style endless grids allowed

---

### L4 — Tabs

- Sticky horizontal category navigation
- Best for larger menus and fast section jumping
- Falls back to category popover when tabs are not visible

---

## 3️⃣ Mood × Layout Compatibility Matrix

Not all combos allowed.

| Mood ↓ / Layout → | List | Card | Grid | Tabs |
| ----------------- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Clean & Calm      | ✅   | ❌   | ✅   | ✅   |
| Warm & Inviting   | ✅   | ✅   | ✅   | ✅   |
| Premium & Minimal | ✅   | ✅   | ❌   | ✅   |
| Bold & Energetic  | ❌   | ✅   | ✅   | ✅   |
| Utility & Fast    | ✅   | ❌   | ❌   | ✅   |

❌ = not allowed at all

> This prevents **bad taste by design**, not education.

---

## 4️⃣ Why This Wins

### For End Customers

- Menus always feel readable
- Mood matches context
- Zero surprises
- Zero fatigue

### For Owners

- Feel expressive
- Can’t break anything
- Proud to share
- Looks intentional, not generic

### For MenuList AI

- No edge-case hell
- Predictable output
- Strong brand consistency
- Impossible to clone casually
