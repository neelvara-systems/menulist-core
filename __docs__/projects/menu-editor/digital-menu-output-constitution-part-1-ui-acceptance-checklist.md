# 📜 Digital Menu Output Constitution

## Part I — Non-Negotiable UI Acceptance Checklist

If **any single item fails**, the menu is **invalid**.

This applies to:

- Restaurants
- Cafes
- Bars
- Salons
- Spas
- Clinics

No exceptions.

---

## A. Access & Speed (Entry Contract)

These rules decide whether the user stays or abandons in **3 seconds**.

### A1. Instant Access

- ☐ Menu opens in **mobile browser** (no app install)
- ☐ No login, no phone number, no OTP, no gate
- ☐ Content visible immediately (not a blank shell)

**Fail = immediate abandonment**

---

### A2. Load Performance

- ☐ First meaningful content visible in **< 3 seconds on 4G**
- ☐ Text loads **before** images
- ☐ No layout jumping while loading (CLS ≈ 0)

**Fail = menu feels “cheap / broken”**

---

### A3. Offline Resilience

- ☐ If connection drops after load, menu **remains usable**
- ☐ Already-loaded categories do not disappear

**Fail = loss of trust**

---

## B. Readability & Environment Safety

Menus are used in **sunlight, dim bars, one hand, bad phones**.

### B1. Sunlight Legibility

- ☐ Body text readable in bright daylight
- ☐ No low-contrast text (grey on white, gold on black, etc.)
- ☐ Dark themes do not sacrifice contrast

**Fail = customers ask staff instead**

---

### B2. Font Safety

- ☐ Minimum readable font size (no pinch-zoom)
- ☐ System fonts or highly legible web fonts only
- ☐ Decorative fonts never used for prices or descriptions

**Fail = cognitive fatigue**

---

### B3. One-Hand Use

- ☐ Primary actions reachable with thumb
- ☐ No essential action placed only at extreme top
- ☐ Touch targets large enough to avoid mis-taps

**Fail = frustration**

---

## C. Information Transparency (Trust Layer)

Menus must never **surprise** users later.

### C1. Price Visibility

- ☐ Price visible **without opening item**
- ☐ Price shown before images, not after scroll
- ☐ Consistent price format across menu

**Fail = suspicion**

---

### C2. Modifier Transparency

- ☐ Add-ons show price upfront
- ☐ Size/variant changes update price instantly
- ☐ No hidden “extra charges” revealed late

**Fail = cart abandonment**

---

### C3. Fees & Charges

- ☐ Service charge / tax clearly indicated
- ☐ No surprise fees at checkout
- ☐ Language is clear, not legalese

**Fail = anger**

---

## D. Navigation & Scanning Behavior

Users **scan**, they don’t read.

### D1. Category Orientation

- ☐ Always visible category context (“You’re in Starters”)
- ☐ Easy jump between categories
- ☐ No forced scrolling marathon

**Fail = disorientation**

---

### D2. List Structure

- ☐ Item name left-aligned
- ☐ Price alignment consistent
- ☐ Clear visual separation between items

**Fail = slow decisions**

---

### D3. Long Menu Safety

- ☐ Long lists broken with headers or images
- ☐ No infinite scroll without anchors
- ☐ “Back to top” or category access always available

**Fail = fatigue**

---

## E. Imagery Rules (Visual Truth)

Images help **decide**, not decorate.

### E1. Image Purpose

- ☐ Images used as landmarks, not wallpaper
- ☐ Images never block price visibility
- ☐ Images never delay text render

**Fail = visual noise**

---

### E2. Image Authenticity

- ☐ Images resemble real item/service
- ☐ No obvious stock mismatch
- ☐ Poor images better hidden than shown

**Fail = loss of credibility**

---

## F. Trust Signals (Safety & Legitimacy)

Users subconsciously verify legitimacy.

### F1. Business Reality

- ☐ Business name clearly visible
- ☐ Physical location or contact visible
- ☐ Menu does not feel “anonymous”

**Fail = ghost-brand suspicion**

---

### F2. Freshness Signals

- ☐ “Updated today / Live menu” visible
- ☐ Sold-out items clearly marked
- ☐ No dead or outdated items

**Fail = fear of wrong order**

---

### F3. Technical Trust

- ☐ HTTPS secure
- ☐ Payment badges shown where relevant
- ☐ No browser security warnings

**Fail = immediate exit**

---

## G. Frustration Kill Switches

These cause **rage quits**.

### G1. Interaction Feedback

- ☐ Every tap gives instant visual feedback
- ☐ No dead buttons
- ☐ No multiple accidental adds

**Fail = user feels stupid**

---

### G2. Back Behavior

- ☐ Phone back button behaves predictably
- ☐ Back does not exit entire menu unexpectedly
- ☐ Modal close ≠ site exit

**Fail = exploration stops**

---

## H. Cross-Vertical Safety

Menu must feel safe for **restaurant + salon + clinic**.

### H1. Neutral Professionalism

- ☐ No forced dark / nightclub aesthetics
- ☐ No gimmicky motion
- ☐ Output would not embarrass a clinic owner

**Fail = vertical lock-in**

---

### H2. Brand Dignity

- ☐ Looks professional even with zero customization
- ☐ Defaults do not feel “template”
- ☐ Owner proud to share link

**Fail = owner hesitation**

---

## Constitution Rule

> **If a menu violates even ONE checkbox above — it is not allowed to ship.**

No debate.
No “but users asked for it.”
No exceptions.
