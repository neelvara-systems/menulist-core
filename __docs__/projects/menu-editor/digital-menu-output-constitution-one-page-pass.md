# 📜 Digital Menu Output Constitution

## The One-Page Non-Negotiable Checklist (PASS / FAIL)

Use this to judge **every live menu**, **every theme**, **every layout**, **every experiment**.

No debates. No exceptions.

---

## I. SPEED & ACCESS (Utility First)

☐ Menu becomes usable (text + price visible) in **≤ 3 seconds** on average 4G
☐ No blocking loaders before menu content
☐ No app download required
☐ No login / phone / email required to view items
☐ Works fully inside in-app browsers (WhatsApp, Instagram, Chrome)

**Fail = immediate abandonment**

---

## II. FORMAT & STRUCTURE (Anti-PDF Law)

☐ Menu is **fully responsive HTML**, never a PDF
☐ Text is readable at default zoom (no pinch required)
☐ Categories are jumpable (no forced linear scrolling)
☐ Layout is visually stable (no content jumping on load)

**PDF behavior = automatic fail**

---

## III. READABILITY & LEGIBILITY (Sunlight Test)

☐ Body text contrast ≥ WCAG AA (4.5:1)
☐ Prices readable without scrolling or interaction
☐ Font size never drops below legibility threshold
☐ Dark modes pass sunlight readability check
☐ No low-contrast gray text on white / dark backgrounds

**If unreadable outdoors → fail**

---

## IV. PRICE TRANSPARENCY (Trust Before Taste)

☐ Price visible **next to item name**
☐ No hidden charges revealed only at checkout
☐ Modifier prices shown **before selection**
☐ Price updates immediately on size/modifier change
☐ Consistent price alignment across category

**Price ambiguity = trust breach**

---

## V. NAVIGATION & ERGONOMICS (One-Hand Rule)

☐ Core actions reachable in thumb zone
☐ Category navigation visible while scrolling
☐ No forced hamburger for primary navigation
☐ Back button behavior is predictable (no exits)
☐ Tap targets ≥ 44×44px (no mis-taps)

**Two-handed menu = fail**

---

## VI. SCANNABILITY & DECISION SPEED

☐ Category headers clearly separated
☐ First item in category is intentional (not random)
☐ Long lists broken with visual anchors
☐ Search present for menus > 20 items
☐ “Best seller / Popular” cues are visible but subtle

**If users hunt instead of scan → fail**

---

## VII. IMAGERY (Truthful, Not Decorative)

☐ Images never block prices or names
☐ Image count capped per section
☐ Images load after text (text-first rendering)
☐ Images represent actual items (no misleading stock)
☐ Low-quality images automatically suppressed or framed safely

**Images serve clarity, not ego**

---

## VIII. TRUST SIGNALS (Interface Credibility)

☐ SSL present (no browser warnings)
☐ Physical address or business identity visible
☐ “Last updated” or live signal present
☐ Sold-out / unavailable items clearly marked
☐ No fake urgency or dark patterns

**If menu looks dead or shady → fail**

---

## IX. OWNER CONTROL (5-Second Rule)

☐ Item can be marked sold-out in ≤ 5 seconds (mobile)
☐ Price can be changed in ≤ 5 seconds
☐ Changes reflect everywhere instantly
☐ No dependency on designer or support
☐ Owner cannot accidentally break layout or readability

**If chalkboard is faster → fail**

---

## X. SHARE & PRESENTATION (Pride Test)

☐ Link unfurls with clean preview (image + title)
☐ QR code can be branded safely
☐ Shared link looks intentional, not spammy
☐ Menu feels “safe to send” to anyone
☐ Brand tone consistent with venue reality

**If owner hesitates to share → fail**

---

## XI. CROSS-VERTICAL SAFETY

☐ Same system works for restaurant, salon, clinic
☐ No food-only assumptions baked into UI
☐ Service duration + price visible when relevant
☐ No nightlife visuals forced on clinics
☐ No clinical stiffness forced on cafes

**If it only fits one vertical → fail**

---

## XII. AGING & MAINTENANCE (12-Month Test)

☐ No trendy visual gimmicks (glass, neon, parallax)
☐ No heavy motion loops
☐ Works on low-end Android devices
☐ Minimal JS dependency for core content
☐ Still usable if images fail to load

**If it ages fast → fail**

---

## Final Constitutional Rule

> **A digital menu is not an experience.
> It is an interface for fast, confident decisions.**

If anything prioritizes:

- aesthetics over clarity
- freedom over safety
- novelty over reliability

→ it is **out of constitution**.

---

### What this gives you

- A **kill switch** for bad ideas
- A **sales differentiator** (“we refuse bad menus”)
- A **design moat** competitors won’t copy
- A product owners can **trust blindly**
