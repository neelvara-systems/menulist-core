## Feature Narrative

### The Problem

Writing menu descriptions is harder than it looks:

- **Time-consuming:** 5-10 minutes per item manually
- **Inconsistent:** Different items get different quality
- **Copywriting skills:** Not everyone can write appetizingly
- **Multi-language:** Even harder in multiple languages

Many menus end up with bland or missing descriptions.

### The Solution

MenuList's AI Description Generation:

- **Instant:** Generate in 3-5 seconds per item
- **Professional:** Copywriter-quality descriptions
- **Customizable:** Choose tone and length
- **Multi-language:** Same quality in all languages
- **Batch:** Generate for entire menu at once

### The Result

Every item on the menu has an **appetizing, professional description**—without hiring a copywriter.

## Sales Talking Points

### Objection: "AI descriptions will sound robotic"

**Response:** "That's why we have tone options. Try 'casual' for friendly language, or 'playful' for something fun. You can also edit anything after generation. But most customers are surprised how natural they sound."

### Objection: "I want unique descriptions"

**Response:** "Every description is generated specifically for your item name and category. Two restaurants with 'Butter Chicken' might get different descriptions based on their chosen tone and context."

### Objection: "What about dietary info and allergens?"

**Response:** "We intentionally exclude allergen information—it needs to be accurate and legally compliant, which requires your input. Our descriptions focus on making items sound appetizing; you can add allergen tags separately."

### Objection: "Can I edit after generation?"

**Response:** "Absolutely. The AI gives you a professional starting point, then you tweak as needed. Most users make minor edits or none at all."
