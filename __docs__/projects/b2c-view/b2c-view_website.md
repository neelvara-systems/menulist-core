## Problem Statement

Many digital menus either look generic or expose too many design choices. Restaurant owners need the menu to look right without spending time managing a design system.

## Solution Statement

MenuList keeps design choices controlled. Owners pick from menu moods and compatible layouts, then preview how the customer menu will look before publishing.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Customer menu preview

See the public menu before publishing. Check phone, tablet, and desktop views where available.

### 2. Controlled brand fit

Apply a brand accent, logo, and supported display settings without creating unreadable customer output.

### 3. Compatible layouts

Choose from List, Grid, or Card layouts based on the selected mood. Category tabs remain a separate mobile/tablet navigation setting.

## How It Works

1. **Open Preview** - Go to the project preview/design area.
2. **Choose Style** - Pick a mood, compatible layout, accent, and display settings.
3. **Check Devices** - Review the customer menu across available preview sizes.
4. **Publish** - Save the design to the customer menu.
