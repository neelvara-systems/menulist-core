## Problem Statement
Most QR menu platforms give you one generic look. Your menu ends up looking exactly like every other restaurant's. No branding, no personality.

## Solution Statement
MenuList lets you customize everything — your colors, your logo, your layout. Preview how it looks on phones, tablets, and desktops before publishing.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Real-Time Preview
See exactly how customers will see your menu. Switch between phone, tablet, and desktop views.
> **Visual:** 4 device frames showing the same menu in different sizes

### 2. Match Your Brand
Set your accent color, upload your logo, choose a background. Your menu looks like YOUR restaurant — not a generic template.
> **Visual:** Color picker + logo upload → branded menu preview

### 3. Multiple Layouts
Choose vertical list or horizontal tab navigation. Pick the mood that fits your vibe — casual, elegant, modern.
> **Visual:** Same menu in 3 different layout styles

## How It Works
1. **Open Preview** — Go to your project's Preview tab
2. **Customize** — Pick colors, layout, mood, upload logo
3. **Check Devices** — Preview on phone, tablet, desktop frames
4. **Save** — Your design goes live on the customer menu
