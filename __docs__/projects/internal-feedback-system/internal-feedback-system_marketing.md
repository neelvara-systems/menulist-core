# Guest Feedback — Marketing Claim Guard

**Status:** Internal source for approved claims; not a performance-claim library
**Last Source Audit:** July 16, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing, sales, website, support

---

## Approved Positioning

> Give customers a simple private way to report a menu, business, or service issue, then review it in the MenuList owner inbox.

Guest Feedback is a correction and listening channel built into MenuList public surfaces. Customers can use a menu link, feedback QR, or direct link. Owners can review store-scoped feedback, use contact details only when voluntarily supplied, copy a reply draft, and mark an item resolved.

---

## Safe Claims

- Customers can send private feedback without creating an account.
- The form supports a 1–5 rating, an optional/configurable note, and optional/configurable contact fields.
- Owners can review feedback in desktop and mobile store-scoped inboxes.
- Low ratings can be filtered as needing attention until resolved.
- Owners can copy a reply draft or open it in WhatsApp when a valid phone number was supplied.
- MenuList does not send the reply automatically.
- Owners can share a feedback link or download a 1024px feedback QR PNG.
- A configured valid Google review link may be offered after submission, independently of rating.
- Guest feedback records have a 90-day retention boundary.

---

## Claims That Are Not Allowed

- “Prevents negative reviews,” “protects your rating,” or “reputation firewall”
- “Hear from customers before Google does” or any claim that implies interception
- “Reduces negative reviews,” conversion, revenue, return-rate, or ROI statistics without current approved evidence
- “Understands patterns,” sentiment analysis, or reputation analytics
- “HQ sees every outlet in one inbox”
- “Sends replies,” “automated replies,” or “reply and resolve in one step”
- “Imports,” “monitors,” or “posts” Google reviews
- “300 DPI” or branded QR claims; the current output is a 1024px PNG
- “Included in every plan” unless the current pricing/entitlement source is checked for the target campaign
- Testimonials, metrics, or customer results represented as real without approved evidence

## Short Copy Options

### One line

Private customer feedback, routed to the store's MenuList inbox.

### Feature card

Let customers report an issue from your menu or feedback QR. Review it privately, contact them when they choose to share details, and mark it resolved.

### Owner explanation

Share the feedback link or QR wherever customers need it. MenuList keeps each submission private to the matching store. Reply drafts are copy helpers; you decide what to send.

---

## Public Review Link Boundary

The optional Google link is not review gating. The implementation uses the same URL availability rule for every rating. Copy must not suggest that unhappy guests are routed away from Google or that happy guests receive a different path.

Do not make legal-compliance guarantees from this document. Any public policy/legal statement requires a current policy and legal review for the target release.

---

## Separate Product Boundary

Do not merge Guest Feedback claims with `reviews-reputation` or `reputation-protection`. Those folders describe disabled, incomplete Google-review scaffolding with no ingestion, inbox, posting, or mobile runtime.
