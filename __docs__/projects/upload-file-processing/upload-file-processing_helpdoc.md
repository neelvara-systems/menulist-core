# Upload & File Processing — Help Documentation

## Quick Summary
Upload photos or PDFs of your physical menu. The system converts them into digital format, ready for automatic extraction and editing.

---

## Getting Started

### Supported File Types
- **Images:** JPG, PNG, WebP
- **Documents:** PDF (multi-page supported)
- **Size limit:** 10MB per file

### How to Upload Your Menu
1. Go to **Projects** → select your project
2. Click **Upload Files**
3. Choose files from your phone or computer (drag-and-drop works too)
4. Preview your files before processing
5. Click **Upload & Continue**
6. Wait for processing (30-60 seconds)

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Upload area with file picker and drag-drop zone

## How-To Guides

### How to upload from your phone
1. Open MenuList on your phone's browser
2. Go to your project
3. Tap **Upload Files**
4. Choose **Camera** to take a new photo, or **Photo Library** to select existing
5. Tap **Upload & Continue**

> 💡 **Tip:** Hold your phone straight above the menu for best results. Avoid shadows.

### How to upload a PDF menu
1. Go to your project → **Upload Files**
2. Select your PDF file
3. Each page automatically converts to a high-quality image
4. Review all pages in the preview
5. Click **Upload & Continue**

### How to upload multiple files
1. Select multiple images at once when browsing
2. Or upload one at a time — they all go into the same project
3. All files are processed together during extraction

## Troubleshooting

### Upload is stuck or failing
**Fix:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Make sure file is under 10MB
3. Try a different browser if on desktop
4. Refresh the page and try again

### PDF pages look blurry
**Why:** Very low-resolution PDFs produce blurry images.
**Fix:** If possible, get a higher-quality PDF. Or take clear photos of each page instead.

### "File type not supported" error
**Fix:** Convert your file to JPG, PNG, or PDF first. HEIC files (iPhone) may need conversion.

## Tips
- 💡 Good lighting = better extraction results later
- 💡 Crop photos to just the menu content
- 💡 One clear photo is better than multiple blurry ones
- 💡 PDF works best for multi-page menus
