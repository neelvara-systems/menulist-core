## Feature Narrative

### The Problem

Restaurant owners face a painful choice when digitizing their menus:

1. **Retype everything** — Hours of manual work, prone to typos
2. **Hire someone** — Expensive, delays, communication overhead
3. **Use basic tools** — Still requires manual formatting and entry

Most restaurants have menus as PDFs or printed materials. Converting these to digital menus has been a tedious, error-prone process.

### The Solution

MenuList's Upload feature eliminates the manual work:

- **Upload once** — Take a photo with your phone or upload your existing PDF
- **AI does the work** — Menu items, prices, categories extracted automatically
- **Edit, don't create** — Review and tweak instead of building from scratch

### The Result

What used to take **hours or days** now takes **minutes**. Restaurant owners can digitize their menu during a coffee break.

## Approved Language

### Terms to Use ✅

- "Upload" (not "import" — simpler)
- "AI-powered" (not "machine learning" — accessible)
- "Minutes, not hours" (concrete time savings)
- "Your existing menu" (no special prep needed)
- "Review and edit" (emphasize control)

### Terms to Avoid ❌

- "OCR" (technical jargon)
- "Data extraction" (sounds cold)
- "Processing" (sounds slow)
- "Upload queue" (sounds complicated)
- "File validation" (users don't care)
