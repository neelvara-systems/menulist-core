## Problem Statement
You've been meaning to put your menu online, but who has time to type out 50+ items with prices, categories, and descriptions? The thought alone is exhausting.

## Solution Statement
Skip the blank page. Upload a photo or PDF of your current menu. MenuList prepares common menu structure into a review draft after processing, then you approve the menu before customers see it.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Common Upload Paths
Photo from your phone, scanned PDF, or a clear image of your menu. Upload the source and review the processed draft.
> **Visual:** Three upload methods shown — phone photo, PDF file, camera shot of chalkboard

### 2. Multi-Page PDFs Supported
Got a multi-page drink menu? Upload the PDF. Pages are processed into one draft for owner review.
> **Visual:** PDF icon with "12 pages" → complete structured menu

### 3. Review Before Publishing
After processing, check the draft, fix anything that needs attention, and publish only the approved menu.
> **Visual:** Review checklist with draft menu and publish action

## How It Works
1. **Choose Your File** — Photo, PDF, or multiple images
2. **Upload** — Click one button. The source is processed into a review draft.
3. **Review & Publish** — Check the draft, make any edits, and publish the approved menu.
