## Feature Narrative

### The Problem

Editing menu data is risky without proper tools:

- **Data loss** — Browser crashes, accidental navigation = lost work
- **Mistakes stick** — No undo means every change is final
- **Tedious workflow** — Click, save, click, save, repeat
- **Disorganization** — Hard to reorder or restructure menus

### The Solution

MenuList's Data Editor provides a professional editing experience:

- **Auto-save** — Changes saved automatically in the background
- **Undo/Redo** — Revert mistakes instantly with Ctrl+Z
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — Work faster with familiar shortcuts
- **Drag-drop reorder** — Reorganize categories and items visually
- **Validation** — Catches errors before you publish

### The Result

A **safe, efficient editing experience** where restaurant owners can focus on perfecting their menu instead of worrying about losing work.

## Sales Talking Points

### Objection: "I can just use a spreadsheet"

**Response:** "You can, but spreadsheets don't auto-save, don't have menu-specific validation, and don't show you a preview. Our editor is built specifically for menus—categories, items, prices, descriptions all in one place."

### Objection: "What if I make a mistake?"

**Response:** "That's exactly why we built undo/redo. Ctrl+Z undoes your last change, and we keep a history of your last 10 changes. Made a bigger mistake? Hit Reset to go back to your last saved version."

### Objection: "I don't trust auto-save"

**Response:** "We show you exactly what's happening. You'll see 'Saving...' when it's saving, and 'All changes saved ✓' with a timestamp when it's done. And you can always hit Ctrl+S to save manually if you prefer."

### Objection: "My menu is huge, this will take forever"

**Response:** "We've built features for exactly that. Use Ctrl+F to search, filters to narrow down items, and batch operations to change multiple items at once. Our drag-drop reorder works on categories and items separately."
