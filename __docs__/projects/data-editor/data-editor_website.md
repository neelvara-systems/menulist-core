## Problem Statement
Managing a digital menu shouldn't feel like programming. Most platforms make you fill out complex forms for every single item change. A price update shouldn't take 5 clicks.

## Solution Statement
MenuList's editor works the way you think. Click a price, type the new one. Drag an item to reorder. Toggle availability with one tap. Saved menu edits pass through the public truth and cache path before customers see them.

## Feature Benefits

### 1. Visual Editing
See your menu as your customers see it — while editing. No abstract forms or confusing interfaces.
> **Visual:** Split screen showing editor view and live preview side by side

### 2. Fast Price Updates
Click any price, type the new amount, and save. After saving, customers see the approved price after the current public menu cache refresh.
> **Visual:** Price being edited inline with "Save" button

### 3. Organize with Drag-and-Drop
Reorder items and categories by dragging. Group items the way your restaurant is organized.
> **Visual:** Item being dragged from one category to another

### 4. Bulk Actions
Mark multiple items as sold out, change prices for a category, generate descriptions for all items at once.
> **Visual:** Multiple items selected with bulk action bar at top

## How It Works
1. **Open Your Project** — Click into any menu project
2. **Edit Anything** — Items, prices, descriptions, images, categories
3. **Save** — Customer menus refresh through the public cache path, usually within 60 seconds.
