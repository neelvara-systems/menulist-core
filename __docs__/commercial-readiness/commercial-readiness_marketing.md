# Commercial Readiness Marketing Boundary

This is an internal certification system, not a marketable feature.

Allowed public outcomes:

- clear plan prices and billing periods;
- clear Content Credit allowances and pack price;
- payment status and available billing documents in Billing;
- factual tax presentation after the applicable configuration is verified.

Do not claim automatic invoice email, universal tax treatment, GST compliance,
international availability, or provider certification before the matching
runtime and owner gates pass.
