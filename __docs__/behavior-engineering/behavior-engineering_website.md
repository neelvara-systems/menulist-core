## Problem Statement

Every day you get dozens of "send me your menu" messages. You open your gallery, find some photos or a PDF, and send them. But that menu is often outdated — wrong prices, removed items, old offers. Customers arrive confused. You waste time explaining. It's a daily headache you've gotten used to.

## Solution Statement

MenuList gives your business one official link that always shows your latest menu. Update once — every customer sees the update instantly. No more resending. No more outdated PDFs floating around. Your staff, your Instagram bio, your table QR — all point to the same always-current menu.
