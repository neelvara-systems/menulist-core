## Feature Narrative

### The Problem Every SMB Owner Faces

Every day, restaurant and café owners get 20-80 "send me your menu" messages. They open their gallery, find photos or a PDF, and send it. But that menu is often outdated — wrong prices, removed items, old offers. Customers arrive with wrong expectations. Arguments happen. The owner looks unprofessional.

### How MenuList Solves It

MenuList gives every business one official link for the **current approved menu**. Update in MenuList, publish where required, and the same link refreshes through the public cache path. No need to resend. No outdated PDFs circulating. Staff sends the same link. QR on your table opens the same link. Your Instagram bio points to the same link.

### The Identity Shift

After 2-3 weeks, owners stop thinking about "which menu to send." They just send the MenuList link. It becomes muscle memory. Their staff does the same. Their customers bookmark it. Their QR codes point to it. MenuList becomes the official customer-facing layer of their business.
