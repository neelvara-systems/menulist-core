## Quick Summary

Your MenuList link is your official menu link. Share it whenever customers ask for your menu. It points to the current approved items, prices, and hours after save or publish and the brief public refresh window.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- A published menu on MenuList

### Your Official Link

After publishing your menu, your official link is ready. It looks like:  
**yourbusiness.menulist.online**

You can find it on your dashboard or in the Share section.

## How-To Guides

### How to share your menu link on WhatsApp

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Find the **Your Official Business Link** card
3. Tap **Copy**
4. Open **WhatsApp** and paste the link in any chat
5. Send it whenever someone asks for your menu

> 💡 **Tip:** Save this link in your WhatsApp "self-chat" for quick access. Next time someone asks for your menu, just forward it.

### How to add your menu link to Instagram bio

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Copy your official link
3. Open **Instagram** → Edit Profile → Website
4. Paste your MenuList link
5. Save

Now customers who visit your Instagram can access your current approved menu.

### How to download and print your QR code

1. Go to **Share & QR Code** (or the Share button on your menu)
2. Click **Download QR Code**
3. Print and place on tables, counter, or packaging

> 💡 **Tip:** Your QR code links to your live menu. Even if you change prices or items, the QR code still works — no reprinting needed.

### How to share the link with your staff

1. Copy your official link from the Dashboard
2. Share it with your team on WhatsApp or any group chat
3. Tell them: "Send this to customers instead of menu photos"

Now everyone on your team sends the same current menu link.

### How to update your menu

1. Go to your **Menu Editor**
2. Make changes (prices, items, availability)
3. Click **Save**
4. That's it — the same link refreshes after the save/publish path and public cache window

> 💡 **Tip:** You don't need to resend the link. Customers who already have it use the same customer link after the public refresh path settles.

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

### My customer says they see an old menu
**Why this happens:** Their browser might have cached an old version.  
**How to fix it:** Ask them to refresh the page. MenuList updates within 1 minute of your changes.

### I changed my menu but the link shows old information
**Why this happens:** Changes take up to 60 seconds to appear.  
**How to fix it:** Wait 1 minute and refresh the page. If it still shows old info, contact support.

### Can I change my link URL?
**Why this happens:** Your link is based on your business name.  
**How to fix it:** Contact support if you need to change your URL.

## Tips & Best Practices

- 💡 **Save in WhatsApp** — Keep your link in your WhatsApp self-chat for instant access
- 💡 **Share with staff** — Make sure everyone on your team uses the same link
