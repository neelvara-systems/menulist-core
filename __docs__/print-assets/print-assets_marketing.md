# Print Assets Marketing

**Status:** Internal
**Last Updated:** June 25, 2026

## One-Liner

All customer-facing print files in one place.

Companion phrasing for QR-heavy assets:

> Branded QR files for every customer action.

## Positioning

Print Assets turns a live MenuList menu into the physical files an owner places around the business. It is not a blank design tool. It removes most design and sizing decisions by reusing the business logo, color, and live menu link, while supported single assets can be adjusted from a governed finished template on desktop.

## Talking Points

- Table, counter, entrance, feedback, and full menu files are together.
- The printed QR opens the current live menu.
- QR-heavy files should tell customers exactly what to scan for.
- Owners do not need Canva or a designer for basic deployment.
- Desktop owners can adjust practical copy/layout details without changing the QR destination.
- Desktop and phone produce the same files.
- Premium stores can remove visible MenuList attribution where the shared policy applies.
- Trust comes from the business identity, current live link, short link, and scan-safe layout, not from decorative QR styling.
- Brand belongs around the QR. The QR itself stays scan-safe.
- QR-to-WhatsApp campaign tests are a separate experiment feature, not a normal Assets claim.

## Avoid

- Do not call it a design studio.
- Do not promise print fulfillment.
- Do not imply owners get unlimited creative control, blank-canvas design, or print fulfillment.
- Do not sell MenuList as a QR maker. QR is one distribution surface for the official customer link.
- Do not use unsupported claims such as "verified", "secure", "no spam", or WhatsApp opt-in language on normal menu/page QR print assets.
- Do not claim Assets can prove WhatsApp opt-ins or campaign winners unless QR WhatsApp Experiments is implemented and enabled.
