# Print Assets Marketing

**Status:** Internal
**Last Updated:** June 4, 2026

## One-Liner

All customer-facing print files in one place.

## Positioning

Print Assets turns a live MenuList menu into the physical files an owner places around the business. It is not a design tool. It removes the design and sizing decisions by reusing the business logo, color, and live menu link.

## Talking Points

- Table, counter, entrance, feedback, and full menu files are together.
- The printed QR opens the current live menu.
- Owners do not need Canva or a designer for basic deployment.
- Desktop and phone produce the same files.
- Premium stores can remove visible MenuList attribution where the shared policy applies.

## Avoid

- Do not call it a design studio.
- Do not promise print fulfillment.
- Do not imply owners can customize fonts, layouts, or themes inside this route.
