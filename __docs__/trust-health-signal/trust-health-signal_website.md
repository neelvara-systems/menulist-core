## Problem Statement

Every business owner worries: "Do customers still trust us?" They check sales numbers, read occasional reviews, ask regulars — but never get a clear, reliable answer. By the time trust drops visibly, damage is already done.

## Solution Statement

MenuList watches how visitors interact with your public page — not who they are, just overall patterns. Once a week, it updates a simple signal: Strong, Stable, or Weak. You glance at it, feel reassured (or alerted), and get on with your day.

## Feature Benefits

1. **One Word, Not a Dashboard** — "Customer Trust: Strong." That's all you see.
2. **Based on Real Behavior** — Not surveys, not guesses. Actual visitor patterns.
3. **Privacy-Safe** — No individual tracking. Only aggregate patterns.
4. **Automatic** — Shows up when you have enough visitors. No setup needed.

## FAQ

**Q: How does MenuList know if customers trust my business?**  
A: By analyzing overall visitor patterns — volume, consistency, and engagement. These naturally reflect trust levels.

**Q: Will I see charts or analytics?**  
A: No. Just one word: Strong, Stable, or Weak. We believe in clarity, not data overload.

**Q: Is my customers' privacy protected?**  
A: Absolutely. We only look at aggregate numbers — no individual tracking whatsoever.
