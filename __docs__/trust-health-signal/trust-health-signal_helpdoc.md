## Quick Summary

MenuList shows you a simple signal about customer trust — Strong, Stable, or Weak — based on how visitors interact with your public page. No dashboards, no complex data. Just a calm awareness signal.

---

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Your MenuList page must have regular visitors (50+ per week)
- The signal appears automatically — no setup needed

### When It Appears
The trust signal shows up on your dashboard after your business has had consistent visitors for at least 4 weeks. Before that, it stays hidden to ensure accuracy.

---

## How-To Guides

### Understanding the Signal

| Signal | What It Means | What To Do |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Strong** | Visitor patterns are healthy and stable | Nothing — keep doing what you're doing |
| **Stable** | Normal patterns, no concerns | Nothing — business as usual |
| **Weak** | Visitor patterns show a decline | Check your reviews, service quality, and hours accuracy |

### What Affects Trust?
- Consistent menu updates (customers see fresh info)
- Accurate hours (customers don't arrive when you're closed)
- Good reviews (reputation matters)
- Regular visitors (people coming back)

---

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

**Q: I don't see the trust signal**  
A: Your page needs at least 50 visitors per week for 4 consecutive weeks before the signal appears. Keep sharing your link!

**Q: My trust signal says "Weak" — what should I do?**  
A: Don't panic. Check recent reviews, make sure your hours and menu are accurate, and think about recent service quality. The signal is an early awareness tool, not an alarm.

**Q: How often does it update?**  
A: Once per week, automatically.

## Tips

- Keep your menu and hours updated — accuracy builds trust
