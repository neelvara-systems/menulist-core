## Elevator Pitch

"Do your customers still trust you? Most business owners rely on gut feeling. MenuList quietly watches visitor patterns and tells you — in one word — whether customer trust is holding strong or needs attention."

## Feature Narrative

Business owners live with a constant background worry: "Are we still good?" They check daily sales, read occasional reviews, ask staff — but never get a clear answer. MenuList already sees customer behavior at the entry point of the journey. By analyzing aggregate visitor patterns, it can give owners a simple, calm signal: Strong, Stable, or Weak. No dashboards. No analytics. Just awareness.

## Pitch Deck Outline

1. **Problem:** Owners don't know if customer trust is weakening until it's too late
2. **Solution:** One calm signal based on real customer behavior
3. **How it works:** MenuList analyzes visitor patterns → computes trust state weekly → shows one word
4. **What owner sees:** "Customer Trust: Strong" — that's it. No charts, no data.
5. **Why this matters:** Trust drops before revenue does. Early awareness saves businesses.
6. **CTA:** This feature activates automatically when your business has enough visitors.
