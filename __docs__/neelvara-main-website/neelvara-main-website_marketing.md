# Neelvara Main Website - Marketing And Messaging

**Status:** Implemented; pending owner/legal launch review
**Audience:** Founder, copy reviewer, implementation agent
**Public tone:** Calm, factual, entity-trust oriented

---

## 1. Messaging Job

The Neelvara website does not sell Neelvara.

It verifies the operating company behind the current operated product lineup, explains the operating focus, and directs product interest to the relevant product site.

The site should make a visitor understand:

- Neelvara Systems builds software infrastructure for customer-facing business information.
- MenuList, Answerlattice, and CampaignCue are operated by Neelvara Systems.
- The company scope is clear and narrow.
- Company, legal, privacy, and product-support routes are separated.

---

## 2. Core Message

Short:

> Software infrastructure for customer-facing business information.

Long:

> Neelvara Systems builds focused products that help businesses keep public facts, approved answers, and business context accurate, usable, and consistent across customer-facing surfaces.

Relationship:

> MenuList, Answerlattice, and CampaignCue are operated by Neelvara Systems.

Entity description:

> Neelvara Systems is the operating trade name used for software infrastructure for customer-facing business information.

---

## 3. Approved Public Claims

Allowed:

- Neelvara Systems builds software infrastructure for customer-facing business information.
- MenuList, Answerlattice, and CampaignCue are operated by Neelvara Systems.
- MenuList focuses on public business facts such as menus, hours, profiles, and customer-facing details.
- Answerlattice focuses on governed support answers, help content, and approved business responses.
- CampaignCue focuses on prepared business context for campaign briefs and reusable marketing assets.
- Neelvara Systems does not claim POS, payroll, accounting, CRM, delivery, or internal operations software on this company website.

Only show products approved for company-site mention. Do not add future-product cards, placeholder products, or private/internal product names.

---

## 4. Forbidden Public Positioning

Do not position Neelvara as:

- A SaaS product
- A restaurant software company
- An agency
- A product studio
- A holding company
- A group of companies
- A marketing company
- A customer support product
- A multi-product suite
- A MenuList-only company page

Do not use public claims that imply incorporation status, subsidiaries, large team size, offices, investors, press, or mature operating breadth unless legally true and documented.

---

## 5. Language Rules

Use:

- builds
- operates
- customer-facing business information
- public facts
- approved answers
- reusable business context
- operated products
- operating trade name
- company contact
- legal information

Avoid:

- the old relationship phrasing that made products sound passively listed instead of operated
- `parent company`
- vague transformation claims
- performance claims
- market leadership claims
- product-suite claims
- cleverness claims
- revenue claims
- automation hype
- `AI-powered`, `Smart`, `Dynamic`, or `Intelligent`

---

## 6. Navigation And CTAs

Allowed navigation:

- Products
- About
- Contact
- Legal
- Privacy
- Terms

Primary header navigation should show only `Products`, `About`, and `Contact`; Legal, Privacy, and Terms remain footer/legal-surface links.

Allowed CTAs:

- View Products
- Email Neelvara
- Contact Routes
- Email Legal
- Email Privacy

Avoid:

- Book a demo
- Trial signup CTA
- Get started
- Talk to sales
- Join the waitlist
- Create account
- Request pricing

---

## 7. Homepage Copy Direction

Hero headline:

> Neelvara Systems

Hero eyebrow:

> Software infrastructure for customer-facing business information

Hero support copy:

> Neelvara Systems builds focused products that help businesses keep public facts, approved answers, and business context accurate, usable, and consistent across customer-facing surfaces.

Do not make the H1 a slogan. The brand/entity name should be the first signal.

---

## 8. Presentation Direction

Use:

- Neelvara blue mesh, grain, and glass-panel treatment as a scoped visual system
- meaningful hero artifact focused on company routing instead of blank decoration or product-count proof
- problem-first bento and spotlight sections
- actual product marks for MenuList, Answerlattice, and CampaignCue
- direct contact-routing sections
- page-specific Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Legal, Contact, Products, About, and 404 content

Reject:

- internal implementation labels
- storage/API/Firebase claims in public copy
- product-count and page-count stats as homepage proof
- defensive homepage boundary rows that say what the site does not do before explaining what it routes
- customer logos or testimonials
- product-conversion claims
- broad SaaS feature grids that make the company site feel like another product

---

## 9. Product Copy Direction

Products section:

> Different products solve different parts of customer-facing business information.

Product summaries:

- MenuList: public business facts, starting with menus, hours, profiles, and customer-facing details.
- Answerlattice: governed answers for support, help content, and approved business responses.
- CampaignCue: prepared business context for campaign briefs and reusable marketing assets.

Product pricing, onboarding, support, documentation, privacy, and terms remain on individual product websites.

## 12. Review Checklist

Before launch, confirm:

- `Neelvara Systems` spelling is consistent.
- The operated-product relationship sentence appears where required.
- No inactive, private, or unapproved product appears as launched.
- No legal structure is overstated.
