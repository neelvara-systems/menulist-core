# Official Business Page — Help Documentation

**Audience:** Existing customers (restaurant/business owners)  
**Tone:** Simple, patient, step-by-step  
**Language:** Zero jargon

---

## Quick Summary

Your official business page is a single link you can share with anyone. When customers open it, they see your business name, photos, Google rating, whether you're open, and a button to view your full menu. They can also call you, message you on WhatsApp, get directions, reserve a table, or place an order — all from one page.

Only secure public links are shown to customers. If a reservation, order, directions, review, website, or social link is missing or not a valid secure public link, MenuList hides that action instead of sending customers to it. Source gate: `npm run verify:official-business-page-boundary`.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- A MenuList account with your business name and phone number entered

### Your Link is Already Ready

You don't need to set up your official page. It's created automatically when you sign up. You can find your link in your dashboard.

To find your link:

1. Open your MenuList dashboard
2. Look for "Your Official Business Link" at the top
3. Your link looks like: **yourbusiness.menulist.ai**

## How-To Guides

### How to Copy and Share Your Link

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Find the "Your Official Business Link" card
3. Tap **Copy Link**
4. Paste it in WhatsApp, Instagram bio, or anywhere you want

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Dashboard showing link card with Copy button

### How to Download Your QR Codes

You get TWO QR codes:

**Share QR** (for Instagram bio, packaging, business cards):

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Find the "Your Official Business Link" card
3. Tap **QR** button
4. Select **Share QR (Business Page)**
5. Tap **Download QR**

**Menu QR** (for table tents, dine-in):

1. Same steps, but select **Menu QR (Direct Menu)**
2. This takes customers straight to your menu without the identity page

> 📸 **Screenshot:** QR toggle showing Share vs Menu options

### How to Update Your Business Info

Your official page shows info from your Business Settings. To update:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**
2. Update your phone number, address, or hours
3. After the save succeeds, changes appear on your official page within the current 1-minute public cache window.

You don't need to "publish" the official page separately. It uses the saved business info after the public refresh path settles.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Business Settings with phone/address fields

### How to Add Your WhatsApp Number

If your WhatsApp number is different from your main phone:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**
2. Find **Official Page Settings**
3. Enter your WhatsApp number
4. The WhatsApp button on your page will use this number

### How to Change Your Page Color

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**
2. Find **Official Page Settings**
3. Tap on the color circle next to **Accent Color**
4. Choose a color that matches your brand
5. Your "View Menu" button and highlights will use this color

### How to Add a Short Description

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**
2. Find **Official Page Settings**
3. Enter a short description (max 40 characters)
4. Examples: "Pure Veg Restaurant", "Modern Indian Kitchen", "Family Pizza Café"

### How to Add Your Google Rating

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile** → **Official Page Settings**
2. Find **Google Reviews** section
3. Paste your Google review link
4. Enter your current star rating (e.g. 4.5)
5. Enter your review count (e.g. 320)
6. Your page will show: "4.5 ★ on Google (320)" with a link to your reviews

### How to Add Business Photos

You can add business photos (storefront, interior, or signature dish). The first 3 appear as the official page preview, and customers can tap a photo to view the rest:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile** → **Official Page Settings**
2. Find **Business Photos** section
3. Click on a photo slot to upload. A new empty slot appears after your current photos.
4. Choose an image from your phone or computer
5. To remove a photo, click the delete button on it

### How to Add a Cover Image

Your cover image appears at the top of your official business page.

1. Go to **More** → **Official Business Page**
2. Find **Cover Image**
3. Upload a photo, or tap **Generate cover**
4. Tap **Adjust** if the framing needs correction
5. Tap **Save Changes**

You can replace, remove, or regenerate the cover later from the same place.

### How to Add Custom Attributes

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile** → **Business Attributes**
2. Find **Custom Attributes**
3. Add the attribute name
4. Pick an icon or emoji
5. Save changes

### How to Add "Known For" Text

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile** → **Official Page Settings**
2. Find **Known For** field
3. Enter what your business is known for (max 40 characters)
4. Examples: "Wood-fired pizza", "Authentic Punjabi food", "Premium haircuts"

### How to Copy a Ready-to-Send Message

Instead of just copying your link, you can copy a full message to paste in WhatsApp:

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Find the "Your Official Business Link" card
3. Tap **Copy Message**
4. Paste it in WhatsApp, Instagram, or any chat
5. The message says: "Here's our menu and details: [your link]"

### How to Hide a Button

If you don't want to show the Call, WhatsApp, or Directions button:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**
2. Find **Official Page Settings**
3. Toggle off the button you want to hide

## What Customers See

When someone opens your link, they see:

- Your **logo** and **business name**
- Your **cover image** at the top, if added
- **Short description** and **"Known for"** text (if set)
- **Price range**, **area**, and **service modes** (dine-in, takeaway, etc.)
- Whether you're **open or closed** right now (with closing time)
- Your **Google rating** (if set) — links to your Google reviews
- **"Serving since [year]"** (if set)
- A big **View Menu** button (takes them to your full digital menu)
- **Business photos** (first 3 preview on the page; all uploaded photos available after tapping a photo)
- Buttons to **Call**, **WhatsApp**, **Directions**, **Google reviews**, **Feedback**, **Reserve**, or **Order** when enabled
- Your **address** and **today's hours**
- **Special note** if you add one
- **Full weekly hours**, **cuisine types**, separate **payment options**, **dietary options**, and amenities
- Links to your **Instagram**, **Facebook**, or **Website** (if added)
- **"Info verified"** freshness signal
- **"Official Page · Powered by MenuList"** footer

## Troubleshooting / FAQ

### My page shows old information

**Why this happens:** Changes take up to 1 minute to appear on your official page.

**How to fix it:**

1. Wait 1-2 minutes after making changes
2. Open your link in a new browser tab (or clear browser cache)
3. If it's still old after 5 minutes, contact support

### My page says "Menu coming soon"

**Why this happens:** You haven't published a menu yet.

**How to fix it:**

1. Go to **Menu** in your dashboard
2. Upload or create your menu
3. Publish it
4. Your official page will show the "View Menu" button automatically

### I can't find my link

**How to fix it:**

1. Log in to your MenuList dashboard
2. Look at the top of the page for "Your Official Business Link"
3. If you don't see it, go to **Settings** → **Business Profile**

### The WhatsApp button opens the wrong number

**How to fix it:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business Profile** → **Official Page Settings**
2. Check the WhatsApp number field
3. Make sure it includes your country code (e.g., +91 for India)
