---

## Feature Narrative

### The Problem

When a customer asks a restaurant "send me your menu" on WhatsApp, the owner scrambles:

- Sends an 8MB PDF that's outdated
- Shares a Zomato link (where competitors appear)
- Posts their Instagram (noisy, no hours or location)
- Links to a Google Drive folder

No single, clean, official link exists.

### The Solution

MenuList auto-creates an official business page for every restaurant. It shows:

- Who they are (name, logo, photos, Google rating, open/closed status)
- How to reach them (call, WhatsApp, directions, reserve, order)
- Their menu (one tap to full digital menu with conversion tracking)
- Structured business info (hours, cuisine, price range, services, payment methods)

The owner just shares one link. That's it. It replaces day-to-day PDFs and manual replies, and it gives their Google profile, Instagram bio, packaging, and website one clean official destination.

### Why This Matters

Once the owner starts using this link everywhere — Instagram bio, Google profile, WhatsApp, packaging, bills — MenuList becomes their default internet presence. The link spreads naturally. Customers recognize it. Removing MenuList becomes difficult.

## Sales Talking Points

### When client says: "I already have a website"

> Your website is for branding. This is your daily-use link for customers. When someone asks for your menu on WhatsApp, you won't send your full website — you'll send this. Both work together.

### When client says: "Instagram is enough"

> Instagram is for photos and marketing. But when customers want menu, hours, or directions, they shouldn't scroll posts. This link gives everything instantly in one tap.

### When client says: "I already have a Google profile"

> Google is for discovery. This is your official link you control and share everywhere. Google can change anytime, shows competitors, shows ads. This stays your permanent business link.

### When client says: "Why not just send PDF?"

> PDFs get outdated and are hard to read on phones. This always shows your latest menu automatically. You never need to resend a new menu again.

### When client says: "This is just a landing page"

> This is not a landing page. It's your official business link — like a Google Business listing, but you control the source. Fast, clean, and updated from your MenuList business data.

### The Killer Closing Line

> "From now on, whenever someone asks anything about your restaurant — just send this one link."
