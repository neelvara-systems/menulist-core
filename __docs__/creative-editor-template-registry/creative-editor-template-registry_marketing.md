# Creative Editor Template Registry - Marketing Notes

**Status:** Internal positioning  
**Last Updated:** June 15, 2026

## Internal One-Liner

Owners can turn a print asset they already customized into a reusable business template without learning a design system.

## Value

- Saves repeated layout work.
- Keeps QR links current.
- Gives owners a personal design shelf without replacing MenuList's governed templates.
- Lets MenuList ship better template catalogs over time while preserving each owner's saved designs.

## Claim Boundaries

- Do not claim a marketplace.
- Do not claim automatic brand generation.
- Do not claim collaboration/versioning.
- Do not claim saved templates sync to external design tools.

## Suggested Owner Copy

- "Save as template"
- "Saved designs"
- "Reuse this design next time"
- "MenuList keeps the QR link current"
