# QR WhatsApp Experiments Help Doc Draft

**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** June 25, 2026

## What It Does

QR WhatsApp Experiments helps you compare two printed QR campaign versions and see which one brings better WhatsApp conversations.

Use it for offers, bookings, feedback, support, or repeat-customer campaigns.

## When To Use It

Use an experiment when you want to test:

- plain QR vs branded QR;
- one printed CTA vs another;
- counter placement vs table placement;
- direct WhatsApp vs a short landing page first;
- one offer message vs another.

Do not use it for your normal menu QR. Your normal menu QR should open your live page directly.

## How It Works

1. Choose the campaign goal.
2. Create two variants.
3. Download both print files.
4. Place them in comparable locations.
5. Let the campaign run long enough to collect useful results.
6. Compare scans, WhatsApp taps, chats started, opt-ins, and outcomes.
7. Keep the winner.

## Good Test Setup

Keep one thing different at a time.

Good:

```text
Same offer, same location, same size.
Only the QR design changes.
```

Bad:

```text
Variant A is on the counter with no discount.
Variant B is on every table with a discount.
```

That does not show which QR is better.

## Consent Note

A scan is not the same as permission to send future WhatsApp messages.

If you want to send future offers or updates, ask the customer clearly and let them opt out.

## Print Check

Before printing in bulk:

- scan each variant on iPhone and Android;
- test from real distance;
- test the final printed material;
- keep the QR area clean;
- do not cover the QR with a logo.

## Result Rule

Do not choose the winner only by scan count. Choose the one that brings better conversations, opt-ins, bookings, orders, or redemptions.
