# QR WhatsApp Experiments Marketing

**Status:** Internal
**Last Updated:** June 25, 2026

## One-Liner

Test which printed QR brings real WhatsApp conversations, not just scans.

## Positioning

QR WhatsApp Experiments helps a business owner compare two physical campaign variants and choose the one that creates useful WhatsApp conversations, opt-ins, bookings, orders, or redemptions.

It is not a QR maker, WhatsApp blast tool, or print shop. It is a measurement layer for permission-based local campaigns.

## Good Claims

- Compare two printed QR campaign variants.
- Use scan-safe branded action templates as the creative shell.
- See scans, WhatsApp taps, chats started, opt-ins, and outcomes separately.
- Keep normal menu QR codes direct and simple.
- Use tracked campaign tokens so WhatsApp conversations can be matched back to the printed asset.
- Decide by qualified outcome, not scan curiosity.

## Avoid

- Do not say MenuList guarantees more sales.
- Do not imply WhatsApp partnership or official WhatsApp certification.
- Do not say scan equals opt-in.
- Do not promote cold WhatsApp outreach.
- Do not market it as automation before provider send, consent ledger, and opt-out handling exist.
- Do not merge this copy into ordinary Assets/Menu Kit pages.
- Do not promote artistic QR patterns unless scan reliability has been proven.

## Buyer Story

An owner prints two offer cards:

```text
A: Plain QR with "Scan to get offer on WhatsApp"
B: Branded QR with the same offer and CTA
```

MenuList shows which one drove more useful WhatsApp chats and opt-ins. If B gets more scans but A gets more bookings, A wins.
