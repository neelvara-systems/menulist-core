# PresenceOS Rules

**Status:** Mandatory
**Created:** August 12, 2026
**Canonical docs:** `__docs__/founder-public-presence/`
**Public identity:** `Proof & State`

## Trigger

Load this rule and the canonical folder when a task mentions or materially
affects:

- X, Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, or
  another social/creator channel for the founder;
- Proof & State, personal brand, founder account, pseudonymity, anonymity,
  build-in-public, creator research, audience growth, posting, replies,
  community participation, content schedules, account metrics, daily progress,
  reminders, missed actions, pending founder work, accountability, or habit
  building;
- any repo, product, or research finding that might become useful public
  builder content;
- an external post, account, platform change, tactic, published result,
  objection, correction, or privacy risk.

## Identity And Privacy Rule

The public identity is `Proof & State`, first-choice handle
`@proofandstate`. It is a transparent pseudonym, not a fake person.

Never expose or place into public material:

- the founder's legal or personal name, face, natural voice, personal
  biography, precise location, personal accounts, personal email, or phone;
- personal Git identity, repository history, local username, home path,
  hostname, device name, or notification data;
- customer content, tenant identifiers, credentials, private conversations,
  unreleased vulnerabilities, abuse thresholds, or strategic sequencing;
- identifying image, video, audio, EXIF, document, browser, terminal, commit,
  QR-code, reflection, or file metadata.

Do not claim platform anonymity. Platforms may privately require accurate
registration, phone, location, payment, recovery, or identity information.

Do not link MenuList, Answerlattice, Neelvara, a product domain, a repository,
an app-store listing, or a founder hub until the product-connection audit in the
privacy doc passes. Unknown means blocked.

## Proactive Capture Rule

During related product, code, docs, audit, research, or strategy work, capture a
new ledger item without another reminder when the work reveals a material
public-useful lesson involving:

- architecture, reliability, security, cost, validation, recovery,
  observability, invalidation, or lifecycle state;
- AI-agent contracts, deterministic guardrails, audit methodology, or a
  corrected assumption;
- product judgment, removed complexity, protected boundaries, or a meaningful
  trade-off;
- a privacy-safe artifact, failure, fix, before/after, or verified result;
- support correctness, canonical authority, drift, public truth, or
  owner/founder relief.

Do not capture routine work, private-only detail, minor refactors, or generic
advice merely to satisfy cadence.

## Evidence And Product Boundaries

- Code, runtime, docs, verifiers, approved proof, and the privacy gate outrank
  content strategy and external advice.
- Label example versus measured result, local versus hosted proof, modeled
  versus actual cost, and inference versus confirmed evidence.
- Answerlattice is the closest audience-to-product bridge, but naming and claims
  remain behind privacy, workload, evidence, and distribution gates.
- MenuList supplies generalized case studies but retains its separate
  owner-focused distribution and claim rules.
- CampaignCue remains absent until its public distribution gate opens.
- Neelvara remains a quiet trust/entity surface.
- Internal, private, add-on, reserved, or planned surfaces are not account
  subjects by default.

## Platform Decisions

- Start X as the primary channel.
- Start Reddit comments carefully and natively; current community rules are
  checked immediately before participation.
- Instagram requires the native-visual and Meta-privacy admission gate.
- Facebook and LinkedIn are not initial alias channels.
- YouTube requires repeated demand for faceless long-form material.
- Never create a fake Facebook profile or fake human avatar.

## Platform Research Rule

- Verify unstable policy, feature, price, recommendation, and community-rule
  claims using current primary sources.
- Treat creator self-reports as evidence of their account, not universal causal
  proof.
- Before borrowing a pattern, record the creator's prior audience, history,
  product timing, format, role, and other advantages.
- Preserve checked dates, links, decisions, and revalidation triggers in the
  research ledger.
- Borrow mechanisms, not persona, wording, visuals, identity, or outcome.
- For open recommender code, pin the reviewed commit and keep candidate
  retrieval, predicted-action scoring, parameter defaults, reranking,
  visibility filtering, and report eligibility separate. Never turn a raw
  coefficient or tool threshold into a growth instruction, and never divide
  coefficients into likes-equivalent engagement targets.
- Prefer the account's own results and `Under the Hood` label evidence when
  eligible. Low impressions alone do not prove a visibility restriction.
- “No material change” is preferred over repetitive research filler.

## Draft And Engagement Rule

- Use the calm, direct, proof-first Proof & State voice.
- Before asking the founder to publish any post, explain the selection rationale:
  why this topic now, which audience problem it serves, why the chosen format
  fits, what evidence supports it, which privacy/product boundary applies, and
  why the strongest obvious alternatives were deferred. Never provide only the
  final copy and expect blind execution.
- Every publication handoff must state the exact date, timezone, bounded
  publication window, why that window was chosen, and the one instruction to
  follow if the window is missed. Treat timing as a controlled operating
  baseline, never as an algorithm guarantee, and never create catch-up volume.
- Target serious AI-assisted founders and small teams productionizing SaaS.
- Native X, Reddit, and Instagram material is written separately. No mechanical
  cross-posting.
- A reply must add a mechanism, trade-off, verified example, material question,
  or sourced correction. Praise-only filler and visibility replies are skipped.
- Reddit is comments-first. No product name, link, solicitation, or concealed
  plug in the first month.
- Builder utility remains the majority; product proof is evidence-led; direct
  promotion stays rare and gated.
- There is no hard one-original-post-per-day prohibition. The initial weekly
  cadence remains the learning baseline, and every additional same-day post
  must independently pass the topic, evidence, privacy, format, timing, and
  non-duplication gates. Founder availability alone is not a publication reason.
- Preserve captured, validated, drafting, founder-review, approved, published,
  measuring, reusable, deferred, rejected, retired, and blocked history.

## PresenceOS Boundary

PresenceOS consists of maintained documentation, approved local visual assets,
manual workflows, and a daily internal Codex research heartbeat.

Do not turn it into a public product, dashboard, database, scraper, reply bot,
auto-posting system, engagement network, lead-harvesting tool, or product
runtime without a separate architecture decision.

The morning and evening automations may research, compare, capture, redact,
draft, read recent task results, and update the local progress tracker. They
must return one bounded action or “No material change,” not a volume-maximizing
content queue.

## Progress Accountability Rule

- The progress tracker is the source of truth for founder-owned PresenceOS
  actions, blockers, dependencies, evidence, and carry-forward.
- Mark an external action `done` only from founder-provided evidence or a clear
  outcome message. Never infer completion from silence.
- A due action without evidence becomes `unconfirmed`, not failed.
- Ask the founder to reply with `DONE <URL or result>`, `BLOCKED <reason>`, or
  `SKIP <reason>`; never request or store credentials, codes, or private
  verification material.
- The automated daily brief gives exactly one primary action. After a completed
  action, additional same-day work may be admitted one action at a time only
  when it is distinct, evidence-backed, useful now, and not quota or catch-up
  volume. Carry forward at most one unfinished action.
- The evening reminder acknowledges recorded completion; otherwise it names
  one smallest pending action in a neutral, non-shaming tone.
- When blocked, guide the founder through the smallest safe unblock step and
  keep external actions under founder control.

## External-Action Boundary

The founder's request for proactive guidance authorizes repository
documentation, read-only research, candidate capture, privacy review, local
asset preparation, and drafts.

It does not authorize external account creation, profile changes, publication,
replies, comments, DMs, follows, subscriptions, purchases, verification,
advertisements, spend, or public disclosure without an explicit instruction
covering that action.

## Required Handoff

When related work changes PresenceOS, report:

- what changed or was captured;
- the reasoning behind any post selected for founder review or publication;
- the evidence and privacy/product boundary;
- the current status and one next action;
- whether any step requires founder review or explicit external authority.
